### Description - HOM202
- Main objective: This course provides an overview of the operations of a mid-size full-service hotel, from lodging to food and beverage, marketing and sales, human resources,... The focus of this course will be on explaining the functions of each department in a hotel, the inter-relationships among them, and management skills development. This course offers up-to-date information of multiple aspects in hotel operations, and essential insights for future hotel managers. -Description: Hotel operational management introduces an overview of thel operation and organization of a midsize hotel. The course focuses on the front office, housekeeping, F&B and how they relate to each other in order to provide excellent service to customers. Other departments are also explained to provide a full understanding of the organization of a hotel. Students are to practice their analyzing and problem solving skills with up-to-date real life example and current trends. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, demonstration, roleplay, project based learning, group work

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HOM202

- **CLO1**: Describe functions of different departments of a hotel and their interrelationships among them.
- **CLO2**: Respect ethics and professional standards of behavior in the hospitality industry
- **CLO3**: Illustrate the basic jobs of each department in a hotel.
- **CLO4**: Solve the problems in common hotel operation situations
- **CLO5**: Categorize hotels by market segmentations: level of service, location, …
- **CLO6**: Apply management skills in practical hotel operation case study
- **CLO7**: Identify the role of service quality and service performance in hotel management
- **CLO8**: Design an overall plan of a mid-size full-service hotel with distinctive marketing ideas
- **CLO9**: Explain the pricing concepts in hotels and revenue management theories
- **CLO10**: Solve accounting problems for practical case study
