### Description - CST491
The Graduation Thesis is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate computer science studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further their knowledge and skills which they have found to be especially interesting or beneficial to them.  As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the graduation thesis and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 2-3 computer science students. Each thesis of student(s) will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, writing a thesis in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.

### Student Tasks - CST491
(1)Choose the project within the selected concentration (2)Divide and schedule work (3)Attend all scheduled meetings with supervisor (4)Produce document and product according to project requirement (5)Defense individually work in front of examination board

## Assessment - CST491

### Progress Reports
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: 5
- Note: 50% of Final CP Mark with weight (On-going assessment)
'- Presentation: (Report 1) 15% (5% from other student group(peer review), 10% from supervisor)
- Plan: (Report 2) 15% (15% from supervisor)
- Implementation: (Report 3) 70% (70% from supervisor where 50% for code and data, 10% for prototype documentation, 10% report of experimentation and transfer)

### Oral Thesis Defense
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- Note: Referee committee give mark for each student 1st times: before defense (based on thesis)

Referee committee give mark for each student 2nd times: after defense (based on student's performance on oral thesis defense)

(Final defense mark = evarage mark of 1st and 2nd times for each student)
