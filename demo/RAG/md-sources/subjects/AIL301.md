# AIL301 - MACHINE LEARNING


## Course Information - AIL301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions
Home study: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Must passed AIG201 - Artificial Intelligent & MAS291 - Probability & Statistics
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - AIL301
The course provided student with knowledge about major machine learning paradigms (supervised & unsupervised), problems (classification, clustering, ...), and important machine learning tools (MaxEnt, SVM, ANN - DNN, HMM, CRF, ...)

### Student Tasks - AIL301
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.
