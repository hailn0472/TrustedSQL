### Description - PFL401
Description: Preparation for entry into the profession of visual communication. Topics include portfolio preparation, written communication, interview, and job search skills. Each student organizes a web-based portfolio or a traditional print portfolio presentation. Goals include demonstrating and understanding design principles while revealing the creative problem-solving abilities of each student.

### Student Tasks - PFL401
1. Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination 2. Actively participate in class activities 3. Fulfil tasks given by instructor after class 4. Use their own laptop in class only for learning purposes. Computer with some version of graphic software such as Illustration, InDesign, Microsoft Word for written assignments, and POWERPOINT or PDF for presentations 5. Read the textbook in advance 6. Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment. 7. Complete course assignments with full contents of exercises and submit on time.

### Tools - PFL401
Individual tools prepared by students: - Sketching papper sheet (A4) - Color, pencil, eraser,... - Laptop

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PFL401

- **CLO1**: Arrange and tell a story about their work in their portfolio in an advanced level of craftsmanship in a way that represents a distinct and personal point-of-view. Combine the knowledge of how to best discuss their work in their portfolio, along with their design process and design philosophy.
- **CLO2**: Know what an employer is looking for in entry-level design positions.
- **CLO3**: Apply the criteria of page layout; grid, composition, balance, and unity to a portfolio of work in both printed and digital media.
- **CLO4**: Construct a basic portfolio of work in an online/digital environment and other networks.
- **CLO5**: Compose a compelling, well-written, grammatically correct Business Package including resume, cover letter, business card, and printed and online portfolio.
- **CLO6**: Actively discuss, critique, and engage in professional reviews of their own and their peers’ works.
