### Description - EVN203
-Objective: To provide an overall and in-depth competence of wedding planning and management -Description: It gives a comprehensive and in-depth knowlodege of different aspects of wedding planning and management, starting from supplying a strong foundation knowledge in the cultural, historical, social, and political influences on weddings and marriage; to accommodating with all of the elements essential to the practice of planning weddings; as well as a clear overview of what it takes to start a wedding consulting business. -Teaching method: lecturing in combination with tutorial classes, case study analysis, real event planning practice

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EVN203

- **CLO1**: To get an insight into the role of the wedding consultants and their appreciation of and sensitivity to the diverse needs of the clients
- **CLO2**: To establish the cultural, religious, historical, political and social context of the weddings
- **CLO3**: To examine the individual fundamental elements that together lead to the design and implementation of weddings
- **CLO4**: To develop procedures for the weddings and manage resources and logistics to ensure weddings are delivered in line with event plans
- **CLO5**: To monitor and evaluate events to ensure customer and business requirements are met
- **CLO6**: To achieve and practice a professional manner of an event planner with high sense of responsibility, detail orientedness, servicemind and teamwork spirit
