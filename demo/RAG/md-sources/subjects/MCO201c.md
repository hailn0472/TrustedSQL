### Description - MCO201c
This course facilitates students' comprehension of the impact of transmedia storytelling on engaging and influencing audiences. Students will gain expertise in creating, distributing, and participating in cohesive stories across various traditional and digital channels—for purposes including entertainment, marketing, and social activism. Ultimately, learners will proficiently create and communicate their own transmedia narratives across diverse media channels.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MCO201c

- **CLO1**: Understand and acquire the capability to develop transmedia storytelling strategies for diverse, engaging narratives.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate proficiency in effectively engaging with and understanding diverse audiences in storytelling contexts.
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate the capacity to create cohesive user experiences across multiple digital platforms
- **CLO4**: Possess the ability to ultilise emerging technologies for effective story dissemination.
