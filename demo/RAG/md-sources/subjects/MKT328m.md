### Description - MKT328m
This Course is based on the Coursera Specialization "Digital Marketing", which covers both the dynamics and the global aspects of digital marketing. You'll learn about the impacts of digital technologies on marketing communication strategies, as well as the core processes of planning a digital marketing campaign and how Digital Revolution impacted Marketing in an Analog World. At the end of the course, you'll be able to select the right marketing channels for the product and know how to plan a digital campaign , as well as apply critical thinking skills for successful marketing of Analog products and services in a Digital age. *Teaching method: This is a blended-learning course, in which students will take a look at the videos & slides and reading on Coursera first at home, then rewatch & discuss key questions with classmates and teachers at classes.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT328m

- **CLO1**: Understand the impacts of digital media and information technologies on communication and marketing
- **CLO2**: Develop a conceptual understanding of several digital marketing strategies
- **CLO3**: Develop a purposeful content marketing strategy to achieve your business and marketing goals
- **CLO4**: Effectively mix paid, earned, owned, and shared media channels to discover, reach, and engage your customers
- **CLO5**: Evaluate and measure the success and risks of digital marketing campaigns
- **CLO6**: Understand the key differences between Analog and Digital and the implications of these differences for marketing.
- **CLO7**: Develop and apply critical thinking skills regarding the role of both the Analog and the Digital upon both firms and consumers.
