## Course Information - SST301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: EDT202c
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - SST301
The course provides students with basic knowledge of working principle, basic characteristic and different sensor manufacturing technologies. The course provides students the knowledge to support classify sensors according to their working principles. The course provides students with the opportunity to hands-on practice with the variety of sensors. At the same time, help students gain more knowledge about the evaluation and application of each specific type of sensor.

### Student Tasks - SST301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Student is reponsible to do all Worklabs - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU LMS at https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information

### Tools - SST301
- TinkerCAD - Arduino IDE - Sensors

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SST301

- **CLO1**: Have an overview of all types of sensors
- **CLO2**: Understand and distinguish the different sensors manufactuing technology
- **CLO3**: Understand the properties, structure, and fucntions of the different types of sensors
- **CLO4**: Understand and have knowledge of the properties, structure, and functions of each specific sort of sensor
- **CLO5**: Knowledge and skills in embedded programming with the different sensors
- **CLO6**: Evaluate the different sensing techniques and technologies to apply to each specific case and environment
- **CLO7**: Applying sensor devices corresponding to sensor manufacturing technologies to specific applications in industrial and residential systems
- **CLO8**: Ability to do small project using the different sensors
