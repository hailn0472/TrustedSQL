### Description - CEM201
This course will cover the definition and history of the "celebrity" concept, as well as the influence of celebrities on relevant fields of art, culture, and society in general. Taking an inter-disciplinary approach, and integrating theory with practice, this course equips students with the celebrity culture knowledge and skills to effectively manage the celebrities within both international and Vietnamese contexts.

### Student Tasks - CEM201
1. Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination 2. Actively participate in class activities 3. Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class 4. Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose 5. Read the textbook in advance 6. Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CEM201

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate an understanding of the concepts, theories and practice of celebrity management
- **CLO2**: Analyse and develop strategies for celebrity promotion using the theories, models and methods of celebrity management
- **CLO3**: Gain the capacity to identify and handle issues and crises relating to celebrity
- **CLO4**: Develop a critical attitude towards celebrity management
- **CLO5**: Identify and apply ethical standards of practice when dealing with celebrity management issues
- **CLO6**: Skills in analysis and problem solving; proficient in presentation and teamwork skills
