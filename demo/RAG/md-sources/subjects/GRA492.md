# GRA492 - Graduation Thesis (For Finance Concentration of BBA and BFB)


## Course Information - GRA492

- **Credits**: 5
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Duration 15 weeks
Contact time: 28 times (totally equivalent to 10 sessions)
Self-study: 180 sessions (=2session/day x 6day/week x 15 week)
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: (1) OJT (2) 5 concentration courses in Finance concentration and RMB301 (Business research method)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - GRA492
The thesis intends to integrate contents already covered in previous courses, as well as to provide an in-depth exploration of a topic of special interest or career relevance to the participant. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 3-5 students, however each student will defend the thesis individually in front of a thesis committee. Students must work closely with an academic supervisor and are required to submit a comprehensive written report as well as make an oral presentation of the research results in front of a professional academic panel. The thesis' topic is concerned with such areas as (1) the behavior and determinants of security prices, including stocks, bonds, options, and futures; (2) the capital budgeting, financing,and working capital management decisions of firms; (3) corporate governance; (4) the management and regulation of financial institutions and financial market; (5) application of new financial products in Vietnamese market.

### Student Tasks - GRA492
(1) Choose the thesis topic within the selected concentration (2) Divide and schedule work (3) Attend all scheduled meetings with the appointed supervisor (4) Produce reports or outcomes according to thesis requirements (5) Defense individually work in front of an examination board (6) All of above must be worked in the appointed group

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GRA492

- **CLO1**: The students would be able to identify topic background, topic relevance, relevance of research question, overview of dissertation structure.
- **CLO2**: The students would be able to understand deeply the necessary steps to carry out a research project and to write a structured report/dissertation.
- **CLO3**: The students would be able to understand and how Financial Models are applied, implemented to solve the real practical problems of the business throughout the process of doing dissertation.
- **CLO4**: The students would be able to implement and possess their own project in the application to all types of business model in the near future.
- **CLO5**: The students would be confident with their final product of dissertation which could be used to apply on solving the real practical problem of the business world
- **CLO6**: The students would be able to conduct an appropriate literature review and review of literature related to the research question.
- **CLO7**: The students would also be able to clearly state the gap in literature with respect to research questions and with selected theoritical models.
- **CLO8**: The students would be able to understand and apply methods used to collect data, data collection methods, sample characteristics, limitations of the research project
- **CLO9**: The students would be able to design and implement appropriate qualitative and/or quantitative methods
- **CLO10**: The students would able to systematically present the data before interpretation of the results.
- **CLO11**: The students would be able to analyse the data and do discussions of findings relatively to the literature review
- **CLO12**: The students would be able to answer to the research questions, explain the relevance of answer to practice and make recommendations
- **CLO13**: The students would be able to understand what Harvard referencing is and how it would correctly be applied during the process of dissertation
