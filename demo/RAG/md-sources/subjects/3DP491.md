# 3DP491 - Digital project 3D_Dự án kỹ thuật số 3D


## Course Information - 3DP491

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)
= 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ANM322 and ANA401
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - 3DP491
-This course introduces students to a professional studio experience where their goal is to create a 3D animated short film from scratch using Autodesk Maya.  -Students will be responsible for all aspects of production, from developing their own original story, creating storyboards and concept art to finalizing textures, animation, character models, and lighting in a complete short film.

### Student Tasks - 3DP491
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose

### Tools - 3DP491
- Internet - Autodeks Maya, Film Editing Software (After Effects or Adobe Premiere) - Textbook

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - 3DP491

- **CLO1**: Coming up with an idea that is suitable to create a short 3D animated.
- **CLO2**: Be able to organize and implement Digital art & design projects effectively.
- **CLO3**: Writing the script. Students will discover a lot when they translate the idea into written words.
- **CLO4**: Creating storyboard to pre-visualize the shorts.
- **CLO5**: Shoot live-action reference. It’s good practice to record yourself on video doing what your 3D character acts in your film.
- **CLO6**: Apply 3D skills such as Modeling, Texturing, Rigging to prepare ready characters for animation.
- **CLO7**: Apply animating workflow to animate the entire film.
- **CLO8**: Shading, lighting and rendering to establish the looks and feels of the film.
- **CLO9**: Adding sounds and editing sequences to create final film.
- **CLO10**: Students have a proficient in presentation, teamworks and evaluation products. Having a positive attitude and a professional working style
