### Description - IMA301
This course provides an overview of the M&A process – from strategy and valuation to execution and post-merger integration management. Students develop an in-depth understanding of the appropriate skills and tools to successfully analyze all major elements of the acquisition process discussed in the local and global context. The course will require the application of what the student previously learned in other courses such as finance, accounting, business law, micro and macroeconomics, negotiation, and management.  - Teaching method: Lectures, in-class practice assignments, problem-solving discussions, and team-based activities.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IMA301

- **CLO1**: Define the common terminology in M&A, key characteristics of expected M&A transactions and the evolution of M&A
- **CLO2**: Evaluate the mechanism of M&A takeover tactics and defenses as well as explain the importance of corporate governance in M&A
- **CLO3**: Apply the typical phases of M&A process from developing strategy and acquisition plan, through preparing for negotiating deals, and postmerger or post-closing integration
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate understanding of business valuations basics
- **CLO5**: Analyze corporate exit strategies as a result of mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, spin-offs, bankruptcy, liquidation
- **CLO6**: Discuss alternative deal structures such as alliances, joint ventures, partnerships, licensing, franchising, etc.
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate the ability to produce a clear oral report and/or academic written report, which follows research ethic, that applies understanding of key concepts in international merger and acquisitions.
- **CLO8**: Practice the ability to investigate and cooperate with others effectively in teams to manage M&A deal structuring process
