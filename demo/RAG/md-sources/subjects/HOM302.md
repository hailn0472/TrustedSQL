### Description - HOM302
Main objective: This course provides students the understanding of the various approaches, design and strategies for achieving operational success in major service sectors such as health care, technical support services, banking and financial services, transportation; yet mainly focus on restaurants, hotels and resorts. -Description: The course addresses strategic analysis and operational decision making in mananing a service bussiness. The course illustrate the steps of creating the service concept and operations strategy, the design of effective service delivery systems, quality management and improvement which ensure operational competitiveness in service businesses. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, demonstration, roleplay, project based learning, group work.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HOM302

- **CLO1**: Understand the key challenges facing service operations managers and the role of service operations management, its contribution to organisational success.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate highly professtional attitude and discipline to the team, instructor and school regulation.
- **CLO3**: Develop and apply the service concept to define and communicate the nature of the business, create organisational alignment, drive innovation and strategic advantage, and design the service.
- **CLO4**: Develop ‘customer’ relationships and gain insight into customers’ expectations and perceptions to create satisfied customers.
- **CLO5**: Develop supplier relationships
- **CLO6**: Design the service process and the customer experience.
- **CLO7**: Organize, measure, control and manage service operations.
- **CLO8**: Arrange human resources effectively to deliver great service.
- **CLO9**: Develop a range of tools and learn from other operations to improve service performance
- **CLO10**: Create and implement an organisation’s service strategy and a world-class organisation and deliver
