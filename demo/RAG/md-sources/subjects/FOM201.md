### Description - FOM201
FRONT OFFICE OPERATIONS & MANAGEMENT introduces concepts of organization, communication, ethics and policy within a hotel. The primary focus is the front office, housekeeping, reservations and night audit departments and other departments are discussed to provide and understanding of how they relate to the front office. An introduction of basic analyses, techniques and trends in both policy and technology are reviewed, and the text gives students a “real world” understanding of the hotel industry balancing its past, present and future. Indepth coverage of The Property Management System is included and additional chapters feature analysis of the physical makeup of hotels, yield management, operational techniques, hotel guest and hotel employee

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FOM201

- **CLO1**: Develop an organization chart depicting the functional relationships among hotel divisions and departments.
- **CLO2**: Explain the function and operation of the various systems, forms, equipment, and computer applications found in the front office.
- **CLO3**: Construct an efficient reservation system that records crucial information while avoiding problems in processing various types of reservations.
- **CLO4**: Construct a registration system that helps ensure a hotel's profitability while meeting the needs of guests by using effective guestroom sales techniques and efficient credit establishment procedures.
- **CLO5**: Develop an efficient communication system to operate within the front office and between the front office and departments such as housekeeping and maintenance.. LO6. Develop procedures regarding hotel safety and security, with particular emphasis on key control and emergencies.
- **CLO6**: Develop procedures for handling complaints.
- **CLO7**: Follow basic hotel accounting procedures ranging from posting accounts to conducting cash and check transactions at the front desk.
- **CLO8**: Develop and follow check-out and settlement procedures.
- **CLO9**: Explain and perform the steps involved in the night audit process.
