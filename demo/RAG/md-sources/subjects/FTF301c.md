## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FTF301c

- **CLO1**: Analyze the foundations of technical analysis
- **CLO2**: Understand the main principles of technical analysis
- **CLO3**: Understand the principles of chart analysis with proficient skills in identifying chart patterns, market trends, support and resistance levels
- **CLO4**: Proficient in drawing and interpreting stock market focasting trendlines with Fibonacci sequences, Dow Theory and other theories
- **CLO5**: Proficient in using moving averages, RSI, MACD, and other key technical indicators in analyzing price movements and trading signals
- **CLO6**: Formulate trading strategies and evaluate the risks and rewards based on technical analysis
- **CLO7**: Make appropriate decisions by considering technical analysis, fundamental analysis, and other forms of market analysis
