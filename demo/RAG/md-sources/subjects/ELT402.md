# ELT402 - Biên dịch tiếng Anh 2


## Course Information - ELT402

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 105 hours self-study (including projects preparation)
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ELT301
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ELT402
This course instructs students how to apply translation techniques in dealing with interlingual translation of different types of texts including administrative forms and documents (degrees, certificate, contracts..); business forms and documents (business transaction agreement, financial statements); technical news and documents; socio-cultural news and documents. After taking this course, students should be able to: - Demonstrate a clear understanding of different types and formats of texts - Demonstrate a clear understanding terms used the administrative documents, technical, economic, scientific, socio-cultural news and documents... - Translate the mentioned kinds of news and documents from Vietnamese into English and vice-versa.

### Student Tasks - ELT402
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptops in class only for learning purposes - Read the theory in advance

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELT402

- **CLO1**: enhance student’s linguistic competence in translating administrative documents, and translate those documents with precision, accuracy and consistency
- **CLO2**: enhance student’s linguistic competence in translating buisness forms and financial documents , and and translate those documents with precision, accuracy and consistency
- **CLO3**: enhance student’s linguistic competence in translating technical documentation, and translate those documents with precision, accuracy and consistency
- **CLO4**: analyse and translate a complex using appropriate analytical methods and the ability to synthesise information in socio - cultural news and documents
- **CLO5**: Have a professional working attitude, nad have ability to work in a team
