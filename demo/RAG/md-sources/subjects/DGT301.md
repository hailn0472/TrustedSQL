### Description - DGT301
The course is to provide students with knowledge in Digital Signal Processing. The course focus on DSP theory and the practical applications of DSP. The course has following contents: 1. Introduction to DSP 2. Discrete-Time Signals & Systems 3. Tools and Softwares for Digital Signal Processing 4. z-Transform 5. Frequency Analysis of Signals & Systems 6. Discrete Fourier Transform 7. Efficient Computation of DFT: Fast Fourier Transform 8. Implementation of Discrete-Time Systems 9. Design of Digital Filters 10. Sampling & Reconstruction of Signals 11. Multirate Digital Signal Processing 12. Power Spectrum Estimation 13. Introduction DS Processor & Realisation of FIR filter on the hardware  Assessment scheme:  1) On-going asessment: - 01 Assignment: 20% - 02 quizzes: 2x15%=30% - exercises: 10% 2) Final Exam: 40% 3) Final Result: 100%  Completion Criteria: 1) Every on-going assessment component >0 2) Final Exam Score >=4 & Final Result >=5

- Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. '- Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time. - Constantly follow https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DGT301

- **CLO1**: Understand the fundamental concepts of DSP theory such as sampling theory, discrete frequency, Discrete-Time Signals & Systems
- **CLO2**: Understand the analytical tools such as Fourier transforms, Discrete Fourier transforms, Fast Fourier Transforms and Z-Transforms required for digital signal processing.
- **CLO3**: Use Tools and Softwares for Digital Signal Processing
- **CLO4**: Get familiarized with various structures of IIR and FIR systems.
- **CLO5**: Design and realize various digital filters for digital signal processing.
- **CLO6**: Implement Discrete-Time Systems
