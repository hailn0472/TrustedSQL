### Description - SYB302
The Entrepreneurship Specialization examines the entrepreneurial mindset and skill sets, indicators of innovation opportunities, critical steps to bring innovations to the marketplace, and innovation strategies to establish and maintain a competitive advantage. Fundamentals of new venture financing are explored with attention to capital structures for new ventures, term sheets and how to negotiate them, and the differences between early-stage versus later-stage financing. You will develop an understanding of how to develop winning investor pitches, who and when to pitch, how to avoid common mistakes, and how to ‘get to the close’. In the final Capstone Project, you will apply the tools and concepts from the specialization courses by crafting a comprehensive, customer-validated business model, and creating a business plan and investor pitch.

### Student Tasks - SYB302

- **CLO2**: Be able to identify and analyze entrepreneurial opportunities.
- **CLO3**: Understand how to enhance your entrepreneurial mindset.
- **CLO4**: Improve your strategic decision-making.
- **CLO5**: Understand how to build innovative business models.
- **CLO6**: Understand how to create strategies to bring innovations to market and to develop an innovation portfolio and business model canvas for your venture.
- **CLO7**: Understand key indicators of innovation opportunities.
- **CLO8**: Understand critical steps for entrepreneurs to bring innovations to the marketplace
- **CLO9**: Determine valuable innovation strategies for new ventures to establish and maintain a competitive advantage.
- **CLO10**: Understand how to secure funding for your venture.
- **CLO11**: Examine the many financing options available to get your new venture funded.
- **CLO12**: Learn the basics of finance, valuations, dilution and non-dilutive funding sources.
- **CLO13**: Understand capital structure for new ventures, term sheets and how to negotiate them, and the differences between early-stage versus later-stage financing.
- **CLO14**: Develop an understanding of how to develop winning investor pitches, who and when to pitch, how to avoid common mistakes that limit the effectiveness of the pitch, and how to ‘get to the close’.
- **CLO15**: Integrate the tools and concepts from the specialization courses to develop a comprehensive business plan.
