# ENS211 - SKILLFUL 2 - LISTENING & SPEAKING 1


## Course Information - ENS211

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENS111 and ENS121 (or equivalence)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENS211
Listening skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Predict with strategies - Listen for main ideas with language signals - Listen for examples /details - Listen for reasons/ time signals - Listen for comparisons / contrasts - Listen to summarize - Listen for vocabulary in context - Listen for opinions / cause and effect - Recognize oganizational phrases - Listen for problems and solutions - Listen for order of events/a process - Listen for details to add to a visual - Listen for pros and cons - Understand speaker's attitude - Listen to how an argument is supported Speaking skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Offer advice and suggestions - Encourage communiaction - Shift the topic - Ask for clarification - Agree and disagree - Explain something you don't know the word for - Manage questions - Be an effective public speaker - Use and refer to visual aids - Refute an argument Pronunciation skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Pronounce stress patterns, stress in compound nouns, sentence stress, emphatic stress and stress in words with suffixes - Fall or rise intonation in tag questions/conditional sentences - Link vowel sound and the same consonant sounds - Be aware of the schwa

### Student Tasks - ENS211

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENS211

- **CLO1**: Predict with strategies
- **CLO2**: Listen for main ideas with language signals, examples and details.
- **CLO3**: Listen for reasons/ time signals, and for comparisons / contrast.
- **CLO4**: Listen to summarize.
- **CLO5**: Listen to understand speakers' attitude.
- **CLO6**: Improve speaking skills to talk in different situations.
- **CLO7**: Pronounce stress patterns, stress in compound nouns, sentence stress, emphatic stress and stress in words with suffixes.
- **CLO8**: Fall or rise intonation in tag questions/conditional sentences.
- **CLO9**: Link vowel sound and the same consonant sounds
