### Description - ACC302
Managerial Accounting is a sub-area of accounting concerned with information needed to effectively plan and control company operations and make good business decisions. The overall objective of this course is to provide you with the concepts and tools needed to understand and effectively use managerial accounting information.

### Student Tasks - ACC302
- Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ACC302

- **CLO1**: Describe the concepts, definitions, and formulas of management accounting.
- **CLO2**: Apply acquired knowledge to analyze information for decision-making.
- **CLO3**: Conduct research and compare learned theories with the practical applications of management accounting in real business environments.
- **CLO4**: Apply technology effectively in solving exercises, case studies, and hypothetical scenarios.
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate a professional attitude; work independently and collaboratively; and take responsibility and initiative in solving management accounting problems in real business contexts.
