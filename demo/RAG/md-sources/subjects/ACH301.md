## Course Information - ACH301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 0.5h final exam + 104.05h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: LOD211, LOD212, LOD222
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ACH301
-Main objective: The aim of this subject is to integrate knowledge with front office, housekeeping and others relevant departments in accommodation and apply them to problem solving in the areas of room division management. The course provides learners the necessary knowledge and skills to become a room division manager. -Description: During the course, students are practiced to design a room division department, apply the plan in real operation. Learners will be trained in management skills in several key aspects of room division management including operational administration, human resource management and departmental revenue optimization. -Teaching method: Lecture, Desmontration, Role Play, Project based learning, Cooperative Learning

### Student Tasks - ACH301
Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Comply with the time and regulation - Professional grooming - Respect and protect school asset. - Strongly communication and teamworking.

### Tools - ACH301
Internet access, Hotel management Software

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ACH301

- **CLO1**: Design an overall standard of procedures plan of hotel or resort operation
- **CLO2**: Develop corporate social responsibility expected of professionals working in accommodation
- **CLO3**: Describe functions of different departments of a hotel and building the interrelationships among departments.
- **CLO4**: Apply management skills in designing the service process in room division
- **CLO5**: Design human resource management program
- **CLO6**: Build departmental budget
- **CLO7**: Evaluate service quality of room division
- **CLO8**: Communicate in a professional and courtesy Brand’s style
- **CLO9**: Develop leadership skills
- **CLO10**: Identify and resolve problems relevant to accommodation areas
