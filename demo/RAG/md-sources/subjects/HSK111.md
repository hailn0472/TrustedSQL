### Description - HSK111
Housekeeping management examines the role of the supervisory function in the housekeeping department, provides a thorough overview of maintaining a quality staff, planning and organizing, the technical details of cleaning a room, managing the laundary, and control of supplies and equipment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HSK111

- **CLO1**: To describe the role of the housekeeping department in hotel operations, and explain the importance of effective communication between housekeeping department and other departments
- **CLO2**: To list and describe criteria for selection of materials and equipment for the hotel, for example, walls, floorings, beddings, linen, carpets, etc.
- **CLO3**: To discuss the proper procedure of cleaning and inspecting guest rooms, public areas and others, as well as able to demonstrate a working knowledge of the procedures.
- **CLO4**: To explain usage of various tools used to plan and organize the housekeeping department – staffing guide, area inventory lists, frequency schedules, performance standards and productivity standards.
- **CLO5**: To utilize various techniques to manage inventories effectively
- **CLO6**: To utilize various methods to control expenses in the housekeeping department
