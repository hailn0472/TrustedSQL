### Description - GDP492
Main objectives: Create a professional digital animation project following the design process: Research, define problems, reasons & goals, target audiences, competitors, define art directions, create moodboards, animation directions, scope of works, production timeline, throughout to the final products. The final products must present strong aesthetic abilities, acting skills in animation following 12 principles of animations, editing & composing techniques. The final works should bring a certain meaning to the community. Selected fields including: 2D Animated short film; 3D Animated short film; Stop-motion film; mixed-media film; Game (if the student passed ANO301). Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students  The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project 1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10; 2) Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor): 5%; Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final product and Capstone Project Document (by council):20%; - Presentation (Oral Project Defense): 30% Project Defense >=5 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Product and Casptone Document must be submitted on time. For Animation film, max 3 students in a group. For other options, max 2 students in a group.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GDP492

- **CLO1**: Communicating and working in groups effectively in academic and practical environments.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate professional behaviors, morality, social responsibilities and a sense of dedication to community. All illustrations used in the final product must be researched and executed by the student group.
- **CLO3**: Forming research thinking to solve problems, reasoning ability based on market research, customers, target audience, competitors in the same segment, doing surveys. Provide appropriate design solutions and art directions to reach final message.
- **CLO4**: Show aesthetic capacity through good management of sahpe, space, light, color, materials, layout, compositions …
- **CLO5**: Show your ability to act through final product, through 12 principles of animation.
- **CLO6**: Be able to use appropiate tools for selected fields such as: Photshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere, Encoder, Maya, Substance Painter, …
- **CLO7**: Be able to master audio productions in the final product. Be able to master editing techniques & visual effect for the final project.
- **CLO8**: Overal outcome and applicability of the final products.
- **CLO9**: Work in a systematic and timely manner. Package final mediums and choose the right platform to promote the the final projects. Presentation and product defense.
