# ENG302 - Advanced English Grammar


## Course Information - ENG302

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 7.5 hrs offline + 1hr exam
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENT503 or TRS601 or IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL (paper) 575-600 or TOEFL (iBT) 90-100
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENG302
This course aims at: - Introducing advanced and complex grammar concepts to students - Instructing students to blend verb tenses effectively and how to punctuate sentences correctly - Helping students improve their academic English *Assessment scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: TE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENG302

- **CLO1**: Review all verb tenses; and practice blending and mixing tenses in English
- **CLO2**: Develop complex sentences using conjunctions and connective devices
- **CLO3**: Practice using noun clauses and conditionals in advanced speaking and writing
- **CLO4**: Practice acquired advanced grammar points in writing
