# EIP401 - Phiên dịch tiếng Anh cho dịch vụ công_English Interpretation for Public Services


## Course Information - EIP401

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1.5 hours final exam + 103.5 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ELT402, ELI402
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EIP401
"-Objective: The course is designed to provide students with theoretical knowledge and practical scenarios related to public service interpreting in different settings including health care, media, police, military services, business, job training programs..  -Description: The course is composed of theoretical lectures familiarizing students with different interpreting settings of public services. Based on authentic audio and video language materials related to health care, media, police, military services... scenarios are created for learners to take turn to accomplish their roles as interpreters.  -Teaching method: Constructive Teaching Method is highly recommended. During the course, lectures may take place in different forms including traditional classroom, conference-styled classrooms, or outdoor activities.

### Student Tasks - EIP401
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purposes - Read the textbook in advance

### Tools - EIP401
- Edunext - Online Platforms

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EIP401

- **CLO1**: Acquire theoretical knowledge of general issues in public service interpreting
- **CLO2**: Understand the ambiguity of public service interpreters. Identify the positions of public service interpreters.
- **CLO3**: Determine the mediated interactions role of interpreter in public service interpreting
- **CLO4**: Understand and perform the roles of interepreter in the social context and cultural assumptions.
- **CLO5**: Acqurire skills & knowledge to apply in different public service interpreting settings
- **CLO6**: Acquire theoretical knowledge of general issues in different public service settings like health care, business, education, community events, court, military services, workshops...,
- **CLO7**: Be able to perfom the role of interpeter in different public service settings with specific scenarios related to health care, business, education, community events, court, military services, workshops...,
- **CLO8**: Be a responsible and ethical interpreter or translator
