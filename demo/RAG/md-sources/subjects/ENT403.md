## Course Information - ENT403

- **Credits**: 0

### Description - ENT403
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises.

### Student Tasks - ENT403
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible for doing all exercises, assignments, and labs given by the instructor in class or at home and submitting them on time - Use a laptop in class only for learning purposes - Access the course website for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online support from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - ENT403
Course book ActiveTeach

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENT403

- **CLO1**: Apply a wide range of vocabulary and structures in the text book to initiate and maintain conversations in familiar situations and to discuss topics in the text book.
- **CLO2**: Perform understanding of the main points, speaker's key messages, important details and speaker's tones of clear standard conversations and speeches on familiar topics
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate ability to identify main ideas, supporting details and organization of B1+ reading articles
- **CLO4**: Make inferences and predictions from context in reading B1+ articles
- **CLO5**: Write simple, compound and complex sentences
- **CLO6**: Write 200-word opinion essays with a logical organization
- **CLO7**: Delegate tasks and cooperate effectively in group work and manage group and individual work effectively
- **CLO8**: Perform critical thinking over others' opinion with logical arguments
- **CLO9**: Manage time effectively and perform independent learning
