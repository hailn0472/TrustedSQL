### Description - HOM301c
This Specialization covers the basics of hotel demand management, distribution, and revenue management. You’ll learn how e-commerce intermediaries such as online travel agencies are impacting the already-complex hospitality sector, and you’ll explore tools and techniques that will help you effectively market hospitality services and maximize business revenue in this new global industry landscape. In the final Capstone Project, you’ll evaluate the importance of hotel branding in business success through a hands-on, real-world case study. During the sessions, you will hear from senior hotel industry executives commenting on distribution, revenue and demand management issues. We will also interview two leading Online Travel Agents on how to maximise online business for your property, and have partnered with Duetto and SnapShot, two leading hospitality industry companies, to gain access to cutting edge knowledge, thus insuring that the competencies that you gain will make you operational immediately as well as bang up to date on industry trends. Note: Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HOM301c

- **CLO1**: Understand how to manage an increasingly complex network of traditional and web based channels of hotel sector.
- **CLO2**: Provide a core understanding of the fundamentals of revenue management, which ties into the larger picture of revenue strategy.
- **CLO3**: Understand the journey from long-term asset development through to short-term profit tactics and how commercial teams can align themselves throughout the organisation.
- **CLO4**: Understand how to apply the knowledge you have gained about distribution, revenue and demand management to a ‘real’ world case.
