### Description - AFA201m
This course provides students with fundamental knowledge of human body structure and proportions, as well as skills in drawing and sketching human figures. It is a foundational course for quick sketching and animation design. The course is offered in conjunction with Coursera's "Anatomy for the Character Design Artist". Throughout the course, students will learn about the history of anatomy, study the structure of the human body in motion, and learn how to draw the proportions of the head, shoulders and arms, legs and feet, and torso. Students will then apply these skills to practice drawing humans in various poses. Finally, students will be able to create character designs that maintain harmonious and visually pleasing human anatomy proportions.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AFA201m

- **CLO1**: Remember and understand the brief history of Anatomy
- **CLO2**: Memorize the structure of the Human Body and know how to draw the proportion of The Head, The Shoulder and Arm, The Leg and Feet, The Torso
- **CLO3**: Apply the knowledge that learned from The Human Portrait Practice to creat the human portrait and the facial expressions
- **CLO4**: Analyze the Human in Action, then apply that learned skill to the practice of drawing human in action
- **CLO5**: Create the stylized character
- **CLO6**: Have a positive attitude and a professional working style
