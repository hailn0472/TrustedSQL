## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DPS201c

- **CLO1**: Understand the foundation of futures thinking and the essential foresight techniques
- **CLO2**: Understand the way to build future forecasting skills
- **CLO3**: Practice simulation techniques for “unsticking” the mind when it comes to seeing what’s possible in the future and accepting how things could be different.
- **CLO4**: Understand collaborative gaming techniques to go beyond our own thinking and see many, many different sides of the same future
- **CLO5**: Be able to write future scenarios.
- **CLO6**: Be able to create and share “preferred futures”, which are highly persuasive, compelling visions of the changes people want to make real.
