# LTG301 - Introduction to Linguistics - Dẫn luận ngôn ngữ


## Course Information - LTG301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1 hour final exam + 104 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENG303 & ENP203
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LTG301
The Study of Language offers: - a fundamental introduction to language study - a vocabulary for talking about language and a solid knowledge of how English works. - major concepts in language study through all the key elements of language are presented

### Student Tasks - LTG301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments, and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

### Tools - LTG301
- Internet -Course book

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LTG301

- **CLO1**: Recall the origins of language
- **CLO2**: Distinguish the sounds and the sound patterns of laguage
- **CLO3**: Report the importance of word formation and describe processes involved in word formation.
- **CLO4**: Analyse syntactic and semantic rules
- **CLO5**: Identify referential meaning and relationships between words; examine the importance of context that contributes to meaning
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate neurolinguistics and the process of first and second language asquisition
- **CLO7**: Have a spirit of self-study, lifelong learning
- **CLO8**: Have a professional working attitude, and have ability to work in a team
