# ENR101 - Reading 1 (Skillful reading & writing level 1)


## Course Information - ENR101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENT10x or equivalent
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENR101
This is Skillful Reading 1 course, extracted from Skillful reading and writing 2 and intended to use for Upper-intermediate students. After the course, students can be able to use reading techniques (previewing, scanning, skimming, etc.) to comprehend reading passages related to academic topics such as psychology, Sociology, Biology, Chemistry, Business Studies.

### Student Tasks - ENR101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENR101

- **CLO1**: Infer pronouns used in the reading texts
- **CLO2**: Understand the information from charts, graphs and sequence of the ideas
- **CLO3**: Identify the author's purpose
- **CLO4**: Understand the annotations in the reading texts
- **CLO5**: make the inferences
- **CLO6**: Determine main ideas and support details
- **CLO7**: Distinguish facts from opinions
- **CLO8**: Use a chart to organise notes
- **CLO9**: Identify similarities and differences
- **CLO10**: Identify reasons

- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9
- Question Type: Multiple choice, short answer questions, embedded questions
- Note: Teacher is allowed to select which units content (topic or learning outcome) to focus on each examination session.

### Project/Assignment
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9, LO10
- Question Type: Each student chooses one book/novel in English (supposed to be a masterpiece in English) to read and write a report following the format provided by teacher.
- Note: Teachers give topics, start and finish time for each Project/Assignment by themself.
