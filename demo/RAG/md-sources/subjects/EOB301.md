# EOB301 - Outcome-based Course Design in English Language Teaching_Thiết kế khoá học theo chuẩn đầu ra trong giảng dạy ngôn ngữ Anh


## Course Information - EOB301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 105 hours self-study (including projects and assignments preparation time)
- **Pre-Requisite**: EAL201, ERW421, EPC301, ESL101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EOB301
Objective: 'This course provides English major students with an ingight to Outcome-based education with the implementation of Problem-based learning, which aims to develop a mindset of more student-centered approaches." Teaching method: lecture + activities + project works

### Student Tasks - EOB301
<- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Students are responsible for doing all exercises, and assignments given by instructor in class or at home by the due dates - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

### Tools - EOB301
textbook + reference materials

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EOB301

- **CLO1**: Identify the role and benefits of OBE in teaching English as a foreign language
- **CLO2**: Identify and explain the alignment of LOs, delivery and assessment in Outcome-based education (OBE)
- **CLO3**: Construct an EFL course's LOs
- **CLO4**: Recognize the nature of project-based learning and its role in OBE
- **CLO5**: Sketch an authentic project for EFL learners that can match the Los
- **CLO6**: Identify the role of assessment in OBE
- **CLO7**: Design the rubrics for the project
- **CLO8**: Work in groups effectively and responsibly upon the implementation of project work

## Assessment - EOB301

### Assignment 1
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >2
- CLO: LO1,2,3,8
- Question Type: Design CLOs for an EFL course
- Note: Group work (3-4 people/ a group) with a clear task delegation

### Assignment 2
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >2
- CLO: LO1,2,4,5,8
- Question Type: Design a webquest for a project of the course in assignment 1 including:
- the project idea and artifacts
- a driving question
- task and process of the project implementation (steps)
- useful sources for students to work on the project
- a conclusion with a feedback form
- Note: Group work (3-4 people/ a group) with a clear task delegation

### Assignment 3
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >2
- CLO: LO 1,2,6,7,8
- Question Type: Construct a rubric/rubrics to grade the artifacts in assignment 2
- Note: Group work (3-4 people/ a group) with a clear task delegation

### Presentation and Defense
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >2
- CLO: LO 1, 2, 3, 5, 6
- Question Type: Deliver a presentation on the alignment of the project, rubric(s) and the CLOs
- Note: Group work (3-4 people/ a group) with a clear task delegation
