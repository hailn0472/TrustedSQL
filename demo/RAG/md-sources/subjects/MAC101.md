### Description - MAC101
This course covers fundamental concepts to prepare for more advanced engineering mathematics courses such as regular caculus, linear algebra and discrete mathematics courses. Topics included: polynomial, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions, solving systems of linear equations, sequences, series, limits, derivatives and integrals.

### Student Tasks - MAC101
Students must attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MAC101

- **CLO1**: Describe basic properties and find the limit of a function
- **CLO2**: Calculate derivatives of a function
- **CLO3**: Use derivatives to analyze a function and to solve applications
- **CLO4**: Calculate integrals and find area under the graph of a function
- **CLO5**: Use intergration to solve applications
- **CLO6**: Analyze a sequence, investigate the convergence of a series and expand a function using Taylor series
- **CLO7**: Analyze a function of multi-variable
- **CLO8**: Evaluate a multiple integral
