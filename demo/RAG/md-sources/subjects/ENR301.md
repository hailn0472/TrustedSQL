# ENR301 - SKILLFUL 3 - READING 3


## Course Information - ENR301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENR201 or (ENS212 and ENS222)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENR301
This is Skillful Reading 3 course, extracted from Skillful reading and writing 3 and intended to use for Upper-intermediate students. After the course, students can be able to use reading techniques (previewing, scanning, skimming, etc.) to comprehend reading passages related to academic topics such as psychology, Sociology, Biology, Chemistry, Business Studies.

### Student Tasks - ENR301
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENR301

- **CLO1**: Identify the main idea.
- **CLO2**: Recognize the writer's attitude and bias.
- **CLO3**: Recognize cause and effect.
- **CLO4**: Understand vocabulary from context.
- **CLO5**: Identify steps in a sequence.
- **CLO6**: Use a graphic organizer to take notes.
- **CLO7**: Distinguish fact from opinion.
- **CLO8**: Annotate text.
- **CLO9**: Identify sources of information.
- **CLO10**: Identify tone and mood.
