### Description - SSC302m
-Course Objectives: Master the art of persuasion through understanding ethos, pathos, and logos. Develop strong argumentation and critical thinking skills. Analyze and adapt to diverse audiences. Improve public speaking delivery, including vocal variety, body language, and eye contact. Utilize research to support persuasive claims. Construct persuasive speeches using various organizational patterns. Provide and receive constructive feedback on persuasive presentations. -Course Description: This course is designed to equip students with the essential skills to craft and deliver compelling persuasive speeches. Through a combination of theoretical knowledge and practical application, students will learn to analyze audiences, develop strong arguments, and effectively communicate their ideas. The course emphasizes critical thinking, research, and public speaking skills. Students will have the opportunity to practice their persuasive abilities through in-class presentations and receive constructive feedback from peers and the instructor. By the end of the course, students will be confident in their ability to persuade and influence others and equiped students with the soft skills necessary to thrive in today's competitive job market. -Teaching method: interactive and contructivist approach and/or Project Based Learning

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSC302m

- **CLO1**: Plan and deliver speeches with a clear introduction, body, and conclusion
- **CLO2**: Adapt to change and embrace new challenges.
- **CLO3**: Research and develop engaging content that is relevant to the audience.
- **CLO4**: Use effective vocal delivery (tone, pace, volume) and body language to enhance their message.
- **CLO5**: Adjust their speaking style to suit different audiences, contexts and situations.
- **CLO6**: Respond effectively to questions and challenges.
- **CLO7**: Use visual aids (e.g., slides, props) to enhance their presentation.
- **CLO8**: Evaluate their own performance and incorporate feedback to improve.
- **CLO9**: Work effectively in a team environment and contribute to shared goals
- **CLO10**: Take responsibility for their actions and their impact on others.

## Assessment - SSC302m

### Assignment 1
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO 1->7
- Question Type: Deliver a five- to seven minute speech on a topic of your choosing

### Assignment 2
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1->10
- Question Type: Analyze the potential attitudes people might hold towards real-life scenarios and how those attitudes might serve different functions (e.g., expressing social identity, maintaining group norms, etc.) and present your findings

### Assignment 3
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1->10
- Question Type: This group project will involve crafting and delivering a persuasive speech on a real-life scenario where someone needs to change a negative behavior.

### Final exam
- Weight: 40.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: CLO: 1->10
- Question Type: Civic engagement project: Students choose a topic (with T's approval) and design a persuasive speech that raises awareness and changes the attitudes, beliefs or behaviors of an intended target audience.
