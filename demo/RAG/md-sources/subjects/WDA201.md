### Description - WDA201
This course offers an introduction to fundamental principles of PR writing at the introductory level, with a specific focus on the influence of integrating artificial intelligence (AI). In the digital age, PR writing has evolved, utilizing diverse channels such as blogs and social media. This evolution surpasses traditional press releases by incorporating the real-time nature of tweets, Instagram stories, LinkedIn posts, and the like. These versatile technologies enable a broad spectrum of approaches to PR writing and engagement, illustrating a noteworthy advancement in public relations techniques adept at navigating the constantly evolving communication environment. Upon successful completion of this course, students shall acquire: 1. A comprehensive understanding of the transformative impact of digital spaces on information flow and knowledge acquisition, including the ability to distinguish between linear and non-linear media. 2. Proficiency in employing digital writing techniques to enhance readability and user engagement. 3. Mastery in editing for digital media to ensure accuracy and clarity, while adapting to the evolving roles of digital editors and content producers. 4. Abilities to strategically utilize multimedia storytelling and public relations techniques, address legal considerations, and combat misinformation to maintain credibility and trust in digital journalism.

Suggested assignment requirement:
Develop a comprehensive multimedia project aligned with your team's interests, such as addressing climate change. Your essay should:
(a) Clearly state the chosen topic, outline specific project goals, define your target audience, and specify the types of multimedia content you plan to incorporate.
(b) Explain your strategy for utilizing public relations techniques to expand the project's reach, address relevant legal and ethical issues, and propose methods for evaluating the project's impact on raising awareness or advocating for change related to your chosen topic.
Opt.2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal

Notes:

Students are required to:
1. Use AI tools for text editing to enhance clarity and ensure the text is free of grammatical errors.
2. Note that while AI tools may be used to edit content for digital media:
• Students must acknowledge all AI-generated information in their reports.
• Students must demonstrate their ability to produce content for digital media independently.
• The use of AI tools is required but will not be graded separately; the quality of their completed work will be assessed as a whole.
• The final assessment will be based on the lecturer's provided rubric.
- Note: Students are strongly advised to carefully review all outputs from AI tools. They must demonstrate the ability to independently produce content for digital media. Students' work will be assessed according to a rubric provided by the lecturer.

### Individual Assignment
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1->CLO4
- Question Type: Opt.1
Suggested topic:
(a) Select a news website or application and conduct an in-depth analysis to better understand its audience. Explore its FAQs and "About Us" section to determine its categorization (news, entertainment, reference, etc.), available content types, demographics of its visitors or subscribers (including age and education), content-related interests or needs addressed, and the nature of user engagement such as feedback, comments, and interaction. Utilizing this data, craft a user profile detailing the characteristics and behaviors of the site or app's audience.

(b) Find an online news article that could be enhanced with an informative graphic, such as a chart or graph. Detail clear instructions for crafting this visual component, specifying the data to be included, the timeframe to be covered, and any additional features or dimensions necessary to aid the reader's comprehension of the story.
