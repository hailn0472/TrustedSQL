# EIT491 - ENGLISH FOR STANDARDIZED TESTS


## Course Information - EIT491

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Hours per course: 30 sessions (including 6 self practical monitored sessions)
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENS411, ENS421, ENR401, ENW401, ENG301, ENP101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EIT491
This course of study is for students of advanced level learners who need to be able to understand and use the English in the academic context. The units are organized thematically linked sections and cover a wide range of subjects while introducing various testaking skills.

### Student Tasks - EIT491
Regularly attend class-meeting hours and complete assignments and homeworks For BBA students: must attend at least 80% of the total course time in order to be admitted to the final exam - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.fap.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment. - Computer with Autodesk Maya 2018 software and Video editing software (Adobe Premiere, Adobe AfterEffects, ...) - Complete course's Portfolio with full contents of exercises and submit to the teacher 1 day after the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EIT491

- **CLO1**: Get familiar with IELTS test format.
- **CLO2**: Be ready to achieve their top score on the IELTS.
- **CLO3**: Use the language knowledge and skills they have learned for effective study in an English-speaking environment.
- **CLO4**: Apply integrated learning skills acquired from the process of language learning in their further study.
