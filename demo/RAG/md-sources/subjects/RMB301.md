### Description - RMB301
This course introduces students to a number of research methods useful for academic and professional investigations of business practices. By examining the applications, strengths, and weaknesses of methodologies drawn from both the qualitative and quantitative traditions, this course permits an understanding of the various steps involved in designing and executing a research project so that students will be able to conduct their research later. A key feature of this course is the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and applications. Students learn to use theses tools to : - support generating and refining research topics, - assist in content suggestions, - assist in answering questions on Edunext, in-class discussions, and identifying credible sources. - supports summarizing and concluding research findings through data-driven insights and structured templates.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RMB301

- **CLO1**: Write a literature review that synthesises and evaluates literature in a specific topic area to justify a research questions
- **CLO2**: Apply appropriate research design and methods to address a specific research question and acknowledge the ethical implications of the research
- **CLO3**: Develop a research proposal as the basis for a thesis
- **CLO4**: Present and defend a research proposal

## Assessment - RMB301

### Class Participation
- Weight: 15.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: OP1: Each student should make at least 1 individual presentation during the course in front of the class.
OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: Participation scores will be given based on students' class attendance, preparation, quantity as well as quality of participation in classroom; quality of participation is demonstrated through asking relevant questions, making statements that add to and facilitate class discussions, and building upon others’ comments. Class participation accounts for 15% of the final score.

### Group Assignment 1
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: OP1: The class should be divided into 4 or 6 groups. Each group will choose a business/management problem to formulate a research topic and corresponding research questions/objectives. The report should include research topic, research rationale, and research questions/objectives. OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal OP3: Complete the Guided Project -Basic data analysis using SPSS
- Note: All assignments should be typed and double-spaced with 12-point font, and must be submitted in both soft and hard copy. A deduction of 10% of the assignment mark will be applied for each day of late submission. Assignment score for each student should be based on: (1) Group Assignment Report (50%); (2) individual presentation, Q&A on Group report or individual feedback to other groups' presentations (50%). Group Assignment 1 accounts for 10% of the final score.

### Group Assignment 2
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: OP1: Based on results of Group Assignment 1, students will continue to write literature review and theoretical framework for their research topic. OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: All assignments should be typed and double-spaced with 12-point font, and must be submitted in both soft and hard copy. A deduction of 10% of the assignment mark will be applied for each day of late submission. Assignment score for each student should be based on: (1) Group Assignment Report (50%); (2) individual presentation, Q&A on Group report or individual feedback to other groups' presentations (50%). Group Assignment 2 accounts for 20% of the final score.

### Group Assignment 3
- Weight: 25.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: OP1: Based on results of Group Assignment 1 and Group Assignment 2, students will complete their research proposal. The final report will include contents of Group Assignments 1 & 2 plus sections on proposed research methods, research scope, and thesis structure. OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: All assignments should be typed and double-spaced with 12-point font, and must be submitted in both soft and hard copy. A deduction of 10% of the assignment mark will be applied for each day of late submission. Assignment score for each student should be based on: ((1) Group Assignment Report (50%); (2) individual presentation and Q&A on Group report or individual feedback to other groups' presentations (50%). Group Assignment 3 accounts for 25% of the final score.

### Final Exam

- Completion Criteria: 4
- Question Type: Multiple choice
