# ENW401 - Writing 4


## Course Information - ENW401

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in English Language
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENW301 or (ENS312 & ENS322)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENW401
The course is designed to develop and sharpen learners' academic and professional writing skills and strategies in English. Each unit guides the student through the structured writing process with plenty of full colour illustrations, examples, and exercises to engage the student and build confidence. This course aims at providing students understanding and skills from writing a paragragh to developing a full essay.

### Student Tasks - ENW401
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENW401

- **CLO1**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full research essay.
- **CLO2**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full comparison-constrast essay.
- **CLO3**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full cause and effect essay.
- **CLO4**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full argumentative essay.
- **CLO5**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full classification essay.
- **CLO6**: Understand the process to write and can develop a full reaction essay.
