# ADP301 - Packaging design_Thiết kế bao bì


## Course Information - ADP301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ADI201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ADP301
'The purpose of this course is to equip students with the detailed understanding and knowledge of the package. It will also equip students with the essential practical and technical skills necessary for packaging design. Scope of evaluation will include target customers, brand language, personality and persuasion, concept creation, colour application, ergonomics, layout design, print finishing and materials... The challenges of designing for the future will also be considered; ensuring that environmental and ethical issues relating to the packaging industry are measured and applied.

### Student Tasks - ADP301
'- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose. Computer have to installed some version of design software such as Photoshop, Illustrator for design assignments, and Powerpoint for presentations. - Read the textbook in advance.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADP301

- **CLO1**: Understanding the Fundamentals of packaging design: Combine the functional, aesthetic, and legal aspects of packaging
- **CLO2**: Analyze Materials, printing techniques, and market research: Merge the knowledge of materials and printing with market analysis skills. Evaluate packaging designs through a sustainability lens, considering material choices, life-cycle impact, and end-of-use strategies (recycling, composting, etc).
- **CLO3**: Develop Branding concepts and design: Focus on the brand creation process, including naming and visual design implementation
- **CLO4**: Research, Ideation and Collaborative design: Combine research, analysis, brainstorming, and teamwork for design development
- **CLO5**: Presentation and Communication skills: emphasize the importance of presenting and defending design choices, along with group collaboration.
- **CLO6**: Market Awareness and adaptability: highlight the need to understand market trends and adjust designs accordingly, building upon the proactive attitude from.
- **CLO7**: Professional Work ethic: reinforce the importance of working under pressure, adhering to legal requirements, and maintaining a proactive approach.
