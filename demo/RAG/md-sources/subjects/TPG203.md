### Description - TPG203
This course will cover the history, theory and practice of letterforms and typography as they apply to graphics, advertising and other areas of design and visual communication. Projects cover typography history, principles of typography, letter structure, typeface selection, fundamentals of computer typesetting.

### Student Tasks - TPG203

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TPG203

- **CLO1**: Classify the feature and style of typography in the historical period
- **CLO2**: Recognizes the anatomy of typeface
- **CLO3**: Explain the fundamental role that typography plays in developing legibility for the reading audience.
- **CLO4**: Compare and contrast different typographic approaches and how they influence and change meaning.
- **CLO5**: Apply a range of typographic approaches in response to specific design problems.
