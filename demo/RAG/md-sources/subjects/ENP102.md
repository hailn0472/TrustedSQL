### Description - ENP102
-Objective: Help students work on their pronunciation and speak English with neutral accent, fluency and comprehension; identify diversity of sound systems. -Description: 'This course will cover different aspects of pronunciation in English for practical uses including letters and sounds, combining sounds, word stress, stress patterns, intonation, speed, tone, accents and phonemic symbols. -Teaching method: optional

### Student Tasks - ENP102
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptops and headphones in class only for learning purposes - Read the textbook in advance

### Tools - ENP102
CDs, DVDs, Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENP102

- **CLO1**: Recognise and demonstrate the way to pronounce and spell consonant sounds of English; the way to make the differences between the sounds clear.
- **CLO2**: Recognise and demonstrate the way to pronounce and spell vowel sounds of English; the way to make the differences between the sounds clear.
- **CLO3**: Recognise and demonstrate the way to pronounce and spell the dipthongs tripthongs of English; the way to make the differences between the sounds clear.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate the fluency when speaking, an ability to present a clear statement of constraint, change intonation and determine suitable sentence stress to be able to show different modes of meanings and feelings.
- **CLO5**: Apply the rules of connected speech to sound more fluent when students speak
- **CLO6**: Practice and refine the lifelong learning behavior of collaboration by working together as a group

## Assessment - ENP102

### Assignment 1
(Individual voice recording)
- Weight: 15.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2 , LO6
- Question Type: Each student will choose one out of 10 texts given by the lecturer. (Each text contains 400 - 450 words)
Students will read the text and record their voice and summit the recording to the lecturer.
- Note: All assignments should be recorded with good sound quality, including:
- Saying student's fullname
- Student ID
- Topic chosen
- Reading the chosen text

### Assignment 2
(pair work )
- Weight: 15.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: Option 1:
Students works in pairs and choose pop songs that provide the following aspects of sounds
- Elision
- Linking consonant sounds to vowel shounds
- Intrusion
- Assimilation
- The schwa
Then submit your report to the lecturer
Option 2:
Students work in pairs and make a dialogue in which they should use the following aspects of sounds
- Elision
- Linking consonant sounds to vowel shounds
- Intrusion
- Assimilation
- The schwa
Option 3: teacher's choice
- Note: Students submit their report to the class teacher
