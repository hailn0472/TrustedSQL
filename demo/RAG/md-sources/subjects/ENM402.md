### Description - ENM402
This course is designed to help students further improve their ability to communicate in English in a wide range of business and business-related situations at upper intermediate level of English. It also helps students develop necessary knowledge and skills to succeed in business. In addition to the business-focused contents, the course is enhanced with supplementary activities corresponding to B2+ level of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. In particular the course will help students: * Communicate effectively in discussions, meetings, negotiations, socialization events * acquire and effectively use professional skills including telephoning, presentations, correspondence exchange, decision making, and problem solving. * enrich knowledge of business trends in the fast-changing world * enhance students' employabilty

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM402

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate knowledge of business vocabulary, terminology and concepts (of level B2+) in digital business, finance, investment, management, relocation, strategic alliances, and operations to convey messages clearly and effectively
- **CLO2**: Build awareness and identify business situations, including how to understand and communicate in different business contexts and working across cultures with authentic content at B2+ level from the Financial Times and other news sources
- **CLO3**: Apply the principles of effective business communication at B2+ level ( language skills, soft skills and business skills), including clarity, conciseness, and formality in various international business settings ; Demonstrate a professional attitude in all forms of business communication, showing respect, politeness, and consideration for others.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate critical thinking skills to analyze business problems and scenarios critically
- **CLO5**: Write e-mails of proposal and business report summary
- **CLO6**: Be cooperative, supportive, and responsible in group work.
- **CLO7**: Apply the processes involved in financing, business change, market research, relocation, partnership, and decision-making that enhances employability
- **CLO8**: Identify key points, main ideas, supporting details, writer/ speaker's tone and attitude, recognize different communication styles used in diverse contexts about a variety of business-related topics of level B2+
