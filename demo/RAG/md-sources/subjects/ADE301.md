### Description - ADE301
In this course, students will learn to design and produce exhibitions for museums, galleries, libraries, private businesses, specialist trade events and conferences, and large commercial public events. …The focus of this course examines the role that exhibitions and events play in communicating knowledge and explores the variety of display methods within the language of exhibits.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADE301

- **CLO1**: Understand the way people communicate visually and communication design organizes experience in variety of structures. Denotation and connotation are two levels of meaning we assign to messages.
- **CLO2**: Understand the concept of The ideal brief and the types of information that may be included. Describe and analyze The visitors and targeted audiences. Analyze The site: opportunities and constraints, their impact on design strategies.
- **CLO3**: Plan an exhibition strategy based on the brief. Devising a major path for visitors.
- **CLO4**: Describes how the exhibition strategy by sketches, models and computer visuals are then used to develop the final design plan
- **CLO5**: Using the right and appropriate graphic elements in each practical space. Ensure legibility of text and images on large-format printing and production issues
- **CLO6**: Apply interactive techniques, both digital and non-digital, can be used to stimulate visitor involvement in an exhibition.
- **CLO7**: Responsibility to use skills, positive attitude and a professional working style
- **CLO8**: Skills in proficient in presentation and teamwork skills.
