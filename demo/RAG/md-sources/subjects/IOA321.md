### Description - IOA321
This course introduces machine learning and deep learning techniques applied in the agricultural industry. AI models (ML, DL) are applied to classify some diseases of plants, using ML and DL models to classify seeding and harvesting stages. The course deals with automation, combined with IoT and AI in harvesting, and checking plant nutrition during fertilization.
