### Description - SAP490
The SAP inter-disciplinary capstone project provides IT and BA students with important skills and initial experiences in participating in the SAP projects. With the team size of 3-5 members (*), student can apply knowledges and skills they have learned to the real current projects, including: - Understand the lifecycle of a SAP project - Know what to do in each phase of the project - Understand the terminology used in the project lifecycle - Practice teamwork and how to interact with colleagues in a project implementation process - Ensure the proper implementation of quality requirements and schedule - Do troubleshooting All the documents in the project implementation process and the progress reports are required to write in English. (*): Unless impossible, the team should consist of IT and BA students with a recommended IT to BA student ratio of 3:2. In case of only IT students, the team will have to do all the work assigned to BA students under the guidance of BA lecturer.

### Student Tasks - SAP490
# Choose appropriate project and form group. # Divide and schedule work among members. # Attend all scheduled meeting with supervisor. # Produce document and product according to project requirement and member work share. # Defend work in front of examination board, showing both project results and individual responsibility. # In case of cheating found, the whole team is not allow to defend.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SAP490

- **CLO1**: Identify and analyze problems as well as develope and discusse possible solutions.
- **CLO2**: Configure the enterprise structure and business processes for involved modules in SAP
- **CLO3**: Implement the solutions, including testing verification in SAP system.
- **CLO4**: Apply project management principles to SAP project.
- **CLO5**: Write a professional research report.
- **CLO6**: Develop professional working style, good teamwork skills, communication skills (including presentation skills) and compliance with the production process
