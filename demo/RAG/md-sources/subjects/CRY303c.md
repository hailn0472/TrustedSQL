### Description - CRY303c
This course is an introduction to applied cryptography and its relationship to secure systems. Students will learn making, breaking codes and ciphers. They can have a good understanding of major concepts in applied cryptography: plain text, encryption, cipher text, block ciphers, decryption, public-key cryptosystems, hash functions, digital signatures, authentication, key management, and cryptographic protocols as a part of securing digital systems.

### Student Tasks - CRY303c

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CRY303c

- **CLO1**: Explain cryptography from the information-theoretical perspectives and discuss the pratical aspects of modern cryptography.
- **CLO2**: Understand basic techniques and algorithms of symmetric cryptography via classical and modern symmetric ciphers
- **CLO3**: Understand the basic principles of asymmetric (or public key) cryptography including the RSA cipher algorithm, the Diffie-Hellman key exchange, and problems with key distribution and key management.
- **CLO4**: Understand the important role of cryptographic hash functions in modern cryptography and their applications in integrity protection.
