# LAW101 - BUSINESS LAW FUNDAMENTALS


## Course Information - LAW101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: 30 sessions; 1 session= 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LAW101
This course addresses the basic concept of the Legal Environment of Business studies. The course highlights the legal and regulatory environment in which people and companies conduct business activities. It appreciates the complexities, ambiguities and many areas of the law in undertaking business.

### Student Tasks - LAW101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - keep in touch with lecturer regularly for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LAW101

- **CLO1**: Describe the legal system and the legal environment of business
- **CLO2**: Describe the relationships of ethics and law in business
- **CLO3**: Define relevant legal terms in business
- **CLO4**: Explain basic principles of law that apply to business and business transactions
- **CLO5**: Describe business law in the global context
- **CLO6**: Describe current law, rules, and regulations related to settling business disputes
