### Description - ANS301
This course focuses on animation and illustrative storytelling. Topics include structural elements of storytelling, storyboarding formats, motion and animation and character development. The course will take a step-by-step approach to story development, using the medium of storyboards. The creation and use of animat- ics will also be introduced and explored.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANS301

- **CLO1**: Knowing the process of building a storyboard for animated short
- **CLO2**: Knowing the role, meaning of a storyboard in a film
- **CLO3**: Understanding how to build a storyboard for an animated movie
- **CLO4**: Firmly grasping knowledge of framing, aspect ratio, camera angles and movements ...
- **CLO5**: Script writing, content creation, making rhythm for film.
- **CLO6**: Drawing backgrounds for a story.
- **CLO7**: Drawing a storyboard for an animated movie
- **CLO8**: Creating an animatic
