### Description - SCM301m
This course explores the central concepts of organisational procurement, global sourcing and interfaces of these to the other areas of an organisation. This course provides opportunities to examine issues such as organisational procurement process, global sourcing process, supplier selection process, supplier management and other strategic issues. *Teaching method: This is a blended-learning course, in which students will take a look at the videos & slides and reading on Coursera first at home, then rewatch & discuss key questions with classmates and teachers at classes. AI Tools will be used to support students in (1) generating ideas/structures for their speaking notes and slide structure of important topics of the course. (2) editing the slide set after students have already finished it.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SCM301m

- **CLO1**: demonstrate basic understanding of procurement of an enterprise
- **CLO2**: practice the ability to conduct negotiation in procurement
- **CLO3**: demonstrate understanding of stakeholder management in the given context of an enterprise
- **CLO4**: analyze the 7-step Strategic Sourcing Process
- **CLO5**: demonstrate the ability to select and evaluate supplier
- **CLO6**: practice the ability to develop a supplier evaluation system for an enterprise
- **CLO7**: discuss various types of contracts that can be used in procurement
- **CLO8**: demonstrate understanding of risk in procurement and the ability to mitigate it
- **CLO9**: discuss some ethical considerations in procurement
- **CLO10**: apply tools to evaluate a Supply Market from Macro, Industry, and Supplier Level
