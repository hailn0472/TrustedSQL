### Description - CAM201
This course is designed to equip participants with the essential skills needed to cultivate innovative concepts and effectively transform them into compelling executions. Through the strategic harnessing of creativity and refined communication proficiency, participants will acquire the capacity to exert influence and reshape perceptions. The curriculum encompasses an exploration of conceptual thinking, mastery of persuasive copywriting, refinement of art direction nuances, and a comprehensive understanding of the diverse array of media crucial for proficient advertising creatives. Participants will acquire the knowledge and expertise necessary to navigate the intricate landscape of creative advertising, especially within the context of the evolving new media landscape.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CAM201

- **CLO1**: To understand the fundamental concepts in New media industry and their values.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend how companies/organizations drive social media, and how they take advantage of the technology to improve their advertising activities
- **CLO3**: To comprehend how to apply knowledge of advertising in reality by creating creative products, building plan, campaign.
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
