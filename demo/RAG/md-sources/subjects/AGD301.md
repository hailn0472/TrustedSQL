# AGD301 - INFORMATION GRAPHIC DESIGN_Thiết kế đồ họa thông tin


## Course Information - AGD301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: TPG302
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - AGD301
Description: The course equips students with the basics of graphic language used to visualize information, the methods and skills needed to create successful, outstanding and communicative infographic works to the audience. This course will also provides a series of accompanying exercises to reinforce the knowledge of visualizing information including a printed infographic artwork, a digital infographic artworks and a deconstruct wayfinding system of an existing place.

### Student Tasks - AGD301
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - AGD301
- Internet access - Microsoft Word - Adobe Creative apps

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AGD301

- **CLO1**: Provides essential knowledge about Infographics
- **CLO2**: Understand the method of refining information and using graphic materials to create successful infographic.
- **CLO3**: Ability to proficient use of Illustrator or Photoshop tools for information visualization.
- **CLO4**: Complete a Printed Infographic
- **CLO5**: Complete an Interactive Infogaphic
- **CLO6**: Redesign/deconstruct a wayfinding system of an existing place
- **CLO7**: Develop ethics, professionalism, and cognition in respect to public audience and users' experience
