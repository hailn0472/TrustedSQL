# DTG302 - Principles of Compositing _Thiết kế hình ảnh và âm thanh trong làm phim


## Course Information - DTG302

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)
= 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - DTG302
- This course will teach students how to make motion graphics and how to create visual effects using Adobe After Effects software. At the end of the course, students will know how to apply the knowledge of timing, rhythm, composition to create a complete animation product

### Student Tasks - DTG302
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/) for up-to-date information and material of the course. - Computer with some version of photograph editing software such as Photoshop, Microsoft Word for written assignments, and POWERPOINT for presentations - Have color papers, Color Pencils, scissors...

### Tools - DTG302
Adobe After Effects CC

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DTG302

- **CLO1**: Students are able to combine principles of graphic design and motion, and able to create animated graphics which highlight harmony and unity between images and sounds
- **CLO2**: Understanding the fundamentals of digital animation
- **CLO3**: Basic design principals as it applies to Motion Graphics
- **CLO4**: Use After Effects efficiently and effectively
- **CLO5**: Creatively integrate text, image, video and sound using motion graphics techniques
- **CLO6**: Create professional animation sequences and composites using a variety of features
- **CLO7**: Having a professional working style. Practice and develop ethics, professionalism.
