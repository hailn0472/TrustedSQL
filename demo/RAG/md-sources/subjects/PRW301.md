### Description - PRW301
This course is designed to help students both learn the fundamentals of public relations writing and practice their writing skills. Ethical and legal issues are woven throughout the text, which covers public relations writing formats for both journalistic and organizational media. Course objectives: - Master fundamentals of public relations writing and practice their writing skills. - Have the ability to expand their coverage of writing for digital and social media—including blogs, websites and wikis, as well as social networking (Facebook), microblogging (Twitter), photo sharing (Instagram and Snapchat) and video sharing (YouTube). - Apply knowledge to plan, write and implement communication pathways and public relations tools. - Write quality and relevant content for all public relations tools. - Be proficient in using English for communication and learning.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PRW301

- **CLO1**: To understand the relationship between entertainment and communication utilising perspectives from the humanities and social sciences.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend the requirements and skills necessary for creating effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO3**: To be able to develop and implement effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
