### Description - AET102
This course provides an introduction to aesthetic and the phylosophy of arts for students and provides them with the knowledge and skills to communicate their ideas and processes in a visual form more effectively. As a means of questioning and applying fundamental theories, concepts and techniques, students conduct research and produce practical projects. The course examines the basic elements of visual communication and how context shapes visual forms production and reception.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AET102

- **CLO1**: (Basic Knowledge) Understand the way people thinking about Arts - Aesthetics.
- **CLO2**: (Research Skills) Understand the contexts of the main styles and movements in Art History, which related to important inventions in manufacture, significant events on the market, talented and pioneering Artist.
- **CLO3**: (Critical Thinking) Analyze the records of the past in Art and Phylosophy History.
- **CLO4**: (Communication Skills): Study optional cases in aesthetic history, organize and express the individual thoughts clearly and coherently both in orally (presentations) and writing (assignments/essays).
- **CLO5**: Be aware of what is aesthetics and understand the stages of aesthetic awareness in history
- **CLO6**: Evaluate manual and/or digital art works, media and materials appropriately to produce creative ways solutions for specific cultural and technical contexts.
- **CLO7**: Having a professional style in arts activity. Love to research in visual arts.
- **CLO8**: Students have a proficient in presentation and teamworks.
