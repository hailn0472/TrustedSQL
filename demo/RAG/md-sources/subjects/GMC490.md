### Description - GMC490
Main objectives: 'As a course within the Media and Communication curriculum, the Capstone Project is comprehensive, allowing students to apply the combined knowledge acquired from earlier courses and projects to the production of more fully-realized work. The main objectives of the course are to: - Exhibit an advanced understanding of the relationship between media and communication; - Exhibit an advanced understanding of the theories of humanities and social science; - Create a self-project as a media and communication professional; - Develop personal/ team work on a professional level of quality; - Construct professional media and communication practices; - Demonstrate proficiency in communication, presentation, and the necessary skills for professional practice, such as the capacity to organise and manage projects as well as collaborate effectively with others. This competency requires knowledge of organisational structures and work patterns in the fields of economics, media, and communication. Description: - This course requires real-work projects completed by a group of 3–5 students, each student must defend their contribution to the group project individually in front of a professional committee/examination board. - Students are strongly encouraged to form groups and actively seek their supervisor(s), who will provide the necessary guidance as they develop their projects from initial concepts or ideas to completion. - By the completion, students are expected to acquire a comprehensive understanding and skills in the field of media and communication.  The project consists of Five (05) main phases: (1) Research and development, (2) Pre-implementation/Pre-production, (3) Implementation/production, (4) Post-implementation/post-production, distribution (if applicable), and (5) Evaluation Students are required to conduct 30% and 70% progress reports to present and defend their work in front of the Department's professional committees and supervisor(s). Students are expected to demonstrate a high level of professionalism when discussing, organising, presenting, and critiquing their work, as well as when implementing research strategies that contribute to the completion of the project in response to constructive feedback from supervisor(s) and professional committees.  Completion Criteria: - Progress on Project (04 reports): 20% - Final report/Production Book: 30% - Oral Project Defense: 50%  Overall Final Project Mark: 100% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10 Final Report/Production Book >= 4/10 Oral Project Defense >= 5/10 Final Report/Production Book must be submitted before deadline

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GMC490

- **CLO1**: Formulate, scope, and contextualise project concepts within the context of relevant media and communication expertise
- **CLO2**: Develop a research plan with the potential to yield results that can inform strategic or planning decisions.
- **CLO3**: Perform effective research, data analysis, and interpretation of the results.
- **CLO4**: Generate a strategy/ plan based on the research findings, a high level of critical thinking, and a comprehensive knowledge of media and communication.
- **CLO5**: Deliver plans with effective media productions, the utmost proficiency of risk management expertise, and the capability to simultaneously evaluate the plan.
- **CLO6**: Produce academically satisfactory reports exemplifying professional competence in the field of media and communication.
- **CLO7**: Conduct a project professionally and ethically while working harmoniously with group members.
- **CLO8**: Present and defend the project in front of examiner boards with professionalism.
