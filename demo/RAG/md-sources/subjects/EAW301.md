## Course Information - EAW301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 40-minute final exam + 104.3 hours self-study
1 session = 45 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENG303
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EAW301
*Description: English Academic Writing is designed to provide a systematic introduction to academic writing and a thorough analysis of common writing assignments, including paragraphs and essays. *Objectives: After the course, students will be able to analyse the organization a paragraph and an essay, understand the conventions and norms of academic writing, develop critical thinking, analysis, reasoning skills, and the ability to support their writing with well-reasoned arguments, and produce different genres of essays/ writing. *Teaching methods: The teaching method is flexible. Teachers can select approaches and methods that are suitable for their class contexts (e.g. using teaching aids such as lecture slides and guided learning materials and interactive tasks throughout pair work activities, small-group or full-class discussions to get students involved in the writing process (e.g. editing, writing, revising and evaluating writing pieces)

### Student Tasks - EAW301
- Attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Do all exercises and assignments given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EAW301

- **CLO1**: *Understand the purposes, and be able to examine the structure and composition of various writing styles or forms
- **CLO2**: *Develop a well-organized paragraph.
- **CLO3**: *Apply useful language and structures in writing
- **CLO4**: *Apply methods of incorporating outside sources, quotations and paraphrases in writing
- **CLO5**: *Develop a well-organized essay.
- **CLO6**: *Characterize ethics and professionalism in writing and peer review
