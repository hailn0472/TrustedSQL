### Description - ELT492
Based on the study results of English language and specialized subjects in the previous semesters, students shall complete a Graduation thesis with the content and format appropriate to the research area to meet all course requirements. Thus, the students are equipped with the knowledge and skills in scientific research and are able to present a research paper in English.  For each student, the Graduation thesis is the capstone project of their undergraduate program. Through the Graduation thesis, the students are equipped with the skills of finding a topic and persistently surveying, researching, and pursuing that topic, as well as practicing writing skills to present their arguments in a logical way. Hence, they can pursue self-learning even after graduation and nurture a capability of expressing their own thoughts and ideas. The Graduation thesis is conducted in GROUPS including 04 students (in case the students cannot be divided evenly, there can be a group of 3 or 5 students) The instruction on the Graduation thesis is organized in two ways: in-class and in separate groups. The in-class instruction is conducted in the first one week with the content regarding the rules, regulations, and things to be noticed during the process of writing the Graduation thesis. From week 2 onwards, the groups shall meet with the Thesis supervisor separately to discuss more in-depth about the group's topic. The discussion with the Thesis supervisor can take place in many different forms appropriate to the availability of the supervisor and the students (meeting in class, discussion via Skype and email etc.). The content shall be recorded in the form of reports (based on the forms provided). Thesis topic: the students can choose their topic in one of the following directions: (1) Specialized English (2) English in IT (3) English in Economics and Trade (4) Issues in applied linguistics (5) Issues in English language teaching and learning

### Student Tasks - ELT492
# Form groups, select and decide on a topic # Plan and divide the workload between the members of the group # Report the work completed on Google Form shared with group members and teacher # Attend all in-class sessions and private group meetings with the supervisor # Conduct, survey, and write the thesis based on the requirements of the thesis and instructions of the supervisor # Submit the reports, thesis, and presentation slides by the deadline # Defend the thesis before an evaluation board

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELT492

- **CLO1**: Understand the rules and principles in academic writing in English
- **CLO2**: Present an academic paper in the standards of the major
- **CLO3**: Understand the requirements and neceessary skills in presenting research results in English
- **CLO4**: Understand the process and methodology in scientific research on the chosen topic
- **CLO5**: Conduct forms of reporting in scientific research both by verbal and written methods
- **CLO6**: Enhance English skills in speaking and writing
- **CLO7**: Understand and have the necessary skills in teamwork
- **CLO8**: Develop ethics, professionalism and respect in research and group work
