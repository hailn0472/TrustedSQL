# ENP101 - English Pronunciation in Use


## Course Information - ENP101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in English Language
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENP101
This course will cover different aspects of pronunciation in English for practical uses including letters and sounds, combining sounds, word stress, stress patterns, intonation, speed, tone, accents and phonemic symbols.

### Student Tasks - ENP101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop and headphones in class only for learning purposes - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENP101

- **CLO1**: Recognise the way to pronounce and spell the sounds of English; the way to make the differences between the sounds clear.
- **CLO2**: Recognise the way sounds are joined together and apply it.
- **CLO3**: Describe basic principles of stress in words and sentences
- **CLO4**: Expain and demonstrate stress of words and sentences - which parts of words and sentences are normally stressed and which parts are normally not stressed.
- **CLO5**: Explain how speech is divided into speech units and how the position of the main stress can change the meaning of a speech unit.
- **CLO6**: Recognise and demonstrate the rising and falling tones.
- **CLO7**: Identify the pronunciation features of fast speech and apply fast speech principles in speech
- **CLO8**: Identify the features of intonation and apply different modes of intonation when speaking
- **CLO9**: Identify and demonstrate the aspects of pronunciation which change across different accents of English, both native and non-native
