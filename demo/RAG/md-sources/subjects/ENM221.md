### Description - ENM221
Listening: Students can listen to understand a speaker’s main ideas, explanations and examples. Students also listen to know strategies needed in academic situations such as listening for results, special terms, advantages and disadvantages, … Students also listen to handle the information they hear by using such strategies as understanding cause and effect, event sequences, recognizing definitions, predicting, interpreting speaker’s attitude.  Speaking: Students know how to express what they think and/or feel, and how to make themselves well understood by handling issues related to techniques or strategies used in daily speaking encounters. Students are also able to use pronunciation strategies to express correctly what they want to say in everyday speaking situations. Students also know how to speak correctly what is related to academic issues such as presentations, debates and self- introductions.

### Student Tasks - ENM221
* be punctual for all the lessons * attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination. * actively participate in class activities. * fullfil tasks and assignments given by instructor

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM221

- **CLO1**: employ general grammar and business vocabulary to express ideas and feelings in various business-like contexts
- **CLO2**: listen to and understand others' talks so as to have appropriate reponses that help maintain and develop conversations and other oral exchanges for 10 - 15 minutes.
- **CLO3**: give individual talk and presentation within 10 minutes to complete various tasks during sales visits or staff meetings.
- **CLO4**: read and understand various business-related texts including articles and reports of upto 700 words as well as, commercial articles
- **CLO5**: write e-mails, letters, memo, brief reports (upto 1,000 words) and other creative commercials
