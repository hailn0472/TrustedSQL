### Description - EBC301c
This course aims at shaping students' effective communication skills in professional contexts using English. The course helps students expand their vocabulary, improve both speaking and writing skills in English, and learn terminology and skills which can be applied in different business situations and contexts.  *Assessment scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.2 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: TE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EBC301c

- **CLO1**: Develop vocabulary and customs related to socializing and networking in English and apply them professionally in interaction with business contacts in different situations. LO1.1 Evaluate your current level of proficiency in business English communication LO1.2 Present relevant information about yourself in a discussion forum LO1.3 Develop your networking skills LO1.4 Write short emails with appropriate formatting LO1.5 Recognize and produce formal and informal language in emails LO1.6 Develop vocabulary related to jobs and companies
- **CLO2**: Develop vocabulary and skills related to meetings and apply them effectively in on-site meetings and teleconfrences. LO2.1 Arrange a meeting through email and by telephone LO2.2 List expectations and protocols related to on-site meetings and teleconferences LO2.3 State phone numbers, dates, currencies, and sales figures accurately LO2.4 Write a proposal with appropriate organization and vocabulary
- **CLO3**: Develop and apply language and strategies for successful negotiations. LO3.1 Negotiate strategically in formal and informal settings LO3.2 Identify and resolve customer issues after a negotiation has taken place. LO3.3 Evaluate and compare services and features of two venues LO3.4 Plan a business event using negotiation skills LO3.5 Write an announcement for a business event after selecting a venue
- **CLO4**: Develop and apply language and techniques to make effective presentations in English. LO4.1 Present information in an organized and engaging way LO4.2 Use charts and graphs to share data LO4.3 Use persuasive language in a presentation
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate the competence and ability to use appropriate language in different business contexts. LO5.1 Use appropriate vocabulary to write a mini business plan LO5.2 Communicate a business plan orally in an organized and engaging presentation
