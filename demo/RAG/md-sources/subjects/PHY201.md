# PHY201 - MODERN PHYSICS


## Course Information - PHY201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions
Lectures: 15
Lab/Tutorials: 15
1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: PHY101 (Physics 1) and MAA101 (Linear Algebra)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - PHY201
The course covers a sufficient understanding of modern physics concepts, and helps students apply this conceptual understanding to quantitative problem solving. It also helps students to perceive underlying physics phenomena through practical home projects. Topic covered: Special Relativity, Quantum Mechanics and Quantum Computing.

### Student Tasks - PHY201
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Students must complete the lab sessions with the average passing grade. - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information
