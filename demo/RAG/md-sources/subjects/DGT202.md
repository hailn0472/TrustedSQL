### Description - DGT202
This course covering basic to advanced digital concepts with an emphasis on problem solving, troubleshooting, and applications. Furthermore, it equips students fundamentals of digital logic design with VHDL. Topics covered: Introductory concepts; Number Systems, Operations and Codes; Logic Gates; Boolean Algebra and Logic Simplifications; Combinational Logic Analysis; Functions of Combinational Logic; Latches, Flip-Flops and Timers; Counters; Shift Registers; Memory and Storage and Introduction to Digital Logic Design with VHDL.
