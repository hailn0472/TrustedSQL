### Description - EVN201
Overview of the event industry including conventions, expositions, and major national and international events and their operational requirements.

### Student Tasks - EVN201

Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - EVN201
- Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EVN201

- **CLO1**: Identify the key characteristics of events, events industry bodies, in order to understand the business of events management as a service activity
- **CLO2**: Understand the scope and scale of the events business and the determinants of demand for events
- **CLO3**: Identify the social, economic, political impact and developmental implications of running events
- **CLO4**: Understand the planning process for events, including the budgetary organization of events, marketing, catering and technical provisions.
- **CLO5**: Identify issues of risk management in terms of event planning, operations and the issues involved in closing down an event
- **CLO6**: Intergrate a theoratical framework in to an event project
