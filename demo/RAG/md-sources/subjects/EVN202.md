### Description - EVN202
-Objective: Upon the rapid growth of the meeting, expositions, events and conventions industry (MEEC), the subject is aimed to provide an introduction to the MEEC industry and an opportunity for the learner to describe, analyse and evaluate the resources required to organise an MEEC, basing on that to develop a full plan and to implement an event. -Description: It gives a comprehensive overview of the burgeoning field of planning and producing MEEC — meetings, expositions, events, and conventions with the study of varied segments of the field, along with discussions of new trends. -Teaching method: lecturing in combination with case study analysis, MEEC practice

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EVN202

- **CLO1**: To describe the profession and the structure of the MEEC industry
- **CLO2**: To critically identify the role and functions of each key player in the MEEC industry, including organisers, sponsors, DMOs, venues, contractors, destination management companies
- **CLO3**: To examine the use of facilities for meetings and conventions
- **CLO4**: To clarify the structure of and the organisation issues in the exhibition sector
- **CLO5**: To analyse different planning elements in MEEC industry, including food & beverage, legal issue, technology, green issue, international issues
- **CLO6**: To develop and implement a full plan for an MEEC of different aspects
- **CLO7**: To build a professional manner as an MEEC planner
