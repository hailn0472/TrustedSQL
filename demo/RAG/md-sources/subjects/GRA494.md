### Description - GRA494
The thesis intends to integrate contents already covered in previous courses, as well as to provide an in-depth exploration of a topic of special interest or career relevance to the participant. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 3-5 students, however each student will defend the thesis individually in front of a thesis committee. Students must work closely with an academic supervisor and are required to submit a comprehensive written report as well as make an oral presentation of the research results in front of a professional academic panel. The thesis' topic is concerned with such areas as (1) the impact of Regulation on the Financial-Services Industry; (2) Financial Statements and Financial - Firm Performance; (3) Tools for Managing and Hedging against Risk; (4) Managing Investment portfolios and Liquidity position for Financial Firms; (5) Managing source of Funds for a Financial Firm; (6) Providing Loans to Businesses and Consumers

### Student Tasks - GRA494
(1) Choose the thesis topic within the selected banking concentration (2) Divide and schedule work (3) Attend all scheduled meetings with the appointed supervisor (4) Produce reports or outcomes according to thesis requirements (5) Defense individually work in front of an examination board (6) All of above must be worked in the appointed group
