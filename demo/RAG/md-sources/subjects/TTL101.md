# TTL101 - Luật và đạo đức kinh doanh trong du lịch - Business laws and ethics in Tourism


## Course Information - TTL101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 15 sessions; 1 session = 180 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENT503 or TRS601 or IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL (paper) 575-600 or TOEFL (iBT) 90-100
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TTL101
This course addresses the concept of the legal environment of tourism business. The course highlights the legal and regulatory environment in which people and companies conduct tourism business activities. It appreciates the complexities, ambiguities and many issue of the law in tourism business activities

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTL101

- **CLO1**: Understand the essential features, institutions and principles of legal systems with particular reference to the English legal system
- **CLO2**: Recognise the major sources of law.
- **CLO3**: Recognise the hierarchy of English courts and to be able to understand the doctrine of stare decisis.
- **CLO4**: Understand the basic principles and theories underlying the law of contract and remedies for breach of contract.
- **CLO5**: Analyse texts and oral presentations
- **CLO6**: Communicate complex ideas effectively
- **CLO7**: Test and refine hypotheses
