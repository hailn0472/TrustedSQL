### Description - EAL202c
This specialization covers the listening and speaking skills that non-native English-speaking students need to be successful in English-speaking colleges and universities. Native English-speaking students who would like to increase their chances of success in university courses will also benefit as well anyone interested in improving their listening comprehension skills. Students will learn how to listen to class lectures and take notes more effectively. Students will also improve your speaking skills for common tasks such as class discussions and presentations. In the capstone, students will create a video presentation on an academic topic.  Assessment Scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: FE>=4 and FR>=5.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EAL202c

- **CLO1**: Know how and use note-taking techniques in listening to lectures. CLO1.1 Identify difficulities in listening and note-taking and make it easier CLO1.2 Know strategies to identify topics and purposes CLO1.3 Listen to main ideas and details, and how to organize them CLO1.4 Identify speakers' stance and attitudes
- **CLO2**: Construct and deliver effective speeches and presentations CLO2.1 Recognize and identify different presentation types and typical structure of a presentation CLO2.2 Select what to include and how to organize ideas in a presentation CLO2.3 Communicate and present ideas effectively
- **CLO3**: Participate and perform well in group discussions CLO3.1 Recognize and identify different types of conversations and how to elaborate in academic discussions CLO3.2 Interpret people's meaning and express yourselves effectively CLO3.3 Maintain smooth discussions with interaction and participation
- **CLO4**: Practice acquired skills and techniques to make a presentation on an academic topic CLO4.1 Construct interviews to collect data and gather information CLO4.2 Plan and outline a presentation CLO4.3 Develop your presentation attractive using visual aids
