# CHN132c - Hán ngữ sơ cấp 3


## Course Information - CHN132c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online: 59 hours
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - CHN132c
- After completing this specialization, learners can communicate in Chinese at a basic level in their daily, academic and professional lives. Learners can manage most communication in Chinese when traveling in China

### Student Tasks - CHN132c
1. Join the online course (spec.) when being invited by the admin. 2. Enroll in each MOOC of spec., regularly study MOOCs at least 5hrs/week by watching videos, reading materials, taking quizzes, doing assgnments... as required 3. Respect the Coursera Code of Conduct, at https://learner.coursera.help/hc/en-us/articles/208280036-Coursera-Code-of-Conduct 3. Keep online learning progress promptly (bonus will be awarded) 4. Communicate and discuss in forum 5. Attend off-line sessions in the Campus (optional) 6. Must complete all required MOOCs with certification in order to take the Final Exam at Campus

### Tools - CHN132c
Internet access

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CHN132c

- **CLO1**: Can express basic personal information such as nationality, name, phone number, family introduction, occupation, time
- **CLO2**: Can express your preferences, can ask prices when shopping and describe the location of your destination
- **CLO3**: Can express experiences and thoughts in personal life such as celebrating birthdays and traveling
