# WBS210 - Wines, Beers, Spirits 1 (Practice)_Nghiệp vụ pha chế đồ uống 1 (Thực hành)


## Course Information - WBS210

- **Credits**: 1
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (50h) = 22,5h (30 sessions) contact hours + 27,5h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - WBS210
-Objective: To prepare and pratice beer and wine service techniques. -Description: The course focus on practical activities of wines, beers and mixology beverages. -Teaching method: demonstration, roleplay, group work

### Student Tasks - WBS210
Attend 100% of class hours in order to obtain the completion criteria - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - WBS210
- Internet - Facility and equipment requirement : Depending on the training condition of each campus, the course will be implemented in school lab designed up to 4-5* hotel standard, real hotel or other places, which fully met with requirements.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - WBS210

- **CLO1**: Develop general knowledge on the origins and development of the beverage industry.
- **CLO2**: Develop ability to work quietly; with cooperation; patience, carefulness, cleanliness and aesthetic values.
- **CLO3**: Indicate the effects of alcohol in the human body, both positive and negative, from health claims to the latest hangover research.
- **CLO4**: Develop an overall concept and tailor it to meet the needs and desires of a particular clientele.
- **CLO5**: Identify and distinguish types of glassware, mixing tools and equipment specifications and uses
- **CLO6**: Distinguish between fermented and distilled beverages and identify them on your shelves.
- **CLO7**: Classify wines according to type and recognize some of their distinguishing characteristics.
- **CLO8**: Prepare and pratice opening techniques of wine bottle and wine service techniques.
- **CLO9**: Outline the origins of beer and the market trends affecting today’s brewing industry.
- **CLO10**: Develop a sense of good discipline, a sense of responsibility, and a sense of serious work in the assigned work as well as with colleagues.

## Assessment - WBS210

### Group practice
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6, CLO7, CLO8, CLO9, CLO10
- Question Type: Four or five students per group. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through: Demonstration of skills on mixing different (in quantity) international alcoholic beverages in a prescribed timeframe; utilizing systematic liquor pouring and proper bar measurement; mixing of alcoholic concoction showmanship skill techniques.
The mark will be given based on each student's performance, not on the whole group's. It means each student will get their own mark.

(Suggestion: Wine menu design skills. Test on matching client's interest and wine, basic presentation of wine bottle, read the wine bottle label, make the client test the wine and serve wine)

### Individual practice
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4, CLO7, CLO8, CLO9, CLO10
- Question Type: Each student must be assessed through practical demonstration in a simulated workplace environment where beverage ingredients and equipment are provided. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through:
- Observation shall be done on the proper classification of alcoholic beverages and glasses; proper mixing procedures on several cocktails and the uses and maintenance of different bar tools and equipment.
- Direct observation of student providing advice to customers or colleagues on wine and its proper serving procedures.
