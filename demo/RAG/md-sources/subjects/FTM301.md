- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5

### Description - FTM301
1. Course Objectives: After completing this course, students will be able to: - Comprehend the evolving relationship between financial technology (FinTech) and managerial applications, analyzing how technological advancements reshape financial decision-making and business strategies. - Identify key challenges and opportunities that traditional financial institutions and businesses face in adapting to fintech innovations. - Evaluate the role of fintech solutions in enhancing operational efficiency, reducing costs, and driving strategic decision-making in financial management. - Apply insights from The FINTECH Book to real-world business scenarios, assessing the implications of financial disintermediation and digital transformation. 2. Course Content: Regulatory frameworks and challenges for financial institutions; the emergence and impact of fintech disruptors; technological innovation in financial services; financial disintermediation and its broader economic implications. 3. Activities and Teaching Methodology: The course combines engaging lectures, real-world case studies on fintech innovations, interactive group discussions, and hands-on projects that analyze the transformative impact of technology on financial systems.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FTM301

- **CLO1**: Understand FinTech Ecosystem and Trends; identify key players, components, and trends shaping the fintech industry.
- **CLO2**: Analyze and evaluate fintech innovations and their effectiveness in driving social and financial inclusion.
- **CLO3**: Explore and analyze successful fintech hubs and understand funding mechanisms and investment trends.
- **CLO4**: Examine enterprise strategies and lessons from successful and failed fintech ventures.
- **CLO5**: Explain Blockchain principles and predict emerging fintech trends and disruptions.
- **CLO6**: Develop the ability to critically analyze fintech case studies and real-world applications.
- **CLO7**: Apply theoretical knowledge to practical scenarios through assignments and case studies.
- **CLO8**: Cultivate teamwork and independent working skills with a high sense of responsibility and professionalism.
