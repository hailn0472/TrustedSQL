# ETP401 - Language Teaching Practice_Thực hành giảng dạy ngôn ngữ


## Course Information - ETP401

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 105 hours self-study (including assessment, projects and assignments preparation time)
- **Pre-Requisite**: ELP311c, EOB301
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ETP401
This course prepares English major students for successful teaching experience with theory and practice in classroom management through authentic teaching Teaching method: lectures + teaching performance + reflection

### Student Tasks - ETP401
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Students are responsible to do all exercises, and assignments given by instructor in class or at home by the due dates - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

### Tools - ETP401
textbook + reference materials

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETP401

- **CLO1**: Construct SMART ESL/EFL lesson outcomes to meet learners' needs
- **CLO2**: Integrate appropriate instructional strategies to meet an ESL/EFL lesson's outcomes
- **CLO3**: Generate engaging ESL/EFL lesson ideas that meet the set lesson outcomes
- **CLO4**: Implement the ideas in LO3 with consideration to learners' background, needs and anxiety
- **CLO5**: Critically reflect on the strengths and weaknesses of an ESL/EFL lesson demonstration
- **CLO6**: Justify the impact of teacher's behaviour on learners' motivation
