# ENW101 - Writing 1


## Course Information - ENW101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in English Language
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENT10x or equivalent
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENW101
The course is designed to develop and sharpen learners' academic and professional writing skills and strategies in English. Each unit guides the student through the structured writing process with plenty of full colour illustrations, examples, and exercises to engage the student and build confidence. This course aims at providing students understanding and skills from writing sentences to a full paragraph.

### Student Tasks - ENW101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENW101

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate the ability to write main idea sentences and supporting detailed sentences
- **CLO2**: Know how to describe places and personal items with correct sentence structure and vocabulary
- **CLO3**: Explain tasks
- **CLO4**: Narrate the past events
- **CLO5**: Explain changes
- **CLO6**: Explain opinions
