### Description - TOC201
The general objective of the course is to elucidate the intricate relationships between culture, heritage and contemporary tourism, analysing the economic, political and social importance of cultural tourism, exploring the phenomenon of cultural tourism from a demand and supply perspective. Introduce the basic issues of tourism culture, tourist culture in tourism development in Vietnam, tourism culture in tourism business in Vietnam, orientation for tourism culture development in Vietnam . The course focuses on the importance of culture and heritage as demand drivers for tourism, the impacts of tourism on Viet Nam and indigenous cultures, and the resulting need for managing cultural tourism.  Lecturing is the main teaching method in the theory part. However, the lecturer is required to provide relevant practical and hands-on experiences by using case studies or sharing personal experiences. The learners are encouraged to actively participate in lectures by active and student-centered teaching methods such as reciprocal questioning, games, rotating chair group discussions, rountable meeting or by self-learning and preparations before the lecture, Practical training sessions are organized to allow the learners to develop their initial skills and behaviors on the tourism and travel occupational/ opereational standards. Furthermore the group assignment project will strengthen leaners' technical and soft skills such as research, team work, leadership, problem solving, work planning and scheduling, presentation and so on.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TOC201

- **CLO1**: Understanding of the relationships between culture, heritage and contemporary tourism and sustainability
- **CLO2**: Analyzing the economic, political and social importance of cultural tourism and current issues in the development and management of cultural and heritage tourism products
- **CLO3**: Exploring the phenomenon of cultural tourism from a demand and supply perspective
- **CLO4**: Identifying the basic issues of tourism culture, tourist culture in tourism development in Vietnam, tourism culture in tourism business in Vietnam, orientation for tourism culture development in Vietnam
- **CLO5**: Evaluating the positive and negative impacts of tourism on indigenous cultures and communities in Viet Nam, and the resulting need for managing cultural tourism.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrating professional skills in cultural and heritage tourism management through the analysis, evaluation and synthesis of academic research with field observations.
- **CLO7**: Have a sense of discipline, professional manners and a high sense of responsibility at work; Flexibility in handling situations, ability to work independently and in groups.
