### Description - DET101c
In this Specialization, you will master design thinking competencies in an engaging hands-on, project-based format. We will guide you through a detailed 14-Step process where you will tackle a human-centered problem that you want to solve. You will learn and practice all of the most important tools from the field of human-centered design to generate ideas and connect with your customers on a human level. You will then deepen your practice by taking an in-depth journey through six key phases of the design thinking process, from immersion to learning-in-action, and explore the shifts in mindsets and skillsets that accompany them.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DET101c

- **CLO1**: Identify the key tools used in human-centered design and apply them to generate creative ideas.
- **CLO2**: Describe in detail the 14-step process in design thinking, from problem discovery to solution implementation.
- **CLO3**: Explain the key phases of the design thinking process, including stages from immersion to learning-in-action, and the shifts in mindsets and skillsets that accompany them.
- **CLO4**: Apply human-centered design techniques to solve specific problems in real-world project contexts.
- **CLO5**: Develop creative thinking and problem-solving skills by utilizing the design thinking process in a project-based learning environment.
