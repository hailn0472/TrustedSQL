### Description - SCM202
Logistics is an important part of the supply chain, involving the planning, implementation, and control of the flow of goods, services, and related information. Today, supply chains have become increasingly complex and global, with the participation of many partners and the use of advanced technologies to optimize processes, from raw material supply and production to storage and the distribution of finished products. Businesses must overcome challenges such as fluctuations in market demand, risks from unforeseen events (such as natural disasters and pandemics), and the need to comply with environmental protection requirements to maintain efficiency and competitiveness. This necessitates closer and more effective coordination between domestic and international organizations, as well as more frequent and widespread use of technologies like automation, AI, and big data to manage and track the flow of goods. This course not only helps students grasp the necessary techniques and tools in logistics and supply chain management but also enables them to understand the importance of optimizing these processes to improve business efficiency and better meet customer needs, especially as global integration continues to rapidly impact the economy of Vietnam.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SCM202

- **CLO1**: Understand the core concepts, roles, and functions of logistics in business operations and supply chains such as transportation, warehousing, inventory management and their contributions to overall supply chain performance.
- **CLO2**: Analyze the structure and dynamics of supply chains, including key stages such as sourcing, production, and distribution, and assess the influence of external factors and the roles of key stakeholders.
- **CLO3**: Evaluate supply chain fundamental strategies to address its common operational challenges.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate understanding of basic digital tools and emerging technologies such as automation, tracking systems, or data analytics to support logistics and supply chain decision-making.
- **CLO5**: Shows self-reliance when working independently and collaboratively
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate ethical awareness and reflection in business context
