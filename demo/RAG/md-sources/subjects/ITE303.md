1 session= 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5

### Description - ITE303
This course addresses several of the major ethical and policy issues that are changing the way engineers think about information and communication technologies  This course will cover three broad categories Cybersafety, Cyberethics and Cybersecurity (C3) and how they are interrelated, providing the opportunity for vigorous discussion and exploration of the complex issues involved in C3 technology policy. Through a variety of case studies and group work, detailing IT issues, recent case law, position papers, original articles and resources, as well as scenarios for discussion, student will give consideration to responses that can be applied in their own organization.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ITE303

- **CLO1**: Define moral, ethic and law. Discuss about their differences.
- **CLO2**: Explain the importance of good business ethics, and the way to establish Corporate Code of Ethics.
- **CLO3**: Explain the way to create an ethical work environment for an organization.
- **CLO4**: Expain a decision making process and discuss about the approaches to ethical decision making.
- **CLO5**: Discuss about privacy, privacy protection, information privacy, privacy law, application and Court Ruling.
- **CLO6**: Discuss about Privacy issues come with Workplace Monitoring Policy and Advanced Surveillance Technology
- **CLO7**: Discuss about computer crimes, freedom of expression, intellectual property, advanced surveillance technologies.
- **CLO8**: Discuss about IT professional relationships: between IT professionals with employers, clients, suppliers, other professionals, IT users and society.
- **CLO9**: Explain Benefits of Professional Code of Ethics
- **CLO10**: Discuss about Professional Organizations and Professional Certificate
- **CLO11**: Discuss Government Licensing and issues associated with government licensing of IT professionals
- **CLO12**: Discuss about IT Professional Malpractice, IT users' ethical issues and the role of IT usage policy in supporting the ethical practices of IT users.
- **CLO13**: Discuss about ethical issues and risks on network era
- **CLO14**: Explain about type of attack on a networked computer such as viruses, worms, trojan horses, botnets, DOS and DDOS attack, Rootkits, Spam, Phishing.
- **CLO15**: Distinguish type of perpetrators such as hackers, crackers, malicious insiders, Industrial Spies, Cybercriminals, Hacktivists and Cyberterrorists
- **CLO16**: Discuss about Laws for prosecuting computer attacks.
- **CLO17**: Practice and Discuss on Law on Cyber Security in Viet Nam
- **CLO18**: Discuss about Trustworthy computing and how to implement it.
- **CLO19**: Discuss about process of assessing security-related risks and estabilishing a security policy.
- **CLO20**: Discuss about 4 approaches used by identity theft and explain of using Identity Theft Monitoring Service.
- **CLO21**: Discuss about consumer profiling methods and how to use checklist for treating consumer data responsibly.
- **CLO22**: Discuss about Privacy issues come with Workplace Monitoring Policy and Advanced Surveillance Technology
- **CLO23**: List about typical Plagiarism detection services and systems, and how to use those systems to combat with plagiarism.
- **CLO24**: Discuss about protecting software from Reverse Engineering by software license agreements, Open Source Code licenses.
- **CLO25**: Explain how the manager's checklist do supporting for running an ethical competitive intelligence.
- **CLO26**: Discuss about Cybersquatting and how to curb it.
- **CLO27**: Discuss about Software Development Processes and security ethical issues related to safety-critical system development
- **CLO28**: State the impacts of IT on quality of life
- **CLO29**: Identify issues related to social networking
- **CLO30**: Explain ethical issues in IT Organization
- **CLO31**: Examine ethical issues on a case study.
- **CLO32**: Organize a group to perform ethical analysis process
- **CLO33**: Plan an ethical analysis project
- **CLO34**: Coordinate group work by applying schedule management techniques
- **CLO35**: Implement tasks which has been assigned
