### Description - RMC201
This course provides knowledge of all basic sociological research methods as well as understanding of specific communication research and assessment instruments. As a result, the students will gain a practical understanding of research, evaluation in academic and professional settings. The course program includes practical acquaintance with contemporary media and social networks monitoring systems.

### Student Tasks - RMC201

Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RMC201

- **CLO1**: To explain the nature of research in communication field and its process with different steps.
- **CLO2**: To distinguish different research methods in communication and select the most appropriate one for their research problems.
- **CLO3**: To apply qualitative research methods in communication field.
- **CLO4**: To apply quantitative research methods in communication field.
- **CLO5**: To create report and presentation to communicate their research project
- **CLO6**: To design and conduct a communication research project to ensure validity and reliablity and avoid ethical issues.
- **CLO7**: To respect research ethics and conduct communication research in an ethical way
