# ELS401c - Academic Listening and Speaking - Nghe Nói học thuật


## Course Information - ELS401c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 7.5 hours offline + 1 hour exam
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENS311/ ENS321/TRS401/ENT303 or equivalent subjects
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ELS401c
This module aims at improving students' listening comprehension and speaking skills. Listening to class lectures and practice of note taking benefit studens in enhancing listening skills. In addition, discussion and presentation tasks offer students opportunities to practice speaking and improve their presentation skills.  Assessment Scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Theoretical Exam (TE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, TE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: FE>=4 and FR>=5.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELS401c

- **CLO1**: Know how and use note-taking techniques in listening to lectures
- **CLO2**: Construct and deliver effective speeches and presentations
- **CLO3**: Participate and perform well in group discussions
- **CLO4**: Practice acquired skills and techniques to make a presentation on an academic topic
