# ITO101 - Inbound and outbound tour operation

Lectures: 30
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ITO101
The course demonstrates the understanding of tour operation, which focuses on inbound and outbound tours. Learners are required to have the comprehensive understanding of the distinction of inbound and outbound tours, how to design tours and elements that a tour operator must take into account while operating tours. The skills that the learners can gain after taking this course are to design, prepare and manage effectively a simple inbound and outbound tour. Learning vehicles serving for this course will be various from textbook reading, group working, simulations, activities to lectures and experiences sharing.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ITO101

- **CLO1**: Introduce the concept of International Tourism and Leisure.
- **CLO2**: Describe the cross cultural issues in tourism and analyze the global tourism statistics.
- **CLO3**: Analyze the Impediments in the growth of tourism in comparative role regional development.
- **CLO4**: Having a positive attitude and a professional working style
