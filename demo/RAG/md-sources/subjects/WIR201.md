### Description - WIR201
This course covers the fundamentals of working Ui/Ux (Interaction Design). It’s only a starting point, though. As with any artistic pursuit, (Interaction Design) is a lifelong process of learning.With what you learn in this course forming a solid foundation, you can continue exploring all the complex.

### Student Tasks - WIR201
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FLM https://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - WIR201
A4, Photoshop, Illsutrator, Adobe XD, Figma, Sketch …

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - WIR201

- **CLO1**: Students understand basic concepts, techniques, and knowledge of interaction design.
- **CLO2**: Analysis the interactive design process of Designing, Modeling, Evaluation, Setting, Finishing.
- **CLO3**: Ability to practice proficiently in all kinds of strokes and shapes in each specific sketch object with details.
- **CLO4**: Students applications prototype, describe prototyping and different types of prototyping activities, the range of tool support available for interaction design.
- **CLO5**: Create drawings with complete layouts that convey the content of the story being told and make good use of knowledge and skills learned of sketch.
- **CLO6**: Students have a proficient in presentation and teamworks.
- **CLO7**: Practice and develop ethics, professionalism.
