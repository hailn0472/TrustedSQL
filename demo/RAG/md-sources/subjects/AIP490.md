### Description - AIP490
The Capstone Project is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate AI studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further apply their knowledge and skills which they have found especially interesting or beneficial to them.  As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the capstone project and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This capstone project will be completed by a group of 2-3 students. Students will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, designing & implementing the product, writing the dissertation in formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.

### Student Tasks - AIP490
(1) Choose the project within the selected concentration (2) Divide and schedule work (3) Attend all scheduled meetings with supervisor (4) Produce document and product according to project requirements (5) Defense individually work in front of examination board

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AIP490

- **CLO1**: Use the knowledge of contemporary issues to identify a problem requiring technology intervention and specify the requiments for the proposed solution, algorithm.
- **CLO2**: Collect data, with consideration of techical & other factors.
- **CLO3**: Design, Implement & Analyze AI solution
- **CLO4**: Apply project management principles to software development project.
- **CLO5**: Write a professional research report
- **CLO6**: Practice oral presentation and inter-personal communications.
- **CLO7**: Exhibit professional working attitudes

## Assessment - AIP490

### Progress Reports
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: 5
- CLO: All LOs
- Note: 50% of Final CP Mark with weight (On-going assessment)
'- Presentation: (Report 1) 15% (5% from other student group(peer review), 10% from supervisor)
- Plan: (Report 2) 15% (15% from supervisor)
- Implementation: (Report 3) 70% (70% from supervisor where 50% for code and data, 10% for prototype documentation, 10% report of experimentation and transfer)

### Capstone Project Defense
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: All LOs
- Note: Referee committee give mark for each student 1st times: before defense (based on thesis)

Referee committee give mark for each student 2nd times: after defense (based on student's performance on oral thesis defense)

(Final defense mark = evarage mark of 1st and 2nd times for each student)
