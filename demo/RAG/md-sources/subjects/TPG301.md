- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TPG301
This course will explore existing modern typographical ideas and techniques and attempt to expand on students’ typographical range. Students will investigate the communication of content and the emotional qualities of text. Students will further develop their visual and conceptual fluency and create integrated communication design solutions. Emphasis will be placed on using typography, aesthetics, and composition to construct meaningful imagery and effectively solve communication problems. This course uses the art of typography, aesthetic element,..., to build up ideas and apply them in designing products for graphic design such as: publication, branding systems.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TPG301

- **CLO1**: Understand typographic and layout conventions used in the work of others
- **CLO2**: Know terminology used within typographic and layout design
- **CLO3**: Know grids systems, structural systems, alignments for typographic layout
- **CLO4**: Apply the principles of hierarchy and dominance to typographic elements.
- **CLO5**: Create works implementing typographic best practices
- **CLO6**: Appreciate typography’s role in design and understand its potential for effective communication
- **CLO7**: Organize words and images in design layouts using typographic structural systems, alignments and grids.
- **CLO8**: Develop an understanding of the language of typography, which includes critical theory, critique, history, technology, and craftsmanship
- **CLO9**: Be able to evaluate own typographic and layout design outcomes.
