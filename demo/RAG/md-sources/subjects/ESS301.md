# ESS301 - Embedded Software Development


## Course Information - ESS301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ESH201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ESS301
The course is to provide students with practical knowledge in ES field: 1. Introduction to Embedded System Development 2. Embedded Software Development Tools 3. Embedded Software Architectures 4. Optimizing Embedded Code 5. Opensource Operating System (Linux) 6. Real-Time Operating Systems 7. Opensource Programming 8. ARM Architecture & Porting OS 9. Setting Up Your Embedded Linux Development 10. Development a simple applications on ARM based Linux 11. Practicing on Embedded Computer 12. Testing techniques for embedded systems & Managing embedded development projects 13. Advanced embedded systems topics  Assessment scheme:  1) On-going asessment: - 01 Assignment: 20% - 02 quizzes: 30% - Lab activities: 10% 2) Final Exam: 40% 3) Final Result: 100%  Completion Criteria: 1) Every on-going assessment component >0 2) Final Exam Score >=4 & Final Result >=5

### Student Tasks - ESS301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. '- Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time. - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.

Board AT91SAM7S128 Dev. Board (for instructor) - The necessary electronic components will be re-used (ready in Lab107): + 8 blank boards for experiments, 100 000 VDN/1 board + 8 LCD, 60 000 VND/1LCD + 8 ISP cable, 10 000 VND/1ISP cable + 8 LM7805 + 8 Max232, 15 000 VND/1Max232 + 8 Crystals, 15 000 VND/1 Crystal + 8 x 5V power, 25 000 VND/1ps + 8 USB cable, 15 000 VND/1cable + 8 RS232 cable, 20 000 VND/1cable + Other components: resistor, capacitor, small electricwire...
