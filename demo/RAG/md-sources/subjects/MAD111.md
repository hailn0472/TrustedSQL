# MAD111 - DISCRETE MATHEMATICS 1


## Course Information - MAD111

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session=90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Student Tasks - MAD111
Students must attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MAD111

- **CLO1**: Manipulate logical expressions and use logic for inference and proofs
- **CLO2**: Perform basic operations on sets, functions and sequences
- **CLO3**: Analyze basic algorithms written in pseudo codes and determine theirs complexities
- **CLO4**: Discuss applications of modular arithmetic and analyze algorithms on integers
- **CLO5**: Use induction and recursive definition to prove a statement or to describe an object, and write recursive algorithms
- **CLO6**: Solve counting problems
- **CLO7**: Analyze recurrence relations
