### Description - SDP201
This course is designed as an introduction to the basic techniques and principles necessary for audio devices (microphone, mixer, ...) and audio editing application like Adobe Audition for students are able to record, voice-over, foley as well as edit and chord audio for animation movie, short movie, TVC, Viral, etc,. As a Multimedia course, the student will understand the aesthetics of sound, musically, voice, and as a result, develop critical listening and analysis skills for a movie like short movie, animation movie, TVC, viral, ... through the completion of regular listening exercises. Class critiques will be part of the projects produced. Objective: After finishing this course, students can make sound for movies, tvc, animation movie,...

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SDP201

- **CLO1**: Explain to students the principles and techniques of sound processing
- **CLO2**: Explain technical principles underpinning audio production workflows and equipment, relating them to use of digital audio production tools.
- **CLO3**: Analyze the sounds used in cartoons, short films, TVC, Viral, ... help students understand and capture the aesthetic of sound
- **CLO4**: Apply effects in Adobe Audition to create special effects for sound
- **CLO5**: Apply sound theories and methods so students can make audio corrections on Adobe Audition
- **CLO6**: Evaluate the quality and effectiveness of the edited sound through effect effects in Adobe Audition
- **CLO7**: Apply sound theory and processing so that students can make sound for cartoons, short films, TVC, Viral, …
