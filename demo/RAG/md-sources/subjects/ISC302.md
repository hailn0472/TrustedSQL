# ISC302 - E-Commerce_Thương mại điện tử


## Course Information - ISC302

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ISC302
Provides current and emerging issues in business transactions over the electronic medium and examines issues and topics in the functional areas of business as they relate to electronic commerce. Includes study of business models and concepts, infrastructure, marketing, security and encryption, retailing, supply chain, ethical, social, and political issues.

### Student Tasks - ISC302
- Attend Class Lecture - Reading assignments before lecture, complete quizzes before lecture - Complete one individual case study assignment - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Participate and complete Group Assignment - Attend Final Exam

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ISC302

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate an understanding of the foundations and importance of E-commerce
- **CLO2**: Planning and implementation of e-commerce and e-business projects.
- **CLO3**: Describe Internet trading relationships including Business to Consumer, Business-to-Business, Intra-organizational
- **CLO4**: Describe the infrastructure for E-commerce
- **CLO5**: Describe the key features of Internet, Intranets and Extranets and explain how they relate to each other
- **CLO6**: Assess electronic payment systems
- **CLO7**: Discuss legal issues and privacy in E-Commerce
- **CLO8**: Explain the key process in implementation of e-commerce SCM, CRM, ERP.
- **CLO9**: Identify the main types of B2B commerce: Net marketplaces and private industrial networks.
- **CLO10**: Identify the major trends in e-commerce, including social networks, cloud applications
