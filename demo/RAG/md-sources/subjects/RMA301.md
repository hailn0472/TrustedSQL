### Description - RMA301
In the swiftly evolving realm of social media, an organization's reputation can be tarnished within minutes, triggering a crisis response. This course on reputation management is designed to provide you with insights into the significance of maintaining a positive reputation. It outlines strategies for establishing a trustworthy image and fostering effective communication with stakeholders, helping you navigate and mitigate potential crises.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RMA301

- **CLO1**: To understand the value and role of personal character in communication and entertainment industries.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend how influencers drive social media, and how they take advantage of the technology to improve their prestige and income.
- **CLO3**: To comprehend how an organization manage its reputation in the New media
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
