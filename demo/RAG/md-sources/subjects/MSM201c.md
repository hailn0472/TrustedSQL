### Description - MSM201c
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the realm of digital marketing, with a specific focus on major social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, and others. It aims to equip students with the fundamental skills and tools required not only for the cultivation and expansion of engaging social communities but also for the strategic development and successful execution of highly effective social media advertising campaigns.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MSM201c

- **CLO1**: Possess a high level of proficiency in the examination of social media and advertising metrics to inform data-centric decision-making and ongoing refinement.
- **CLO2**: Possess the ability to build a loyal fan base, create an engaging online persona, and handle many social media platforms with ease in order to drive online interaction.
- **CLO3**: Be able to design targeted Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns using Meta Ads Manager to drive tangible business results with effective strategies.
- **CLO4**: Have the ability to create engaging, impactful social media content that strengthens your brand presence through strategic messaging.
- **CLO5**: Develop self-study and lifelong learning spirit and capabilities to adapt to the constant change of technology and society.
