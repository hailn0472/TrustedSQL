### Description - FIN308
This course provides basic knowledge of concepts and analytical techniques in international corporate finance, why MNCs make capital expenditures in productive capacity in foreign lands, how to manage cash in MNCs, and how to apply them to international market transactions nowadays. These include the ability to understand the governance of multinational corporations, and their investing, financing, and risk management decisions in a perspective of maximizing the value to their shareholders.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN308

- **CLO1**: Discuss the financial operations of a firm within the international environment.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate basic understanding of Banking and Money market and types of instruments.
- **CLO3**: Apply knowledge of interest rate and currency in solving problems in swap operations
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate basic understanding of the logic and the mechanism of raising the capital, and managing cash and tax by multinational companies
- **CLO5**: Analyze multinational capital budgeting to enable firms to make informed investment decisions.
- **CLO6**: Discuss trade financing and countertrade.
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate the ability to produce a clear oral report and/or academic written report, which follows research ethic, that applies understanding of key concepts in international finance.
- **CLO8**: Practice ability to interract and cooperate effectively with others in the conduct of business.
