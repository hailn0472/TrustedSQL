### Description - IAA202
The course provides a comprehensive view of managing risk in information systems. It covers the fundamentals of risk and risk management and also includes in-depth details on more comprehensive risk management topics. Areas of instruction include how to assess and manage risk based on defining an acceptable level of risk for information systems. Elements of a business impact analysis (BIA), business continuity plan (BCP), disaster recovery plan (DRP) and computer incident response team (CIRT) plan are also discussed.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IAA202

- **CLO1**: Understand the fundamental concepts of risk management and its importance.
- **CLO2**: Describe the process of planning risk mitigation throughout an organization.
- **CLO3**: Describe the process of implementing a risk mitigation plan.
- **CLO4**: Perform a business impact analysis.
- **CLO5**: Review a business continuity plan (BCP) based on the findings of a given risk assessment for an organization.
- **CLO6**: Review a disaster recovery plan (DRP) based on the findings of a given risk assessment for an organization.
- **CLO7**: Review a computer incident response team (CIRT) plan for an organization.
- **CLO8**: Understand methods of mitigating risk by managing threats, vulnerabilities, and exploits.
- **CLO9**: Identify compliance laws, standards, best practices, and policies of risk management.
- **CLO10**: Describe the components of an effective risk management plan.
- **CLO11**: Describe approaches for identifying and analyzing relevant threats, vulnerabilities, and exploits.
- **CLO12**: Describe the process of performing risk assessments.
- **CLO13**: Identify assets and activities to be protected within an organization.
- **CLO14**: Identify and analyze threats, vulnerabilities, and exploits.
- **CLO15**: Identify and analyze risk mitigation security controls.
