### Description - IPR102
The course provides fundamental concepts, principles and rules concerning concerning different types of intellectual property rights relating to graphic design and/or multimedia communication, particularly, copyright, trademark and registered design. Students will learn how to apply these principles and rules in protection of their intellectual property rights in relation to their graphic design works and/or multimedia communication work. During the course, there are some typical cases regarding intellectual property rights relevant to graphic designs and/or multimedia communication available for students to analysize and discuss. Students are also provided with basic skills such as how to acquire intellectual property protection for their creative products, how to prevent from infringing on other people's intellectual property rights when they produce their creative products, and how to commercialize their intellectual property rights. In addition to common teaching methods such as lecture, brainstorming, group discussion, instructor will use some teaching methods unique to law, such as case studies, problem solving, moot court, etc.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IPR102

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic rules and doctrines regulating different types of intellectual property rights in connection with graphic design and/or multimedia communication
- **CLO2**: Apply the aforesaid basic rules and doctrines to protect their intellectual property rights relating to their creative works
- **CLO3**: Understand elements of some typical infringements of intellectual property rights in connection with graphic design and/or multimedia communication
- **CLO4**: Be able to discover infringement and take action against a person who infringes on their intellectual property rights relating to their design and/or multimedia communication
- **CLO5**: Understanding legal issues in relation to licensing
- **CLO6**: Apply what they learn to negotiate, form and perform a licensing agreement to transfer their intellectual property rights to other person.
- **CLO7**: Be able to study related law by themselves to prevent infringements of intellectual property rights of other author/ registered owner.
- **CLO8**: Understand how to take action against infringers
