### Description - PIA201c
The Art of Sales Specialization is designed to make you more effective and efficient as you pursue your sales goals. Understand how to stand out in the crowd, attract customers, and build support for your initiatives within your company. Knowing how to “get to yes” is a crucial skill that can improve many facets of your life. Prepare to be tested, taught, and transformed as you learn to locate new customers and get great results.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PIA201c

- **CLO1**: Make you as efficient and effective in selling as possible
- **CLO2**: Run high-impact meetings, anticipate and handle sales objections, tell powerful stories and to give and receive performance feedback
- **CLO3**: Give great presentations with dashing style and self-confidence, ask the looming closing question, develop your brand and go above and beyond for your clients
- **CLO4**: Review the insights and use the tools to curate your Sales Toolkit
