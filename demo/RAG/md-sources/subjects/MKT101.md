### Description - MKT101
The course is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in marketing based on five key activities: (1) identifying customer needs, (2) providing customers with the right products or service to meet their needs, (3) assuring availability to customers through the right distribution channels, (4) using promotional activities in ways that motivate purchase as effectively as possible, (5) setting an appropriate price that maximizes firm profitability while maintaining customer satisfaction. The "Principle of Marketing" course by Philip Kotler will incorporate AI tools to enhance idea generation, structuring of documents, and practical application of marketing concepts. Students will use AI to brainstorm ideas, structure documents, and create marketing materials, while ensuring that the core content is developed independently. Tools Utilized: - lmarena AI: For generating marketing-related data and insights. - Statista: For accessing statistical data and market research. - ChatGPT: For idea generation, structuring documents, and simulating real-life marketing scenarios. - Canva: For creating visual marketing materials like infographics, templates, and videos.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT101

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate understanding of marketing terminology and concepts
- **CLO2**: Identify wants and environmental factors that shape marketing activities for certain target markets
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate knowledge of the individual components of a marketing mix
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate knowledge of key business communication strategies within the marketing field
- **CLO5**: Identify the organizational processes involved in the planning, implementation and control of marketing activities
- **CLO6**: Understanding basic characteristic of B2B and B2C marketing.
- **CLO7**: Understanding the differences of goods and service characteristic in marketing

- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7
- Question Type: OP1: A group of four or five students will choose a real company to write on selected marketing issues in that company. The input for the assignment must be based on your observation, direct interviews with managers and staff of that company, and the company's documents.
OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal

Evaluation will be based on both presentation (40%) and written report (60%). Specific contacts of interviews including phone number, addresses, websites must be provided in the appendix of the report.
