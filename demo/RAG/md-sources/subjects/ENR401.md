# ENR401 - SKILLFUL 4 - READING 4


## Course Information - ENR401

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENR301 or (ENS312 & ENS322)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENR401
This is Skillful Reading 4 course, extracted from Skillful reading and writing 4 and intended to use for Upper-intermediate students. After the course, students can be able to use reading techniques (previewing, scanning, skimming, etc.) to comprehend reading passages related to academic topics such as psychology, Sociology, Biology, Chemistry, Business Studies.

### Student Tasks - ENR401
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENR401

- **CLO1**: Identify the writer's position and identifying reasons and evidence of an argument
- **CLO2**: Identify causes and humor
- **CLO3**: Identify and express contrasts; identify references to things outside the text
- **CLO4**: Understand analogies in a text and identify sources of information
- **CLO5**: Assess whether research supports an argument
- **CLO6**: Identify different perspectives and recognize trends and patterns
- **CLO7**: Use headings to understand the main ideas and identify fact, speculation and opinion
- **CLO8**: Identify persuasion techniques and assess the logic of an argument
- **CLO9**: Identify concepts and theories and inferring criticism
- **CLO10**: Identify logical fallacies and express causality

## Assessment - ENR401

### Progress Test
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO7, LO8, LO9
- Question Type: Multiple choice, short answer questions, embedded questions
- Note: Teacher is allowed to select which units content (topic or learning outcome) to focus on each examination session.

### Project/Assignment
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9, LO10
- Question Type: Each student chooses one book/novel in English (supposed to be a masterpiece in English) to read and write a report following the format provided by teacher.
- Note: Teachers give topics, start and finish time for each Project/Assignment by themself.

### Final exam
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9, LO10
- Question Type: Multiple choice. Other types of questions may be included.
- Note: Not allow to use any document/information in any form in exam room
