### Description - IPR121
Teach the fundamental concepts, principles, rules and doctrines concerning types of intellectual property rights relating graphic design, particularly, copyright, trademark and registered design. Students will learn how to apply these principles and rules in protection of their intellectual property rights in relation to their graphic works. During the course, there are some typical cases regarding intellectual property rights in respect of graphic designs available for students to analysize and discuss. Students are also taught basic skills to prevent from infringement of other person's intellectual property rights when they produce their graphic works. How to commercialize intellectual property right is the incorporated part of the subject as well.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IPR121

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic rules and doctrines regulating different types of intellectual property rights in connection with graphic design
- **CLO2**: Apply the aforesaid basic rules and doctrines to protect their intellectual property rights relating their graphic works
- **CLO3**: Understand elements of some typical infringements of intellectual property rights in connection with graphic design
- **CLO4**: Be able to discover infringement and take action against a person who infringes their intellectual property rights relating their graphic works
- **CLO5**: Understanding legal issues in relation to licensing
- **CLO6**: Apply what they learn to negotiate, form and perform a licensing agreement to transfer their intellectual property rights to other person.
- **CLO7**: Be able to study related law by themselves to prevent infringements of intellectual property rights of other author/ registered owner.
- **CLO8**: Understand how to take action against infringers
