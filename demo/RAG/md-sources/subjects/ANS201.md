### Description - ANS201
'Description: This course is designed to enable students to write scripts for short movie or TVC based on an idea or a literature work. The students can understand the principles and structure to develop a script. Through a series of instruction sessions, work analysis and personal exercises, after the course, students can write comprehensive scripts for short movies or TV by themselves. Objective: · Understand the concept of scripts for short movies and TVC · Construct a script in the standard format. · Analyze and understand structure of the scripts for short movies and TVC. · Develop the character arches and their worldviews. · Develop a comprehensive script based on an idea or a literature work. · Understand steps to build up idea for an effective TVC.  Teaching method: Under lecturer’s instruction, through a series of instruction sessions, work analysis and personal exercises, students will be equipped with knowledge and skills to develop and complete a comprehensive script for short movies and TVC based on an idea or a literature work.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANS201

- **CLO1**: Understand the role of script in the process of making movies or TVC
- **CLO2**: Understand the method to turn an idea into a script
- **CLO3**: Understand script structure and techniques to write scripts
- **CLO4**: Develop characters and character arches
- **CLO5**: Understand TVC structure
- **CLO6**: Write a comprehensive script for short film (3-5 mins) and TVC.
