- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - GRT491
It enables students to further their knowledge and skills in tourism management and related topics which they have found to be especially interesting or beneficial to them.  As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the graduation thesis and gain an understanding of the standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 4-6 students, however each student will defend the thesis individually in front of a thesis committee. Each group of students will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, writing a thesis in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.  The thesis' topic should concern with the following areas: Tourism and Customer Satisfaction, Tourism Marketing and Destination Management, Tourism Management, Service Quality Management, Strategic Management in the Tourism Industry, Human Resource Management, Tourism sustainable development.

### Student Tasks - GRT491
(1) Choose the project within the selected concentration (2) Divide and schedule work (3) Attend all scheduled meetings with supervisor (4) Produce document and product according to project requirement (5) Defense individually work in front of examination board

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GRT491

- **CLO1**: Achieving depth and breadth of knowledge: A systematic understanding of knowledge, including, where appropriate, relevant knowledge outside the field and/or discipline, and a critical awareness of current problems and/or new insights, much of which is at, or informed by, the forefront of their academic discipline, field of study, or area of professional practice;
- **CLO2**: Creating a consciousness of the ethical aspects of research and development work.
- **CLO3**: Applying established techniques of research and inquiry are used to create and interpret knowledge in the discipline
- **CLO4**: Applying a critical evaluation of current research and advanced research in the discipline or area of professional competence and treatment of complex issues and judgments based on established principles and techniques;
- **CLO5**: Applying knowledge: Competence in the research process by applying an existing body of knowledge in critically analyzing a new question or specific problem or issue in a new setting.
- **CLO6**: Planning and using adequate methods to conduct qualified tasks in given frameworks and to evaluate this work
- **CLO7**: Creating, analyzing, and critically evaluating different technical/architectural solutions.
- **CLO8**: Being able to critically and systematically integrate knowledge.
- **CLO9**: Being able to clearly present and discuss the conclusions as well as the knowledge and arguments that form the basis for these findings in written and spoken English.
- **CLO10**: Identifing the issues that must be addressed within the framework of the specific thesis in order to take into consideration all relevant dimensions of sustainable development.
