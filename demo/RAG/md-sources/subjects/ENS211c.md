# ENS211c - Advanced Presentation Skills 1 - Kĩ năng thuyết trình nâng cao 1


## Course Information - ENS211c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 4.5 hrs offline + PE (Interview 7-10 mins/student)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENS211c
The course is designed to enable students to: - develop and deliver compelling presentations. - balance proven ideas with lots of speech practice drawn from the established knowledge in communication, rhetoric, linguistics, and argumentation, each course  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

Communicate and discuss in forum 5. Attend off-line sessions in the Campus (optional) 6. Must complete all required MOOCs with certification in order to take the Final Exam at Campus

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENS211c

- **CLO1**: Organize talks clearly, write them memorably, and deliver them confidently; Use rehearsal techniques to develop a strong, vibrant speaking voice, and perform speeches with dynamic movement and gestures.
- **CLO2**: Explain complex ideas vividly and accessibly, design clear and compelling presentation slides, convey passion for a topic while maintaining professional credibility, and speak dynamically from notes and/or a manuscript.
