# TTO301 - Tour Operations Management_Quản trị điều hành tour


## Course Information - TTO301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: TTM201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TTO301
Objectives: This course cultivates a comprehensive skillset for students aspiring to thrive in the dynamic field of tour operations. Graduates will emerge proficient in crafting compelling itineraries, implementing effective marketing and sales strategies, and navigating the intricacies of financial management and risk mitigation. Furthermore, the course fosters the development of critical thinking and problem-solving abilities, alongside strong communication skills and a commitment to ethical conduct – all essential for a flourishing career in the ever-evolving tourism industry.  Description: This course provides a comprehensive introduction to tour operations and management. Students will gain a thorough understanding of the key concepts and practical skills involved in crafting, organizing, and executing successful tours for travelers.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTO301

- **CLO1**: To articulate the core principles and evolving landscape of tour management within the tourism industry (combines concepts, roles, importance & trends).
- **CLO2**: Craft comprehensive tour itineraries through a strategic selection of destinations, transportation, accommodation, dining, and sightseeing experiences.
- **CLO3**: Employ effective sales techniques to captivate potential clients in tourism industry
- **CLO4**: Deliver exceptional customer service by adeptly handling feedback and resolving complaints with professionalism.
- **CLO5**: Master tour budgeting, cost management, and cash flow control to ensure financial viability.
- **CLO6**: Develop superior communication and problem-solving skills to navigate tour operations effectively.
- **CLO7**: Think critically and adapt by proposing creative solutions to unforeseen challenges and making decisive actions during tours.
