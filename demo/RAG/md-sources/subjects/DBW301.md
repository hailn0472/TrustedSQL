# DBW301 - Data Warehouse_Kho dữ liệu


## Course Information - DBW301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: Recommend to have knowledge of Introduction to Database (DBI201/DBI202)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - DBW301
The course is to provide students knowledge about Data Warehouse technologies : - The need for Data Warehouse - Review formal definitions of a Data Warehouse - Data marts, metadata -Trends in Data Warehouse - Plan and Project management - Defining the business requirements - Requirement as the driving force for Data warehouse - The Architectural Components - Infrastructure as the Foundation for Data Warehousing - The Significant Role of Metadata - Dimensional Modeling - Data Extraction, Transformation, and Loading - Data Quality and Data Warehousing and the Web - Data Mining Basics - The Physical Design Process - Data Warehouse Deployment and Maintenance It also provides students oppotunities working on Microsoft SQL Server's Data warehousing  Assessment scheme:  1) On-going asessment: - 1 Assignment: 30% - Lab activities 10% - 2 quizzes: 10x2= 20% 2) Final Exam: 40% 3) Final Result: 100%  Completion Criteria: 1) Every on-going assessment component >0 2) Final Exam Score >=4 & Final Result >=5

### Student Tasks - DBW301
- Student is responsible to do all exercises and quiz given by lecturer in class or at home and submit on time. - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/LMS at https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information.
