### Description - CIH201
-Objective: The aim of this course is to identify and analyse some of the key contemporary issues that are pertinent to the Tourism and Hospitality sector.  This course helps develop the skills of searching, selecting, analyzing informations, reconstructing them and conveying them to classmates through creative activities, thereby developing students' self-study ability.  Each year 5-10 key issues will be explored on the module, including: Sustainability, Technology, New Trends in Travel and Tourism, New trends in Transport, Global hotel brands, New Trends in Hotel Design and Operational Management...  -Description:The course provides an overall knowledge of Sustainable tourism development, The futur trends of Travel, Tourism and Hospitality; the factors affecting the operation of these sectors such as pandemics, the legal environmental.…. The course allows students to explore many different aspects of each of these topics by reading references and discussing together, conducting interesting activities in class under the guidance of lecturers.  -Teaching method: This course is delivered by means of participative lectures utilising an enquiry based approach and constructivism approche with supporting tutorials. Additional materials include Management textbooks, academic journals, news articles, news media, relevant industry publications/research related to the special topic selected. Lecturers are available for guidance and feedback during the course. EDUNEXT software can be used to support teaching

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CIH201

- **CLO1**: Research, identify and describe the comtempory issues of tourism and hospitality
- **CLO2**: Interpret, discuss and debat the issues with their own arguments
- **CLO3**: Select, evaluate reference materials and incorporate them appropriately into group assignment.
- **CLO4**: Convey understandable information to the audience and convince them with logical reasoning
- **CLO5**: Organize interesting activities to convey ideas and concepts
- **CLO6**: Develop independent thinking, analytical capacity and eloquence
- **CLO7**: Develop brainstorming, communication, responsibility in team corporation.
