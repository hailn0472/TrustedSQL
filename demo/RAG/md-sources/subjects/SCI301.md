### Description - SCI301
This course guides students to embark on an exploration of innovation in stardom, celebrity, and social media influence. It facilitates the investigation of tangible and intangible impacts, encompassing disruptive technology to business model innovation. Students will uncover evolving fame dynamics and symbiotic interactions, acknowledging stardom's authority shaped by achievement. Additionally, the course enables students to analyze the art-commerce relationship within digital transformation and comprehend the process and challenges of celebrification for traditional celebrities in the digital marketing era.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SCI301

- **CLO1**: To understand the role and influence of stars, influencers, and celebrities in communication and entertainment industries.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend how influencers drive social media, and how they take advantage of the technology to improve their prestige and income.
- **CLO3**: To comprehend how Hollywoods stars work not only as actors/actresses but also as business brands.
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
