- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: MGT101 or MGT103, MKT101, ACC101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ETR401
The course is organized around five parts: 1) Entrepreneurs and ideas: the basis of small business; 2) Small business paths and plans; 3) Marketing in the small business; 4) Cash, Accounting, Finance in the small business; 5) Management and Organization in the small business. Particular emphasis will be placed on internal, external business analyis, business strategies, business plans, marketing plans, asset management, and human resource management. Vigorous class discussions, cases, activities, along with group projects and self quizzes will provide the basis for the learning environment in the classroom.

### Student Tasks - ETR401
- Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend more than 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETR401

- **CLO1**: Analyse the business environment in order to identify business opportunities
- **CLO2**: Identify the elements of success of entrepreneurial ventures
- **CLO3**: Consider the legal and financial conditions for starting a business venture,
- **CLO4**: Evaluate the effectiveness of different entrepreneurial strategies
- **CLO5**: Specify the basic performance indicators of entrepreneurial activity
- **CLO6**: Explain the importance of marketing and management in small businesses venture
- **CLO7**: Interpret their own business plan
