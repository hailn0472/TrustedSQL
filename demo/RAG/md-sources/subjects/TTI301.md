### Description - TTI301
The course demonstrates concepts, technology, and applications of advanced computer networking. Topics include networking hardware, topologies, media, protocols, and standards; network operating system server and client software, internetworking devices; network planning and administration. Networks falling under the traditional categories of local area networks, metropolitan area networks, and wide area networks will be considered, with possible emphasis on local networks. Hands-on experience installing and managing network components will be arranged regularly during the course.
