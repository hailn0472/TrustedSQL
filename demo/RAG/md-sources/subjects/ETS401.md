# ETS401 - English Translation for specific purposes_Biên dịch tiếng Anh chuyên ngành


## Course Information - ETS401

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours+ 105 hours self-study
1 session = 45 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ELT402
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ETS401
"-Objective: Develop students' competence in applying linguistic and specific knowledge for handling translation of ESP documents. -Description: The course aims to delve into the translating practice of specialized documents in various fields. In addition to assisting learners to build up their own specialized glossaries, techniques and common specific knowledge, the course focuses on the translating practice of divergent documents covering such fields as literature, technology/IT, Legal/politics, medicine, and so on. The course can be implemented in the forms of projects, exhibits or workshop, and so on.  -Teaching method: Communicative approach

### Tools - ETS401
- Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETS401

- **CLO1**: Apply linguistics and specific knowledge in handling translation tasks
- **CLO2**: Analyze ESP documents in term of specific norms, language and knowledge
- **CLO3**: Translate ESP texts with precision, accuracy and consistency
- **CLO4**: Formulate a wide range of knowledge as well as topic-based vocabulary and structures for ESP translation
- **CLO5**: Foster skills of critical thinking and team work with professional working attitude and ethics
