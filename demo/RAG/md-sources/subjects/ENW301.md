# ENW301 - Writing 3


## Course Information - ENW301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in English Language
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENW201 or (ENS212 & ENS222)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENW301
The course is designed to develop and sharpen learners' academic and professional writing skills and strategies in English. Each unit guides the student through the structured writing process with plenty of full colour illustrations, examples, and exercises to engage the student and build confidence. This course aims at providing students understanding and skills from writing a paragragh to developing a full essay.

### Student Tasks - ENW301
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENW301

- **CLO1**: Understand and use paragraph structure, the topic sentence, unity and coherence of a paragraph and short essay, short essay organization
- **CLO2**: Understand process to write and can develop a full descriptive essay.
- **CLO3**: Understand process to write and can develop a full narrative essay
- **CLO4**: Understand process to write and can develop a comparison-constrast essay.
- **CLO5**: Understand process to write and can develop an opinion essay
- **CLO6**: Understand process to write and can develop a cause and effect essay

- Weight: 20.0%

- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6

- Note: Teacher is allowed to select which unit content (topic or learning outcome) to focus on each examination session.

### Project/Assignment
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Note: Teachers give topics, start and finish time for each Project/Assignment by themself.

### Final exam
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: Time duration: 40 mins
Writing an essay: Descriptive/Narrative/ Comparison or Contrast/ Opinion/ Cause - Effect essay

- Note: Not allow to use any document/information in any form in exam room
