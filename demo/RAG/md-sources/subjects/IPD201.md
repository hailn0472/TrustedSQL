### Description - IPD201
First part in this course provide: the theory related to the function of pictograms; the methodology to create a pictogram then family of icons, plan project from concept to develop icons for different media. Second part is learn how to design a pattern by understanding its anatomy and experimenting with different design techniques. The methodology to create patterns through research and explore. - The course provides knowledge about the history of development, the role and application of pictograms in graphic design. - Provide techniques in designing an icon and icon system; Skills in managing and using icon systems on different platforms. - The course provides knowledge about the development and application of patterns through each stage. Design trends and pattern usage in modern graphic design - Provide knowledge in researching, shaping and developing patterns. - Provide flexible application techniques of patterns on each object.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IPD201

- **CLO1**: Understand what icons are, the history of Icons and the importance of icons in the field of communication design
- **CLO2**: Know how to apply the basic knowledge of icon design to the field of creative design
- **CLO3**: Create an icon set by applying design software skills and learned tips. Contribute to updating, creating and orienting design trends
- **CLO4**: Define what Motifs are and distinguish the common types of motifs used in pattern design
- **CLO5**: Operate design repeats in pattern design and combine design software to create pattern sets
- **CLO6**: Construct collections of patterns that convey a message, a story and an art style. Contribute to updating, creating and orienting design trends
- **CLO7**: Have a professional style in design activities
