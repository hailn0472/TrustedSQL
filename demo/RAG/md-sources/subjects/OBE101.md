### Description - OBE101
The course is organized around three determinants of behavior in organizations: 1) individuals, 2) groups, and 3) organizational structure. Particular emphasis will be placed on individual difference, attitude, motivation, job satisfaction, communication, leadership, stress, change, and organizational culture. Vigorous class discussions, cases, activities, along with group projects and self quizzes will provide the basis for the learning environment in the classroom.

### Student Tasks - OBE101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - OBE101

- **CLO1**: Analyze the behavior of individuals and groups in organizations in terms of the key factors that influence organizational behavior
- **CLO2**: Assess the potential effects of organizational-level factors (such as structure, culture and change) on organizational behavior
- **CLO3**: Analyze organizational behavioral issues in the context of organizational behavior theories, models and concepts
- **CLO4**: Able to appropriately apply a motivational theory to a realistic motivational problem in an organizational context; able to provide management recommendations consistent with theory
- **CLO5**: Able to demonstrate an understanding of the five conflict management styles by explaining the appropriateness (advantages, disadvantages) of a particular style to a specific context
- **CLO6**: Understand what is meant by organizational culture, and able to conduct an organizational cultural assessment.
