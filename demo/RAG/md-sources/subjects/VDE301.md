### Description - VDE301
The digital video editing course aims to equip students with basic knowledge of the digital video industry, to master the video production process and basic techniques from scenarios, filming, editing, post-production, audio etc. The course will introduce basic lessons from script preparation, production organization, editing, sound processing, post-production and publishing finished products with software such as Adobe Premiere, Adobe Audition, Adobe After Effect, and other supporting software.

### Student Tasks - VDE301

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - VDE301

- **CLO1**: Mastering the basics of Digital Film
- **CLO2**: Skilled in scripting
- **CLO3**: Understand the basic design principles as it applies to Digital Video
- **CLO4**: Used proficiently software Adobe Premiere effectively
- **CLO5**: Creatively integrate text, image, video, and sound using video editing
- **CLO6**: Post-production skills for finished products.
- **CLO7**: Having a positive attitude and a professional working style
