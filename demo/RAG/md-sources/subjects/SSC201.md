# SSC201 - Effective Presentation Skills


## Course Information - SSC201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - SSC201
This course will give you helpful tips for making effective speeches and delivering them well. You'll learn how to organize a presentation, how to make it memorable, and how to communicate clearly. In the course, you'll have several opportunities to demonstrate the presentation skills that you learn. This will help you gain the experience you need to be more confident when you give a speech in a classroom or on the job.  Some materials in this course are taken from the Coursera MOOC "Presentations: Speaking so that People Listen" at https://www.coursera.org/learn/presentations-speaking-so-that-people-listen/home/welcome

### Student Tasks - SSC201
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSC201

- **CLO1**: Define the basic concepts of public speaking
- **CLO2**: Construct an informative speech
- **CLO3**: Compose an outline for a well-supported persuasive speech
- **CLO4**: Identify types of reasoning and logical fallcies
- **CLO5**: Construct and deliver an effective presentation in academic and professional settings
- **CLO6**: Define the concepts relating to audience analysis and listening and apply this knowledge to improve personal preparation of a speech
- **CLO7**: Compose appropriate specifc purpose and central idea statements for informative, persuasive, and inspirational/entertaining speeches
- **CLO8**: Distinguish between types of research and sources of information for a speech
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate the structure of a presentation and construct an outline for a presentation
- **CLO10**: List the types of supporting materials
- **CLO11**: Construct presentation introductions and conclusions
- **CLO12**: List types of presentation aids and apply them in presentations appropriately
- **CLO13**: Explain and apply methods of delivery
