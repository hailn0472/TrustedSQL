### Description - ENM302
This course is designed to help students further improve their ability to communicate in English in a wide range of business and business-related situations. It also helps students develop the necessary knowledge and skills to succeed in business. In addition to the business-focused contents, the course is enhanced with supplementary activities corresponding to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. In particular, the course will help students: * Communicate effectively in discussions, meetings, negotiations, socialization events * acquire and effectively use professional skills including telephoning, presentations, correspondence exchange, decision-making, and problem-solving. * enrich knowledge of business trends in the fast-changing world * enhance students' employability

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM302

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate knowledge of business vocabulary, terminology and concepts in digital business, finance, management, and operations to convey messages clearly and effectively
- **CLO2**: Build awareness and understanding of business situations, including how to communicate in different business contexts and working across cultures with authentic content from the Financial Times and other news sources
- **CLO3**: Apply the principles of effective business communication, including clarity, conciseness, and formality; Demonstrate a professional attitude in all forms of business communication, showing respect, politeness, and consideration for others.
- **CLO4**: Apply language skills, soft skills and business skills to work and communicate effectively in various international business settings
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate critical thinking skills to analyze business problems and scenarios critically
- **CLO6**: Write e-mails, cover letters, memo, proposal and brief reports and other creative commercials
- **CLO7**: Be cooperative, supportive, and responsible in group work.
- **CLO8**: Apply the processes involved in job hunting, including writing resumes and cover letters, preparing for interviews, and networking that enhances employability
- **CLO9**: Identify key points, main ideas, and specific details in business news reports and conversations, recognize different communication styles used in business settings and distinguish between different speakers and identify their roles in a conversation.
