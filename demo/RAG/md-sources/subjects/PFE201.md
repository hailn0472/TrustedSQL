### Description - PFE201
This course helps students answer three important questions: what is event public relations, why do we do it, and how do we do it? This course applies the knowledge and skills needed by a public relations practitioner to design, organize and publish a special event as part of a broader public relations program or as an independent communication tool. Students will learn how to execute a PR campaign for an event and how to effectively lead and motivate a team. Students also participate in practical training to organize an event. - Instructors will use a number of teaching methods such as group discussions and experience sharing, practical work practice instead of conventional teaching. - Students will have the opportunity to present their work and thought processes related to this work.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PFE201

- **CLO1**: Identify basic concepts and principles of effective public relations for event.
- **CLO2**: Identify, analyse and evaluate strategic public relations campaigns for event across a wide range of media and communication disciplines
- **CLO3**: Plan public relations campaign: decision-making for an event
- **CLO4**: Plan and execute public relations campaign: Strategies and Tactics, Risk management for PR of event
- **CLO5**: Plan and execute public relations campaign: Budget for PR of event
- **CLO6**: Apply the PR campaign for event
- **CLO7**: Appreciate and develop a professional and critical attitude towards the job of public relations for event.
