### Description - IBI101
Introduction to International Business is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles and practices of conducting business in a globalized economy. It explores the dynamic interplay of socio-cultural, economic, technological, and political-legal factors that shape the international business environment. Students will gain insights into the strategies and techniques for entering and operating in foreign markets, including trade policies, global supply chains, and cross-border management. A key feature of this course is the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and applications, enabling students to analyze international market trends, optimize decision-making processes, and develop innovative solutions to real-world business challenges. By the end of the course, students will be prepared to navigate the complexities of international business with a forward-thinking, technology-driven approach.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IBI101

- **CLO1**: Recognize the key issues of globalization, and the national differences in political, economic, legal systems and culture
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate understanding about the ethical, corporate social responsibility, and sustainability faced by international business, and explaination how global managers can incorporate such issues into their decision making
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate understanding about the global trade and investment environment in which international business occurs
- **CLO4**: Describe characteristics of the foreign exchange market, different theories relating to exchange rate and how to manage foreign exchange risks
- **CLO5**: Identify the different global strategies and entry modes that firms use to compete in the global marketplace
- **CLO6**: Explain how firms can perform their key functions including global production, global supply chain management, global marketing, global research and development to compete and succeed in the international business environment
- **CLO7**: Evaluate external environment of a specific foreign market
- **CLO8**: Propose the suitable entry mode for a specific company to enter foreign market.
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate attitude to adapt to differences and complex issues in doing international business
