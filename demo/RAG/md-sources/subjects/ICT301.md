### Description - ICT301
The course focuses on the mission, vision and educational philosophy of FFPT Education and stresses 14 standards of IBSTPI (International Board of Standards for Training, Performance and Instruction). These 14 standards help you understand how to effectively organize, manage and teach a course. Mastery of these standards together with expertise in the course subject area will ensure a high quality of training. Upon the completion of this course, the learners will be able to: (1) Understand the mission, vision and philosophy of FPT Education. (2) Understand the core cultural values of FPT and FE. (3) Understand 14 standards of IBSTPI and apply them effectively in the course of teaching the subject.

### Student Tasks - ICT301
- Students must attend at least 80% of lectures in class in order to take the final exam. - Students are responsible for completing all assignments given by the lecturers in class or at home and submitting them on time. - Students quickly access FLM page at http://flm.fpt.edu.vn to update course information. - Use laptop in class for learning/exam purposes only.

### Tools - ICT301
- Internet - Computer, laptop, headphone

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ICT301

- **CLO1**: Understand mission, vision, and educational philosophy of FPT Education and apply them effectively in the course of teaching.
- **CLO2**: Understand the core values of FPT and FE, and apply them effectively in the real life of teaching.
- **CLO3**: Understand the chalenges of higher education in the 4.0 era.
- **CLO4**: Master the concepts, standards and training methods according to IBSTPI (Interational Board of Standards for Training, Performance and Instruction).
- **CLO5**: Improve skills in classroom organization, classroom management, and use effectively all devices to support teaching activities.
- **CLO6**: Master all teaching methods and apply them effectively to the teaching process.
- **CLO7**: Improve skills to handle pedagogical situations arising in the teaching process.
- **CLO8**: Understand and apply effectively pedagogical ethical values in teaching process.
