### Description - AET102c
This course provides an introduction to aesthetic and the phylosophy of arts for students and provides them with the knowledge and skills to communicate their ideas and processes in a visual form more effectively. As a means of questioning and applying fundamental theories, concepts and techniques, students conduct research and produce practical projects. The course examines the basic elements of visual communication and how context shapes visual forms production and reception.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AET102c

- **CLO1**: Understanding - initial awareness and ability to evaluate art and aesthetics within different cultural and historical contexts
- **CLO2**: Analyzing the influence of cultural and historical contexts on the development of aesthetic values and practices
- **CLO3**: Exploring the formation and development of different aesthetic movements and their influence on contemporary art and design
- **CLO4**: Developing an understanding of how aesthetic preferences shape human perception and emotional responses
- **CLO5**: Applying aesthetic theories and principles to evaluate the effectiveness of visual elements in art and design
