### Description - ENG301
This course is designed to review and build upon students’ grammatical command of English structures in order to enhance their written and spoken performances in academic settings. The course seeks to achieve this goal in a manner that differs from typical foundational grammar courses, in that this course does not examine grammatical features in isolation from one another (e.g., spending a week on the past tense without looking at other tenses); instead, this class examines grammar points as smaller systems lodged within a larger system (e.g., examining all verb tenses together to understand how they function as a group). Students will practice covered structures, and will produce several original works that will grammatically evolve over the course of the quarter (as more and more material is covered).

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENG301

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate deep understanding and fluent use of different tenses in English and tense-related points
- **CLO2**: Describe clearly the use of nouns, pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, conjunctions, noun clauses, adjective clauses, adverbial clauses, conditional sentences and different types of sentences in English
- **CLO3**: Distinguish and fluently use common and complicated grammatical structures of English in oral and written academic presentations
- **CLO4**: Recognise how different English grammar points in the system relate to one another
- **CLO5**: Value other people's writing or speech
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate the ability to write or rewrite ideas based on a deep understanding of the intrinsic traits of language
- **CLO7**: Construct correct grammartical structures in practical activities required in all other subjects of writing and speaking activities in the classroom.
