### Description - ENG303
The course is designed to consolidate and enhance students' grammatical knowledge. It aims to achieve this by giving learners exposure to grammar as it is used in real life situations, offering them comprehensive explanations for grammatical forms and meanings, providing contextualized practice of various structures, and developing their use of such structures as well as their critical thinking skills through communicative activities. Students will also be informed of how to recognize common grammatical errors in their written work and encouraged to apply learned grammar in writing.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENG303

- **CLO1**: Analyze how grammar is used in many contexts from a variety of reading and listening texts
- **CLO2**: Assess whether structures being used are appropriate for written and spoken communication
- **CLO3**: Compose grammatically accurate spoken and written texts of different topics CLO3.1: Recall basic and advanced structures of English and their meanings CLO3.2: Identify and correct common grammatical errors in both written and spoken English CLO3.3: Explain how and why basic and advanced structures of English are used in different contexts
- **CLO4**: Proactively collaborate with peers for group work and problem-solving

### Project
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 3
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4
- Question Type: *Option 1:
Students work in a group of 5:
- Each group contacts lecturers of ENT/TRS classes, BEN's writing classes (EAW, ERW, ENB) or ENM classes to ask for some written work (paragraphs, essays or emails) in which grammatical mistakes are largely present.
- Each member in a group is in charged of identifying, correcting any grammatical mistakes in 2 pieces of writing obtained, and rewriting the texts.
- Each group collects their corrections and rewritten versions of the texts in the form of a booklet. In the booklet, they write a summary of the most common grammatical errors they can find in the pieces of writing they are in charged of and put forward suggestions on how to avoid them.
- The groups send their booklets to the lecturer in charge for marking and give a presentation on how they have worked on the texts in class for feedback.
- The groups edit their booklets and send them to the lecturers of the classes mentioned above, who will send the booklets to their students or use them as teaching materials.

*Option 2: Lecturer's choice
- Note: Instructions and schedules for the project must be presented in the course implementation plan approved by the head of the department.

Instructors are responsible for informing students about the project at the beginning of the course and reviewing them after grading.
