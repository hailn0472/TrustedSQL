# EVP201 - Event Planning_Tổ chức sự kiện


## Course Information - EVP201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EVP201
Overview of the event industry including conventions, expositions, and major national and international events and their operational requirements.

### Student Tasks - EVP201
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - EVP201
- Internet - Laptop

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EVP201

- **CLO1**: Comprehensive Understanding of Blockchain and Cryptocurrency Ecosystem
- **CLO2**: Comprehensive Analysis and Evaluation of Blockchain Technology
- **CLO3**: Application of Blockchain in Payments and Digital Asset Management
- **CLO4**: Assessment of Impact of Blockchain on Traditional Financial Markets
- **CLO5**: Understanding and Application of DeFi in Decentralized Finance
- **CLO6**: Analysis of Legal and Environmental Impact of Blockchain and Cryptocurrency
- **CLO7**: Integration of AI and Blockchain into Payment Applications

## Assessment - EVP201

### Class Participation
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: 1E-05
- CLO: All CLOs
- Question Type: Discussion questions and Constructive questions
- Note: Participation grade will be given based on students' preparation, quantity as well as quality of participation in classroom; quality of participation is demonstrated through asking relevant questions, making statements that add to and facilitate class discussion, and builds upon others’ comments.

### Group Assignment
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 1E-05
- CLO: All CLOs
- Question Type: Group Assignment topic based on chosen case study/discussion topics/small project/review and evaluate crypto projects.
- Note: - Case study topic/real situation
- Every member must present and contributes to contents

### Individual assigment
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: 1E-05
- CLO: All CLOs
- Question Type: Students analyze a blockchain/cryptocurrency project related to the content of the chapters or discussion sections. Compare theory and practice to provide a full analysis.
- Note: - Students must submit their individual reports in time (on or before the given deadline).If students submit late without a legit reason, then students will receive a grade 0 for the assignment.
- Students' individual reports will not exceed 5 pages in length (1,500 to 2,000 words, typed, double-spaced, excluding Works Cited page).
- Any proof of plagarism will result in grade 0 for the assignment.
- Individual assignment grade will be given based on the quality of the report with regard to the correctness and clarity of the calculations, answers and explantion.

- Note: - Closed-book test.
- Students are allowed to bring calculators, pens, pencils, erasers, and blank papers into classroom.
- Any proof of cheating during the test will result in grade 0 for the students.
- Grade will be given based on the correctness and clarity of student's answers.
