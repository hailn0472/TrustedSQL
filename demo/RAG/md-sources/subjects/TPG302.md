### Description - TPG302
This course will explore existing modern typographical ideas and techniques and attempt to expand on students’ typographical range. Students will investigate the communication of content and the emotional qualities of text. Students will further develop their visual and conceptual fluency and create integrated communication design solutions. Emphasis will be placed on using typography, aesthetics, and composition to construct meaningful imagery and effectively solve communication problems. This course uses the art of typography, aesthetic element,..., to build up ideas and apply them in designing products for graphic design such as: publication, branding systems.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TPG302

- **CLO1**: Analyze and Apply Typographic Principles and Conventions: Understand and apply established typographic terminology, layout grids, alignment techniques and structural systems.
- **CLO2**: Employ Typography for Visual Hierarchy and Communication: Apply principles of hierarchy and dominance to create impactful typographic layouts that effectively convey meaning.
- **CLO3**: Develop Typographic Fluency and Best Practices: Demonstrate proficiency in typographic design, incorporating best practices for legibility and aesthetic impact.
- **CLO4**: Develop a Critical Eye for Typography: Analyze the historical and theoretical context of typography, and evaluate its role in design.
- **CLO5**: Evaluate and Refine Own Typographic Work: Critically assess own typographic designs and identify areas for improvement.
