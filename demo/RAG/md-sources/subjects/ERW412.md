### Description - ERW412
Objectives: The course is designed to help students develop skills of critical reading, thinking and writing in various academic topics. Through interacting actively with texts of different kinds, students will be able to read, think and write better, at an upper-intermediate level.  Description Read, Think & Write 1 focuses on reading and writing in one course to help students read from a writer's viewpoint and write with a reader in mind. It is also designed to provide a systematic introduction to and an in-depth analysis of how to integrate reading and writing skills. The course provides a cohesive instructional framework to accelerate college readiness through unique resources.  Teaching Methods: The teaching process includes a variety of methods, from teachers’ side: lectures, guided learning, feedback and corrections and from students’ side: discussions, idea brainstorming and writing and self-study.  Assessment scheme: 1) Assessment - Assignments 04*15% 60% - Final Exam 01*40%. 40% 2) Final Result 100% 3) Completion Criteria: a) On-going assessment >0, Each component >0; Final exam >= 4 b) Final Result >=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ERW412

- **CLO1**: Annotate texts and develop vocabulary LO2.1 use reading to improve writing LO2.2 develop annotation skills LO2.3 develop vocabulary."
- **CLO2**: Preview texts and work with assigned writing topics LO3.1 use pre-reading strategies effectively LO3.2 work with assigned writing topics LO3.3 develop and narrow a topic for an essay LO3.4 use pre-writing stategies to generate ideas.
- **CLO3**: Identify main ideas and compose effective thesis statements LO4.1 identify topic sentences and implied main ideas in paragraphs LO4.2 identify (implied) main ideas in essays LO4.3 compose effective thesis statements.
- **CLO4**: Identify and use supporting details for main ideas LO5.1 recognize major supporting points and supporting details in texts LO5.2 use outlining or graphic organizers to identify major supporting points and supporting details LO5.3 use specific supporting details in writing.
- **CLO5**: Identify and analyze text purposes and text patterns LO6.1 identify the purpose of a text LO6.2 connect purposes and text patterns LO6.3 recognize, analyze and use narration/ definition/ illustration/ classification/ comparison-contrast/ cause-effect/ process analysis/ argument/ and mixed patterns in reading and writing.
- **CLO6**: Organize, draft and summarize texts for writing LO7.1 Identify common strategies for ordering information LO7.2 use information-ordering strategies in writing LO7.3 use outlines and graphic organizers to analyze a text's organization LO7.4 use outline to draft essays and to write detailed/brief summaries LO7.5 write paraphrases.
- **CLO7**: write titles, introductions and conclusions effectively LO8.1 analyze and create title LO8.2 read, analyze and create introductions LO8.3 read, analyze and create conclusions.
- **CLO8**: Identify, analyze and use inferrences as well as tones LO9.1 use details and common knowledge to make sound inferences LO9.2 draw accurate inferences from visuals LO9.3 analyze tone in texts LO9.4 choose an appropriate tone for writing assignment LO9.5 analyze figurative language to make accurate inferences LO9.6 use figurative language in writing.
- **CLO9**: Develop emotional intelligence and critical thinking; characterize and integrate the awareness of emotional intelligence and critical thinking into writing practice LO1.1 develop emotional intelligence and apply it to writing LO1.2 think critically and understand how critical thinking affects reading and writing skills LO1.3 identify features that boost successful writing.
