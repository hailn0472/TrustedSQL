### Description - TAB201
Objectives: After completing the course, students will understand the background, operations, and structure of travel agency and tour operation. Students will understand the differences between tour operator and travel agency, and will be able to examine and evaluate how these two types of businesses operate as a foundation for future professional growth.  Description: This subject covers Travel Agency philosophy, ideas, and prerequisites for running a travel agency firm. Practical case studies from travel agencies worldwide and in Vietnam will be interspersed with these principles. In these case studies, students must discuss and work in groups so that teachers can assess their knowledge and expertise through presentations and tests.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TAB201

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic operations, organizational structure, historical evolution, main characteristics and quality standards of a travel agency"
- **CLO2**: Describe the primary functions of a travel agency and clarify how these functions seamlessly integrate with the operations of other service businesses throughout a tour.
- **CLO3**: Examine the similarities and contrasts between a travel agency and a tour operation to better understand the distinctions between these two types.
- **CLO4**: Maintain ethical and professional conduct standards is of the utmost importance in the tourism industry
- **CLO5**: Determine the structure of the travel agency, and list of businesses along with their respective functions in the tourism industry is provided.
- **CLO6**: Identify the environmental repercussions, the social, cultural and economic effects that travel agencies have on the countries that they serve as destinations.
- **CLO7**: Desmontrate good skills, tactics, and attitudes to carry out the basic duties of a travel agency executive.
- **CLO8**: Discuss the relationship between the transportation industry, tour operators, and travel agencies.
- **CLO9**: Apply learned knowledge to analyze travel agency strategies through case studies
