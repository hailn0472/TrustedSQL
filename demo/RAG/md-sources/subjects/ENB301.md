### Description - ENB301
Objectives: The course is designed to help undergraduate students enhance their business communication via authentic and various written forms such as emails, cover letters, and CVs. With practical tips and detailed guidelines, students will be able to write more effectively and appropriately in business contexts, assuring success in delivering key messages.  Description ENB101 addresses a wide-ranging spectrum of business communication with the straightforward seven-step method. These easy-to-follow steps save students time from start to finish, and helpful checklists will boost their confidence in business writing. Students will learn to promote themselves and their ideas clearly and concisely―whether putting together a persuasive project proposal or dealing with daily email. The course also provides comprehensive examples for all the most popular forms of communication, including slide presentations, résumés, cover letters, web copy, and a thorough guide to the art of crafting e-mails and instant messages. Insightful sidebars from experts in various fields demystify the skills of self-editing, creating content, and overcoming writer’s block, and the author’s reference-ready resources on style, punctuation, and grammar will keep students' writing error-free.  Teaching Methods: The teaching & learning process includes a variety of methods: From teachers’ side: lectures, guided learning, feedback and corrections; From students’ side: discussions, reading, research, writing and self-study.  Assessment scheme: 1) On-going assessment - Assignment 01*20% 20% - Progress Tests 02*20% 40% 2) Final Examination: 01*40% 40% 3) Final Result 100% 4) Completion Criteria: a) On-going assessment >0, Each component >0 b) Final Examination >=4 c) Final Result >=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENB301

- **CLO1**: Write emails effectively and appropriately LO2.1 Identify the basics of emails LO2.2 Understand the tips of email writing LO2.3 Write emails in an effective and appropriate way
- **CLO2**: Write typical employment documents effectively and appropriately LO3.1 Write CVs (résumés) effectively and appropriately LO3.2 Write cover letters effectively and appropriately
- **CLO3**: Write business memos effectively and appropriately LO4.1 Identify the format of a business memo LO4.2 Understand the tips of memo writing LO4.3 Write business memos in an effective and appropriate way
- **CLO4**: Improve general business writing skills LO5.1 Overcome writer's block LO5.2 Use appropriate writing styles LO5.3 Avoid common and confusing grammatical issues in business writing LO5.4 Avoid common and confusing lexical isssues in business writing
- **CLO5**: Understand and integrate their awareness of the seven-step method in business writing LO1.1 Get the ask clear LO1.2 Write for the reader LO1.3 Start the writing strong and specific LO1.4 Keep the writing concise LO1.5 Fill in missing content and remove extraneous content LO1.6 Write in plain English LO1.7 Fix the wrong information or content
