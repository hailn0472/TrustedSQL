# JFE301 - Japanese fundamental information technologyskill standards_Kỹ năng CNTT cơ bản của Nhật Bản


## Course Information - JFE301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - JFE301
Students will proactive review with the guidance and support from lecturers. This course is arranged in form of tutorial sessions, not focus on lectures.

### Student Tasks - JFE301
1. Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. 2. Student is responsible to learn on Internet CMS system by your-self 3. Student is responsible to do all exercises and practical given by instructor in class or at home and submit them on time. 4. Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - JFE301

- **CLO1**: Understand the importance, applications and basic concepts of ITSS, IT career development
- **CLO2**: Understand the basic bridge engineer to exchange with IT expert from Japan following Japanese ITSS Frameworks
- **CLO3**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of Computer Science Fundamentals
- **CLO4**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of Computer Systems
- **CLO5**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of System Development
- **CLO6**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of Network Technology
- **CLO7**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of Database Technology
- **CLO8**: Review, summarize and systematized knowledge of Security and Standardization
- **CLO9**: Review, summarize and systematize knowledge of Computerization and Management
