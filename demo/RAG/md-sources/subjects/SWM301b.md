### Description - SWM301b
After studying this subject, you will know the basic concepts in IT project management. The course will lead you step by step to complete an IT project. As a project administrator, you will be instructed to take steps from Project Startup, Planning, Performing monitoring and control and finally Project Completion Management. Students who can finish their studies can: - Understand the concepts of projects, project management - Implement all project stages from beginning to end - Participate in implementation of start-up, planning, implementation, control and completion of projects.

### Student Tasks - SWM301b
- Students should self-study all lessons on LMS in time - Pass all conditions before taking final exam in campus

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SWM301b

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic concepts of projects and project management
- **CLO2**: Know how to prepare and create a project
- **CLO3**: Ability to identify constraints, project scope from which to estimate and schedule work
- **CLO4**: Understand and determine costs, budget for project implementation
- **CLO5**: Understand the requirements of resources and project quality
- **CLO6**: Can identify and control project risks as well as change requirements
- **CLO7**: It is possible to deploy, monitor, monitor and complete project work according to plan and schedule
- **CLO8**: Understanding of IT projects, project management cycles and common project implementation approaches (Waterfall, Agile)
