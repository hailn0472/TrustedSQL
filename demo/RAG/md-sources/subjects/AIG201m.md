### Description - AIG201m
This course based on the Deep Learning Specialization at https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning. This break into AI by Deeplearning. Deep Learning is a superpower. With it you can make a computer see, synthesize novel art, translate languages, render a medical diagnosis, or build pieces of a car that can drive itself. If that isn’t a superpower, I don’t know what is.  — Andrew Ng, Founder of deeplearning.ai and Coursera

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - AIG201m

- **CLO1**: Describe main concepts of AI, ML, DL
- **CLO2**: Know the ML algorithm and application
- **CLO3**: Describe Sequence models & Attention mechanism
- **CLO4**: Describe the Foundations of Convolutional Neural Networks
- **CLO5**: Apply the Deep convolutional models: case studies
- **CLO6**: Descibe the Object detection
- **CLO7**: Apply the Special applications: Face recognition & Neural style transfer
- **CLO8**: Apply the Practical aspects of Deep Learning
- **CLO9**: Describe Optimization algorithms
- **CLO10**: Describe Recurrent Neural Networks
- **CLO11**: Describe the Natural Language Processing & Word Embeddings
