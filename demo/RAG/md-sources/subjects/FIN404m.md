### Description - FIN404m
This Course is based on the Coursera Specialization "Investment Management Specialization", it will help students understand how investment strategies are designed to reach financial goals in a global context. You will learn the theory that underlies strong investment decisions, as well as practical, real-world skills that you can apply when discussing investment proposals with your advisor, managing your personal assets or your client’s investment portfolio. Students will then learn how to adequately build and manage a portfolio with a long-term view while gaining an appreciation for novel research advances in finance and related areas as well as future trends that are shaping the investment management industry.  *Teaching method: This is a blended-learning course, in which students will take a look at the videos & slides and reading on Coursera first at home, then rewatch & discuss key questions with classmates and teachers at classes.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN404m

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate basic understanding of financial markets, fixed income securities and other markets such as gold, real estate, hedge funds and private markets.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate the ability to produce an appropriate wealth plan for clients
- **CLO3**: Understand the central banks policies and the impact on financial markets
- **CLO4**: Understand the link between cognitive biases, emotions and moral values and investment decisions
- **CLO5**: Understand the concept of efficient market hypothesis and behaviral finance theory
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate basic understanding of building porfolio methodologies
- **CLO7**: Understand the relationship between risk and return in building a investment portfolio
- **CLO8**: Apply key risk management tools to hedge market and currency risks
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate basic understanding of evaluating investment performance methods
- **CLO10**: Apply key models to measure investment performance
