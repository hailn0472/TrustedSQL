## Course Information - HMO101

### Description - HMO101
The course address to the structure, scope and measurement of tourism and hospitality industry. The students will be exposed to the roles of industry business players and governments. This course addresses the knowledge and skill needed for students to understand the development of the hospitality and tourism industry as well as its challenges.

### Student Tasks - HMO101
- Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend more than 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HMO101

- **CLO1**: Describe the definition, charateristics of the hospitality and tourism industry
- **CLO2**: Identify future trends in the hospitality and tourism industry (Chapter 10-13)
- **CLO3**: Describe and classify different hotel businesses
- **CLO4**: Identify main duties and responsibilities of the room division function (operations)
- **CLO5**: Describe the duties and responsibilities of the food and beverage function (operations)
- **CLO6**: Classify wine, beer, spirits, nonalcoholic beverages and describe bar & beverage operations.
- **CLO7**: Classify and differentiate characteristics of the restaurant business
- **CLO8**: Describe restaurant operations and its functional areas.
- **CLO9**: Outline and distinguish different managed services.
- **CLO10**: Explain the importance of tourism and describe key impacts on tourism organizations.
