### Description - OBE102c
This specialization is designed to help students become an adaptable leader, ready to face the challenges of the disrupted workplace. This specialization will equip students with the skills to lead and navigate the ever-changing global environment we now work in. Through four themed courses students will: learn how to motivate staff with meaningful work (Become a Meaning Maker); better enable your organization to deliver on its strategic objectives (Know Your Organisation); harness your team’s diversity and use teamwork to innovate and increase your organization’s output (Know Your People), and strengthen your personal leadership skills (Adapt Your Leadership Style).

### Student Tasks - OBE102c
Access the specialization on Coursera and complete all courses' requirements to earn a certificate of completion for the specialization, in order to be qualified for the Final Exam.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - OBE102c

- **CLO1**: Define the key organisational capabilities required for a firm’s positioning strategy as the anchoring point for leading an organisation
- **CLO2**: Identify the organisational misalignment issues that a leader should prioritize and focus their change plan on
- **CLO3**: Assess the degree of alignment between key capabilities and organisational architecture (culture, structure, people practices, systems)
- **CLO4**: Develop and present concrete, suitable, and feasible organisational change recommendations that can resolve organisational misalignment issues
- **CLO5**: Explain how individuals may differ across a range of attributes including their personality, values, perceptions, and emotions
- **CLO6**: Explain the motivational characteristics of work and apply these to job design
- **CLO7**: Analyse the implications of workplace diversity, including challenges and potential benefits
- **CLO8**: Analyze the role of team dynamics and composition in the team performance
- **CLO9**: Critically evaluate the similarities and differences between traditional and contemporary theories of leadership
- **CLO10**: Analyse how post-industrial forms of participative and self-organising leadership might enable or disable effective cross-functional teamwork
- **CLO11**: Examine the complexities and challenges of day-to-day organisational life might require agile shifts between different leadership styles
- **CLO12**: Evaluate approaches to influencing and creating meaning at work
- **CLO13**: Evaluate, create and apply strategies to leverage meaning making through branding
- **CLO14**: Evaluate and apply approaches to meaning making that play a role in aligning employee buy-in of organisational narratives
- **CLO15**: Situate discussions of meaning making within broader social contexts and concerns.
