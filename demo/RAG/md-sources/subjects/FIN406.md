### Description - FIN406
Financial Risk Management course explains all aspects of financial risk and financial institution regulation, helping you better understand the financial markets—and their potential dangers. Inside, you’ll learn the different types of risk, how and where they appear in different types of institutions, and how the regulatory structure of each institution affects risk management practices.

### Student Tasks - FIN406
* Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. * Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments given by instructors in class or at home and submit on time * Use laptop in class only for learning purpose * Promptly access to http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN406

- **CLO1**: Articulate applicability of conduct risk in various types of financial institutions, its drivers and mitigation techniques
- **CLO2**: Classify and comprehend core risk measurement and management tools to a variety of risks faced by institutions
- **CLO3**: Evaluate key risks applicable to financial institutions using core risk measurement tools
- **CLO4**: Evaluate the effectiveness of the risk monitoring frameworks used by financial institutions
- **CLO5**: Identify and articulate the key aspects of Basel (II, and III) framework and the shortcomings of the Basel Frameworks.
- **CLO6**: Shows self-reliance when working independently and collaboratively
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate ethical awareness and reflection in business context
