### Description - ECL301
Objective This course aims to introduce students to the foundational principles of corpus linguistics while providing hands-on experience with corpus tools and analysis. Students will learn how to build and interpret corpora, apply basic statistical methods, use a register-based analytical framework, present their research findings, and explore advanced directions in the field. Description This course offers a practical introduction to corpus linguistics through hands-on work with real-world language data. Students will use accessible tools like AntConc and BYU corpora to build, explore, and analyze their own corpora. Emphasis is placed on developing search strategies, interpreting findings, and using basic statistics. The course also introduces a register-based approach to guide analysis and includes support for research writing and presentations. Designed for beginners, it aims to inspire further study and prepare students for more advanced corpus research. Teaching method Lectures and discussion, demonstration and practice-based teaching, flipped classroom, peer collaboration, project-based learning

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECL301

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the foundational principles of corpus linguistics, including the differences between prescriptive and descriptive language rules, language variation, and the application of corpora for linguistic research.
- **CLO2**: Be able to apply register analysis in their corpus research, analyzing linguistic variations across different contexts (spoken vs. written, academic vs. informal) and interpreting language use in relation to communicative functions.
- **CLO3**: Gain proficiency in conducting corpus-based research, including identifying linguistic features, searching corpora for specific language patterns.
- **CLO4**: Develop skills in applying quantitative and qualitative analysis techniques, building their own specialized corpora tailored to specific research questions, as well as using corpus software like AntConc to analyze the linguistic data they collect.
- **CLO5**: Be able to design and execute a corpus-based research project, from data collection to statistical analysis and functional interpretation, and present their findings through written reports and oral presentations.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate effective communication, teamwork, and leadership skills by engaging in collaborative projects, applying ethical and professional standards, and evaluating the efficiency of individual and group performance in class activities/tasks and assignments.
- **CLO7**: Develop and implement self-directed learning strategies to enhance expertise and skills concerning corpus linguistics, cultivate a lifelong learning mindset, and exhibit confidence and adaptability in diverse social contexts while maintaining identity and physical resilience.
- **CLO8**: Utilize information technology tools efficiently and responsibly in learning, adhering to ethical guidelines and demonstrating competence in planning, executing, and overseeing class activities/tasks and assignments.
