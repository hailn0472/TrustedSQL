## Course Information - ECB101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Advanced
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1 hour final exam + 104 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ECB101
*British Culture enables students of British society to understand and enjoy a fascinating some of the most significant features of cultural and artistic life in modern Britain. *American Cultural Studies offers a wide-ranging overview of different aspects of American cultural life such as religion, gender and sexuality, regionalism, and ethnicity and immigration. Student will develop a broader and deeper understanding of various aspects of American culture.  Assessment structure: 1) On-going assessment - Assignments: 04*15%: 60% - Group Project Essay: 01*20%: 20% 2) Final Examination (FE) 20% 3) Final Result 100% 4) Completion Criteria: On-going assessment >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10.

### Student Tasks - ECB101
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purposes

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECB101

- **CLO1**: Recognize the social constructions of American culture & identity and engage with the lenses in assignment and discusison; Practice and refine the lifelong learning behavior of collaboration by working together as a group;
- **CLO2**: Know 28 UNESCO World Heritage sites in the UK and its festival or event
- **CLO3**: Identity the social constructions of ethnicity and immigration and engage with the lenses in assignment and discusison
- **CLO4**: Illustrate the social constructions of religion and engage with the lenses in assignment and discusison
- **CLO5**: Recognize the social constructions of gender, sex and engage with the lenses in assignment and discusison
- **CLO6**: Assess the relationship between new social media and the processes of Americanisation; explore the impact of specific areas of American popular culture like music, Hollywood, television on other societie
- **CLO7**: Develop a broader and deeper understanding of various aspects of social and culture context in Britain
- **CLO8**: Recognize some social aspects of language use in Britain and identify the ‘correct’ or ‘prestige’ accent
- **CLO9**: Identify some of the main phenomenons in British publishing
- **CLO10**: Identify distinctive characteristics of the British cinema since 1945 and discuss performers and movements in British pop since the 1960s
