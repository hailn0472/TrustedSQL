### Description - ETB201c
This course provides students with the knowledge and experience necessary for success in the entertainment industry. It comprises two parts: First, "Entertainment Business Foundations," which focuses on the music industry, provides a comprehensive overview, including the industry's history and role dynamics. In the second section, you'll explore the intersection of fandom, popular culture, and digital media, learning how to analyse popular culture products, map fan networks, and understand the links between popular culture and the political and commercial factors that shape its production, distribution, and consumption.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETB201c

- **CLO1**: Gain comprehension of theoretical and practical expertise for a music industry vocation
- **CLO2**: Possess a high level of proficiency proficiency in shaping artist identity, cultivating fanbases, and mastering entrepreneurship
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate the ability to analyze links between popular culture, politics, and commercial influences.
- **CLO4**: Develop the capacity to influence fan identity online and engage in cooperative efforts to establish a community-driven platform for fandom.
- **CLO5**: Develop self-study and lifelong learning spirit and capabilities to adapt to the constant change of technology and society.
