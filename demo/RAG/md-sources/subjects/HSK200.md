### Description - HSK200
Main objective: This course aims to equip students with the knowledge of resources, administer assets and several key procedures of housekeeping cleanliness process. -Description: Housekeeping management examines the role of supervisory function in housekeeping management such as inventory & equipment management, characteristics of materials, linen & laundry management, cleaning functions, the technical details of cleaning a room and control of supplies and equipment. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, project based learning, group work

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HSK200

- **CLO1**: To describe the role of the housekeeping department in hotel operations, and explain the importance of effective communication between housekeeping department and other departments
- **CLO2**: To understand the responsibility of executive housekeeping and how to manage efficiently deparmental human resource
- **CLO3**: To list and describe criteria for selection of materials and equipment for the hotel, for example, walls, floorings, beddings, linen, carpets, etc.
- **CLO4**: To operate the key procedure of cleaning and inspecting guest rooms, public areas and others, as well as able to demonstrate a working knowledge of the procedures.
- **CLO5**: To explain usage of various tools used to plan and organize the housekeeping department – staffing guide, area inventory lists, frequency schedules, performance standards and productivity standards.
- **CLO6**: To utilize various techniques to manage inventories effectively
- **CLO7**: To utilize various methods to control expenses in the housekeeping department
- **CLO8**: To operate the method of protecting property from damage and risk of insect
- **CLO9**: To demonstrate highly cooperation and discipline the team, instructor and school regulation
- **CLO10**: To communicate english fluently and professtionally in working place
