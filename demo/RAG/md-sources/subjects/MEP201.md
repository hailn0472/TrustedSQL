### Description - MEP201
This course will describe five primary stages in multimedia project, determine the scope of a multimedia project. They may include phrases, tasks, cost, timeline, elements of a multimedia project proposal. In addition, this course gives students opportunities to practice in a real-life project. Lecturer will use several teaching methods such as conducting group discussion and sharing field work experiences in the market on top of regular teaching. Students will have the chance to present their work as well as their thinking process related to this firm..

### Student Tasks - MEP201
- Attend more than 80% of hours class in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information, course material, online supports from teachers and other students and for pratices and assessments.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MEP201

- **CLO1**: Describe the five primary stages in a multimedia project
- **CLO2**: Examine the intangible elements needed to make good multimedia production
- **CLO3**: Recognise the typical members of a multimedia project team and describe the skills that they need for their work
- **CLO4**: Understand common hardware and software tools most often used in making multimedia and choose (an) appropriate platform(s) for a project
- **CLO5**: Determine the scope of a multimedia project
- **CLO6**: Learn to schedule phases, tasks, and work items required to complete a project
- **CLO7**: Analyse the cost, timeline, and tasks required to complete a project
- **CLO8**: Recognise creative strategy, target audience, budget
