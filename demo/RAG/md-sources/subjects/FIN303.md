### Description - FIN303
The course provides students with continuous corporate finance theories relating to a firm's financial decision, working capital management, how to manage its financial performance more effectively to survive competition and takeover in a practical environment. Upon successful completion, students will have the knowledge and skills to: * Discuss theories and practices of capital structure selection, and payout policy; * Discuss practices to optimize the use of net working capital; * Design a long-term financial plan for an organization; * Critically evaluate finance theories, concepts, assumptions, limitations and arguments. Topics include: Capital Budgeting, Capital Rationing and The Cost of Capital, Working Capital Management, Capital Raising for a Firm, Financial Planning and Forecasting, Capital Structure and Dividend Policy, Corporate Risk Management.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN303

- **CLO1**: Evaluate investment and financing decisions
- **CLO2**: Explain the implications of theory for practical financial management
- **CLO3**: Compare various models of investment decision making under uncertainty
- **CLO4**: Distinguish various types of corporate risk and hedging management choice
- **CLO5**: Apply critical thinking, problem solving and presentation skills to individual and/or group activities dealing with advanced corporate finance and demonstrate in an individual summative assessment task the acquisition of a comprehensive understanding of the topics covered in corporate finance.
- **CLO6**: Practice ability to work collaboratively within a team to prepare to prepare a clear oral report and/or academic written report
