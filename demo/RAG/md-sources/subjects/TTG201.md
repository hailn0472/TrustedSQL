- **Credits**: 3

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5

### Description - TTG201
-Objective: • Understand the concepts of international travel and related topics; • Be familiar with world’s major tourism destinations; • Perceive and classify tourism resources and its meanings; • Introduce famous destinations in Vietnam; • Build specific routes and itineraries of travel destinations.  -Description: This course introduces the overview about international tourism major destinations as well as Vietnamese ones. It provides understandings and knowledge about the general organization of tourism geography. Furthermore, it researches about tourism resources, its potentials and its relationships based on the division of locations and typography. Therefore, it helps the students to have orientations and plans in using and exploiting tourism resources properly. This subject also improves the student’s awareness of protecting and responding to tourism environment and provides them with interdisciplinary knowledge and skills essential to further study in specialization.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTG201

- **CLO1**: Describe the definition, concepts and themes of tourism geography
- **CLO2**: Classify and identify the locations and general characteristics/features of different regions of the world and Vietnam.
- **CLO3**: Summarize and interpret the multi tourism resources and main tourism products offered of different regions and destinations.
- **CLO4**: Remember and describe main attractions of all kinds of different countries or provinces
- **CLO5**: Create and explain appropriate tourism routes of regions and countries.
- **CLO6**: Evaluate the tourism development potentials of each region and country.
- **CLO7**: Know how to read, research and collect information from textbooks and other sources
- **CLO8**: Show a professional attitude in studying the subjects and applying it in reality
- **CLO9**: Have ability to work in a team and individually, to control time and issues
