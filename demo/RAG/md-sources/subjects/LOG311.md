# LOG311 - Customs Operations - Nghiệp vụ hải quan


## Course Information - LOG311

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: SCM201 & IEI301/IIP301
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LOG311
This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the entire customs process and practice in international trade. It is suitable for anyone who needs to know more about the processes involved in getting your goods through customs smoothly and efficiently. AI Tools will be used to support students in (1) generating ideas/structures for their speaking notes and slide structure of important topics of the course. (2) editing the slide set after students have already finished it.

### Student Tasks - LOG311
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - LOG311
- Laptop computer - Internet - Class room for lecture sessions

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LOG311

- **CLO1**: To explain the concept and practice of customs procedures
- **CLO2**: To get insight into globlization, import & export, Incoterm 2010, 2020
- **CLO3**: To describe customs examination.
- **CLO4**: To understand customs surveillance and control
- **CLO5**: To summerize and interpret State management on customs
- **CLO6**: To identify risk management on customs operations.
- **CLO7**: To evaluate the development of customs internationally and in Vietnam
- **CLO8**: To apply the understanding of customs procedures into customs declaration and taxation
- **CLO9**: To practice the professional attitude and skills of achieving understanding of customs operation in international business
