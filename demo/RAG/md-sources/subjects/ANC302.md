### Description - ANC302
This course focuses on the complete process of character design for animated films, from creating rough representations based on research to executing full designs with color, turnaround sheets, facial expressions and action poses. Students will analyze characters, evaluate designs for improvement, and apply their knowledge to create character sets for 2D animations, while developing teamwork, self-study, and professional skills. The final outcome of the course is a product in digital format.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANC302

- **CLO1**: Understand and memorize the basic knowledge of animation character design, Key design principles, and the character construction process.
- **CLO2**: Analyze characters based on plot, script, and character descriptions.
- **CLO3**: Create rough representations based on character research.
- **CLO4**: Carry out a complete character design, including cleanup, color, turnaround sheet, facial expressions, and action poses.
- **CLO5**: Check and evaluate character designs, identifying areas for improvement.
- **CLO6**: Create a character set for a 2D animated movie by applying learned knowledge to design.
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate teamwork skills, develop self-study abilities, and apply professional attitudes in a working environment.
