### Description - BKG303
This course introduces students to the principles and techniques of investment project appraisal in both private and public sectors. It covers the critical aspects of evaluating investment opportunities, including financial modeling, risk assessment, cost-benefit analysis, and the impact of financing and taxation on investment decisions. The course aims to equip students with the analytical tools necessary to assess the viability of investment projects and make informed financial decisions.  Learning Objectives Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to: (i) Understand and apply the fundamental principles of investment appraisal. (ii) Conduct financial analyses using discounted cash flow (DCF) techniques. (iii) Evaluate the impact of risk and uncertainty on investment decisions. (iv) Compare and contrast different investment appraisal methods. (v)Prepare comprehensive investment appraisal reports.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - BKG303

- **CLO1**: Explain the fundamental concepts and principles underlying the appraisal of investment projects, including the time value of money, cash flow estimation, and the cost of capital.
- **CLO2**: Apply discounted cash flow (DCF) techniques such as Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Adjusted Net Present Value (ANPV) to analyze and evaluate investment decisions.
- **CLO3**: Identify and evaluate the sources of risk and uncertainty in investment projects, employing methods such as sensitivity analysis, scenario analysis, and Monte Carlo simulation to assess their impact on project viability.
- **CLO4**: Compare and contrast various investment appraisal methods, including payback period, discounted payback period, and Accounting Rate of Return (ARR), and understand their limitations and applications.
- **CLO5**: Analyze the effects of financing and taxation on investment decisions, understanding the implications of different financing structures and tax regimes on the appraisal of investment projects.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate the ability to apply investment appraisal techniques to real-world scenarios, using case studies and project work to evaluate actual investment projects and make informed recommendations.
- **CLO7**: Practice ability to work as part of an effective team to prepare report and presentation
