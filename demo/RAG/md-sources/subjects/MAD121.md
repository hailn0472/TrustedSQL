# MAD121 - DISCRETE MATHEMATICS 2


## Course Information - MAD121

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions
Lectures: 15 ; Tutorials: 15
1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: MAD111 (Discrete mathematics 1)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Student Tasks - MAD121
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - use laptop in the class for learning purpose only - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information regarding assignment submission and feedback on assignments '

### Tools - MAD121
Preferable to have computerized tools (such Maxima, specific software…) to support a better understanding of graph theory.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MAD121

- **CLO1**: Describe properties of a relation and perform operations on relations
- **CLO2**: Analyze the structure of a graph and find shortest paths in a weighted graph
- **CLO3**: Analyze the structure of a tree and describe algorithms using trees
- **CLO4**: Manipulate boolean functions and build logic gates
- **CLO5**: Discuss different models for computation
