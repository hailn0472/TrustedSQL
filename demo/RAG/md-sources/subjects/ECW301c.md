### Description - ECW301c
This course will instruct students how to write well-organized, clear business documents; to design elegant presentation slides, reports, and posters; and to present and speak with confidence and power. In the final Capstone Project, students are required to develop a portfolio of work—including a memo, a slide deck, and a presentation—to showcase their communication skills and represent their personal brand.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECW301c

- **CLO1**: Posses a better understanding of using effective communication to deliver ideas powerfully in the workplace.
- **CLO2**: Be able to apply the top ten principles of good business writing to your work, how to deploy simple tools to dramatically improve your writing, and how to execute organization, structure, and revision to communicate more masterfully
- **CLO3**: Be able to create professional looking PowerPoints, reports, resumes, and presentations.
- **CLO4**: Be able to fear, develop a creative formula, use verbal and body language, and anticipate the room—so that you can discover your personal power as a speaker and give excellent presentations.
- **CLO5**: Be able to develop a portfolio of work including a memo, slide deck, and a presentation to showcase your communication skill and represent your personal brand.
