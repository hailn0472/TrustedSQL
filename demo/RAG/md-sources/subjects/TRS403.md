# TRS403 - English 4 (Speak Your Mind level 3)_Tiếng Anh 4


## Course Information - TRS403

- **Credits**: 0
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 70 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: passed LUK3 or ENT203
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TRS403
Objective: 'This project-based course equips students with language and soft skills including team-work and problem solving skills to be able to use English quite independently in common daily interactions and some limited academic situations Teaching method: lecture + acitivities + project works

### Student Tasks - TRS403
<- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible for doing all exercises, assignments, and labs given by the instructor in class or at home and submitting them on time - Use a laptop in class only for learning purposes - Access the course website for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online support from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment.

1. textbook + app for extra practice  2. An English community room with glass walls: 2.1. ## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TRS403

- **CLO1**: Get the key message and some important details of speakers’ utterances in popular accents that contain some unfamiliar vocabulary without much difficulty.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate understanding of the essential points and ability to respond when engaged in controlled conversations on topics such as narration of factual materials, description, or topics that involve familiar fields of knowledge with some lack of fluency.
- **CLO3**: Initiate, maintain and end face-to-face or telephone conversations on familiar topics containing some complex grammatical structures such as perfect tenses, passive voice, or some adverbial clauses
- **CLO4**: Identify main idea and key points to unfamiliar reading passages at the intermediate level
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate some basic critical thinking over others' opinions in written documents or speeches
- **CLO6**: Write simple, compound and some complex sentences with minor errors that do not affect understanding
- **CLO7**: Write a letter in the education domain and a narrative paragraph of approximately 100 words within 45 minutes
- **CLO8**: Actively and effectively apply group work and problem-solving skills in fulfilling projects

## Assessment - TRS403

### Presentations
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO5
- Question Type: 2 problem-solving performances in 2 units (talk show, presentation, role-play, V-log, mini-debate)Problems are in Language and Life of each unit in the text book.
- Note: Group work (maximum 5 members a group)

### Progress tests
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7
- Question Type: Listening: multiple choice, short answersReading: multiple choice, short answers, true/false/not given, matchingWriting: PT1 an email (~100 words); PT2 a narrative paragraph (~150 words)
- Note: Listening: 30% of 1 PT
Reading: 30% of 1 PT
Writing: 40% of 1 PT

### Project
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO5, LO8
- Question Type: Project performance/ demonstration. Project artifacts can be one or some of the following:+ A debate+ A campaign poster design and presentation+ A talk show+ A drama performance+ A TED-talk-style speech
- Note: Group work (maximum 5 members a group)

### Final
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7
- Question Type: Reading: multiple choice, short answers, true/false/not given, matchingWriting: an email (~100 words) and a narrative paragraph (~150 words)
- Note: Reading: 50%
Writing: 50%
