# ENS311 - SKILLFUL 3 - LISTENING & SPEAKING 1


## Course Information - ENS311

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Upper-intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENS211 & ENS221
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENS311
A- Listening: Upon completion of the course, students should be able to - Listen to talk shows or radio shows to identify main ideas (U1) - Listen for rhetorical questions via an invention description (U2) - Listen to radio news programs or reports to look for bias (U2), reasons and explanations (U3), summaries (U3) , or listen to understand a sequence of events (U4) - Listen to lectures for similarities and differences (U5), for supporting statements (U5), or listen to know phrases used to introduce examples (U1) - Listen to a presentation for organization cues to distinguish facts and opinions (U4)  B- Speaking: Upon completion of the course, students should be able to - Use appropriate language to let other people know that they are paying attention and that they are interested in what people are saying during a conversation or informal discussion (U1) - Give examples to express ideas clearly and convincingly (U2) - Give reasons to contribute to an academic discussion (U3) - Change and return to topics in a conversation (U4) - Interrupt politely during a lecture or a classroom discussion to understand it clearly (U5) - Pronounce correctly thought groups (know how to pause correctly) (U1) and weak forms of ‘that’ and ‘as’ (U2) - Use appropriate language express disagreement politely (U3) - Know and use intonation patterns in questions and to express feelings and attitudes (U4) - Stress correctly to express the right meanings and to emphasize important information (U5) - Introduce themselves (U1) - Discuss the result of an experiment, research findings and applications, and water issues (U3) - Present the pros and cons of an invention; about a city, a plan for a public space, future problems and personal and community values. (U2, U5) - debate an argument.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENS311

- **CLO1**: Use appropriate language to let other people know that they are paying attention and that they are interested in what people are saying during a conversation or informal discussion.
- **CLO2**: Give examples to express ideas clearly and convincingly and reasons to contribute to an academic discussion.
- **CLO3**: Change and return to topics in a conversation and interrupt politely during a lecture or a classroom discussion to understand it clearly.
- **CLO4**: Use appropriate language express disagreement politely and know and use intonation patterns in questions and to express feelings and attitudes.
- **CLO5**: Listen to talk shows or radio shows to identify main ideas.
- **CLO6**: Listen for rhetorical questions via an invention description, and listen to radio news programs or reports to look for bias, reasons and explanations, summaries, or listen to understand a sequence of events.
- **CLO7**: Listen to lectures for similarities and differences, for supporting statements, or listen to know phrases used to introduce examples.
- **CLO8**: Listen to a presentation for organization cues to distinguish facts and opinions.
