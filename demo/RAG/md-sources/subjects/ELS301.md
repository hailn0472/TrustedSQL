# ELS301 - Listening Skill


## Course Information - ELS301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENS221
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ELS301
Listening skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Identify listening strategies - Listen for main ideas - Listen for gist - Listen for reasons - Understand personal qualities - Understand jobs responsibilities - Understand complaints - Distinguish between facts and opinions - Understand vague descriptions - Distinguish between varieties of English - Follow the academic lectures - Recognise approval and disapproval - Understand ad messages - Understand business data - Understand sequence of events - Understand directions and telephone conversation - Identify purpose, attitute and solutions - Understand interviews and talk shows - Identify arguments for and against an issue - Listen critically to alternative viewpoints - Understand a scientific explanation - Identify a topic of monolog - Focus on pronuciation in each unit

### Student Tasks - ELS301
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Access the course websites for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practice and assessment.

### Tools - ELS301
Internet access

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELS301

- **CLO1**: employ general grammar (of intermediate level) and business vocabulary to describe people, things and activities, to express opinions, ideas and feelings in various business-like contexts
- **CLO2**: understand and interpret information presented in verbal, numerical or graphical form;
- **CLO3**: listen to and understand others' talks so as to have appropriate reponses that help maintain and develop conversations and other oral exchanges for 10 - 15 minutes.
- **CLO4**: organize and present ideas and statements in a clear, logical and appropriate form of presentation within 10 minutes
- **CLO5**: read and understand various business-related texts including articles and reports of upto 1,000 words as well as, commercial articles
- **CLO6**: write e-mails, letters, memo, brief reports (upto 1,000 words) and other creative commercials
