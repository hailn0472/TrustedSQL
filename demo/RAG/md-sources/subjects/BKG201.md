# BKG201 - PRINCIPLES OF BANKING


## Course Information - BKG201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: FIN201 (Monetary Economics and Global Economy)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - BKG201
The course is intended for those undergraduate students in the Finance and Banking major. This course is designed to further develop the student’s understanding of the banking environment. The course introduces basic knowledge of commercial banks and their services and financial statements. It also helps the student obtain a thorough understanding of deposit base, lending, and the policies and procedures of each of these areas. Emphasis is also placed upon the areas of new legislation, regulation, financial market, deposits, and lending.
