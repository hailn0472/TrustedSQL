# MKT328 - Digital Marketing 2


## Course Information - MKT328

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: MKT318
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - MKT328
This Course is based on the Coursera Specialization "Digital Marketing", which covers both the dynamics and the global aspects of digital marketing. You'll learn about the impacts of digital technologies on marketing communication strategies, as well as the core processes of planning a digital marketing campaign and how Digital Revolution impacted Marketing in an Analog World. At the end of the course, you'll be able to select the right marketing channels for the product and know how to plan a digital campaign , as well as apply critical thinking skills for successful marketing of Analog products and services in a Digital age. This is a blended-learning course, in which students will take a look at the videos & slides and reading on Coursera first at home, then rewatch & discuss key questions with classmates and teachers at classes.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT328

- **CLO1**: develop an overview of the digital marketing communication landscape
- **CLO2**: understand the roles & functions of traditional & digital media in marketing communication
- **CLO3**: align the marketing goals with appropriate digital marketing strategies and media channels
- **CLO4**: develop a digital marketing communication plan
- **CLO5**: explain how to utilize SEO & CRM to make your website more attractive to search engines
- **CLO6**: plan and execute a successful influencer marketing campaign
- **CLO7**: Evaluate the effectiveness of a digital campaign, as well as the challenges & pitfalls
- **CLO8**: Understand the differences between Analog and Digital and the implications of these differences for marketing.
- **CLO9**: Understand the four ways in which the Analog World is being affected by the Digital Revolution.
- **CLO10**: Apply critical thinking skills regarding the changing nature and role of the Analog World upon both firms and consumers.
