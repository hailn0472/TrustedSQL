### Description - ITA203c
This intensive and hands-on series of courses gives you the skills to utilize conceptual frameworks to align IT investments with business strategy, assess the fit between business requirements and enterprise systems features, specify business requirements as information system specifications and evaluate technological alternatives for developing the IT infrastructure for the organization.

### Student Tasks - ITA203c
