### Description - ELP321c
Demonstrate basic foundational principles, recognize second or foreign language learning theories, and design and plan lessons to teach English as a second or foreign language effectively.  This specialization is the first of two specializations that make up the TESOL Certificate from Arizona State University. In this specialization, students will learn about the basic foundational principles of language acquisition and the basic techniques founded upon those principles. It will describe second or foreign language theories, and basic studies in second language acquisition and their pedagogical implications. It will elaborate upon basic design for lesson plans and provide access to professional resources. In the final Capstone Project, in collaboration with Arizona State University’s Global Launch, students will apply the skills they learned by designing a lesson plan which includes learning outcomes, assessments, materials, classroom management techniques and the learning activities themselves. When completing this specialization, students are eligible to continute the second specialization in order to complete the TESOL certificate experience.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELP321c

- **CLO1**: Develop knowledge and skills in teaching reading and writing, and how to include grammar instruction within teaching-learning activities.
- **CLO2**: Develop knowledge and skills in teaching and learning listening and speaking, and how to include pronunciation in listening and speaking classes and offers strategies to incorporate pronunciation activities.
- **CLO3**: Effectively integrate technology into teaching, without letting it overshadow language learning; Discover current and future trends in educational technology, along with strategies for implementing these trends in the classroom and beyond.
- **CLO4**: Apply the learned skills you in teaching.
