## Course Information - EVN204

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: EVN201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EVN204
-Main objective: The main aim of the coures is to train students in analyzing, marketing, planning and in successfully running a event.Therefore,The course provides an opportunity for students to involve with producing events followed the school academic structure. Students will learn the skills necessary to write a project proposal, realistic timelines, budgets and financial records, and create professional documentation; to practice interacting with the necessary support staff needed to produce events. -Description: In the process, Students will be divided into 2 or 3 teams to run an event by their own concept. Students will meet with the instructor group by group and one by one throughout the course discussing production issues to ensure event completed step by step. -Teaching Method: Lecture, Project based learning, Cooperative Learning

### Student Tasks - EVN204
Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Comply with the time and regulation - Respect and protect school asset. - Strongly communication and teamworking.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EVN204

- **CLO1**: Identify the event organization process; organizational structure, functions and tasks of the executive and event management department
- **CLO2**: Explain the steps in the process of operating and managing events;
- **CLO3**: Explain the process and principles of management of incidents and risk situations in the operation and management of events;
- **CLO4**: Plan, manage and organize meetings; Manage and use human resources to organize and organize events.
- **CLO5**: Supervise and manage activities at the event, personnel, budget, sponsors, partners;
- **CLO6**: Have a sense of discipline, professional manners and a high sense of responsibility at work; Flexibility in handling situations, ability to work independently and in groups.

## Assessment - EVN204

### Event implementation
- Weight: 35.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6
- Note: (Team based)
The grading of the Group Proposal and Event Implementation is a measure of the quality of the Proposal and the quality of the event (due to the team's efforts) that has not been focused on and scored on each student yet.

### Final assignment (Individual assessment)
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6
- Question Type: Verbal Question
- Note: (Individual-based)
Teachers offer grades to students depending on their performance in their assigned task and their overall contribution to the event's success via 5-10 questions

### Final assignment (Peer assessment )
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6
- Question Type: Ratio scale question
- Note: (Individual based)
Students assign grades to their classmates depending on the teacher's grading standards. Students are graded based on both their collaboration with team members and individual performance entire the event.

### Group Proposal
- Weight: 25.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2, CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6
- Question Type: Presentation and written report
- Note: Students will be divided into groups of 10 to15 people to create their own event and concept. When presenting the project, the team needs to ensure all ideas are clear in all aspects from marketing, logistics, decoration, entertainment ...etc... The proposal must be presented in a detailed with demonstration and submisstion of specific products such as videos, social networking sites, posters. All processes must be ready to execute the event in phase 2.
