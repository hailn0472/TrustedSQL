# PRN292 - C# and .NET


## Course Information - PRN292

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Sessions: 33
Lectures: 14
Lab/Tutorials: 19
Self-study: 120 hours
- **Pre-Requisite**: PRO192 & DBI201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - PRN292
This course helps students learn and practice 1. C# language, 2. .NET platform, including.NET framework, data access, ADO.NET, WinForms, WebForms, and related issues.

### Student Tasks - PRN292
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information
