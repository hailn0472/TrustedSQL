### Description - RMD301
This course, "Research for Designers," dives into the world of strategic design research. Students will explore qualitative and quantitative research techniques, the importance of data-driven design, and ethical considerations within the research process. This course teaches how to integrate these insights into design thinking, producing evidence-based solutions that resonate with the target audience. By the end of the course, students will gain essential skills in critical analysis, research methodology, empathy building, evidence-based decision-making, and clear communication of findings. This course will transform students into more strategic, insightful, and user-focused designers, ready to tackle the complexities of real-world design challenges. The course can also help students apply the knowledge they have gained in delivering scientific reports and build and develop report presentation skills at seminars and conferences.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RMD301

- **CLO1**: Explain the importance of conducting human-centric research before making design decision, as well as basic definition of design research and design thinking
- **CLO2**: Apply essentials of design research to conduct a research proposal
- **CLO3**: Analyse different research methods and the level of appropriation of each method
- **CLO4**: Create a research plan logically and execute properly
- **CLO5**: Deliver the research report in a logical and easy-to-understand manner to other stakeholders
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate professionalism in conducting research and show respect for intellectual properties
