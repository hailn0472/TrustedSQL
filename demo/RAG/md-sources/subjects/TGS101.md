- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TGS101
-Description: The course aims to provide knowledge on tour guide profession and equip students with required professional skills of international tour guide (inbound and outbound tour guides). The course helps to enhance the learners' understanding in how to work with tourists, drivers, hotels, restaurants, travel agents and airlines, to present effectively, to handle situations such as emergencies on the tour and to contact the local embassy when necessary. The course guides students how to prepare presentations on various themes, select the professional guiding language. Through the course students understand the process of C.I.Q procedures (Customs, Inspection & Quarantine), the importance of cheerleading skills when travelling on vehicles and team-building skills. The course makes use of some daily field trips and field work to build up knowledge, background and simple skills of a tour guide. -Assessment scheme 1) On-going assessment: -Class participation/preparation: 10% -Individual practice (10): 80% 2)Final exam: 10% Total 100% 3) Completion Criteria: Every on-going assessment component > 0; Final Result >=5 & Final Exam Score >= 4

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TGS101

- **CLO1**: Have good overview of tourism and hospitality; have communication skill and tour marketing, geographic tourism, Vietnamese culture and history, tourists’ psychology
- **CLO2**: Communicate fluently with guests in English
- **CLO3**: Have good knowledge of tour guiding activities, travel agencies, procedures of tour services.
- **CLO4**: Understand activities and tasks of a tour guide
- **CLO5**: Understand customers’ needs, tour resources and related services such as hotels, restaurants, transportation and additional services; knowing how to allocate tour destination upon weather or customers’ finance and health.
- **CLO6**: Understand interrelations among service providers, procedures of tour allocation, pricing methods
- **CLO7**: Organize and solve tour-related situations in tour guiding
- **CLO8**: Understand the significance of communications, marketing & promotions, analysing information to support sales activities
- **CLO9**: Know how to design a tour programs
- **CLO10**: Host games, activities and holding events
