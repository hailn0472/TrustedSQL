# ENH301 - Business English for Hospitality - Intermediate_ Tiếng Anh Chuyên ngành Khách sạn - Du lịch Trung cấp 1


## Course Information - ENH301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1.6 hours final exam + 103.4 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENH301
1. Objectives: • build confidence in professional skills such as dealing with enquiries, marketing destinations, offering advice, negotiating, writing emails and speaking to groups • develop language awareness through an integrated grammar and skills syllabus • acquire the specialized vocabulary needed by tourism professionals • practise language skills in realistic Case studies that reflect issues in the tourist industry today. 2. Description: English for International Tourism is a three-level series designed to meet the English language needs of professionals working in the tourism industry and students of tourism in further education. 3. Teaching method: lecture and seminar format 4. Assessment scheme: On-going assesment: 50% Final examination: 50 % Completion Criteria: Every on-going assessment component >0, Each part of the final exam must be >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10.

### Student Tasks - ENH301
- Student must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the CMS at https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENH301

- **CLO1**: Use present and past tenses Use vocabulary relating to money matters, describing locations Apply professional skills in marketing Design a tour
- **CLO2**: Use modal verbs Describe types of accommodation, facilities and services Apply professional skills when dealing with complaints Investigate customer complaints
- **CLO3**: Use articles in spoken and written language Describe weather, climate, features and attractions Apply professional skills in offering advice Develop a destination
- **CLO4**: Use conditional structures with if Descibe geographical features, attractions, activities, works of art Apply professional skills when speaking to a group Plan a coach tour
- **CLO5**: Use verb + infinitive or -ing form Use vocabulary to describe sectors in niche tourism Apply professional skills when dealing with figures Improve client security
- **CLO6**: Use the passive Use vocabulary to describe culture, use linking words Apply professional skills when taking part in meetings Present a proposal
- **CLO7**: Use have/get something done Use adjectives ending in -ed/-ing Apply professional skills when making presentations
- **CLO8**: Use question forms Use vocabulary to dedscribe customer service terms, personal qualities, multi-word verbs Apply professional skills when handling telephone conversations Improve customer service
- **CLO9**: Use past perfect Use idioms and metaphors Apply professional skills when socializing and making small talk
