### Description - IEI301
This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the entire import/export process and practice in international trade. The topics mainly include significant elements of conducting an export/import business : Distribution channels, pricing the international trade, international sales contracts, trade document and transportation, method of payment, export regulation and tax incentives, import procedure and technique, problem and solution for international logistic, risk and insurance. The course will incorporate AI tools for class discussions/critical thinking in activities on the Edunext platform; as well as utilize AI to solve assignments and establish group work ideas for the Group Assignment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IEI301

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate the importance of international trade to the Global Economy and the impact of international and regional agreement on trade
- **CLO2**: Distinguish the term of sales (Incoterm)
- **CLO3**: Explain and analyze the export contract and major clauses in export contracts
- **CLO4**: Classify the factors behind the move toward harmonization of International contract/Commercial Law
- **CLO5**: Demonstate the documents frequently used in export- import transactions
- **CLO6**: Explain the role and function of freight forwarders and distinguish among air freight, ocean freight and land transport.
- **CLO7**: Interprete and discuss different methods of payment in international trade (consigment sales, open-account sales, documentary draft, L/C, cash in advance)
- **CLO8**: Explain the origins, benefits, theories and forms of countertrade
- **CLO9**: Define and understand regulations and policies affecting exports
- **CLO10**: Analyze the methods of levying the tariffs and import restrictions via non-tariff barriers
- **CLO11**: Explain the role of WTO, FTA, APEC in trade and functions of trade intermediaries and serivces such as customs brokers, free-trade zones and bonded warehoses.
- **CLO12**: Identify the determinants of trade, trade voume and direction
- **CLO13**: Examine the criterias to find, assess and select import products and suppliers
- **CLO14**: Demonstate the import and export process, rules of origin and other marking offers and requirements
- **CLO15**: Demonstrate ethical awareness in business context
- **CLO16**: Demonstrate how to assess and select the trading products based on systematic approach and reactive approach
- **CLO17**: Clarify the necesity of conducting international market research in term of making business decision and steps in international market assessment
- **CLO18**: Identify and explain the export channels of distribution as well as determinants of channel selection to market products abroad
- **CLO19**: Analyze major clauses in represenation agreement
- **CLO20**: Demonstrate the concept and steps of international logistic process and identify the external factors infulence on international logistic decisions
- **CLO21**: Identify the potential risks in foreign trade and understand the principles of insurance, terms of policy, claims and procedures
- **CLO22**: Clarify the export pricing objective and identify the derminants of export prices
