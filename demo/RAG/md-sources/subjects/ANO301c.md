### Description - ANO301c
This Specialization lays a primary foundation for experimentation and exploration of video game design, story, character development, and winning gameplay before programming begins. These four courses emphasize the self-reliance and personal expression of the gaming artist, and encourage you to take conceptual risks and develop new modes of expression and form through gaming. In the final Capstone Project, you’ll put your creative skills to work by generating an engaging game design document for a personal game project, outlining the conceptual, narrative and aesthetic elements of your game.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANO301c

- **CLO1**: Understand primary concepts of gaming, and an exploration of how these basic concepts affect the way gamers interact with our games.
- **CLO2**: Understand what defines a “game” and the mechanics and rules behind different types of games.
- **CLO3**: Examine how storytelling acts as a vital mechanism for driving video gameplay forward.
- **CLO4**: Understand game story flows and the strategic elements of gameplay.
- **CLO5**: Investigate key components of environment and level design.
- **CLO6**: Create successful character designs that can be applied to video games
- **CLO7**: Gameplay & game demo
