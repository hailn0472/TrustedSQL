# SSG103 - Communication and In-group working skills


## Course Information - SSG103

- **Credits**: 3
- **Time Allocation**: 30 sessions, 1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - SSG103
This course will cover both working in groups and communication skills.  Assessment structure: * On-going Assessment: - Activity: 10% - Quiz: 15% - Group Assignment : 15% - Group Project : 30% * Final Exam: 30% * Completion Criteria: Every on-going assessment component > 0, Final Exam >=4, Final Result >=5

### Student Tasks - SSG103
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - SSG103
- Internet - PDF reader

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSG103

- **CLO1**: Establish relationships between class members and group organization
- **CLO2**: Understand leadership theories and model of leadership effectiveness.
- **CLO3**: Describe and identify importance of verbal and nonverbal in teamwork and how to use them effectiveness.
- **CLO4**: Understand how conflicts appear in groups and how to manage them in cohesion.
- **CLO5**: Develop a professtional meeting and practice dealing the behavior problems of members in the meeting time.
- **CLO6**: Apply of parliamentary principles and summary the basic procedures when organizing the meeting.
- **CLO7**: Identify words and develop clear, concise,logical, coherent,and effective paragraphs.
- **CLO8**: Format business letters using the full block,modified block,and simplified styles.
- **CLO9**: Format memos and e-mail properly.
- **CLO10**: Prepare competently a variety of positive and neutral messages using the direct plan.
- **CLO11**: Prepare effective negative messages for a variety of purposes using the indirect plan
- **CLO12**: Form a group and conduct group work
- **CLO13**: Write messages that are used for the various stages of collection
- **CLO14**: Write formal and informal reports
- **CLO15**: Prepare targeted and general résumés in chronological, functional,or combination format.
- **CLO16**: Prepare for a successful job interview
- **CLO17**: Review the knowledge learned and practice overview
- **CLO18**: Define common teamwork skills such as group communication, membership, leadership, planning and conducting meeting, verbal and nonverbal communication, conflict and cohesion,
- **CLO19**: Identify key elements, advantages, disavantages and the nature of group communication.
- **CLO20**: Describe stages of group development and types of group norms.
- **CLO21**: Explain why member join, stay or leave groups through theory of needs.
- **CLO22**: Determine group roles and how each role helps hinders groups from achieving a common goal.
- **CLO23**: Describe and apply strategies for reducing communication apprehensions.
- **CLO24**: Analyse ways to become effective and powerful leaders.
