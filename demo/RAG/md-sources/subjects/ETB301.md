### Description - ETB301
This course covers general guidelines to creating effective data visualizations, practices to consider best category of graph that meet audience needs. Guide to explore data and how it might be visualized. Show the most common and exible graphs, building a data visualization style guide and how to pull together in a series of visual model.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETB301

- **CLO1**: understand and distinguish the different types and form of data visuliasation andd information design, the process of implementing an data visualisation project, the factors to have a good data visualisation project.
- **CLO2**: Understand the concept of The brief and the types of information that may be included. Describe and analyze the user.
- **CLO3**: Describe and analyze The Data
- **CLO4**: Understand and using different chart types in data visualisation
- **CLO5**: Understand and implement the storytelling in data visualisation
- **CLO6**: Apply graphic elements & interactive techniques in data visualisation
- **CLO7**: Responsibility to use skills, positive attitude and a professional working style
