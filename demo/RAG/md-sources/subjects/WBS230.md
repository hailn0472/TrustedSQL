# WBS230 - Wines, Beers, Spirits 2 (Pratice)_Nghiệp vụ pha chế đồ uống 2 (Thực hành)


## Course Information - WBS230

- **Credits**: 1
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (50h) = 22,5h (30 sessions) contact hours + 27,5h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - WBS230
-Objective: To apply the theoretical knowledge to F&B practical activities -Description: This course is the continued part of RES213. It focuses on how to mix and serve wines and drinks. -Teaching method: demonstration, role play, group work

### Student Tasks - WBS230
Attend 100% of class hours in order to obtain completion criteria - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - WBS230
- Internet access - Facility and equipment requirement : Depending on the training condition of each campus, the course will be implemented in school lab designed up to 4-5* hotel standard, real hotel or other places, which fully met with requirements.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - WBS230

- **CLO1**: Explain the Stirring, Shaking, Blending, and Building methods of drink mixing.
- **CLO2**: Develop a sense of good discipline, a sense of responsibility, and a sense of serious work in the assigned work as well as with colleagues.
- **CLO3**: Develop ability to work quietly; with cooperation; patience, carefulness, cleanliness and aesthetic values.

## Assessment - WBS230

### Group practice
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2 , CLO3
- Question Type: Four or five students per group. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through: Demonstration of skills on mixing different (in quantity) international alcoholic beverages in a prescribed timeframe; utilizing systematic liquor pouring and proper bar measurement; mixing of alcoholic concoction showmanship skill techniques.
The mark will be given based on each student's performance, not on the whole group's. It means each student will get their own mark.

### Individual practice Tests
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO1, CLO2 , CLO3
- Question Type: Each student must be assessed through practical demonstration in a simulated workplace environment where beverage ingredients and equipment are provided. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through:
- Observation shall be done on the proper classification of alcoholic beverages and glasses; proper mixing procedures on several cocktails and the uses and maintenance of different bar tools and equipment.
- Direct observation of student providing advice to customers or colleagues on cocktail and its proper serving procedures.

### Participation
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: CLO4
