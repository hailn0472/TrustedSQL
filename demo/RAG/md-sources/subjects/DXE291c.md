### Description - DXE291c
This subject is designed to provide Information Technology (IT) students at FPT University with fundamental knowledge and skills in digital transformation across the following areas: State management and public administration; Business and organizational management; Digital economy and finance; Customer experience and digital marketing. This knowledge and these skills will help students gain a broader understanding of business and management, preparing them to readily apply their specialized IT knowledge to real-world digital transformation projects within organizations and enterprises, and to become the young, pioneering workforce in Vietnam's Digital Transformation in the near future.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DXE291c

- **CLO1**: Understand and apply fundamental knowledge of public administration management in the context of digital transformation, grasp the current situation and technical infrastructure requirements, recognize the necessity of organizational and management mechanism reforms, as well as develop skills in monitoring and evaluating the effectiveness of digital transformation in public administrative services and delivering public services efficiently.
- **CLO2**: Develop strategic thinking and leadership skills in the digital environment, from building organizational culture, optimizing processes, managing human resources, to driving innovation and restructuring business models to adapt flexibly to technology and market dynamics.
- **CLO3**: Understand and analyze the concepts of the digital economy, including opportunities, challenges, digital business models, digital markets, and value chains; gain knowledge of digital finance, banking, accounting, and auditing concepts, along with the applications of fintech and AI, while exploring concepts of digital currency, digital assets, blockchain, and decentralized finance.
- **CLO4**: Understand customer behavior in the digital age, design personalized customer journeys and experiences, and apply MarTech ecosystems and data to optimize marketing activities, while becoming familiar with customer experience metrics and communication decision-making to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.
