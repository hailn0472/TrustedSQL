### Description - MKT204
This course examines the opportunities and challenges associated with global marketing. The impact on marketing of the cultural, economic, political, and technological environments in different countries will be discussed. The relationship between global marketing and global business strategy will be reviewed. Strategies and tactics for developing each of the four "P's" internationally and globally will be examined, including developing: • Product, service, and branding policies, • Advertising, promotion, and communications plans, • Channels of distributions • Pricing policies in the global context. Approaches for deciding the extent to which marketing should be globalized versus localized will be explored. This course will examine key considerations in marketing both globally and in each of the major regions of the world.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT204

- **CLO1**: Use the product/market growth matrix to explain the various ways a company can expand globally and Briefly explain how exchange rates impact a company’s opportunities in different parts of the world.
- **CLO2**: Describe the various positioning options available to global marketers and Understand the difference between export selling and export marketing.
- **CLO3**: Describe the various national policies that pertain to exports and imports and Explain the structure of the harmonized tariff system.
- **CLO4**: Describe the various organizations that support and facilitate the export process and Compare between home-country and market-country export organization considerations.
- **CLO5**: Discuss the factors that contribute to the successful launch of a global strategic partnership.
- **CLO6**: Compare and contrast local products and brands, international products and brands, and global products and brands and the five strategic alternatives that marketers can utilize during the global product planning process.
- **CLO7**: Identify the different pricing strategies and objectives that influence decisions about pricing products in global markets and some of the environmental influences that impact prices.
- **CLO8**: Identify and compare the basic structure options for consumer channels and industrial channels and Describe the different categories of retail operations that are found in various parts of the world.
- **CLO9**: Define sales promotion and identify the most important promotion tactics and tools used by global marketers and Compare between publicity and public relations and identify global companies that have recently been impacted by negative publicity.
- **CLO10**: Analyze the major innovations and trends that underline the digital revolution and Describe the different organizational structures that companies can adopt and they grow and expand globally.
- **CLO11**: Define hypercompetitive industry and list the key arenas in which dynamic strategic interactions take place.
- **CLO12**: Describe the available alternatives for conflict resolution and dispute settlement when doing business outside the home country and compare and contrast single-country marketing strategy with global marketing strategy (GMS).
- **CLO13**: Compare and contrast the four main categories of preferential trade agreements in Latin America and Asia
- **CLO14**: Define culture and identify the various expressions and manifestations of culture that can impact global marketing strategies.
- **CLO15**: Explain the concept of environmental sensitivity and give contrasting examples of product categories that are deeply embedded in culture and those that are not.
- **CLO16**: Understand the elements of a country’s political environment that can impact global marketing activities.
- **CLO17**: Define international law and describe the main types of legal systems found in different parts of the world.
- **CLO18**: Discuss the role of information technology in a global company’s decision-making processes.
- **CLO19**: Identify the variables that global marketers can use to segmentation, targeting, and positioning global markets and give an example of each.
