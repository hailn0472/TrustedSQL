# HTM101 - Heritage tourism management


## Course Information - HTM101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: HMO102
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - HTM101
The course explores and focuses around the basic theories of cultural and heritage tourism. The course is designed to provide knowledge of resources, planning and development, and impacts of cultural and heritage tourism. The course will also emphasize the role as well as the trend of cultural and heritage tourism. Learning in the class will be facilitated through the use of vehicles such as textbook readings, class discussion, exercises/activities, cases, self quizzes and lectures.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HTM101

- **CLO1**: explain the situation of complex management of heritage sites as cultural tourism increases.
- **CLO2**: aimed at professionals and students of leisure, tourism and heritage management
- **CLO3**: provides a background to cultural tourism before focusing on important issues involved with managing a heritage site - education
- **CLO4**: entertainment and preservation heritage site
- **CLO5**: considering appropriate ways of dealing with the needs of the tourist, the presenters and the cultural site
