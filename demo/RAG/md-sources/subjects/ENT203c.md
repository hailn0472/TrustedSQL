# ENT203c - English Reading - Tiếng Anh Đọc


## Course Information - ENT203c

- **Credits**: 0
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 7.5 hrs offline + Final Exam (Interview 7-10 mins/student)
- **Pre-Requisite**: Nil
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENT203c
The course is designed to enable students to: - Apply language in introducing oneself, one's job or one's preferences - Utilize language to start a conversation and communicate with someone new form other countries - Indicate one's opinion about family, friendship, hometown, or jobs  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.25 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

### Student Tasks - ENT203c
1. Join the online course (spec.) when being invited by the admin. 2. Enroll in each MOOC of spec., regularly study MOOCs at least 5hrs/week by watching videos, reading materials, taking quizzes, doing assgnments... as required 3. Respect the Coursera Code of Conduct, at https://learner.coursera.help/hc/en-us/articles/208280036-Coursera-Code-of-Conduct 3. Keep online learning progress promptly (bonus will be awarded) 4. Communicate and discuss in forum 5. Attend off-line sessions in the Campus (optional) 6. Must complete all required MOOCs with certification in order to take the Final Exam at Campus

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENT203c

- **CLO1**: Practice and apply a good knowledge of conversational English skills.
- **CLO2**: Practice and apply a good knowledge of primary English reading and writing skills in daily life.
- **CLO3**: Practice and apply a good knowledge of intermediate English reading and writing skills in daily life.
- **CLO4**: Practice and apply a good knowledge of general academic English learning skills.
