# TTG321 - Tour Guiding Profession 2_Nghiệp vụ hướng dẫn du lịch 2


## Course Information - TTG321

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 0,75h final exam + 104,25h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: TTG311
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - TTG321
-Objective: To help students to apply learned knowledge and skills of tour guide to the fieldtrip that the class and lecturer will build up together; to develop the ability to evaluate effectively a tour program. -Description: The course focuses on the implementation of the tour guide skills to a specific fieldtrip. The course aims to give to the students an opportunity to experience and realize the tour guide job as a professional domestic tour guide through a 10 - day fieldtrip in any part of Vietnam (Northern, Central, Southern, Highland...). The course helps advance the leaners’ ability to handle situations. The course determines cheerleading skills, teambuilding skills, organizing games. The course guides students to prepare presentation on various themes. -Teaching method: lecturing and practice in class and in a long fieldtrip.

### Student Tasks - TTG321
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Students must attend the whole fieldtrip in order to pass this course. - Student is responsible to do all distributed tasks, practical assignments given by instructor in class and on site, also submit on time the final report. - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - TTG321
- Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTG321

- **CLO1**: Recall relevant basic knowledge and skills related to Tour Guiding
- **CLO2**: Apply learned knowledge to build an entire fieldtrip itinerary including defining tasks for guides in each activity
- **CLO3**: Apply presentation skill, story-telling skill, group management skill, solving skill and animation skill to different activities on sites, on bus and in class.
- **CLO4**: Identify and evaluate the characteristics and needs of guests, then deploy appropriate strategies to satisfy them and/or handle arising problems effectively
- **CLO5**: Show professionalism, responsibility, ethics, compliance with the regulations of the tour guide profession
- **CLO6**: Develop the ability to evaluate a complete tour program through team work and peer review
