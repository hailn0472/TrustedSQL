# LIT301 - British and American Literature _Văn học Anh - Mỹ


## Course Information - LIT301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1 hour final exam + 104 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ECR201/ECR202, EAW221/EAW222
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LIT301
1. Objective: Showing knowledge and understanding of classic and modern English and American prose, their social and cultural contexts. Helping students develop emotional literacy and social and cultural understanding. 2. Description: This module will appeal to students with an interest in exploring the diversity and vibrancy of classic and contemporary American and English literature. Students will be studying a range of engaging English and American texts, from the major genres of social novel, romance novel, detective fiction, and thriller. 3. Teaching method: lecture and seminar format 4. Assessment scheme: On-going assesment: 70% Final examination: 30 % Completion Criteria: On-going assessment >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10

### Student Tasks - LIT301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible for doing all exercises, and assignments given by the instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

### Tools - LIT301
Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LIT301

- **CLO1**: demonstrate an understanding of the nature and development of the story genre
- **CLO2**: demonstrate a capacity for developing independent, responsive and analytical readings of stories
- **CLO3**: relate stories to their social and cultural context
- **CLO4**: analyze characters in literature
- **CLO5**: effectively communicate an understanding of English and American literature, and express their ideas effectively and with fluency
- **CLO6**: Characterize and integrate the awareness of literature and culture into practice; Develop team-working practice.
