# CTS101 - Conference and trade show management


## Course Information - CTS101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
Lectures: 15
Tutorials/Lab: 15
- **Pre-Requisite**: Recommend to have knowledge of Event Planning (EVN201) in advance
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - CTS101
-Objective: Upon the rapid growth of the meeting, expositions, events and conventions industry (MEEC), the subject is aimed to provide an introduction to the MEEC industry -Description: It gives a comprehensive overview of the burgeoning field of planning and producing MEEC — meetings, expositions, events, and conventions with the study of varied segments of the field, along with discussions of new trends. -Teaching method: lecturing in combination with case study analysis, MEEC practice -Assessment scheme: small seminar organisation, midterm test, final report

### Student Tasks - CTS101
- Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend more than 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.
