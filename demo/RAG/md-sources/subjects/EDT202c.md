### Description - EDT202c
This course is intended for researchers and business experts seeking state-of-the-art knowledge in advanced science and technology. The course covers details on Big Data (Hadoop, Spark, Storm), Smartphones, Smart Watches, Android, iOS, CPU/GPU/SoC, Mobile Communications (1G to 5G), Sensors, IoT, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LP-WAN, Cloud Computing, AR (Augmented Reality), Skype, YouTube, H.264/MPEG-4 AVC, MPEG-DASH, CDN, and Video Streaming Services. The Specialization includes projects on Big Data using IBM SPSS Statistics, AR applications, Cloud Computing using AWS (Amazon Web Service) EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud), and Smartphone applications to analyze mobile communication, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth networks.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EDT202c

- **CLO1**: Know Emerging Technologies and how companies and individuals use these technologies in advantage of their works and lives
- **CLO2**: Know how big data analysis is possible based on the world’s most popular three big data technologies Hadoop, Spark, and Storm
- **CLO3**: Practice data analysis with one of the most famous and widely used big data statistical analysis systems in the world - the IBM SPSS Statistics
- **CLO4**: Explain the core technology and components of the world’s most popular smartphones (i.e., the Samsung Galaxy Note8 and Apple iPhone X) and smart watches (i.e., Samsung Gear S3 and the Apple Watch Series 3), along with details of the iOS and Android smartphone OSs (Operating Systems) and mobile communications 1G to 5G
- **CLO5**: Know the core technologies and platforms of IoT and Clouds, including the start-of-the-art IoT and wireless networks and Cloud technologies
- **CLO6**: Analyze Bluetooth and W-Fi wireless networks and setup and use an EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud) Virtual Computer in AWS (Amazon Web Service)
- **CLO7**: Know the core technologies used in state-of-the-art AR, Skype, and YouTube video and multimedia products and services, including the advanced video and real-time multimedia delivery mechanisms based on H.264/MPEG-4 AVC, MPEG-DASH, CDN, and mobile CDN
