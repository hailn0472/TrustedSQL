### Description - CAA201
'- Objective: The course aims to provide students a solid foundation on different aspects of advertising and other major integrated marketing communications tools, and the role of these tools in the marketing process.  - Description: In particular, attention will be given to discussions on (1) understand basic marketing communication and advertising related terms (2) understanding the communication processes, (3) understand creative process and creative choice (4) conducting research materials for creative work, (5) devising effective creative strategies for achieving marketing communication objectives, and (6) implementing and evaluating the creative strategy.

### Student Tasks - CAA201
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CAA201

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic elements of Advertising, Integrated Marketing Communication and its role in the marketing process
- **CLO2**: Apply theories and practices to discuss useful criteria for evaluating IMC effectiveness
- **CLO3**: Understand strategic planning stages including marketing strategy, communication strategy, creative strategy, media strategy and their relationship.
- **CLO4**: Understand the research method, data in the marketing process
- **CLO5**: Understand advertising audiences' (consumer) behaviour and able to identify them using segmentation, targeting and profiling
- **CLO6**: Understand the creative side and promotinal writing languages
- **CLO7**: Understand different advertising mediums requirement and its essential
- **CLO8**: Demonstrate ability to apply Advertising, PR concepts to analyse and solve real-world situations.
- **CLO9**: Apply different tools for researching, planning, executing and presenting advertising campaigns
- **CLO10**: Develop basic communication and media materials such as marketing copy, press release and online content.
