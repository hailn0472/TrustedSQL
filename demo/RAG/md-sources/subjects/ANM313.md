### Description - ANM313
This course provides knowledge of working with Nurbs, polygon Modeling, character building topology, hardsurface modeling, blocking, advanced UV, working with camera completing a project.

### Student Tasks - ANM313
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance > - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/) for up-to-date information and material of the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANM313

- **CLO1**: Understand two main skills nurbs, pologon and topology
- **CLO2**: Understand the strengths of each method
- **CLO3**: Apply the theory to shape the hand and foot
- **CLO4**: Apply the theory to shape the head
- **CLO5**: Apply the theory to shape the body
- **CLO6**: Students will be able to model, UV, assign the material, the character clothing
- **CLO7**: Students can skills in analysis and problem solving; Proficient in presentation and teamwork skills.
- **CLO8**: Having a professional working style.
