### Description - ENT104
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises. Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: 1. Use a vocabulary range covered in lessons to describe (healthfulness of food/trips/travel experience/good or bad deals/ products), express disappointment, state and sympathise with the problem 2. Understand grammar rules taught in lessons and use them in both the spoken form for social communication and write well-organized forms for topics covered in the course (simple present, present continuous, simple past, modal verbs, conjunctions, comparison, and simple sentence structures) 3. Understand various types of recordings and videos and apply different listening techniques (classifying, inferring, identifying main ideas or details, summarizing) to respond correctly to the social situations covered in the course 4. Understand different reading texts by applying a variety of reading strategies (skimming and scanning, making inferences and predictions, bar graph interpretation, summarising and paraphrasing, identifying supporting details, applying information, drawing conclusions, clarifying information) 5. Use appropriate colloquial expressions, and conversational strategies covered in the course in social conversations 6. Use different learning tools (books, Internet, online platforms, audios, videos, etc.) appropriately to perform various learning tasks creatively 7. Develop effective communication and teamwork in different academic and social tasks 8. Develop ethics, self-study, professionalism, respect in different disciplines, group work, and creativity
