### Description - GDF201
Description: This course provides an introduction to visual design for students and provides them with the basic knowledge of design, visual principles, and visual form of a composition of art. Students will be able to lay out the composition of art works, analyze, and evaluate the effect of design elements in each composition on human eyes. Through this course, students will gain the knowledge and skills necessary to develop concepts, manipulate materials, and apply art elements and design principles to create effective and appropriate visual works based on the learned knowledge and skills.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GDF201

- **CLO1**: Describe basic design and visual principles.
- **CLO2**: Explain how people communicate visually and how visual principles affect human eyes.
- **CLO3**: Apply the art elements and knowledge of design principles to construct basic visual art compositions of graphic design.
- **CLO4**: Analyze constructure in an art composition.
- **CLO5**: Create the learner's own work of art based on learned knowledge and skills.
- **CLO6**: Have a positive attitude and a professional working style.
