### Description - GDP493
Create a digital product follow the design process: research, empathize with users, identify pain points, finding solutions, create wireframes and prototype, test and iterate on designs. Practice UX research: like planning research studies, conducting interviews and usability studies, and synthesizing research results Apply foundational UX concepts, like user-centered design, accessibility, and equity-focused design - Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students - The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project 1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10. 2) Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor): 5%; Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final product and Capstone Project Document (by council):20%; - Presentation (Oral Project Defense): 30% Project Defense >=5 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Product and Caspton Document must be submitted before deadline

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GDP493

- **CLO1**: +The project should have a master plan, phrase plans, and work breaking structure... The group must use a project management tool such as trello, jira for management + Meeting minutes for every meeting are required +The data and products collected during the research and work should be structure managed and easily accessible when needed. + Well-preparation before any presentation during the project working
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate professional behaviors, morality, social responsibilities and a sense of dedication to community.
- **CLO3**: +Build a clear research structure: field, user, technology… +Know how to analyze the collected data: Insight Statements, build affinity diagram... +Representing the research results into deliverables (conclusion, persona, empathy mapping, user experience mapping)
- **CLO4**: Proficiently use common ux and design tools: Figma, XD, photoshop, maze.io, Illustrator…
- **CLO5**: Understand the target platform of the project's product (Android, IOS, watch) and design in compliance with the platform's standards

- **CLO7**: +Clearly state the problems would be solved with the support of the final product '+Identify the target users +Clearly describe user requirements that align with research results and the solving problem +Divide tasks coherently, describe the flow clearly, and use the standard notation to describe the flows
- **CLO8**: Well understand the stages of the project from the beginning Understand and implement Ux testing methods: Usability test, A/B testing, Heuristic evaluation…
- **CLO9**: Plan and define backlog for the entire project, and backlog for stages. Develop a planning mindset. Clearly assign tasks for each member using a management tool Struct the team with roles and responsibility Proficiently use of project management and collaboration tools (jira, trello, miro, fix jam ...)
