### Description - VMC201c
This course explains how things catch on and helps you apply these ideas to be more effective at marketing your ideas, brands, or products. Students will learn how to make ideas stick, how to increase your influence, how to generate more word of mouth, and how to use the power of social networks to spread information and influence. The course also instructs students how to write a complete, feature-length screenplay for film or television, be it a serious drama or romantic comedy or anything in between. Students will learn to break down the creative process into components, and you’ll discover a structured process that allows you to produce a polished and pitch-ready script by the end of the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - VMC201c

- **CLO1**: Possess a better understanding of how to craft contagious content, build stickier messages, and get any product, idea, or behavior to catch on.
- **CLO2**: Possess the ability to make ideas stick, how to increase your influence, how to generate more word of mouth, and how to use the power of social networks to spread information and influence.
- **CLO3**: Be able to create Logline, Dramatic Question for a feature film, and write with three act structure, create concept for a feature film, pitch document, and build characters.
- **CLO4**: Possess a better understanding of the unique formatting in screenplay writing and you will learn to use screenplay software.
- **CLO5**: Be able to break down the creative process into components, then complete a full-length, feature film screenplay.
