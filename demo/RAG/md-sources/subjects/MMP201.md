### Description - MMP201
-Objective: Understand and can be applied the cognitive psychology theories of mass comunication. -Description: This course will focus on a range of psychological theories, processes, and principles in the context of mass communication. Students will explore the application of these theories and principles to several prominent issues will be discussed. Such issues may include politics, sex, and violence, to lesser-studied topics, such as sports, music, emotion, and technology of various media. In addition, this course examines how our experiences with media affect the way we acquire knowledge about the world, and how this knowledge influences our attitudes and behavior.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MMP201

- **CLO1**: Understand the changing media landscape
- **CLO2**: Understand and evaluate about effects of sex in the media to people.
- **CLO3**: Analyse Socially Positive Media and Understand responding to Media: Getting Our Two Cents In
- **CLO4**: Explain the types of research methodologies that have been used to study media and Identify mass communication from a psychological perspective
- **CLO5**: Explain and apply psychological theories used in mass communication's research
- **CLO6**: Apply and create about psychological application in sport, music.
- **CLO7**: State and evaluate the issue of group portrayals in the media
- **CLO8**: Analyse the world as created by advertising, including techniques of persuasion, the psychology of deceptive advertising, subliminal advertising, the emergence of advertising in new places
- **CLO9**: Analyse how the media’s coverage of news affects our understanding of and attitudes to events in the world
- **CLO10**: State how politicians manipulate media coverage to convey their own intended reality
- **CLO11**: Explain media violence, including induced fear, modeling, catharsis, desensitization, and the cultivation of fear
