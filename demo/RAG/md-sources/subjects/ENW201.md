### Description - ENW201
The course is designed to develop and sharpen learners' academic and professional writing skills and strategies in English. Each unit guides the student through the structured writing process with plenty of full colour illustrations, examples, and exercises to engage the student and build confidence. This course aims at providing students understanding and skills from writing a sentence to developing a full paragraph.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENW201

- **CLO1**: Understand and use paragraph structure, the topic sentence, unity and coherence of a paragraph.
- **CLO2**: Understand process to write and can develop a descriptive paragraph.
- **CLO3**: Understand process to write and can develop an example paragraph.
- **CLO4**: Understand process to write and can develop a process paragraph.
- **CLO5**: Understand process to write and can develop a narrative paragraph
- **CLO6**: Understand process to write and can develop an opinion paragraph.
