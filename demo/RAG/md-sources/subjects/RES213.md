# RES213 - Wines, Beers, Spirits 1_Nghiệp vụ pha chế đồ uống 1


## Course Information - RES213

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: FBM201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - RES213
-Objective: To provides the basic knowledge about the berverage industry as well as the knowledge of the world beverage culture in operating the Restaurant - Bar. -Description: A study of the beverage service of the Hospitality Industry including spirits, wines, beers and mixology beverages. Topics include purchasing, resource control, beverage storage and issuing, bar stocks and sales control, bar customer care, safety and security, managing bar wastage, the law and sale of liquor. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, demonstration, roleplay, project based learning, group work - Facility and equipment requirement : Depending on the training condition of each campus, the course will be implemented in school lab designed up to 4-5* hotel standard, real hotel or other places, which fully met with requirements.

### Student Tasks - RES213
- Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (http://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - RES213

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RES213

- **CLO1**: Develop general knowledge on the origins and development of the beverage industry.
- **CLO2**: Develop ability to work quietly; with cooperation; patience, carefulness, cleanliness and aesthetic values.
- **CLO3**: Indicate the effects of alcohol in the human body, both positive and negative, from health claims to the latest hangover research.
- **CLO4**: Develop an overall concept and tailor it to meet the needs and desires of a particular clientele.
- **CLO5**: Identify and distinguish types of glassware, mixing tools and equipment specifications and uses
- **CLO6**: Distinguish between fermented and distilled beverages and identify them on your shelves.
- **CLO7**: Classify wines according to type and recognize some of their distinguishing characteristics.
- **CLO8**: Prepare and pratice opening techniques of wine bottle and wine service techniques.
- **CLO9**: Outline the origins of beer and the market trends affecting today’s brewing industry.
- **CLO10**: Develop a sense of good discipline, a sense of responsibility, and a sense of serious work in the assigned work as well as with colleagues.

## Assessment - RES213

### Class participation and preparation
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Each student shoud make at least 1 individual presentation, case analysis during the course in front of the class. Topics will be varied based on the topics in the textbook.
- Note: Participation grade will be given based on students' preparation, and quantity as well as quality of participation in classroom; quality of participation is demonstrated through asking relevant questions, making statements that add to and facilitate class discussion, and builds upon others’ comments. Class preparation and pre-reading before lecture will be assessed upon individuals' understanding and ability to answer questions in the class. Individuals will be called up randomly to be assessed.

### Group assignment
- Weight: 25.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Four or five students per group.
Groups and groups ideas are determined on the third session of the course. Work will be break down into stages and examined during the course. Final work to be submitted prior to the presentation of the topic.
- Note: All assignments should be typed with 13-point font, and must be submitted in soft copy. A deduction of 10% of the assignment mark will be applied for each day of late submission
Evaluation will be based on both presentation and written report (reccommded 30%, 70% accordingly)

### Group practice
- Weight: 15.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Four or five students per group. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through: Demonstration of skills on mixing different (in quantity) international alcoholic beverages in a prescribed timeframe; utilizing systematic liquor pouring and proper bar measurement; mixing of alcoholic concoction showmanship skill techniques.
The mark will be given based on each student's performance, not on the whole group's. It means each student will get their own mark.

### Individual practice
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Each student must be assessed through practical demonstration in a simulated workplace environment where beverage ingredients and equipment are provided. Competency is to be demonstrated by effectively preparing and serving beverage in accordance with the performance criteria and the range listed within the practical learning sessions.
- Note: Competency in this unit may be assessed through:
- Observation shall be done on the proper classification of alcoholic beverages and glasses; proper mixing procedures on several cocktails and the uses and maintenance of different bar tools and equipment.
- Direct observation of student providing advice to customers or colleagues on wine and its proper serving procedures.

### Quizzes
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Multiple choice, exercises or essay questions
- Note: These quizzes will be given at various times during class (beginning, middle or end). Total number of quizzes: 2

### Final exam
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- Question Type: Multiple choice . Other types of questions may be included.
- Note: Not allow to use any document/information in any form in exam room
