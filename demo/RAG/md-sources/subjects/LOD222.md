- **Credits**: 3

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LOD222
Main objective: The course aims for equipping students hands-on experiences as real front office employees through handle variety of tasks in each position under front office department. Description: Taking school laboratory with standard of 3 star hotel as a real working environment, Front office management 2 focuses particularly on training practical skills in front office deparment for students. The course allows student to experience detailed daily tasks of each position cross front office department such as receptionist, phone operator, cashier, night auditor…etc…With various day to day situation to deal and to handle, learner will be familiar with front office operations enhancing competitive advantage on the hotel job market. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, demonstration, roleplay, project based learning, group work - Facility and equipment requirement : Depending on the training condition of each campus, the course will be implemented in school lab designed up to 4-5* hotel standard, real hotel or other places, which fully met with requirements.

### Student Tasks - LOD222
Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Comply with the time and regulation - Professional grooming - Respect and protect school asset. - Strongly communication and teamworking.

### Tools - LOD222
Internet access, Smile/IHotel/Opera Software.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LOD222

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate highly cooperation and discipline the team, instructor and school regulation
- **CLO2**: Present a professional and courtesy reception style
- **CLO3**: Understand and know how to use hotel management system
- **CLO4**: Explain the reservation concept & procedure and master receptionist profession
- **CLO5**: Describe and master bell and concierge profession
- **CLO6**: Comprend and master the phone operator functions
- **CLO7**: Analyze and solve situations skillfully in reservation and guest relation service.
- **CLO8**: Explain and perform front office cashier function.
- **CLO9**: Explain and execute the night audit function
- **CLO10**: Communicate fluently and confidently in english in every guest services tasks
