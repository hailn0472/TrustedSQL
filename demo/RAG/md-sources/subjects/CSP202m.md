### Description - CSP202m
In this course, students are introduced to a fundamental understanding of Public Relations basics, strategic planning, and media relations. Students will learn in-depth about types of content, including written, visual, multimedia, interactive, and social media content. Students also comprehend the importance of storytelling, content development, and management for PR. Especially, students are instructed in leveraging AI tools to enhance the creative process. Additionally, students are provided with an understanding of strategies of content so that they can flexibly apply them in different PR scenarios. They will learn how to use metrics to evaluate PR campaigns and craft comprehensive communication strategies. After the course, students will be able to effectively and ethically plan, execute, and measure PR campaigns.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CSP202m

- **CLO1**: Comprehend the role and importance of PR in organizations, the basics of PR strategy, and media relations.
- **CLO2**: Comprehend types of PR content, including written, visual, multimedia, interactive, and social media content, as well as the role of storytelling in producing PR content.
- **CLO3**: Produce, manage, and edit various types of PR content effectively in different scenarios, particularly by using Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools throughout the process.
- **CLO4**: Select and execute rational strategies for PR content effectively in different scenarios.
- **CLO5**: Plan, execute, and measure the effectiveness of PR campaigns effectively, and assemble overview reports after campaigns.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate a professional and positive attitude, and be accountable, ethical when professionally applying PR strategies and execution in real life.
