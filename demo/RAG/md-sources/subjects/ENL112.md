# ENL112 - ACADEMIC ENGLISH 2


## Course Information - ENL112

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Course Time: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENL112
This course of study is for students of advanced level learners who need to be able to understand and use the English in the academic context. The units are organized thematically linked sections and cover a wide range of subjects while introducing various test taking skills.

### Student Tasks - ENL112
Regularly attend class-meeting hours and complete assignments and homeworks For BBA students: must attend at least 80% of the total course time in order to be admitted to the final exam

## Materials - ENL112

### Main Materials
- **Improve your IELTS - Writing Skills** | Author: Barry Cusack and Sam McCarter

## Assessment - ENL112

### Progress Tests
- Weight: 60.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: 1. A report to describe and summarize information using data given in graphs/ diagram / tables;
2. An essay to present opinions, arguments, solutions
- Note: The tests must be designed based on the skills covered in the tested units.

### Final exam
- Weight: 40.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- Note: Do not allow to use any document/information in any form in exam room
