# FMH101 - Financial management in tourism and hotels


## Course Information - FMH101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Class hours: 30 slots ; 1slot =90'
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - FMH101
This course is designed to develop students' understanding of the use of financial and operating information in planning, control, evaluation and decision making in hotels. During this course, students are trained to use practical approach to effectively analyze hospitality industry management reports and financial statements; prepare accurate business forecasts, and effective cost-control systems; manage working capital; develop and finance growth strategies; perform investment analysis and ultimately increase shareholder value and personal wealth.
