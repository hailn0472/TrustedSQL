### Description - ENT203
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises. Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: 1. Apply the knowledge of a vocabulary range covered in lessons in academic and social disciplines 2. Understand grammar rules taught in lessons and use them in both the spoken form for social communication and writing well-organized forms for topics covered in the course 3. Understand different reading texts by applying a variety of reading strategies (skimming, scanning, making inferences and predictions, summarising and paraphrasing, comparing and contrasting) 4. Understand various types of recordings and videos and apply different listening techniques (classifying, inferring, identifying main ideas or details, summarizing) to respond correctly to the social situations covered in the course 5. Develop ethics, self-study, professionalism, respect in different disciplines, group work, and creativity 6. Use appropriate colloquial expressions and various conversational strategies covered in the course in social conversations 7. Develop effective communication and teamwork in different academic and social tasks 8. Apply cultural knowledge or personalize or express appropriately in social situations 9. Use different learning tools (books, Internet, audio, videos, etc.) appropriately to perform various learning tasks creatively
