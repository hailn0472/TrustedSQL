### Description - ANB402
This course focuses on the standards for TV and features. Students will first study backgrounds and will gain the technical knowledge needed to create them. Students will then make thumb- nail paintings and continue on to finished paintings, including large pan scenes.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANB402

- **CLO1**: Students understand the role of backgrounds, the process of building the background in 2D animation.
- **CLO2**: Understand the foreground and background in a scene.
- **CLO3**: Know the camera movements to separate objects from backgrounds.
- **CLO4**: Analyzes the storyboard to find out the number of backgrounds needed for each movie.
- **CLO5**: Splitting layers of each scene and arranging them from back to front.
- **CLO6**: Choosing right colors for each layer according to the content.
- **CLO7**: Setup camera movements and position the layers based on the storyboard.
