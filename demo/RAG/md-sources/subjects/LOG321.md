# LOG321 - Logistics and Transportation Management - Quản trị Logisitcs và Vận tải


## Course Information - LOG321

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: SCM301m
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - LOG321
This course will allow you to have a big picture of the orchestration of logistics/transportation presenting the different means of transportation. A key feature of this course is the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and applications. These tools will be used to support students in (1) generating ideas/structures for their speaking notes and slide structure of important topics of the course. (2) editing the slide set after students have already finished it.

### Student Tasks - LOG321
'- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - LOG321
- Laptop computer - Internet - Class room for lecture sessions

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LOG321

- **CLO1**: Explore the role of transportation from a micro perspective and focuses upon transportation
- **CLO2**: Address the ever-expanding role of global transportation in the supply chain, focusing on the extensive set of planning activities related to the timely and efficient movement of freight between countries, international freight and customs clearance.
- **CLO3**: Discus global transportation execution
- **CLO4**: Discuss regulation and public policy as it applies to transportation.
- **CLO5**: Tackle the major issues of environmental sustainability, fuel price volatility and availability, and infrastructure inadequacy, as well as other challenges.
- **CLO6**: Examine the role of transportation from a macro perspective with special emphasis upon transportation's effect upon the development of global economies.
- **CLO7**: Explain the importance and use of current technology.
- **CLO8**: Examine costing and pricing in transportation.
- **CLO9**: Focus on the motor carrier industry and differentiates between both truckload (TL) and less-than-truckload (LTL) carrier operations.
- **CLO10**: Examine the railroad industry and its current operations.
- **CLO11**: Understand the airline industry.
- **CLO12**: Discuss bulk carriers (water and pipeline).
- **CLO13**: Explain the roles and industry composition of third party logistics (3PL) provider.
