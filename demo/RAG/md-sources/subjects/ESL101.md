### Description - ESL101
Objectives: This course is designed to introduce to the students the basic concepts of Sociolingusitics and the relationship between language and society, and how language is used in multilingual speech communities. Description: The course helps students: ~Understand sociolinguitic termology or concepts to apply in research and real-life practices. ~Use language appropriately in different social contexts. ~Interpret the social relationships in a community, and the way people convey and construct aspects of their social identity through their language. ~Examine some similarities and differences in the way people use their language to convey meaning in association with society. Teaching method: This course applies instructional, communicative, and interative disccusions with: ~instructions on lessons and exercises meant to apply learnt skills. ~researching features of sociolinguistics through presentations and project work. ~open-minded approach to questions and issues that may arise during lessons. ~lectures and seminars to enhance interest in the field of sociolinguistics. Assessment scheme: +On-going assessment: 50% 1. Progress Test 1: 10% 2. Progress Test 2: 10% 3. Assignment 1:Presentation: 15% 4. Assignment 2:Discussion Leading: 15% +Final exam: 50% On-going assessment >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ESL101

- **CLO1**: Understand and define sociolinguistic theories. Explain how language is used in mutilingual communities (differnt languages used in different settings or situations)
- **CLO2**: Examine how language is maintained and some factors lead to language shift.
- **CLO3**: Analyse and how language varies in nations with different types of language used and how a language is planned to become a national language.
- **CLO4**: Explain how language differs in regions in terms of accents and dialects and the influence of age and gender on language use.
- **CLO5**: Understand the way people use their language to signal their enthicity, the influence of social networks on the way people speak, and reasons for language change
- **CLO6**: Examine how language varies according to its style and context.
- **CLO7**: Analyse different functions of speech, politeness, cross culture communication, gender and stereotypes
- **CLO8**: Study the language-perception relationship as well as language-culture relationship; and how discourse is viewed by pragmatics.
- **CLO9**: Practice and refine the lifelong learning behavior of collaboration by working together as a group.
