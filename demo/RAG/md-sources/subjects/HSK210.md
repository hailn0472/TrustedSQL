# HSK210 - Housekeeping Management (Pratice)_Quản trị buồng phòng (Thực hành)


## Course Information - HSK210

- **Credits**: 1
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (50h) = 22,5h (30 sessions) contact hours 27,5h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - HSK210
Main objective: This course aims to equip students with the skills to manage resources, administer assets and familiar with several key procedures of housekeeping cleanliness process. -Description: This course focus on practicing house keeping procedure. -Teaching method: ldemonstration, roleplay

### Student Tasks - HSK210
Attend 100% of class hours in order to obtain the completion criteria. - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Comply with the time and regulation - Professional grooming - Respect and protect school asset. - Strongly communication and teamworking.

### Tools - HSK210
- Internet - Facility and equipment requirement : Depending on the training condition of each campus, the course will be implemented in school lab designed up to 4-5* hotel standard, real hotel or other places, which fully met with requirements.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HSK210

- **CLO1**: To operate the key procedure of cleaning and inspecting guest rooms, public areas and others, as well as able to demonstrate a working knowledge of the procedures.
- **CLO2**: To utilize various techniques to manage inventories effectively
- **CLO3**: To operate the method of protecting property from damage and risk of insect
- **CLO4**: To demonstrate highly cooperation and discipline the team, instructor and school regulation
- **CLO5**: To communicate english fluently and professtionally in working place
