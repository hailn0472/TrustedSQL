### Description - EPE301c
This Specialization is intended to prepare students with professional skills in the workplace.  Through four courses, students will explore the use of emotional and social intelligence, practice a formula for problem solving, cultivate a growth mindset, and build skills related to adaptability and resilience in an ever-changing environment. These skills show up in business relationships and communication and ultimately impact professional effectiveness. Students will be able to demonstrate sound judgment by engaging in critical thinking to reach decisions and solve problems independently. Students will be able to develop a competitive advantage by learning, adapting, and harnessing insights from past endeavors. Students will assess your own expectations in their current or anticipated work roles and settings, and how those may align with or differ from what is required by employers and other stakeholders now and in the future.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EPE301c

- **CLO1**: After completing this course, students will be able to: - explain the value of emotional and social intelligence for professional success - assess current emotional and social intelligence skills - understand, use and manage emotions through the Foundational 4 Quadrants of Emotional and Social Intelligence (ESI) - understand how stress can impact application of emotional and social intelligence skills, and reflect upon needed actions to take to create connections and build relationships for greater professional effectiveness.
- **CLO2**: After completing this course, students will be able to: - apply critical thinking skills to complex problems. - apply a model for solving problems and pose questions to further understanding of specific problems.
- **CLO3**: After completing this course, students will be able to: - analyze beliefs and actions that contribute to a growth mindset within an organization. - evaluate opportunites to increasingly develop a growth mindset. - apply practices that foster growth mindset within an organization.
- **CLO4**: After completing this course, students will be able to: - define the concepts of adaptability and resilience - apply knowledge and tools to become more adaptable and resilient in their workplace and life.
