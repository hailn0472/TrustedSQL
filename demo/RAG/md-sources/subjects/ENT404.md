### Description - ENT404
The main objective of this course is to help students acquire the vocabulary range and to enhance all language skills required for the advanced level. Additional writing and grammar lessons will be incorporation in the course.

### Student Tasks - ENT404
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Use CD-ROM provided with the textbook - Access the course website (http://cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - ENT404
- Internet access - Foxit pdf editor

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENT404

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate knowledge of a vocabulary range covered in lessons (including meaning, opposites, spelling and pronunciation).
- **CLO2**: Know of the role of conjunctions, articles and prepositions in writing
- **CLO3**: Understand all the tenses system
- **CLO4**: At the end of the course, write an argumentative essay based on the the studied topics .
- **CLO5**: Speak confidently and use acceptable expressions in daily communication.
- **CLO6**: Listen and respond (orally and in writing) to each other, recorded texts and video clips
- **CLO7**: Show understanding of the reading texts by responding to questions
- **CLO8**: Respond to writing exercises in the textbook appropriately
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate understanding of grammar rules taught through exercises.
- **CLO10**: Show understanding of new words and idioms learnt in textbook
- **CLO11**: Know of meaning of synonyms, antonyms and homonyms
- **CLO12**: Write simple, compound and complex sentences correctly.
