# ENL111 - ACADEMIC ENGLISH 1


## Course Information - ENL111

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Course Time:30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENL111
The main objective of the course is to help students develop the academic language and academic skills especially Listening and Speaking skills. Besides, the course aims at providing students with strategies necessary to achieve proficiency in English. 1. Developing skills: 1.1 Listening skills: Prediction, Listening for signpost words, Classification, Understanding illustration: map, visual information, Note taking, Listening for paraphrase, etc. 1.2 Speaking skills: Giving factual information, Expressing opinions /attitudes /preferences / reasons/feelings, Describing, Comparing and contrasting, Speculation, etc. 2. Developing language: Increase academic vocabulary, including collocations, and sentence structures through the topics in academic context. 3. Dealing with tests/exams: provide students with different kinds of listening and speaking tasks simulating exams for practice.

### Student Tasks - ENL111
Regularly attend class-meeting hours and complete assignments and homeworks For BBA students: must attend at least 80% of the total course time in order to be admitted to the final exam

## Assessment - ENL111

### Progress listening Tests
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Form completion / sentence completion /multiple choices /matching /short answer …
- Note: The tests must be designed based on the skills covered in the tested units.
Students are allowed to listen twice

### Progress speaking Tests
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Independent mini-talk and interview with instructor
- Note: The tests must be designed based on the topics and skills covered in the tested units. Depending on the class size, several teachers may be involved to interview students within the time limit.

### Final Exam - Listening
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Listening: 04 recordings Students answer various questions covering listening skills learnt in 10 units.
- Note: Listening: Students are allowed to listen twice

### Final Exam - Speaking
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: Speaking: 03 parts 1. Part 1: Speak about yourself / familiar topics 2. Part 2: A talk about a topic in the course 3. Part 3: Discussion
- Note: Speaking: Task 1 dealing with self-introduction. Therefore, it is assumed that students can handle it at this level without class instruction during the course.
