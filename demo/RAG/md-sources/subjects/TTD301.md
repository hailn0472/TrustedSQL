### Description - TTD301
Objective: Students understand the various components that contribute to the design of a tourism product, such as local culture, important holidays, nearby resorts, modes of transportation, the availability of intermediary service providers, and the development prospects of that tourism product in the country's tourism industry's future. Students must generate an idea, create a product, and persuade the class that it is effective, viable, sustainable, and has the potential for development, in which students must present analysis of the strategic products of various organizations using case studies.  Description: This course teaches the structure, functions, operations, strategies, and other aspects of tourism goods, as well as how to build and implement one or more comprehensive tourism products. The course also analyzes and provides information on specific tourism items such as specific fascination, cultural, religious, and so forth. The course also includes real-life case studies on tourism product design.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTD301

- **CLO1**: Define and analyze the extent of services, evaluate the relevance of services in the economy as a whole, and tourism services in particular.
- **CLO2**: Distinguish between the marketing mix for tangible products and the marketing mix for intangible services, and investigate the use of service in tourism.
- **CLO3**: Understand all of the aspects that drive tourism product ideation, design, and implementation, such as destination selection, culture, entertainment, and so on.
- **CLO4**: Investigate the processes of tourism product development, from concept design to actual execution, as well as the method of tourism product operation.
- **CLO5**: Understand several fundamental and unique forms of tourist products, such as cultural, religious, resort, beach, and event types, among others.
- **CLO6**: Define a communication and promotion strategy to extensively spread typical tourism products and stress the sustainability of tourism products using communication methods.
- **CLO7**: Analyze and evaluate several organizations' tourism product strategies using common case studies.
- **CLO8**: Develop products that address contemporary issues in the development of tourist products for events, resorts, cruises, and other tourism products.
- **CLO9**: Acquire professional attitudes and abilities to obtain an understanding of designing tourism products for a wide range of customers.
