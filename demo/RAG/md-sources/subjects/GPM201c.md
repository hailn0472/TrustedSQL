### Description - GPM201c
This course helps you gain an immersive understanding of the practices and skills needed to succeed in an entry-level project management role, learn how to create effective project documentation and artifacts throughout the various phases of a project, learn the foundations of Agile project management, with a focus on implementing Scrum events, building Scrum artifacts, and understanding Scrum roles, practice strategic communication, problem-solving, and stakeholder management through real-world scenarios.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GPM201c

- **CLO1**: Describe project management skills, roles, and responsibilities across a variety of industries
- **CLO2**: Describe how to estimate, track, and maintain a budget.
- **CLO3**: Draft a communication plan and explain how to manage it.
- **CLO4**: Implement the key quality management concepts of quality standards, quality planning, quality assurance, and quality control.
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate how to prioritize and analyze data and how to communicate a project’s data-informed story.
- **CLO6**: Discuss the stages of team development and how to manage team dynamics.
- **CLO7**: Describe the steps of the closing process and create project closing documentation.
- **CLO8**: Explain the Agile project management approach and philosophy, including values and principles.
- **CLO9**: Discuss the pillars of Scrum and how they support Scrum values.
- **CLO10**: Describe the five important Scrum events and how to set up each event for a Scrum team.
- **CLO11**: Explain how to coach an Agile team and help them overcome challenges.
- **CLO12**: Explain the project management life cycle and compare different program management methodologies
- **CLO13**: Examine project documentation and conduct research to identify tasks for a project and organize those project tasks and milestones in a project plan.
- **CLO14**: Determine quality standards and evaluate against those standards to ensure that the project is achieving the required level of quality.
- **CLO15**: Develop effective stakeholder reports by applying storytelling strategies to describe data.
- **CLO16**: Define organizational structure and organizational culture and explain how it impacts project management.
- **CLO17**: Understand the significance of the initiation phase of the project life cycle.
- **CLO18**: Understand the key components of project charters and develop a project charter for project initiation.
- **CLO19**: Complete a stakeholder analysis and utilize RACI charts to define and communicate project team member responsibilities.
- **CLO20**: Evaluate various project management tools to meet project needs.
- **CLO21**: Describe the components of the project planning phase and their significance.
- **CLO22**: Identify tools and best practices to build a project plan and risk management plan.
