### Description - ISM201
In this course, students are introduced fundamental understanding about strategic communication by approaching from definitions to current case studies. A range of important elements of strategic communication such as strategy concept, messages, stakholders, media, etc. are listed and well described. Also, students will comprehend the importance of research before creating strategies and plans. The role of strategic communication in branding and campaigns is highlighted. Ethics and societal issues have a strong impact while we are practicing strategic communication. Additionally, students will discuss about current changes in digital media industry and society so that they can adjust their strategic communication plans become more appropriate. Last but not least, students will know how to act ethically and professionally, and be accountable when professionally applying strategic communication in real life

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ISM201

- **CLO1**: Explain fundamental knowledge about strategic communication, the concept of strategy, three management levels of strategic communication, the ethics and societal issues in strategic communication.
- **CLO2**: Reasearch and develop plans for strategic communication which require in-depth understanding of objectices, strategies, and tactics.
- **CLO3**: Comprehend the stakholders, message tactics, and different media including traditional and digital media which significantly involve with strategic communication.
- **CLO4**: Effectively apply strategic communication in branding and campaigns, well estimate time, budget, and accuratly evaluate the results.
- **CLO5**: Recognise significant changes in society and the tendency of media industry then predict and appropriately adjust strategic communication plans.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate a professional and positive attitude, and be accountable when professionally applying strategic communication in real life
