# ECO203m - International Economics Relations - Quan hệ kinh tế quốc tế


## Course Information - ECO203m

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: IBI101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ECO203m
This course will aim to develop student’s knowledge and understanding of international economics and its application on international business. It will provide a deep contextualization of contemporary issues and debates within international business. The course prepares students the understanding of how the world economy is changing and how international business are deeply embedded in a complex web of relationships. The course will also explain globalization, trade theory and the way businesses can take advantage in contemporary world. The course’s ultimate goal is that students have a good understanding of theoretical, practical, and ethical issues associated with international economics and international business.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECO203m

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate understanding of key aspects of international business and international business at a intermediate level
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate the ability to think critically and solve problems
- **CLO3**: Publicly speak and convey ideas in a fluent and convincing manner
- **CLO4**: Appply theories to explain real-world situations
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate the ability to write international business proposals, as well as economics analysis
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate the ability to cooperate in team to work towards the same goal
