# MAC102 - ADVANCED MATHEMATICS FOR BUSINESS


## Course Information - MAC102

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session=90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: (Students completed MAC101 can be waived)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Student Tasks - MAC102
Students must attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MAC102

- **CLO1**: Find the limit of a function.
- **CLO2**: Calculate derivatives of a function
- **CLO3**: Use derivatives to analyze a function
- **CLO4**: Use derivatives to solve applications
- **CLO5**: Find integrals and calculate the area under a graph of a function
- **CLO6**: Use integration to solve applications
- **CLO7**: Analyze a multi-variable function using partial derivatives
