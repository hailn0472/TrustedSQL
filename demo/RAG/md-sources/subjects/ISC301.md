# ISC301 - E-Commerce_Thương mại điện tử


## Course Information - ISC301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ISC301
By the end of the course, students will be able to understand: - E-Commerce and E-Marketplaces - Internet Consumer Retailing - Social media and commerce topics - Business-to-Business E-Commerce - Other EC Models and Application - EC Support Services - EC Strategy and Implementation - Launching a Successful Online Business

### Student Tasks - ISC301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises and practical given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time. - Constantly follow https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information.

### Tools - ISC301
Technical facility: Internet access

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ISC301

- **CLO1**: Defne all types of e-commerce systems and describe their major business and revenue models, drivers, and benefts.
- **CLO2**: Describe the connected e-commerce and Internet of things and its smart applications.
- **CLO3**: Understand online consumer behavior.
- **CLO4**: Describe marketing and advertising in the Web environment.
- **CLO5**: Describe the use of e-payments, including mobile payments, and digital currency in e-commerce.
- **CLO6**: Understand order fulfllment in e-commerce and its relationship to supply chain management.
- **CLO7**: Understand e-commerce strategy and describe its process and steps, including justifcation, planning, implementation, and assessment.
- **CLO8**: Describe the global aspects of e-commerce.
- **CLO9**: Explain the issues of using e-commerce by small- and medium-sized companies.
- **CLO10**: Understand the ethical, legal, social, and business environments within which e-commerce operates.
- **CLO11**: Understand how to Launch a Successful Online Business and EC Projects
- **CLO12**: Describe all the major mechanisms that are used in executing e-commerce.
- **CLO13**: Describe all methods and models of selling products and services online from business to individual customers.
- **CLO14**: Understand all online business-to-business activities, including selling, procurement, auctions, and collaboration.
- **CLO15**: Describe EC activities other than selling online, such as e-government, e-learning/e-- training, e-health, and sharing economy.
- **CLO16**: Describe the major applications of artifcial intelligence in EC.
- **CLO17**: Describe social networks, social customers, and social software as facilitators of social commerce.
- **CLO18**: Describe the landscape of social commerce applications, including social shopping and advertising, social CRM, social entertainment, and crowdsourcing.
- **CLO19**: Describe social commerce
