# FIN307 - Financial Taxation_Thuế doanh nghiệp


## Course Information - FIN307

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ACC101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - FIN307
The goal of this course is to provide students with fundamental knowledge of taxation as well as a framework for understanding how taxes influence company decisions. Topics to be covered include: Fundamentals of taxation, Personal Income Tax, Corporate Income Tax, Value Added Tax (VAT), Foreign Contractor Taxes, Excise Duty, Custom Duty, Other Taxes in Vietnam and International Taxation.

### Student Tasks - FIN307
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Promptly access to http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - FIN307
- Internet - Net-Challenge Simulation Software (CD accompany with the textbook)

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN307

- **CLO1**: Remembering the tax systems and Vietnamese legal framework for taxation.
- **CLO2**: Understanding the nature and function of the Vietnamese tax system in society.
- **CLO3**: Applying legal framework to compute the company's tax liabilities of companies.
- **CLO4**: Analyzing tax policies and potential tax advantages for various organizations.
- **CLO5**: Evaluating tax strategy of companies.
- **CLO6**: Developing a compliance mindset and an awareness of self-updating new tax prerequisites.
- **CLO7**: Creating an effective tax strategy for the company.
- **CLO8**: Show the ability of interpersonal communication in business context
