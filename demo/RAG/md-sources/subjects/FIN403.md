### Description - FIN403
This course provides students with knowledge on how to apply advanced analysis to the valuation of companies involved in the mergers and acquisitions process. Students will gain an overview of the private equity industry and the practical issues involved in leveraged buyouts and buy-ins. Students will also gain an understanding of the tools and techniques used in the valuation of a company, and a complete overview of key elements in successful M&A and private equity transactions. - Teaching method: Lecturing, in-class assignment, problem-based discussions, and team-based activities.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN403

- **CLO1**: Explain the rationale behind the M&A environment.
- **CLO2**: Identify the tactics of hostile takeovers and define antitakeover measures.
- **CLO3**: Develop integrative, adaptive planning frameworks for use before, during and after a negotiation by applying a thorough understanding of negotiation concepts, theories and principles.
- **CLO4**: Apply industry-accepted valuation methods (DCF, relative valuation) to analyse merger, acquisition and related transactions.
- **CLO5**: Practice ability to work as part of an effective team to prepare a financial valuation and a negotiation plan
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate the ability to produce a clear oral report and/or academic written report, which follows research ethic, that applies understanding of key concepts in M&A.
