# MKT318 - Digital Marketing 1


## Course Information - MKT318

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: MKT101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - MKT318
This Course is based on the Coursera Specialization "Digital Marketing", which covers both the dynamics and the global aspects of digital marketing. You'll learn about the introduction of digital world, basics of Digital Analytics and Marketing Analytics Process. At the end of the course, you'll be able to use digital tools to promote your product, as well as apply data analytics to real-world challenges, and demystifying the use of date in marketing & realize the power of visualizing data. This is a blended-learning course, in which students will take a look at the videos & slides and reading on Coursera first at home, then rewatch & discuss key questions with classmates and teachers at classes.

### Student Tasks - MKT318
- Access the learning materials on Coursera for self-study purposes - Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be eligible for the final assessment - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Access FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - MKT318
Internet access

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT318

- **CLO1**: explain the concept of digital marketing
- **CLO2**: evaluate the digital marketing mix
- **CLO3**: create pricing strategies for the product
- **CLO4**: demonstrate an understanding of the motivations behind data collection and analysis methods
- **CLO5**: understand marketing performance measurement & management
- **CLO6**: recognize how digital technologies changed the business
- **CLO7**: explain key trends & notable technological developments
- **CLO8**: understand the Marketing Analysis Process (MAP)
- **CLO9**: plane and collect data for the project
- **CLO10**: ensure the data is error-free to analyze
