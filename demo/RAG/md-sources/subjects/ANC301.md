# ANC301 - Character Development_Tạo hình nhân vật hoạt hình


## Course Information - ANC301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)
= 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: DRD20x or IPD201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ANC301
Students will work on specific character development skills necessary to work in the animation industry. Students will also be exposed to animating and cleaning up ‘on model’ from existing and created model sheets

### Student Tasks - ANC301
'- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment. - Computer with graphic design softwares including Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere - Have color papers, Color Pencils, scissors... - Complete course's Portfolio with full contents of exercises and submit to the teacher 1 day after the course.

### Tools - ANC301
Tools: Learning tool requirements 1. School preparation: a) For each student - 01 light table  b) To the studio: - Glass table - 01 projector  2. Students preparation: - Pencil, eraser, ruler, A3 paper. A4 - Computer with some version of photograph editing software such as Ps, Ai, Ae, Pr

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ANC301

- **CLO1**: Know the basics of Animation films.
- **CLO2**: Define what is a cartoon.
- **CLO3**: Know the process of producing an Animation film.
- **CLO4**: Understand the role and responsibility of a cartoon artist in film making.
- **CLO5**: Understand how to build characters for an animated movie
- **CLO6**: Be able to draw a size comparison chart for characters.
- **CLO7**: Draw details of main-characters, sub-characters and coloring them.
- **CLO8**: Creating a character turnaround sheet.
- **CLO9**: Drawing action poses and facial expressions of characters
