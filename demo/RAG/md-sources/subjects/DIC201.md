### Description - DIC201
This course equips students with a comprehensive understanding of digital circuits, beginning with foundational topics such as number systems, logic gates, Boolean algebra, and logic expression simplification. It progresses to the design and implementation of logic circuits, including combinational and sequential circuits, shift registers, counters, and data storage elements. The module also introduces hardware description languages (VHDL, FPGA) and basic concepts in signal and data processing. Additionally, the course emphasizes professional ethics, social responsibility, and the development of creative thinking and problem-solving skills through hands-on assignments and laboratory experiments.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DIC201

- **CLO1**: Understand the properties and characteristics of semiconductor materials and diodes, and apply diodes in practical circuit designs.
- **CLO2**: Explain the operation of Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs) and design and analyze BJT amplifier circuits.
- **CLO3**: Describe the operation of Field-Effect Transistors (FETs) and analyze their biasing configurations.
- **CLO4**: Investigate the functionality of operational amplifiers (Op-Amps) and design practical Op-Amp circuits for various applications.
- **CLO5**: Utilize simulation software to model, simulate, and analyze electronic circuits effectively.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate proficiency in constructing and testing electronic circuits, while applying safety practices and responsibility in laboratory settings.
- **CLO7**: Integrate professional ethics, social responsibility, and environmental considerations into the selection, design, and lifecycle management of electronic components.
