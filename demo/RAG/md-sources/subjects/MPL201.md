### Description - MPL201
This course provides a comprehensive overview of the processes of media planning with an emphasis on the design and implementation of strategic media plans. Our course concentrates on understanding media plans, media choices, media scheduling as well as tangible costs. By taking this course, students will learn how to measure media effectiveness, make comparisons of media vehicles, interact with target audiences via different media platforms, evaluate the impact of media on its users, determine media schedule and find out the involvement of audiences with media and the data placed within it. Assessments will involve practical media and campaign planning tasks which showcase the course content as it will require a sufficient understanding of media characteristics and audience measurement. At the completion of the course, students are expected to gain a general understanding of the extensive range of issues and challenges related to media selection and planning in the dynamic communication environment.

### Campaign Analysis
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO2, LO3, LO6
- Question Type: Option 1: Report Option 2:(For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: Option 1: Deadline will be set after Chapter 15
Students will be tasked with completing an individual (minimum) 1800-word report concerning a current advertisement in the world. Students will be the one to choose an advertisement/a media product which played an important part in a communication campaign. And remember, a current campaign is important so you can identify easily all the media it is displayed in. At the moment, very few advertisements appear in one medium only. The identification of the range of media used will give you important insights to the target market.
Your 1800-word report should contain sections which include:
Identifies and classifies the product/service of the organization that you chose
Identifies its direct competitors
Identifies the appeal(s) used and the inferred advertisement objectives
Reports the media vehicles used and their scheduling
Reports the viewership/readership/listenership of the media if possible
Identifies and justifies your interpretation of the likely target market(s)
Explains why you think these media choices have been made in relation to the communication objectives
Notes: The detailed marking criteria for this assessment will be provided to you by your lecturer.
