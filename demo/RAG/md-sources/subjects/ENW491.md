# ENW491 - Writing Skill


## Course Information - ENW491

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: B2-C1
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENW301 or (ENS312 & ENS322) or ENT503
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENW491
Writing Research Papers from essay to research paper develops learners' academic writing sklls offering: -review and extenstion of the typical parts and formats of essays -a clear guide to choosing and researching topics -step-by-step practice of the drafting process, using acade,ic language and presenting research -special sections on citing references and avoiding plagiarism

### Student Tasks - ENW491
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENW491

- **CLO1**: Write a research paper (~1000 words)
- **CLO2**: Describe the steps of the process of essay writing
- **CLO3**: Differenciate two types of research papers: persuasive and expository, practice to write a thesis statement and an essay outline.
- **CLO4**: Report the importance of introductions, conclusions and peer review.
- **CLO5**: Select online resources to determine which are the the best to use.
- **CLO6**: Create a detailed tree outline for the research paper.
- **CLO7**: Recognize the importance of crediting sources, and two strategies to avoid plagiarizing: quotating and paraphrasing
- **CLO8**: Use academic language in terms of style and tone in the research paper
- **CLO9**: Apply APA style for in-text citations
- **CLO10**: Write an abstract for the research paper
