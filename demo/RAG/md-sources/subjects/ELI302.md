### Description - ELI302
This introductory course provides an instructional manual to equip students with a theoretical basis for, and essential skills in, Interpretation. Two common types are put into focus; Consecutive Interpreting and Simultaneous Interpreting. The objective is to train students to be capable of interpreting in various fields and settings.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELI302

- **CLO1**: Explain the correlation between a good public speaker and an interpreter.
- **CLO2**: Organize, select and develop interpreting skills in various fields and settings.
- **CLO3**: Recognize the preparation stage and the attitute to overcome several problems that may occur during the actual interpreting setting.
- **CLO4**: Develop a personal note-taking technique.
- **CLO5**: Use several strategies to help cope with the combined difficulty of complexity and speed.
- **CLO6**: Identify units of meaning in the speech and recognize the importance of two kinds of clusters.
- **CLO7**: Use general adverbial clauses in various proper ways.
- **CLO8**: Translate the importance of identifying figures of speech and technical measurement to convey the meaning.
- **CLO9**: Use several strategies to cope with quotations, allusions, and transposition.
- **CLO10**: Understand the value of teamwork skills and attitudes in interpretation.

### Progress Test 1
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO9
- Note: The teacher will prepare a text for each student (≈200words, comprised of two parts, one in English [100 words], one in Vietnamese [100 words]), and inform students the topics of those texts in advance. The student shall prepare materials related to the topics to facilitate interpreting performance. On the test day, each pair of students will randomly pick 2 texts and take turns to read and interpret.

- Completion Criteria: >0

### Progress Test 3
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO7, LO8, LO9
- Note: The teacher shall prepare 5-6 audio files (≈2minutes/file) relating to several specific topics, of which the students will be informed in advance. On the test day, students will be divided into groups of 4 or 5. All members will be standing at different positions in class, simultaneously listening to the same audio and recording their interpretation using a smartphone. Upon completion, students are asked to send the record to the teacher immediately.

### Final Exam
- Weight: 40.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: All LOs
- Note: The teacher will prepare a text for each student (≈360-400 words, comprised of two parts, one in English [180-200 words], one in Vietnamese [180-200 words]), and inform students the topics of those texts in advance. The student shall prepare materials related to the topics to facilitate interpreting performance.
Students take the exam individually. Students will randomly pick 2 texts, then listen to the audios or to the examiners to read the texts and interpret.
Students are allowed to take notes in the exam room.
