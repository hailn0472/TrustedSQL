### Description - GRP490
The Graduation Thesis (Business Plan) is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate business studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further their knowledge and skills in conducting real business and apply creative ideas related to business field which they have found to be especially interesting or beneficial to them.  As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the business plan and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete writing and pitching for a business plan. This graduate business plan will be completed by a group of 4-6 students; however, each student will defend the business plan individually in front of an evaluation committee. Each group of students will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a market research, business plan components, deploying creative ideas and writing a business plan in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.  The business plan's topic is concerned with such areas as (1) project background (2) market analysis, (3) value proposition (4) key activity plan, (5) financial analysis, (6) Risk analysis and future roadmap.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GRP490

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate an ability to integrate knowledge and handle complexity, and to plan and carry out advanced tasks independently and as a group member, within specified time limits
- **CLO2**: Use appropriate market research methodology to identify market problems, potential demands and to suggest suitable and more sustainable solutions to real business
- **CLO3**: Develop more critical thinking, creative thinking, operation and management capapility and a capability of analyzing management, marketing and financial aspects both theoretically and practically for business project
- **CLO4**: Write, present and defend the thesis fulfilling well-defined academic criteria and correctly referencing relevant business market research and other materials, which reflect the student’s academic and non-academic development throughout the whole process
- **CLO5**: Support for real business operation activity or develop feasible start-up plan for entrepreneurship
