### Description - MGT103
The course explores and focuses around the managerial functions of management, including planning, organizing, leading and controlling. The course is designed to provide basic knowledge and skills required in management and give students a comprehensive insight into human relations componnents that characterise any managerial roles, regardless of industry or functions. Various aspects of management theories will be examined and linked to current management practice in the world and Vietnam. Learning in the class will be facilitated through the use of interactive tools such as group exercise, case study discussion, role-play/activities, and projects.

### Student Tasks - MGT103

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MGT103

- **CLO1**: Define management, its functions including planning, decision-making, and strategy development processes, and influence of organizational environments.
- **CLO2**: Describe the basic elements of organization design, encompassing forms, key situational influences, and components involved in designing effective operations systems.
- **CLO3**: Discuss human resource management practices, including recruitment, development, motivation, and leadership strategies, within the context of environmental factors.
- **CLO4**: Identify different types of organizational control mechanisms and analyze their characteristics to determine effectiveness in achieving organizational objectives.
- **CLO5**: Apply the knowledge and understanding gained from the course to evaluate real-life management situations/dilemma and practices in both local and global business contexts.
- **CLO6**: Develop leadership and management potential through the integration of theoretical knowledge with practical skills and self-awareness.
- **CLO7**: Understand the ethical and social responsibility issues in real-life management situations.
