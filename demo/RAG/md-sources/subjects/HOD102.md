### Description - HOD102
This course provides knowledge of Design History: The milestones of achievements in Design History with understanding of contexts; The relationship between design history and the development of technical capabilities, materials, and other social issues; The information of representative designers, characteristics of design styles and movements.  This course also provides some basic skills such as research methods, critical thinking, and mind mapping in the study of design history. In addition, this subject requires teamwork skills, oral presentation and writing skills in the process of assessment.

### Student Tasks - HOD102
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - HOD102

- **CLO1**: (Basic Knowledge) Summarize the development process of Design History; Identify and describe the main stakes concerned with design styles and movements.
- **CLO2**: (Research Skills) Understand the contexts of the main styles and movements in Design History, which related to important inventions in manufacture, significant events on the market, talented and pioneering designers.
- **CLO3**: (Critical Thinking) Analyze and evaluate critically the records of the past in Design History.
- **CLO4**: (Communication Skills): Study optional cases in Design History, organize and express the individual thoughts clearly and coherently both in orally (presentations) and writing (assignments/essays).
