- **Credits**: 1
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 15 hours (1 credit):
- 10 online sessions (1 session = 90 minutes)
- 1 final exam includes Theoretical Exam (30 minutes)
- **Pre-Requisite**: BDP303b
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - BDP304b
This course help students to explore blockchain platforms including, 2 permissioned blockchain platforms: Linux Foundation’s Hyperledger Fabric và Microsoft Azure’s Blockchain as a Service; 2 Dapp platforms: Augur and Grid+ ; Important Challenges and solutions to develop blockchain platform; other decentralized solutions with Interplanetary File System (IPFS) và Hashgraph; and especially explore 2 blockchain platforms developed by Vietnam: Akachain, Tomochain

### Student Tasks - BDP304b
- Students should self-study all lessons on LMS in time - Pass all conditions before taking final exam in campus

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - BDP304b

- **CLO1**: Explain the goals and working of Linux Foundation's Hyperledger project
- **CLO2**: Explain scalability of the blockchain and possible solutions.
- **CLO3**: Discuss privacy and confidentiality and their solutions.
- **CLO4**: Explain the escrow and multi-sig solutions in the context of smart contract.
- **CLO5**: Explain the structure of IPFS.
- **CLO6**: Describe the operations of IPFS.
- **CLO7**: Discuss the structure of a hashgraph.
- **CLO8**: Discuss the Social Imperative of Blockchain
- **CLO9**: Explore blockchain product of Akachain
- **CLO10**: Explore blockchain technology of Akachain
- **CLO11**: Explore blockchain product of Tomochain
- **CLO12**: Explain the architecture and working of Hyperledger Fabric.
- **CLO13**: Explore blockchain product of Tomochain
- **CLO14**: Explore tools and application development for Hyperledger Fabric
- **CLO15**: Understand a high level overview of Microsoft Azure's blockchain as a service
- **CLO16**: Discuss the architecture and working of the Augur platform.
- **CLO17**: Explore use cases for Augur.
- **CLO18**: Explain the architecture and working of Grid+ platform.
- **CLO19**: Explore usage of Grid+.
- **CLO20**: Describe a consensus problem and some of the existing solutions
