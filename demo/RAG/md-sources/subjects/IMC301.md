### Description - IMC301
The course provides a better understanding of emerging new technologies (AI) as well as digital and legacy media through the process of developing and implementing a comprehensive Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC) plan for companies, organizations, and brands. Along with knowledge of IMC's crucial elements such as market, creative conceptualization, target audience, brand positioning, intensity of advertising, sales promotion, publics relations, etc., the course helps provides students a comprehensive understading of IMC in digital age by emphasizing the importance of social media, website development, search engine optimization, mobile marketing, brand promotion events, and retail store connectivity. Upon successful completion of this course, students shall: 1. Gain a comprehensive understanding of the theoritical foundations of IMC and its role in brand building and growth. 2. Demonstrate proficiency in using tools, software, and AI for data analysis and multimedia creation to enhance IMC plans. 3. Demonstrate outstanding analytic abilities that are critical for effectively tackling obstacles in marketing operations and reaching well-informed decisions. 4. Develop, implement, and evaluate a comprehensive IMC plan while considering ethical, privacy, and cultural factors.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IMC301

- **CLO1**: Understand the theoretical foundations of IMC and its role in brand building and company growth.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate proficiency in using tools, software, and AI for data analysis or multimedia creation to enhance the effectiveness of IMC plans.
- **CLO3**: Develop, implement, and evaluate a comprehensive IMC plan and message strategically.
- **CLO4**: Understand ethical, privacy, and cultural considerations, as well as social responsibility, in the development and execution of IMC strategies.
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work independently or collaboratively with a high level of professionalism, both in in academic pursuits and practical applications
