# PSP301 - Public speaking and presentation_Nói trước công chúng


## Course Information - PSP301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 0.75h final exam + 104.25h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - PSP301
Learn how to keep your audience engaged by focusing on the content and delivery of public address in this course. It is the responsibility of the tourism-related lecturer to provide each student with a variety of themes and requirements for their presentations. Most importantly, the training will help students gain the confidence and skills necessary to deal with a wide range of circumstances and customer files in the tourism sector (narrative about scenic spots, presentation of highlights of local culture, introduction and typical tourist attractions of the country, .. are topics that should be covered in the course)

### Student Tasks - PSP301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - PSP301
- Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PSP301

- **CLO1**: Understand the fundamentals of writing and outline a presentation related to tourism topics
- **CLO2**: Master the tactics required to prepare for a speech and speak in public.
- **CLO3**: Develop and present logical arguments to persuade the audience.
- **CLO4**: Build and present logical arguments to convince the audience, helping them better understand the cultural values of tourist destinations
- **CLO5**: During keynotes, present a professionally to inspire travelers
- **CLO6**: Apply the theory to your own speech in the most sensible and appealing way possible.
- **CLO7**: Understand the requirements and show self-confidence, control face, emotions and attitude while presenting to tourists
