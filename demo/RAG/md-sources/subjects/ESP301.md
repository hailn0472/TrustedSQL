# ESP301 - Speaking Skill


## Course Information - ESP301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENS221
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ESP301
Speaking skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Make a good impression - Solve problems - Understand announcements and signs/ understand and use gesture - Understand crime and punishment/ solve crimes - Understand school system - Remember things - Understand people, languages and customs - Understand and use technology - Stay healthy - Cope with stress - Understand and describe weather events - Understand friendship and personality - Understand qualities in making a good impression - Manage their lives - Understand travel/ Describe vacations - Use the phone - Understand jobs

### Student Tasks - ESP301
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Access the course websites for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practice and assessment.
