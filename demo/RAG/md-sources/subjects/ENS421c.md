# ENS421c - Academic Listening and Speaking 2 - Nghe Nói học thuật 2


## Course Information - ENS421c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 4.5 hrs offline + PE (Interview 7-10 mins/student)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENS421c
The course is designed to enable students to: - develop and deliver compelling presentations. - balance proven ideas with lots of speech practice drawn from the established knowledge in communication, rhetoric, linguistics, and argumentation, each course  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

### Student Tasks - ENS421c
1. Join the online course (spec.) when being invited by the admin. 2. Enroll in each MOOC of spec., regularly study MOOCs at least 5hrs/week by watching videos, reading materials, taking quizzes, doing assgnments... as required 3. Respect the Coursera Code of Conduct, at https://learner.coursera.help/hc/en-us/articles/208280036-Coursera-Code-of-Conduct 3. Keep online learning progress promptly (bonus will be awarded) 4. Communicate and discuss in forum 5. Attend off-line sessions in the Campus (optional) 6. Must complete all required MOOCs with certification in order to take the Final Exam at Campus
