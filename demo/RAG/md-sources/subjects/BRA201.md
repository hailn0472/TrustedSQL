### Description - BRA201
This course is designed to introduce basic knowledge, skills, and perspectives to support the application of managing products and developing brand strategies in the marketing area.  Course objectives: Master the fundamental strategies, tactics, and tools of brand management:  - Exhibit an advanced understanding of branding, the important of branding for all types of businesses, including start-ups, SMEs, NGOs, and large corporations. - Exhibit an advanced understanding of the theories of the main concepts, theories and emerging issues of branding management. - Explore the role of branding from both a corporate and a consumer perspective and highlights implications for employability and future career options. - Be proficient in using English for communication and learning.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - BRA201

- **CLO1**: To understand the relationship between entertainment and communication utilising perspectives from the humanities and social sciences.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend the requirements and skills necessary for creating effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO3**: To be able to develop and implement effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
