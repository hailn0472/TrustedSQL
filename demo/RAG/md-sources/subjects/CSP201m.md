### Description - CSP201m
Description: In this course, student will learn the best ways to engage an audience they want and need by smartly implementing their important, strategic stories and information and by using proven tools and techniques to enhance an audience’s experiences and interest. Students will learn how to develop, organize and implement a content marketing strategy, analyze and measure the effectiveness of content marketing, write compelling copy, use a strategic framework when writing, and build your professional brand and authority through content marketing. Lecturer will use several teaching methods such as conducting group discussion and sharing field work experiences in the market on top of regular teaching. Students will have the chance to showcase their presentation and media products as well as their thinking process related to this field.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CSP201m

- **CLO1**: Understand the role of Content strategy at all levels of an organization to engage an audience they want and need by smartly implementing their important, strategic stories and information.
- **CLO2**: Understand how best to engage your audience by implementing their stories and using proven tools and techniques to enhance an audience’s experience and interests.
- **CLO3**: Knowledge of manage content once created by learning how best to tell an organization’s “content story” across media platforms using a human-centered design.
- **CLO4**: Finding actionable ways to grow internal and external audiences, and expand your content’s reach.
- **CLO5**: Create and implement engaging content across media platforms that the people you want to reach will really value.
- **CLO6**: Apply the skills you've learned throughout the courses in response to a real challenge facing organizations today.
