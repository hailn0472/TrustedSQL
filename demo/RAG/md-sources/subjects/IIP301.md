### Description - IIP301
This course is designed to provide students with a deep understanding of international payment including: parties to the international payment transaction; four primary payment methods: documentary letters of credit, documentary collections, pre-payment and credit transactions with detail description of documentary letters of credit (both standard and special letters of credit) and documentary collections; payment currency; ... It also covers: foreign exchange (forward contracts); documents used in trade; Letter of credit application and instructions; trade terms (Incoterms 2020); Cyber transaction on the Internet. Topics mainly include: (1) Key Issues in International Payments. (2) Buyer and the Seller. (3) Basic Terms of Payment (4) Foreign Exchange (5) Various trade terms (Incoterms 2010). (6) Drafts and Acceptances. (7) Documentary Collections. (8) Documentary Credits. (9) Document and Document Checklists. (10) Cyberpayments. AI Tools will be used to support students in (1) generating ideas/structures for their speaking notes and slide structure of 4 important topics of the course: Incoterms, International contract, Documentary Collection, and Documentary Credits; and for their group presentation topic. (2) editing the slide set after students have already finished it.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IIP301

- **CLO1**: Understand the key concepts of international trade and international payment
- **CLO2**: Introduce the parties to a documentary collection, describe process and analyze advantages and disadvantages of documentary collection
- **CLO3**: Utilize URC 522 to a documentary collection
- **CLO4**: Categorize three types of collections
- **CLO5**: Perceive the important principles regarding the role of banks
- **CLO6**: Discuss about notes, tips, and cautions for sellers, buyers and banks
- **CLO7**: Classify types of credits basic
- **CLO8**: Utilize UCP600 to a documentary credits
- **CLO9**: Understand regarding the role of banks in documentary credits and settlement by payment
- **CLO10**: Understand and distinguish the standard credits
- **CLO11**: Understand and distinguish the special letters of credit
- **CLO12**: Distinguish international payment with domestic payment
- **CLO13**: Discuss about notes, tips, and cautions for sellers, buyers and banks in documentary credits
- **CLO14**: Understand and construct documents (commercial invoice, bill of lading, insurance document, certificate of origin, packing list, inspection certificate...)
- **CLO15**: Apply document checklists
- **CLO16**: Define cyberpayments, electronic commerce, secure electronic transaction standards.
- **CLO17**: Understand the business concerns of the buyer and seller and how these concerns affect decisions relating to which payment method is used in an international transaction.
- **CLO18**: Identify the key factors in determining the payment method
- **CLO19**: Distinguish international payment methods
- **CLO20**: Understand and analyze the currency in international payment and foreign exchange risk.
- **CLO21**: Identify the drafting of a contract, performance or modification of contractual obligations, resolution of disputes, and enforcement of judgments or orders.
- **CLO22**: Distinguish the before finalize a contract.
- **CLO23**: Understand the formal definition of draft, acceptances, holding or discounting acceptances.
