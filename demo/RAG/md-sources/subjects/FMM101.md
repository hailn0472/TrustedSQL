# FMM101 - FUNDALMENTALS OF MULTIMEDIA


## Course Information - FMM101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: 30 sessions; 1 session= 90 minutes
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - FMM101
This course introduces multimedia elements, theories, and hardware/software components of multimedia including image, graphics, sound, and video components. In related with the multimedia elements, students learn the fundamental of multimedia processing, multimedia design, including principles of graphic design;introduction to authoring; on-screen layout; and introduction to authoring tools. This class will also include a brief introduction to authoring software. By teaching not only fundamental technologies but also emerging technologies, the course provides the most essential skills in handling multimedia tools and designing multimedia content in a evelopment environment.

- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FMM101

- **CLO1**: Define common multimedia terms such as multimedia, integration, interactive, HTML, and authoring and qualify various characteristics of multimedia: nonlinear versus linear content
- **CLO2**: Design, produce, and test the project
- **CLO3**: Acquire the best content and talent for your budget
- **CLO4**: Discuss the current state of multimedia on the Internet and tools for the World Wide Web
- **CLO5**: Create apps for mobile devices, including tablets, readers, and smartphone
- **CLO6**: Deliver multimedia over the Internet, in an app store, and on CD-ROM and DVD
- **CLO7**: Describe several different environments, several different aspects of multimedia as well as the primary multimedia delivery methods
- **CLO8**: Master the essential elements of multimedia, including text, images, sound, animation, and video
- **CLO9**: Differentiate among bitmap, vector, and 3-D images and describe the capabilities and limitations of all three
- **CLO10**: Use digital audio to record, process, and edit sound as well as use MIDI and understand its attributes, especially relative to digitized audio
- **CLO11**: Create computer-generated animations from multiple still images
- **CLO12**: Work with digital video containers, codecs to select the best video recording formats for multimedia projects as well as shoot and edit video for use in multimedia
- **CLO13**: Select the best hardware, software, and authoring tools for your project
- **CLO14**: Determine the scope, cost, timeline, and tasks required to complete a multimedia project
