### Description - RES301
This course provides tools required to maintain sales and cost histories (the past), develop systems for monitoring current activities (the present), and the techniques required to anticipate what is to come (the future).

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RES301

- **CLO1**: Understand the income statement of an food and beverage business
- **CLO2**: Know how to analyze menu for profitability and create system to prevent internal and external threats to revenuue
- **CLO3**: Know how to develop a procedure to record current sales and develop a procedure to predict future sales.
- **CLO4**: Know how to use sales histories and standardized recipes to determine the amount of food products to purchase in a cost-effective manner, and implement proper procedures for receiving food and beverage products.
- **CLO5**: Understand how to implement an effective product storage and inventory system to control the issuing of products from storage and food and beverage production process
- **CLO6**: Understand how to accurately calculate food and beverage costs and their cost percentages for better controlling operation’s cost of sales and its cost of sales
- **CLO7**: Know how to choose the best menu format for a specific foodservice operation and Identify the variables to be considered when establishing menu prices.
- **CLO8**: Understand how to indentify the factors that affect employee productivity and to develop labor standards and employee schedules used in a foodservice operation.
- **CLO9**: Know how to classify and categorize other expenses in terms of being fixed, variable, or mixed to compute other expense costs in terms of cost per guest and as a percentage of sales
- **CLO10**: Know how to prepare income (profit and lost) statement to analyze sales and expese
