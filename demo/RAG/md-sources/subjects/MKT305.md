# MKT305 - MARKETING STRATEGY


## Course Information - MKT305

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: 30 sessions; 1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: MKT304 (Integrated Marketing Communications)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - MKT305
This course is about evaluating, developing and managing innovative and competitive marketing strategies. It aims to deepen the understanding of how marketing relates to the key issues in business strategy: choosing customers, defining and creating value, delivering and appropriating value, and sustaining value in against competitors. Students will be required to synthesize the theories and concepts that you have learned in earlier marketing and business courses to develop well-informed, thoughtful, strategic marketing decisions.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT305

- **CLO1**: Compare and contrast the key principles of marketing strategy
- **CLO2**: Explain marketing and strategy concepts and ideas
- **CLO3**: Think strategically about marketing issues and provide recommendations
- **CLO4**: Successfully work as a team
- **CLO5**: Deliver an oral presentation in a professional, engaging manner
- **CLO6**: Prepare a professional, logical and coherent report in the form of a marketing plan.
