### Description - SSC302c
The course is designed to enable students to: - Instruct, develop and deliver presentations and speeches in different levels and styles; - Build up students confidence and performance as a dynamic speaker; - Develop voices, movements and gestures to engage audiences while speaking; - Prepare, develop and deliver effective speeches for a range of purposes, occasions, and audiences; - Make use of speechwriting techniques with storytelling, argument, style, topic framing, and discussing evidence.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSC302c

- **CLO1**: Recognize and identify features of Public speaking, and structure and deliver a presentation in strong and confident manner
- **CLO2**: Construct and illustrate complex ideas with clear explainations and dynamic slides
- **CLO3**: Develop solid arguments and persuasive language
- **CLO4**: Design meaningful and informative speeches
