## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GDF102

- **CLO1**: Breakdown the elements of Design
- **CLO2**: Remember and understand basic rules of visual principle
- **CLO3**: Understand how the visual principle effects on human eyes
- **CLO4**: Apply the art elements and knowledge of design principle to Construct basic visual art compositions of graphic design
- **CLO5**: Analyze and Eluavate constructure in an art composition.
- **CLO6**: Create the learner's own art works of design occupation
- **CLO7**: Understand a constructive critique
