### Description - SEM101
This course is designed to help introduce to the students the basics of Semantics in a step by step process through Semantics 101. In addition to the Semantics a Coursebook unit outlines, the course is enhanced with supplementary activities corresponding to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. The course will help students:  Have a grasp of the basics of Semantics.  Understand and use the basics of Semantics in oral and written communication.  Take the exercises seriously at the end of each topic/ lesson.  Interact confidently with the lecturer and fellow students in dissolving disagreements about their answers.  Talk about things to help in understanding when working through a problematic case with the help of fellow students and the lecturer.  Engage in preparing a group presentation using appropriate language in oral and written communication skills.  Teaching and Learning Methodology This course applies instructional, communicative, and interactive discussions with: ~ instructions on pre-lesson and post lesson exercises meant to apply learnt skills. ~ encouragement on opening the field in learning philosophical and practical study of meaning. ~ open minded approach to questions and any misunderstandings that may arise during lessons. ~ ancillary lectures and seminars to enhance active interest in the topics.  Assessment scheme: On-going assessment >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SEM101

- **CLO1**: Recognize the basic word function in a sentence by labelling the different parts of speech.Define the vocabulary used with understanding of the appropriate usage or sense.Recall previous lessons learned and their applications to rectify grammatical errors.
- **CLO2**: Understand and use the different concepts of semantics and reaffirm grammar rules at the advanced level.Characterize vocabulary and its definition.Explain ambiguity in sentences and extend understanding by describing basic concepts
- **CLO3**: Use existing knowledge and/ or apply acquired knowledge in new situations. Apply logical notations to Semantics for practice. Identify problems in organizing data.
- **CLO4**: Examine and break information in sentences into parts to explore relationships. Analyze the principles of logic to its own Semantics to formulae the situation it describes.
- **CLO5**: Deduce sentences into its logical notation by drawing conclusions from logical premises involved in the lessons. Assess different cases whether the conclusions follow the valid rules of inferences or show the application of the rules. Justify the ideas of compositionality of meaning from the visual tables presented in the lessons.
- **CLO6**: Design direct and indirect illocutions of utterances for interpersonal and non-literal meanings.Formulate differences between literal and non-literal, abstracts and metaphors, and metonymy and its various kinds.
- **CLO7**: Practice and refine the lifelong learning behavior of collaboration by working together as a group.
