### Description - GDP495
Main objectives:  - Forming research orientation in the project development process: specifically in the culture and commerce environment - Applying the essential skills for the final project - Getting students to apply the knowledge they have learned and researched in the field of advertising communication: brand identity, packaging, information graphics... to come up with products that are practical and highly applicable. - Students must work in a group. The number of members for each group, decided by the Head Academic of campus, could be variable from 2 to 3 students  The project is done in 4 stages with feedback and discussion at each stage. Students are expected to discuss, organise, present and critique their work with a high level of professionalism, and implement research strategies that contribute to the overall finished project  1) Progress on Project (04 reports): 45% - Report 1: 15% - Report 2: 10% - Report 3: 10% - Report 4: 10% Mark of each report must be >= 4/10; 2) Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor): 5%; Final Product and Caspton Document (by instructor) >=4 3) Project Defense - Final product and Capstone Project Document (by council):20%; - Presentation (Oral Project Defense): 30% Project Defense >=5 4) Final results:100%; Final Result >=5/10 Final Product and Caspton Document must be submitted before deadline

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GDP495

- **CLO1**: Forming the habit of in-depth research on the history of problem development, cultural attachment (specifically by topic of the project), product life cycle (from ideation-production-consumption-end of use phase) Demonstrate project management competence, divide work in a team according to the project planning timeline.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate professional behaviors, morality, social responsibilities and a sense of dedication to community.
- **CLO3**: Producing a clear research structure with summary: problem, scope of target audience, problem-solving orientation... Analyze and store collected data Use tools to process research data, know how to present research results (conclusion,...)
- **CLO4**: Be able to use manual skills and appropriate tools for selected fields such as: Illustrator, Indesign, Photoshop, Premiere, After Effects… Demonstrate aesthetic competence through the management of shapes, spaces, colors, materials to match product needs. Meet the needs of target groups of users.
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate the ability to present the project size: high-aesthetics and highly applicable, clear structure, maintain design style consistency. Implement quality standards according to specialization or should be agreed to by the Design Department (Material tab)
- **CLO6**: Build style guides, logo, mood board, illustrations, materials… suitable for the final product Creating a neat, well laid-out and carefully formatted thesis, suitable for the design style
- **CLO7**: Describe the core users clearly (end user, target user...) Specify user requirements, align with research results Divide product functions coherently, describe the product cycle experiences clearly
- **CLO8**: Understand and implement test methods: survey target audience (directly), test print and post production, ... to support the project completion phase
- **CLO9**: Understand product manufacturing and packaging processes. Display final product and presentation.
