### Description - PST202
Perspective is a subject that helps students draw third dimention objects on a flat surface like the image when we look at the object in real life. Perspective drawing is concerned with achieving a sense of space, of depth and of the third dimension, within the limits of the flat drawing surface. Students can use the rules of perspective in sketching, finding ideas and creativity in designing.For those concerned with freehand perspective is provided all the important printciples, insights and "shortcuts" necessary for effective drawing and life sketching. Essentially, perspective drawing deals with this appearance of things, i.e., with how three dimensional reality "look" and how it is best drawn on two dimensional surface as a canvas, sketch pad, or illustration board.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PST202

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic rules, concept of perspective
- **CLO2**: Understand How we see for perspective drawing and Why appearance differs from reality theory, concept of Picture plane, Cone of Vision Understand concept of Vanishing points and eyes level, how to find vanishing point, eyes level on the picture plane, vanishing point of specical lines.
- **CLO3**: Classify types of perspective: One point perspective, two point perspective, three point perspective. Understand and be able to draw one, two, three points perspective of objects with the determined size
- **CLO4**: Know about concept and application of four, five, six, perspective
- **CLO5**: Classify shade and shadow.Be able to draw shade and shadow of objects with sunlight source and Local point Sources of light to draw pictures.
- **CLO6**: Understand concept of Reflection. Be able to draw water, mirror reflections of perspective drawing
- **CLO7**: Understand Method of perspective sketching , such as: - Draw perspective of objects from simple shape. -How to determine heights, depths and widths of objects in perspective -Use Diagonals to divide to equal spacing and determine the center point of objects -Dividing a Surface into Unequal Space and Equal Space by using a measuring line and special vanishing point. Apply perspective drawing methods to draw sketches. Having a positive attitude and a professional working style
