## Course Information - BRA301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)
= 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: MKT101 or CCO201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - BRA301
This course equips students with the knowledge, skills, and perspectives essential for success in brand management. Students will develop a clear understanding of how effective brand management builds and sustains customer loyalty. The course also introduces AI-based systems, teaching students how to integrate these technologies into brand management to enhance decision-making, improve customer engagement, and maintain competitiveness in a rapidly evolving market.

### Student Tasks - BRA301
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance

### Tools - BRA301
Internet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - BRA301

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate understanding of brands and brand management theories
- **CLO2**: Apply analytical thinking skills and/or utilize AI tools to assess brand management strategies and decisions.
- **CLO3**: Evaluate how a brand management system can capture customer mindset and market performance
- **CLO4**: Develop strategies and tactics to build, measure and manage brand equity.
- **CLO5**: Enhance student ability to plan, evaluate and communicate brand strategies
- **CLO6**: Develop a critical attitude towards brand management
- **CLO7**: Enhance the ability to predict the consequences of ethics in brand equity
