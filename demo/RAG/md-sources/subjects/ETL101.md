# ETL101 - 21ST CENTURY METHODOLOGY


## Course Information - ETL101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENS411 & ENS421, ENW401, ENR401
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ETL101
Objective: 'This course provides English major students with an ingight to some popular ESL teaching methods in the 21st century which aim to develop a mindset of more student-centered approaches. Teaching method: lecture + project work Assessment scheme: On-going assessment: - Assignment 1: 30% - Assignment 2: 30% - Assignment 3: 30% - Oral defense: 10%

### Student Tasks - ETL101
<- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, and assignments given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information>

### Tools - ETL101
Text books journal articles

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETL101

- **CLO1**: Identify and explain the alignment of LOs, delivery and assessment in Outcome-based education (OBE)
- **CLO2**: Construct an ESL course's LOs
- **CLO3**: Recognize the nature of project-based learning and its role
- **CLO4**: Sketch a project for ESL learners that can match the LOs
- **CLO5**: Design the rubrics for the project
- **CLO6**: Identify the role of assessment in OBE
- **CLO7**: Compare different kinds of assessment and their purposes
- **CLO8**: Construct an assessment scheme for an ESL course with different components
