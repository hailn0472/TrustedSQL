### Description - RES212
A study of the beverage service of the Hospitality Industry including spirits, wines, beers and mixology beverages. Topics include purchasing, resource control, beverage storage and issuing, bar stocks and sales control, bar customer care, safety and security, managing bar wastage, the law and sale of liquor.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - RES212

- **CLO1**: Develop general knowledge on the origins and development of the beverage industry.
- **CLO2**: Explain the purpose of effective beverage receiving and storage practices and how to calculate an inventory turnover rate.
- **CLO3**: Interpret financial data and use decision-making skills and abilities in a variety of managerial and service positions.
- **CLO4**: Identify local laws and customer in Vietnam pertaining to the service and purchase of alcoholic beverages and liquor law liability
- **CLO5**: Recognize equipment and glassware used for beverage preparation and service
- **CLO6**: Assemble the hand tools and equipment needed to mix and serve drinks and prepare garnishes.
- **CLO7**: Explain the procedures for internal beverage control
- **CLO8**: Explain the production processes for spirits, wine, beer, and other beverages
- **CLO9**: Identify and provide the process of preparation, presentation, and service of alcoholic beverages.
- **CLO10**: Identify and present the knowledge of wines by grape and other fruit variety, country, growing region, and production process.
- **CLO11**: Explain how to create a workforce of the beverage business operations.
