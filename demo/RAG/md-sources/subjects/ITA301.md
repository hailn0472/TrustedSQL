### Description - ITA301
This course aims to explain to students all aspects of analysis and design an information system. Students will learn how to translate business requirements into information systems that support a company’s strategic objectives. Students will improve their analytical reasoning, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills when they do projects, assignments, and read other support materials in this course. This course is designed to: - explain about systems analysis and design - introduce project management concepts early in the systems development process. - provide multi-method coverage, including a comparison of structured, object-oriented, and agile systems development methods. - explain how IT supports business requirements in today’s intensely competitive environment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ITA301

- **CLO1**: To understand the role of information technology in today’s dynamic business environment.
- **CLO2**: To be able to translates the logical design of an information system into a physical blueprint - system architecture
- **CLO3**: To understand how to concentrate on quality assurance throughout the activities of application development, coding, testing, documentation, and installation.
- **CLO4**: To understande systems support and security tasks that continue throughout the useful life of the system
- **CLO5**: To be able to analyze the business case
- **CLO6**: To understand about project management and how to plan, schedule, monitor, and report on IT projects.
- **CLO7**: To comprehend the requirements engineering process
- **CLO8**: To understand data and process modeling techniques that analysts use to show how the system transforms data into useful information
- **CLO9**: To understand object modeling techniques that analysts use to create a logical model
- **CLO10**: To be able to do evaluation of alternative solutions and to perform preparation of the system requirements document
- **CLO11**: To understand how to design an effective user interface and how to handle data security and control issues
- **CLO12**: To enhance the data design skills that are necessary for a systems analyst to construct the physical model of the information system
