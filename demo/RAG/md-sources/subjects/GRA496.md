### Description - GRA496
The Graduation Thesis is an integrative experience that brings together various components of the undergraduate international business studies in a more applied setting and under a new perspective. It enables students to further their knowledge and skills in international business and related topics which they have found to be especially interesting or beneficial to them.  As part of the course, students will learn about all aspects of the process of developing and carrying out the graduation thesis and gain an understanding of standards and expectations needed to successfully complete their dissertation. This thesis project will be completed by a group of 3-5 students, however each student will defend the thesis individually in front of a thesis committee. Each group of students will be supported by a supervisor, who will provide them with essential guidance in conducting a research, writing a thesis in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.  The thesis' topic is concerned with such areas as (1) international business strategies, (2) contemporary issues of cross-cultural business management, (3) global entrepreneurship, (4) global human resource management - current practices and future trends, (5) FDI in Vietnam, (6) ethical issues in international business, (7) international trade in the current global geo-political landscape and (8) international business practices towards sustainability

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - GRA496

- **CLO1**: The students would be able to identify topic background, topic relevance, relevance of research question, overview of dissertation structure.
- **CLO2**: The students would be able to understand deeply the necessary steps to carry out a research project and to write a structured report/dissertation.
- **CLO3**: The students would be able to understand deeply the necessary steps to carry out a research project and to write a structured report/dissertation.
- **CLO4**: The students would be able to implement and possess their own project in the application to all types of business model.
- **CLO5**: The students would be able to be confident with their final product of dissertation which could be used to apply on solving the real practical problem of the business world
- **CLO6**: The students would be able to conduct an appropriate literature review and review of literature related to the research question.
- **CLO7**: The students would also be able to clearly state the gap in literature with respect to research questions and with selected theoritical models.
- **CLO8**: The students would be able to understand and apply methods used to collect data, data collection methods, sample characteristics, limitations of the research project
- **CLO9**: The students would be able to design and implement appropriate qualitative and/or quantitative methods
- **CLO10**: The students would able to systematically present the data before interpretation.
- **CLO11**: The students would be able to analyse the data and do discussions of findings relatively to the literature review
- **CLO12**: The students would be able to answer to the research question, explain the relevance of answer to practice and make recommendations
- **CLO13**: The students would be able to understand what Harvard referencing is and how it would correctly be applied during the process of dissertation
