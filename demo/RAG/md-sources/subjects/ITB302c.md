### Description - ITB302c
Business intelligence deal with collecting, organizing, interpreting, and reporting on data to help organizations make informed business decisions. Other tasks include measuring performance, tracking revenue or spending, and monitoring progress. Students’ll get introduced to BI tools and platforms and key technical skills required for an entry-level job. After the course, students’ll be prepared for jobs like business intelligence analyst, business intelligence engineer, business intelligence developer, and more.

### Student Tasks - ITB302c

### Tools - ITB302c

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ITB302c

- **CLO1**: Explore the roles of business intelligence (BI) professionals within an organization
- **CLO2**: Design data visualizations that answer business questions
- **CLO3**: Practice data modeling and extract, transform, load (ETL) processes that meet organizational goals
- **CLO4**: Create dashboards that effectively communicate data insights to stakeholders
