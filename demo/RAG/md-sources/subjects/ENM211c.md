## Course Information - ENM211c

- **Credits**: 3

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENM211c
The course is designed to enable students to: - communicate effectively in English in professional contexts. - expand their English vocabulary, improve your ability to write and speak in English in both social and professional interactions, and learn terminology and skills which can be applied to business negotiations, telephone conversations, written reports and emails, and professional presentations.  Assessment scheme: 1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM211c

- **CLO1**: Evaluate your current level of proficiency in business English communication; Present relevant information about yourself in a discussion forum; Develop your networking skills ; Write short emails with appropriate formatting; Recognize and produce formal and informal language in emails; Develop vocabulary related to jobs and companies.
- **CLO2**: Arrange a meeting though email and by telephone; List expectations and protocols related to on-site meetings and teleconferences; State phone numbers, dates, currencies and sales figures accurately; Write a proposal with appropriate organization and vocabulary
