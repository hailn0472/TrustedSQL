### Description - ADA201c
The course provides knowledge and skills to collect, sort, evaluate, and visualize marketing data to capture insights from target audiences. Students will summarize and analyze data using marketing analytics methods; design experiments and test hypotheses to evaluate advertising effectiveness; use Meta Ads Manager to run tests, learn what works, and optimize ad performance. Students will have some skills: statistics for marketing, performance marketing, data analysis, advertising effectiveness evaluation, and marketing mix optimization.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADA201c

- **CLO1**: Use AI to enhance customer experiences and protect data effectively
- **CLO2**: Master prompt design for large language models, enhancing productivity and creativity across various domains.
- **CLO3**: Develop expertise in leveraging ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis to enhance productivity and creativity across various tasks without needing programming skills.
- **CLO4**: Apply prompt engineering techniques to ensure reliable outputs and mitigate risks when using Generative AI tools.
- **CLO5**: Develop self-study and lifelong learning spirit and capabilities to adapt to the constant change of technology and society.
