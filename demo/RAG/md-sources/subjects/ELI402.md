# ELI402 - Phiên dịch tiếng Anh 2


## Course Information - ELI402

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 0.5 hour final exam + 104.5 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ELI301
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ELI402
This course is designed to supplement Interpretation 1 through offering more advanced theoretical approaches to interpretation, ensuring an advanced understanding of professional interpreting practice. By providing a guidance in developing sufficient background knowledge in subject areas for interpreting, students are expected to be equipped with experience of dealing with problems in interpreting at the advanced level, being capable of working in various interpreting settings.

### Student Tasks - ELI402
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by the teacher after class - Use their own laptops in class only for learning purposes - Read the textbook in advance

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELI402

- **CLO1**: Identify the issue that some meanings could not be brought out totally and how to improvise by using equivalents.
- **CLO2**: Deeply and broadly understand the principles of advanced interpretation, and master interpreting skills used for various fields and settings.
- **CLO3**: Understand the role of logic and emotion when argueing and the professionalism of interpreters when interpreting arguments.
- **CLO4**: Understand the importance of word choice and how to handle shades of language.
- **CLO5**: Understand the strategies to interprete in formal situations with a proper consideration to balance the language richness.
- **CLO6**: Identify the challenges posed by the interpretation of policy address.
- **CLO7**: Have a well-developed political and/or economic knowledge in order to successfully convey the meaning of related problems.
- **CLO8**: Identitfy challenges when conveying the concept of humour.
- **CLO9**: Understand how to deal with tasks that involve the use of Latin.
- **CLO10**: Understand the value of teamwork skills and attitudes in interpretation.

## Assessment - ELI402

### (Field Trip) Project
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: All LOs
- Note: Teacher is asked to write a field trip proposal with specific details and send it to the person in charge; or organize a project that facilitates the subject.

### Progress Test 1
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO9
- Question Type: Pair work
- Note: The teacher will prepare a text for each student (≈100words/text), and inform students the topics of those texts in advance. The student shall prepare materials related to the topics to facilitate interpreting performance. On the test day, each pair of students will randomly pick 2 texts and take turns to read and interpret.

### Progress Test 2
- Weight: 20.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO4, LO5, LO6, LO9
- Question Type: pair work
- Note: The teacher will prepare a text for each student (≈100words/text), and inform students the topics of those texts in advance. The student shall prepare materials related to the topics to facilitate interpreting performance. On the test day, each pair of students will randomly pick 2 texts and take turns to read and interpret.

### Progress Test 3
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9
- Note: The teacher shall prepare 5-6 audio files (≈2minutes/file) relating to several specific topics, of which the students will be informed in advance. On the test day, students will be divided into groups of 4 or 5. All members will be standing at different positions in class, simultaneously listening to the same audio and recording their interpretation using a smartphone. Upon completion, students are asked to send the record to the teacher immediately.

### Final Exam
- Weight: 40.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: All LOs
- Note: The length of one audio is 220-250 words.
Students take the exam individually. Students will randomly pick 2 topics to interpret. Students will listen to one audio in English for E-V interpretation, and listen to audio or the examiners reading a text in Vietnamese for the V-E process.
Students are allowed to take notes in the exam room.
