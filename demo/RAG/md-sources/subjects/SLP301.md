### Description - SLP301
This speech processing course comprehensively explains how humans and machines generate, transmit, and process speech signals. The course covers speech production, phonetics, acoustic analysis, speech perception, speech recognition, natural language processing, and speech synthesis. Students will learn about the principles and algorithms behind various speech-processing techniques, including signal processing, machine learning, and deep learning. The course also covers speech processing applications in speech recognition, speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech enhancement. Upon completing the course, students will have a solid foundation in speech processing and be equipped with the necessary skills to tackle real-world speech-processing problems.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SLP301

- **CLO1**: Gain a comprehensive understanding of fundamental principles in processing speech signals, covering digital signal processing (DSP) methods, feature extraction, and speech signal analysis
- **CLO2**: Develop expertise in speech recognition and synthesis, exploring and applying various algorithms for automatic speech recognition (ASR) and speech synthesis, including rule-based and statistical approaches
- **CLO3**: Apply relevant machine learning algorithms and models to address speech-processing challenges, including feature extraction, classification, and other tasks within the speech context
- **CLO4**: Acquire practical skills by using and implementing widely-used speech-processing tools, software, libraries, and frameworks, such as Praat, HTK, or other relevant platforms
- **CLO5**: Demonstrate the ability to assess speech-processing system performance, applying appropriate metrics to evaluate accuracy and efficiency in speech recognition and synthesis models. Critically analyze the strengths and limitations of various approaches
