### Description - PSH101
Objectives: The course provides theoretical knowledge about psychology, communication and behavior (concepts, characteristics, functions, roles and psychosocial factors affecting communication - behavior). Structure of communication behavior, forms and means of communication - behavior. The nature of communication. Skills to communicate and deal effectively with customers in different situations.  Teaching Methods: The teaching & learning process includes a variety of methods: From teachers’ side: lectures, guided learning, feedback and corrections; From students’ side: projects, discussions, and self-study.  Assessment scheme: 1) On-going assessment 60% Attendance: 10% Assignments 1, 2, 4 03*10%=30% Assignment 3 01*20%=20% 2) Final Examination: 01*40% 40% 3) Final Result 100% 4) Completion Criteria: a) On-going assessment >0, Each component >0 b) Final Examination >=4 c) Final Result >=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - PSH101

- **CLO1**: Understand communicaion as a psychological phenomenon Demonstrate its characteristics Apply understanding in sociocultural aspects of communication
- **CLO2**: Understand language of non-verbal communication with its characteristics and types Apply and guidelines for improving non-verbal communication
- **CLO3**: Understand psychological features of verbal communication Improve language skills Apply stages and skills of language acquisition into communication
- **CLO4**: Understand the rules of effective expression and self-expression Apply strategies and techniques in making impressions
