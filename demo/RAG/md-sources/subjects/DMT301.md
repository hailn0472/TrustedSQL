# DMT301 - Data mining with Tensorflow_Khai phá dữ liệu với Tensorflow


## Course Information - DMT301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: MLT301
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - DMT301
* Understand Data Mining concepts and techniques, such as information extraction, ranking and search, online advertising, opinion mining, social network analysis, etc. * Aware of the important role of domain experts in data mining. * Be able to choose or design appropriate models to solve Data Mining problems, and be able to proceed with the chosen/designed approach, avoiding common mistakes in conducting data mining projects, and reach the high quality knowledge from data.

### Student Tasks - DMT301
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all presentations and projects/problems assigned by instructor and submit on time. - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.

### Tools - DMT301
Python and Tensorflow 2.0

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DMT301

- **CLO1**: Knowledge in the field of multidisciplinary data mining.
- **CLO2**: Knowledge of the general data features.
- **CLO3**: Knowledge in techniques for data preprocessing
- **CLO4**: Knowledge of data warehouses, OLAP (online analytical processing), and data cube technology.
- **CLO5**: Use methods for mining frequent patterns, associations, and correlations in large data sets with TensorFlow 2.0.
- **CLO6**: Knowledge and ability to apply methods for data classification in TensorFlow 2.0.
- **CLO7**: Knowledge and ability to apply methods for Cluster analysis in TensorFlow 2.0.
- **CLO8**: Knowledge trends, applications, and research frontiers in data mining.
