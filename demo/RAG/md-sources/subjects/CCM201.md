### Description - CCM201
This course covers crisis communication theory, crisis management approaches, classic cases and the influence of culture on crisis communication. This course will introduce the definitions of crisis and how they come about, the importance of the Enterprise Risk Management framework in crisis prevention and preparation, and how we can recognise and address crises in case they happen. This course focuses on the role of communications throughout the crisis management process, as well as how to utilise media relations and social media to prevent and/or respond to crises.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CCM201

- **CLO1**: Identify organisational, industrial and environmental challenges and disasters
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate an understanding of the concepts, theories and practice of issues management and crisis communication.
- **CLO3**: Analyse and develop strategies for organisational issues using the theories, models and methods of issues management and crisis communication
- **CLO4**: Gain the capacity to critique organisational and industrial responses to issues and crises In terms of attitude and ethics
- **CLO5**: Develop a critical attitude towards crisis communications management
- **CLO6**: Identify and apply ethical standards of practice when dealing with issues management and crisis communication
- **CLO7**: Skills in analysis and problem solving; Proficient in presentation and teamwork skills
