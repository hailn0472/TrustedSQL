# IOT101 - Internet of Things


## Course Information - IOT101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: "Contact time: 30 sessions; 1 session = 90'
Lectures: 7
Labs: 19
Check Presentation: 2
Check Project: 2
(Total: 15 sessions offline)"
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENT403
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - IOT101
"This is a 3-credit course. This course has two parts online and offline. The content includes basic concepts and applications of IoT, practical exercises on the learning KIT. Students are taught how to learn online, and practice some parts at home. Q & A sessions, the Guidance for important issues, as well as performance assessments, will be conducted in the classroom."

### Student Tasks - IOT101
"- Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. '- Student is responsible to learn all VIDEOs (theory) online given by instructor on Syllabus at home. '- Student is responsible to do all LABs given by instructor on Syllabus at home. - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information."

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IOT101

- **CLO1**: LO.01. Understand the concept of IoT and applications.
- **CLO2**: Concept planning, Draw and Install simple IoT circuits
- **CLO3**: Understand the concept of things in IoT.
- **CLO4**: Understand the Networking in IoT
- **CLO5**: Understand the Securing in IoT.
- **CLO6**: Select and calculate the electronics components, IoT Programming.
- **CLO7**: Program IN/OUT Digital with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO8**: Program Analog Input with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO9**: Program Sensors/Actuators with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO10**: Program Communication with Arduino UNO.
