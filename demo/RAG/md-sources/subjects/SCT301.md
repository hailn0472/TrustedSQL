# SCT301 - Supply Chain Management Technologies_Công nghệ quản lý chuỗi cung ứng


## Course Information - SCT301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - SCT301
This course will provide students with: a. What is supply chain management, supply chain management process b. What is a supply chain management system? The role of the supply chain management system in the enterprise c. Structure of a supply chain management system d. Methods and processes of building a supply chain management system e. Technologies and techniques applied to the supply chain management system

### Student Tasks - SCT301
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - SCT301
- Internet - Net-Challenge Simulation Software (CD accompany with the textbook)

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SCT301

- **CLO1**: Understand the important role of supply chain management in today’s business environment
- **CLO2**: Become familiar with current supply chain management trends
- **CLO3**: Be able to use and apply computer-based supply chain optimization tools including the use of selected state of the art supply chain software suites currently used in business
- **CLO4**: Be able to utilize critical management skills such as negotiating, working effectively within a diverse business environment, ethical decision making and use of information technology
