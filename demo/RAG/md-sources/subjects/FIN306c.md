### Description - FIN306c
The Financial Reporting Specialization focuses on the role of financial accounting principles and processes in creating and reporting an organization’s financial statements. Learners who complete this specialization will be able to (1) use financial accounting principles to create and/or process an organization’s financial statements and (2) analyze financial statements to assess an organization’s financial position. Note: Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN306c

- **CLO1**: Using financial accounting principles to create and/or process an organization’s financial statements.
- **CLO2**: Ability to combine the concepts and techniques obtained through all the courses in this specialization.
- **CLO3**: Apply the concepts and techniques obtained through all the courses in this specialization to a real world accounting project.
- **CLO4**: Practising the project
- **CLO5**: Analyzing financial statements to assess an organization’s financial position.
- **CLO6**: Explain the accounting concepts, principles and theory with an emphasis on problems that arise in applying these concepts for external reporting purposes.
- **CLO7**: Measuring of assets, liabilities, equities and income, as well as disclosure of additional information that may assist users understand the financial reports.
- **CLO8**: Identify the current liabilities and contingencies and continues with long-term debt and bonds.
- **CLO9**: Explain the accounting model for leases, both lessees and lessors.
- **CLO10**: Explain the deferred tax assets and liabilities.
- **CLO11**: Explain the recognition, measurement and subsequent accounting for equity, pensions, share-based compensation.
- **CLO12**: Explain the cash flows utilizing the FASB Accounting Standards Codification and other resources
