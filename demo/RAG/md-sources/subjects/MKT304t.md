### Description - MKT304t
The course aims to provide students a solid foundation on different aspects of advertising and other major integrated marketing communications tools, and the role of these tools in the marketing process. In particular, attention will be given to discussions on (1) understanding the communication processes of consumers and marketers, (2) conducting situation analyses of business and social environments, (3) devising effective creative strategies for achieving marketing objectives, and (4) implementing and evaluating the creative strategy.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT304t

- **CLO1**: Understand the role and practice of marketing within an organization, including theoretical and applied aspects of the marketing discipline
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate proficiency in promotion skills and apply to build an IMC strategy
- **CLO3**: Understand how to develop, analyze, and evaluate strategies and tactical IMC plans and programs and to assess their performance
