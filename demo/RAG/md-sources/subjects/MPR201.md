### Description - MPR201
The course provides students with an in-depth exploration of the exciting field of media production from concept development through to the final stages of postproduction and considers all the technical, interpersonal and creative elements needed for success. Students learn the principles and methods of producing both traditional and social media, including all phases of research and script development/presentation; essential preproduction activities and production styles; equipment; equipment; editing; distribution and evaluation methods; and the role of social media as distribution platforms. Students apply knowledge to produce media products in the main media industries and professions: television, journalism, social media, advertising and public relations, digital games, music, etc.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MPR201

- **CLO1**: To understand development from fundamental concepts through to the final stages of post-production in the field of media production
- **CLO2**: To comprehend all the technical, interpersonal and creative elements needed for success and methods of producing both traditional and social media.
- **CLO3**: To comprehend how to produce media products in the main media industries and professions
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
