# ENT503 - Tiếng Anh 6


## Course Information - ENT503

- **Credits**: 0
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (300h) = 105 contact hours (70 slots) + 2 hours final exam + 193 hours self-study
Interactive Classroom Teaching
1 slot =90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENT403 or equivalent
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENT503
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises.

### Student Tasks - ENT503
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible for doing all exercises, assignments, and labs given by the instructor in class or at home and submitting them on time - Use a laptop in class only for learning purposes - Access the course website for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online support from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - ENT503
Course book ActiveTeach

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENT503

- **CLO1**: Apply a wide range of vocabulary and structures in the text book to deliver discussions and talks confidently on topics in the text book.
- **CLO2**: Perform understanding of the main points, speaker's key messages, important details, speaker's tones and purposes ofnative conversations and speeches at a natural speed on familiar topics
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate ability to identify main ideas, supporting details and organization of B2 reading articles
- **CLO4**: Make inferences and predictions from context in B2 reading articles
- **CLO5**: Write a variety of simple, compound and complex sentences
- **CLO6**: Write 250-word argumentative essays with a logical organization
- **CLO7**: Perform assigned tasks and engage in all stages of group work actively, responsibly and effectively
- **CLO8**: Perform critical thinking over others' opinion with logical arguments and supporting details
- **CLO9**: Manage time effectively and perform high-level independent learning
