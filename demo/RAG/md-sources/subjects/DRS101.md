### Description - DRS101
Drawing subject illustrates the basic content of the painting to give students the general concept for further studying of ratio, morphology, shape, characteristics, state of people and objects in relation to space and environment by dark-bright contrasts. Through the process of research, students will grasp the basic principles of descriptive geometry while enhancing the aesthetic awareness. Drawing is the knowledge basement for students to understand the structure, proportions, shapes to serve the art works. In the process of practice and studies descriptive geometry, students shall gain a wealth of knowledge and resources to support artistic creation. Drawing helps students understand the aesthetic standards and enhance the aesthetic emotions, which is the basis for artistic creativity. -The subject will be carried out in the drawing studio with specific requirements on learning materials. -Students familiarize themselves with drawing tools and correct pencil holding style, the notions of color, shadows, dark shadow, reflection, etc. and with the basic fragment and hatch techniques in drawing. Then, students study methods of rendering, theory of light and materials through the study of the basic shapes: spherical shape, cubic shape, etc. .. still life objects and plaster cast fragmentation head.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DRS101

- **CLO1**: Understand what the drawing is.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate the unique properties of objects.
- **CLO3**: Transfer the color of objects into monochrome drawing.
- **CLO4**: Know how to make layout a plaster cast fragmentation head drawing. Explain how to determine the ideal. proportion of human head. Determine the facial basic line.
- **CLO5**: Understand why to sketch by using straight lines.
- **CLO6**: Analyze the relation between objects and light sources.
- **CLO7**: Define hightlights, shadow areas, cast shadows and reflected light. Understand what the value is.
- **CLO8**: Analyze the unique properties of objects.
- **CLO9**: Memorize the ideal proportion of human head.
- **CLO10**: Use drawing tools effectively. Use the ways that measure objects. Create the illusion of three dimensions in a two-dimensional space.
- **CLO11**: Know how to make layout a drawing. Use the ways to make fragmentation and hatch.
- **CLO12**: Know how to measure angles and find measurement unit.
