### Description - ECC301c
This course is designed to provide students with cultural and linguistic knowledge and skills to help them prepared for diverse, international and multicultural workplace. The course focuses on enhancing communication skills in working environment, both internally and externally, with practices that represent real-life contexts or highly potential cases.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECC301c

- **CLO1**: Build effective communication and relationships in a multicultual and linguistically diverse workplace. LO1.1 Raise awareness of and highlight cultural values in a cultural and linguistic diverse environment, LO1.2 Practise 7 communication principles to form appropriate and effective communications, LO1.3 Adapt and adopt appropriate intrapersonal and interpersonal, verbal and nonverbal, and communicative strategies in building relationships with colleagues, LO1.4 Build personal profile aligning in a workplace,
- **CLO2**: Develop relationships and networks in a culturally diverse environment. LO2.1 Familiarise with organisational structure and hierachy of a workplace that is diverse in languages and cultures LO2.2 Present your views and demonstrate awareness of communication principles in different methods LO2.3 Present your views and assert your position at different communication contexts
- **CLO3**: Structure and express persuasive and credible communication in a globalised context LO3.1 Structure and express a persuasive and cohesive manner in writing LO3.2 Apply principles of persuasive writing in workplace LO3.3 Practice persuasive and credible communication across different cultures
- **CLO4**: Enhance ones' professional persona, confidence and adaptability of communication skills in cross-cultural environments LO4.1 Analyse factors of motivation among workplace counterparts LO4.2 Recognise impacts of culture on factors of motivation LO4.3 Critique and craft persuasive verbal communication products for exchanges with work teams and critical stakeholders LO4.4 Apply principles of effective PowerPoint slide design with consideration for cultural sensitivy LO4.5 Identify and adopt strategies on the use of voice and body language for engaging presentations
- **CLO5**: Build a project showing understanding of course content, integrating knowledge and skills acquired in real workplace contexts LO5.1 Collect relevant primary data through different methods LO5.2 Process data and make a handbook or support guide as a product for the project
