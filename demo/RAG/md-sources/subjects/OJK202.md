# OJK202 - Đào tạo trong môi trường thực tế


## Course Information - OJK202

- **Credits**: 10
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 14 weeks ~ 560 hours
- **Pre-Requisite**: Students attain 90% of the total credits Prior to OJT (excluding Physical Education and OTP Programs)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - OJK202
Students are sent to enterprises in fields related to their specialty, experiencing a real work process under a plan previously agreed by FPT University and the enterprise, students will have the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills that they have gained, capture more learnings, set goals and make the choice of their academic concentration at the end of the internship.

### Student Tasks - OJK202
- Join the internship with a serious spirit. - Perform assigned tasks from enterprises, organizations and internship units. - Participate in internship-related activities. - Completing the evaluation and reporting work during the internship.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - OJK202

- **CLO1**: Knowledge and specialized skills: Apply knowledge and skills learned to successfully complete assigned tasks.
- **CLO2**: Soft skills: Ability to grasp and handle situations at work, understand and integrate with the culture of the receiving enterprise.
- **CLO3**: Attitude to work: Comply with discipline, work in a serious and strict manner, have a spirit of teamwork…
