# MKT301 - Marketing Research_Nghiên cứu Marketing


## Course Information - MKT301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in Business Administration
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: MKT101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - MKT301
The course is to introduce the student to the field of marketing research which serves a central basis for marketing decision making. This course covers the basic concepts in measurement, research design, data collection, data analysis and presentation of findings. AI supports idea suggestion by providing comprehensive information about the fields and topics that students are interested in for research assignments in the field of marketing.

### Student Tasks - MKT301
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.flm.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - MKT301
AI tools

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MKT301

- **CLO1**: To apply the process of marketing research and its different processes
- **CLO2**: To able to identify sources of information
- **CLO3**: To understand different research methods and able to apply selected research methods
- **CLO4**: To able to analyze and interpret both qualitative and quantitative data
- **CLO5**: To conduct and analyze a focus group discussion
- **CLO6**: To build a simple questionnaire from a web-based survey administration site

## Assessment - MKT301

### Class participation and preparation
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- Question Type: OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: Participation grade will be given based on students' attendance, preparation, and quantity as well as quality of participation in classroom; quality of participation is demonstrated through asking relevant questions, making statements that add to and facilitate class discussion, and builds upon others’ comments.

### Essay Test
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: OP1: essay questions
OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal

### Individual Presentation of Milestones
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: OP1: Individual Presentation
OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: Each member of the group will be assign to present with 1 specific part of the 4 milestones.
The purpose of the milestone presentations is to check the progress of the project.
Based on the content of the milestone presentation, feedback will be given to the whole group on the project so that they can improve their performance in the final presentation
The assessment will be conducted individually and individual marks are given to the presenters base on the students' performance during their presentation and their overall understanding, contribution of the group project

### Project Final Presentation and Report
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: OP1: Group presentation with the evaluation from the industry and the lecturer (15%)
The final written report (15%)
OP2 (For Constructivism Approach only): Follow lecturer's proposal
- Note: The presentation (15%) will be evaluated either by the business and the Lecturer. The mark is given based on both the depth and width of the goal achievement of the research project, the feasibility of the recommendations and the presentation skills. The language of this presentation might be either in Vietnamese or in English as it is easier for the industry to evaluate.

The written report (10%) is marked by the instructor based on the research project, the feasibility of the recommendations and the overall presentation of the report . A copy of this written report will be sent to the business also. The language for this report must be in English

### Quizzes
- Weight: 10.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3
- Question Type: Multiple choice questions, Case study exercises
- Note: These quizzes will be given at various times during class (beginning, middle or end) depending on the lecturer. Total number of quizzes: 2

### Final exam
- Weight: 30.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6
- Question Type: Multiple choice questions. Other types of questions may be included at the discretion of the Lecturer
- Note: This is a closed-book exam.
