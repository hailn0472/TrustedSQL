### Description - FTF301m
Wharton’s Fintech Specialization introduces the core concepts of financial technology and its real-world applications. Through case studies, you’ll explore topics like cryptocurrency, portfolio optimization, digital payments, robo-advising, and blockchain. By the end, you’ll be equipped to make informed decisions about using fintech solutions for personal or business advantage.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FTF301m

- **CLO1**: Understand FinTech Fundamentals: Grasp the core concepts, technologies, and evolution of FinTech, and their transformative impact on financial services.
- **CLO2**: Analyze Applications of Technology in Finance: Evaluate the integration of emerging technologies like AI, blockchain, and data analytics into payments, lending, investing, and insurance.
- **CLO3**: Examine Market Dynamics and Consumer Trends: Identify and interpret market trends, consumer behavior, and the factors driving the adoption of FinTech innovations.
- **CLO4**: Navigate Regulatory and Ethical Challenges: Understand the regulatory frameworks and ethical considerations, including data privacy and cybersecurity, in the FinTech ecosystem.
- **CLO5**: Critically Assess Risks and Opportunities: Analyze risks such as cybersecurity threats and operational challenges while identifying opportunities for growth and innovation in financial technology.
- **CLO6**: Apply Strategic Thinking to Financial Innovation: Develop the skills to apply FinTech solutions to real-world problems, enhancing efficiency and value in financial operations.
- **CLO7**: Present the understanding of the subject as well as skills through individual work and group work.
