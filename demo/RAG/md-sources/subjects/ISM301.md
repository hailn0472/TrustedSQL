# ISM301 - Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)_Lập kế hoạch nguồn lực DN


## Course Information - ISM301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: Recommended to take this course after OJT
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ISM301
* Describes basic business function area and explain how they are related. * Illustrates how unintergrated information system fail to support business functions and business processes that cut across functional area boundaries. * Demonstrated how intergrated information system can help a company prosper by improving business processes and by providing business managerswith accurate, consistent, and current data.

### Student Tasks - ISM301
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. '- Student is responsible to do all exercises and practical given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time. - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.
