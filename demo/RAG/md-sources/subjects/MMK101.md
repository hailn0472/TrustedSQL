### Description - MMK101
The course aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of both classical and contemporary marketing theories, as well as their practical applications. Throughout the course, students will engage with new classical and contemporary cases, exploring the interdisciplinary and cross-functional implications of business management theories, contemporary marketing management principles, and futuristic applications of marketing management theories and concepts. Upon successful completion of this course, students shall: 1. Acquire an understanding of the significance of management and marketing in various organisational settings. 2. Gain a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles that govern management and marketing. 3. Demonstrate outstanding analytic abilities that are critical for effectively tackling obstacles in business and marketing operations and reaching well-informed managerial judgements.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MMK101

- **CLO1**: Display comprehensive understanding of marketing terminology and concepts.
- **CLO2**: Demonstrate proficiency in utilizing marketing and marketing mix components strategically.
- **CLO3**: Define management and management functions
- **CLO4**: Discuss human resource management practices, including recruitment, development, motivation, and leadership strategies
- **CLO5**: Develop effective communication strategies based on an understanding of and the ability to apply marketing terminology and concepts.
- **CLO6**: Show professionalism, ethical behavior, and social responsibility while collaborating effectively on group projects.
