# IOC311 - Smart City_Thành phố thông minh


## Course Information - IOC311

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: 45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - IOC311
The course provides students with basic knwoledge of smart cities. The course provides the concepts of smart city, the steps to establish a smart city from technology to management policy. Students gain background knowledge and overview of smart cities.

### Student Tasks - IOC311
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Student is reponsible to do all Worklabs - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU LMS at http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - IOC311
- TinkerCAD - Arduino IDE - Fritzing - Sensors - Debian OS (Raspberry Pi)

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IOC311

- **CLO1**: Have an overview of smart city from past, present and future
- **CLO2**: Understand the definition of smart cities, needs, and challenges of smart cities
- **CLO3**: Understand the properties, structure, and fucntions of each elements for building smart cities
- **CLO4**: Understand and have knowledge of building a smart cities from zero to fully functions
- **CLO5**: Understand and have knowledge in basis of using technologies to build smart cities
- **CLO6**: Evaluate the different technologies to apply to each specific case and environment for smart cities's applications
- **CLO7**: Selecting and applying IOT/ICT technologies for building smart cities
- **CLO8**: Ability to do small project using the different technologies of IoT
