### Description - IOS301
This course is designed based on the course of IoT Fundamentals: IoT Security (Cisco). The advent of the Internet of Things (IoT) has created many new opportunities for connecting people, places, and things. It has also brought with it an ever-expanding attack surface for threat actors to exploit. Today's organizations are challenged with securely implementing many new devices into the existing information technology (IT) infrastructure. The IoT Security course arms students with crucial knowledge they need to intelligently discuss and evaluate, at a basic level, the IoT security environment for a given business context.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IOS301

- **CLO1**: Explain the need and importance of cybersecurity
- **CLO2**: Perform threat modeling activities to evaluate physical device security vulnerabilities in IoT systems
- **CLO3**: Perform threat modeling activities to evaluate communication security vulnerabilities in IoT systems
- **CLO4**: Perform threat modeling activities to evaluate application security vulnerabilities in IoT systems
- **CLO5**: Use threat modeling and risk management frameworks to recommend threat mitigation measures
- **CLO6**: Explain the impact of emerging technologies on IoT Security
- **CLO7**: Explain the characteristics and value of personal data, and data within an organization.
- **CLO8**: Recognize the characteristics and operation of a cyber attack.
- **CLO9**: Understand how to protect devices from threats.
- **CLO10**: Recognize the behavior-based approach to cybersecurity.
- **CLO11**: Understand legal and ethical issues in cybersecurity
- **CLO12**: Explain the opportunities for pursuing an education and a career in cybersecurity.
- **CLO13**: Evaluate IoT security risks in an industry sector
- **CLO14**: Use industry-standard models to explain security requirements in IoT systems
