### Description - TTS302
This course focuses on the sustainability of tourism’s relationship with the natural, social, cultural and built environments. Using case studies of the environments in which tourism occurs, students explore tourism’s role in our contemporary world, both as an agent of change, and as a response to it. Creative methods are used to think differently about the future of tourism impact management. A systematic examination of several approaches to tourism planning and policy making at both national/regional scales and from a business perspective. Develops a critical understanding of the role of decision-makers and planners on tourism development. Approach the current status of sustainable tourism projects in the world and in Vietnam.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTS302

- **CLO1**: Understand and analyse issues and challenges related to the environmental, socioeconomic impacts of tourism.
- **CLO2**: Understand and analyse issues and challenges related to the impact of tourism on local cultural heritage.
- **CLO3**: Understand and analyse the roles and interactions of stakeholders in sustainable tourism planning and development.
- **CLO4**: Analyse collaborative planning and management actions open to stakeholders for dealing with tourism-related impacts in the different environments in the future.
- **CLO5**: Apply theories, concepts and principles underpinning sustainable tourism planning and development.
- **CLO6**: Understand and apply sustainable tourism criteria for Vietnam's tourism industry and for enterprises planning in practice.
- **CLO7**: Analyse and utilise specific techniques and tools in the planning process for sustainable tourism development.
- **CLO8**: Understand and evaluate Corporate social responsibility (CSR) of enterprise for sustainable tourism
- **CLO9**: Have a sense of discipline, professional manners and a high sense of responsibility at work; Flexibility in handling situations, ability to work independently and in groups.
