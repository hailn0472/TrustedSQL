### Description - IAP301
This course delivers a logical sequence of discussions about major concepts and issues related to information assurance policy implementation. Organizational objectives,, threats, risk mitigation and cost-benefit analysis will be explored. The student will utilize industry accepted methodologies to create practical security policy that will communicate the organization’s asset protection objectives.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IAP301

- **CLO1**: Identify the role of an ISS (Information Systems Security) policy framework in overcoming business challenges.
- **CLO2**: Describe the different issues related to defining, tracking, monitoring, reporting, automating, and organizing compliance systems and emerging technologies.
- **CLO3**: Analyze how security policies help mitigate risks and support business processes in various domains in the IT infrastructure.
- **CLO4**: Describe the components and basic requirements for creating a security policy framework.
- **CLO5**: Describe the different methods, roles, responsibilities, and accountabilities of personnel, along with the governance and compliance of security policy framework.
- **CLO6**: Describe the different ISS policies associated with the User Domain.
- **CLO7**: Describe the different ISS policies associated with the IT infrastructure.
- **CLO8**: Describe the different ISS policies associated with risk management.
- **CLO9**: Describe the different ISS policies associated with IRTs (Incident Response Teams).
- **CLO10**: Describe different issues related to implementing and enforcing ISS policies.
