### Description - ADP201
This course is designed as an introduction to the basic techniques and principles necessary for audio production in today's media. The course develops principles for creative music making and production techniques using studio music technologies and digital audio workstations. As a Multimedia course, the student will understand the aesthetics of sound, musically, voice, and as a result, develop critical listening and analysis skills for music production through the completion of regular listening exercises. Class critiques will be part of the projects produced.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADP201

- **CLO1**: To obtain an understanding of audio production and be able to use the basic vocabulary related to the term "audio"
- **CLO2**: Be able to critically evaluate and reflect on own work and the methods used, then independently develop knowledge and skills in response.
- **CLO3**: Gain an understanding of how the skills of audio production relate to other forms of media productions.
- **CLO4**: Explain and interpret technical principles underpinning audio production workflows and equipment, relating them to use of digital audio production tools.
- **CLO5**: Relate aesthetic, design and psychoacoustics principles to the production of audio
- **CLO6**: Relate management, organisational and business theories to the process of producing audio and wider career development
- **CLO7**: Utilise testing methodologies to objectively measure, compare and calibrate audio production equipment.
- **CLO8**: Effectively and safely use of a variety of hardware and software tools with a basic working understanding of audio production equipment.
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate proficiency of the skills basic to audio production, including recording, mixing and interconnecting various pieces of equipment.
- **CLO10**: Use industry standard approaches to planning and organising productions such as: group/collaborative work; implementing and working within production workflows or pipelines and taking iterative or progressive approaches to production development
- **CLO11**: Collaborate in production teams and critique your peers work in a constructive manner
