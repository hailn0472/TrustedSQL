# SCI201 - INFORMATION SECURITY


## Course Information - SCI201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 sessions
Home study: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Must passed MAD111 - Discrete Mathematics 1 & MAD121 - Discrete Mathematics 2
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - SCI201
This course has two parts. - The first part go through detailed techniques on crytography, a fundamental building bolck of information security system. - The second part give technical background on one domain of information security, which is cyber security, where cryptography is applied. Half of the contact hours are designed for paracticing on computer the techniques of cryptography, as well as technique of attack and defense on cyber security.

### Student Tasks - SCI201
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact sessions in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.
