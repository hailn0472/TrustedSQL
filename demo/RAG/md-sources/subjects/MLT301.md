# MLT301 - Machine Learning with Tensorflow_Học máy với Tensorflow


## Course Information - MLT301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: PRP201c
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - MLT301
Upon finishing the course, students must acquire: 1. the following knowledge: (ABET a1) • Concepts of machine learning, its main challenges and testing and validating in machine learning. • Concepts of artificial neural networks, deep neural networks and its applications using TensorFlow. 2. the following skills: (ABET a2) • Implement neural networks with Keras, TensorFlow. • Fine-tune neural network hyperparameters. • Train deep neural networks. • Customize train models and algorithms. • Load and preprocess data with TensorFlow. • Solve problems using Convolutional Neural Network (CNN).

### Student Tasks - MLT301
- Students must attend not less than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in the class for learning purpose only - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cmshn.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information regarding assignment submission and feedback on assignments

### Tools - MLT301
Students should have a computer with Google Account to use Google Colab: https://colab.research.google.com

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MLT301

- **CLO1**: Understand the basics of machine learning, its challenges, testing and validating in machine learning, artificial neural networks, deep neural networks.
- **CLO2**: Understand and implement deep feedforward networks architecture
- **CLO3**: Understand convolution and various aspects of regularization for deep learning
- **CLO4**: Perform optimization for training deep models
- **CLO5**: Develop convolutional networks
