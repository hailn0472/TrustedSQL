### Description - SAP331
Description:This course provides an in-depth understanding of how to configure key business processes in SAP ERP. In this course, the processes configured include 3 main phrases: Financial accounting (Phase 1), Procurement (Phase 2) and Fulfillment (Phase 3). Objective:This course is designed for BA students with a vision beyond becoming SAP Consultant. After this course, students will be able to analyze scenarios, identify management problems that entreprises are facing; then recommend, build specific or custom configurations to help solving problems. After graduation, students will be ready to participate in professional training courses to become an SAP consultant. Teaching Method: Theory, practical sessions, workshop are arranged alternately, helping students understand the operation of SAP modules.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SAP331

- **CLO1**: Understand the concept of ERP integration and understand the importance and advantages of implementing enterprise structures as well as the business processes in an S/4HANA system.
- **CLO2**: Identify and analyze problems as well as develope and discusse possible solutions.
- **CLO3**: Configure the enterprise structure and business processes for Financial Accounting, Procurement and Fulfillment.
- **CLO4**: Set up the organization units and process parameters then create necessary master data and test the business process.
- **CLO5**: Develop professional working style, good teamwork skills and compliance with the production process.
