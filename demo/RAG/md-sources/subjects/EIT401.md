### Description - EIT401
The course is designed to familiarize students with translation and interpretation in tourism. The course focuses on (1) interlingual translation of different types of tourism documents and (2) the interpreting practice of tourism. During the course, the lesson may be conducted in various forms including documents, news, traditional lectures, exhibitions, tours, field trips... After taking this course, students should be able to: - Demonstrate a clear understanding terms used in tourism - Clearly understand different types and formats of texts used in tourism - Demonstrate a clear understanding of different types and settings of tourism interpretation - Translate and interpret and news and documents in tourism from Vietnamese into English and vice-versa

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EIT401

- **CLO1**: Identify key concepts and terminology used in tourism translation and interpretation
- **CLO2**: Explain the cultural nuances and their impact on translation and interpretation within the tourism industry
- **CLO3**: Apply appropriate translation techniques to effectively convert tourism-related texts from the source language to the target language
- **CLO4**: Interpret oral communications accurately in tourism contexts, ensuring clarity and cultural sensitivity
- **CLO5**: Evaluate the quality of translated and interpreted texts, identifying areas for improvement
- **CLO6**: Create culturally appropriate and accurate translations of promotional materials for the tourism industry
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate an awareness of and respect for cultural diversity in translation and interpretation task
- **CLO8**: Display professionalism and ethical behavior in translation and interpretation practices, particularly in tourism contexts
- **CLO9**: Commit to continuous learning and improvement in the field of tourism translation and interpretation
