### Description - IMP301
This course provides an introduction to basic concepts and methodologies applicable to digital image processing, and to develop a foundation that can be used as the basis for further study and research in this field. To achieve these objectives, this course focused again on material that is fundamental and whose scope of application is not limited to the solution of specialized problems. The mathematical complexity in this course remains at a level well within the grasp of college seniors and first-year graduate students who have introductory preparation in mathematical analysis, vectors, matrices, probability, statistics, linear systems, and computer programming. The course provides tutorials to support students needing a review of this background material.
