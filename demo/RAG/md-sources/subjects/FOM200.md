# FOM200 - Front Desk and Receptionist Skills (Theory)_Nghiệp vụ tiền sảnh, lễ tân (Lý thuyết)


## Course Information - FOM200

- **Credits**: 2
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (100h) = 22,5h (30 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 76,5h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: HMO102
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - FOM200
Main objective: The course provides students the theory of the profession as a real front office employee by handling the diverse tasks in each position in the front-office department. Description: The course provides the theory of daily tasks of each position cross front office department such as receptionist, phone operator, cashier, night auditor…etc…With various day to day situation to deal and to handle, learner will be familiar with front office operations enhancing competitive advantage on the hotel job market. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, project based learning, group work

### Student Tasks - FOM200
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU CMS at http://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information

### Tools - FOM200
Internet access, PMS Software.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FOM200

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate highly cooperation and discipline the team, instructor and school regulation
- **CLO2**: Understand and know how to use hotel management system
- **CLO3**: Explain the reservation concept & procedure and master receptionist profession
- **CLO4**: Describe and master bell and concierge profession
- **CLO5**: Comprend and master the phone operator functions
- **CLO6**: Analyze and solve situations skillfully in reservation and guest relation service.
- **CLO7**: Explain and perform front office cashier function.
- **CLO8**: Explain and execute the night audit function
