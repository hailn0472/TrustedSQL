### Description - ANO301
Description: - This course will arm students with the conceptual and technical knowledge needed to develop 2D game projects. There are a series of foundations for experimentation and exploration of video game design, story, character development, and winning gameplay before programming begins. The courses emphasize the self-reliance and personal expression of the gaming artist, and encourage you to take conceptual risks and develop new modes of expression and form through gaming. There will be also a series of tutorials to develop gameplay without coding.  Objective: - Understand primary concepts of gaming, and an exploration of how these basic concepts affect the way gamers interact with our games. - Understand what defines a “game” and the mechanics and rules behind different types of games. - Examine how storytelling acts as a vital mechanism for driving video gameplay forward. - Understand game story flows and the strategic elements of gameplay. - Investigate key components of environment and level design. - Create successful character designs that can be applied to video games - Complete a 2d game project. Teaching method: Under lecturer’s instruction, through a series of instruction sessions, tutorials, work analysis and group exercises, students will be equipped with knowledge and skills to complete a game project.
