### Description - WBS220
-Objective: To provide the basic knowledge about the berverage industry as well as the knowledge of the world beverage culture in operating the Restaurant - Bar. -Description: This course is the continued part of RES213. This course focus on the beverage service of the Hospitality Industry including spirits, wines, beers and mixology beverages. Topics include purchasing, resource control, beverage storage and issuing, bar stocks and sales control, bar customer care, safety and security, managing bar wastage, the law and sale of liquor. -Teaching method: lecture, discussion, demonstration, project based learning, group work

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - WBS220

- **CLO1**: Manage and correctly arrange sanitation standards and routines for the bar setup.
- **CLO2**: Outline the history of Mixology.
- **CLO3**: Explain the Stirring, Shaking, Blending, and Building methods of drink mixing.
- **CLO4**: Identify and outline the types of jobs in the beverage industry.
- **CLO5**: Identify the functions and relations of the purchase order, the invoice, and the credit memo.
- **CLO6**: Identify the needs of targeting a clientele.
- **CLO7**: Create and use a workable business plan for a bar.
- **CLO8**: Present basic knowledge about tea and coffee beverages
