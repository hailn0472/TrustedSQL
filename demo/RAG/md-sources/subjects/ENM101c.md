# ENM101c - Business English 1 - Tiếng Anh thương mại 1


## Course Information - ENM101c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 4.5 hrs offline + PE (Interview 7-10 mins/student)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENM101c
The course is designed to enable students to: - develope English language and communicate skills for the workplace, do business, cross-cultural understanding, and business case study analysis - apply these skills in the final Capstone Project by analysing a current business case study and providing recommendations to a target audience in both presentation and written formats.  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM101c

- **CLO1**: Enhance your vocabulary and reading skills, and your understanding of tone, style and knowledge of communication methods.; and apply these language skills to enhance audience analysis, business case analysis and basic business communication strategies.
- **CLO2**: Develop use of vocabulary, grammar, understanding of different business writing genres, and your ability to write professional business documents.
