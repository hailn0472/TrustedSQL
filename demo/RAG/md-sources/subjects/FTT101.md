### Description - FTT101
-Description: The main content of the course is to help students practice the knowledge that they have learned in Tour Operation and Tour Design courses. Also they can experience and see the reality of the Tour Operator and Tour Planner jobs: how tours and situations can be diversified, managed and operated. In addition to the general knowledge of the tour, the course focuses on specific tasks and skills required by a Tour Operator in designing, booking and operating a tour. With focus on practical skills, this course requires collaborative efforts from the students to complete the field trips, conduct small research, set up excel worksheet/spreadsheet and search for material for subjects and future works. Besides, the course aims to strengthen knowledge on the tour guide profession and allow students to practice to be an international tour guide. The course helps to enhance the learners' ability to work with tourists, drivers, hotels, restaurants, suppliers, travel agents and airlines, to present effectively, to handle situations such as emergencies on the tour and to contact the local authorities when necessary. The course gives chances for students to make presentations on various themes and selects the professional guiding language. The course uses long field trips to enlarge students’ knowledge background, to allow students to be aware of the actual state of Vietnam’s heritages and get access to searching information applicable to field trips in the country and abroad. -Teaching method -Assessment scheme (ex: 1) On-going assessment: -Class participation/preparation: 20% -Individual assignment (01): 40% -Group assignment: (01): 40% Total 100%  3) Completion Criteria: Every on-going assessment component > 0; Final Result >=5 )

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FTT101

- **CLO1**: Understanding the diversity of tours.
- **CLO2**: Establish practical knowledge of tour design and travel operation
- **CLO3**: Check out the components of a tour
- **CLO4**: Understand the design and the price of a basic tour
- **CLO5**: Familiarize with the basic job of the tour operators
- **CLO6**: Familiarize yourself how to develop, update and practice a tour bargain
- **CLO7**: Understand and apply the rules and criteria for tour bargain
- **CLO8**: Recognize the diversity of situations arising in the administration of tourism.
- **CLO9**: Solve some basic situations of tourism operation
- **CLO10**: Understand the importance and know how to follow the tour
