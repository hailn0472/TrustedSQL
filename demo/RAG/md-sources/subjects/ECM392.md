### Description - ECM392
This course is organized into three part: - "Basic Concepts" offers a survey on mobile and wireless networks and the major security basics - "Discovering" ;ooks at the issue of security of current mobile and wireless networks, namely Wi-Fi, WiMAX, Bluetooth and GSM/UMTS, and concludes with a description of the mechanisms for the protection of downloaded applications in the context of mobile operators. - "Emerging Technologies" focuses on the security of new communication technologies, namely the new generation of telecommunication networks such as mobile IP networks, and self-organized ad hoc and sensor networks.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ECM392

- **CLO1**: Understand the principles of wireless and mobile communications systems: propagation, cellular concept, channel coding, multiple radio access and multiple division techniques.
- **CLO2**: Understand current typical wireless communications networks: mobile communications systems, satelite systems, Ad Hoc and sensor networks, WMAN, WLANs, WPANs, Bluetooth, Zigbee, RFID.
- **CLO3**: Understand the various technologies with a focus on vulnerabilities and security solutions and the related security of the current mobile and wireless technologies.
- **CLO4**: Analyze and Evaluating Wireless and Mobile Network Security
- **CLO5**: Skill of presentation and working in group
