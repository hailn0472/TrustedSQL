### Description - FIN301
Course is aimed at study of the fundamentals of financial markets and financial instruments, the features of the formation of modern financial markets, on the practical application of financial instruments, the types of financial institutions and their roles and functions in the financial markets  Upon successful completion, students will have the knowledge and skills to: * demonstrate a working knowledge of financial terms and concepts analyse how financial markets and instruments operate and how they can be used to achieve economic objectives; * understand and criticaly engage in profits and risks faced by investors and strategies to control these profits and risks; * demonstrate the ability to think independently, reflectively and analytically for work in regulating the financial sector, financial institutions or various areas of government.  Topics include: Debt, equity, derivative markets, commercial bank, non-bank operation.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN301

- **CLO1**: Understand the basic functions and structures of the major financial markets and financial institutions
- **CLO2**: Understand the simple models for determining the pricing and behavior of interest rate, foreign exchange, securities, derivatives
- **CLO3**: Understand the key role of central bank plays in the financial systems and how does central bank conduct monetary policy
- **CLO4**: Understand the regulatory and risk management issues of financial institutions
- **CLO5**: Analyze and evaluate the current financial market news and issues and their implications
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate the ability to produce a clear oral report and/or academic written report and deliver presentations on topics related to financial markets and institutions
- **CLO7**: Practice ability to work as part of an effective team to prepare report and presentation
