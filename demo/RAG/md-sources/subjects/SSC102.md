### Description - SSC102
The course stresses a balanced approach to communication by in written and oral essential form of business communication. It is also to give the learners opportunities to practice face to face communication, listening and non-verbal messages, presentation skills, job interview; technology, multicultural and global communication, business ethics and teamwork are integrated in content and applications throughout the course.his course addresses the fundamental elements of communication in business environment to equip learners with essential communication tools to ensure success for both individuals and organization. Upon the completion of this course, the learners will be able to: (1) Communication effectively in writting the basic and practical business correspondence including letters, emails, and advanced ones as reports, proposals, business plans… (2) communicate effectively face-to-face communication as presenting, listening, feedback, nonverbal messages in different business contexts including socialization, staff meetings, meetings with colleagues, customers and partners... (3) successfully in the job search, résume and interviewing communication including writing application letters, CV, and prepare job interview in variety situations.. (4) personalize their communication style to enhance communicative effects and leave good impresson on readers/audience.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSC102

- **CLO1**: Apply business communication strategies and principles to prepare effective communication for domestic and international business situations
- **CLO2**: Utilize analytical and problem solving skills appropriate to business communication
- **CLO3**: Participate in team activities that lead to the development of collaborative work skills
- **CLO4**: Select appropriate organizational formats and channels used in developing and presenting business messages
- **CLO5**: Compose and revise accurate business documents using computer technology
- **CLO6**: Communicate via electronic mail, Internet, and other technologies
- **CLO7**: Deliver an effective oral business presentation.
