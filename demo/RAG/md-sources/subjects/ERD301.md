# ERD301 - Reading Skill


## Course Information - ERD301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENS222
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ERD301
Reading skill Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: - Guess meaning from context - Make inferences - Indentify main ideas and supporting ideas through skimming and scanning - Distinguish facts from opinion - Identify pronoun references - Understand text organization - Understand patterns of organization - Understand figurative language - Recognize oganizational phrases - Take notes - Summarize - Understand paragraph transitions - Paraphrase -

### Student Tasks - ERD301
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Access the course websites for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practice and assessment.
