### Description - CCP301
This course is designed to help students explorate of the exciting field of corporate media production from concept development through to the final stages of postproduction and considers all the technical, interpersonal and creative elements needed for success.  Course objectives: Master the fundamental strategies, tactics, and tools of communication production.  - Students will gain a foundation for understanding media production theory, facilitating video production processes as well as creating and evaluating media products relating to a particular issue set by the course lecturer. - Students will have the ability to record, voice-over, foley as well as edit and chord audio for animation movie, short movie, TVC, Viral, etc. - Student will understand the aesthetics of sound, musically, voice, and as a result, develop critical listening and analysis skills for a movie like short movie, animation movie, TVC, viral, ... through the completion of regular listening exercises. - Students will be proficient in using English for communication and learning.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - CCP301

- **CLO1**: To understand the relationship between entertainment and communication utilising perspectives from the humanities and social sciences.
- **CLO2**: To comprehend the requirements and skills necessary for creating effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO3**: To be able to develop and implement effective public relations plans for a wide range of entertainment activities.
- **CLO4**: Show a constructivist approach to learning and/or the ability to work independently and collaboratively to accomplish assigned projects in a professional manner
- **CLO5**: Have the ability to work with a high level of professionalism, both in studying the subject and in applying it in reality.
