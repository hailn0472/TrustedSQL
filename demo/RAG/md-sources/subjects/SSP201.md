### Description - SSP201
Description: This course helps students to persuade employers, sell ideas to investors and pitch a client for new business. The course also introduces students to the tools and techniques they will need to convince people. For more details, it introduces and examines the STRONG method of pitching, which can be put to use immediately: Setting the Frame, Telling the Story, Revealing the Intrigue, Offering the Prize, Nailing the Hookpoint and Getting a Decision. Lecturer will use several teaching methods such as conducting group discussion and sharing field work experiences in the market on top of regular teaching. Students will have the chance to showcase their presentation and persuasion skills as well as their thinking process related to this field. Teaching methods: Direct Instruction, Inquiry-based Learning, Cooperative Learning, Case Study Analysis

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSP201

- **CLO1**: Identify basic concepts and principles of pitching ideas effectively
- **CLO2**: Understand how to create a coherent and captivating presentation out of any content
- **CLO3**: Understand, demonstrate and analyse methods to structure your content and deliver it with strong body language and personal presence
- **CLO4**: Enhance the skills to communicate effectively to different audiences
- **CLO5**: Understand how to alter your message when engaging different stakeholders and end-users
- **CLO6**: Analyze a variety of persuasive situations using available research and theory
- **CLO7**: Demonstrate an understanding of persuasive techniques and be able to explain why they work or fail
- **CLO8**: Evaluate how team roles and processes contribute to team performance
- **CLO9**: Identify how to act ethically and professionally, and be accountable when professionally pitching an idea
