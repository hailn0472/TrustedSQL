# ENM112c - Business English 2 - Tiếng Anh thương mại 2


## Course Information - ENM112c

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Online study + 4.5 hrs offline + PE (Interview 7-10 mins/student)
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENM112c
The course is designed to enable students to: - develope English language and communicate skills for the workplace, do business, cross-cultural understanding, and business case study analysis - apply these skills in the final Capstone Project by analysing a current business case study and providing recommendations to a target audience in both presentation and written formats.  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENM112c

- **CLO1**: Utilize English language in delivering effective job interviews; giving persuasive business pitches, delivering informative business presentations, speaking persuasively in English, adapting content to purpose, context and audience, and using appropriate style and tone of writing for business purposes
- **CLO2**: Utilize English in identifying possible cross-cultural issues and misunderstandings in communication; applying strategies to overcome possible cross-cultural issues and misunderstandings in communication; producing documents with an appropriate style for business communication purposes within a cross-cultural situation; and delivering a speech with an appropriate style for business communication purposes within a cross-cultural situation
