### Description - FIN304
This course aims to introduce students to behavioral theories of finance and financial decision making. Behavioral foundations including biases; heuristics; and framing effects, will be examined. It then can compare and contrast rational and behavioral theories of finance and financial decision making. This extends students’ knowledge of finance by demonstrating how “real” investors and markets do not always correspond to the rational agents and outcomes described in standard finance courses.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - FIN304

- **CLO1**: Critically review the foundations of conventional finance;
- **CLO2**: Have a good understand of the main principles of cognitive psychology as applied in behavioural finance;
- **CLO3**: Understand the causes and nature of market anomalies;
- **CLO4**: Critically evaluate behavioural influences involving individuals’ securities pricing, financial analysis, investment decisions.
- **CLO5**: Critically evaluate behavioural influences involving corporate (executive) financial decisions.
- **CLO6**: Apply the knowledge of behavioural finance to improve investment and corporate performance.
- **CLO7**: Develop strategies to manage wealth effectively and wisely in inefficient financial markets such as mispriced assets, shocks, news.
- **CLO8**: Shows self-reliance when working independently and collaboratively
- **CLO9**: Demonstrate ethical awareness and reflection in business context
