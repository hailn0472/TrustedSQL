### Description - ADB201
Description: This course introduces students to the fundamentals of book design and printing technology. Students will learn about book anatomy, layout principles, typography, image integration, and basic binding techniques. Emphasis is placed on audience-centered design, effective visual communication, and hands-on experimentation with materials. Objectives: Upon completion of this course, students will be able to: - Design comprehensive book layouts, including covers, interior pages, and structural elements. - Select and apply appropriate typefaces, sizes, and visual elements to enhance readability and engagement. - Integrate illustrations and hands-on application of materials effectively within book layouts. - Demonstrate proficiency in basic bookbinding techniques. - Adapt design choices to suit specific audiences and contexts.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADB201

- **CLO1**: Apply the Fundamentals of book design: Understand the essential elements of book anatomy and design principles for effective layouts. Select appropriate typefaces, visual elements to effectively communicate content and engage different audiences.
- **CLO2**: Master Manual bookbinding techniques and Creative material use: Demonstrate proficiency in basic bookbinding methods (folding, stitching, gluing). Experiment with materials and techniques to achieve unique visual and tactile effects.
- **CLO3**: Explore Art book design concepts and Ideation: Analyze diverse examples of art books and their unique approaches. Develope creative concepts for book projects, considering form, content and audience engagement.
- **CLO4**: Conduct Design research and Project execution: Formulate research questions to guid the book design process. Design and execute the book project, demonstrating conceptual thinking and technical skills.
- **CLO5**: Evaluate Design solutions and Adapt to context: Critically assess book designs, considering their suitability for cultural and technical contexts. Select and justify the use of manual and/or digital methods to achieve specific design goals.
- **CLO6**: Develop Professionalism and Collaborative skills: Exhibit a dedicated work ethic, attention to detail, and a willingness to experiment and refine designs. Contribute effectively to team projects and the ability to give and receive constructive feedback.
