# ADG301 - Package Design


## Course Information - ADG301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Intermediate
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 slots
1 slot = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ADG201
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ADG301
By the end of this course Students will be able to: 1. Understand the purpose of packaging design 2. Create effective and innovative package design solutions. 3. Utilize effective packaging principles and techniques to influence consumer choice 4. Understand the form and function requirements that packaging must, enclose, inform and persuade. 6. Evaluate effective packaging methods and processes 7. Implement recommendations from focus groups in order to understand consumer behaviour and create a better 8. Collaborate within a group 9. Prepare professional presentations from initial phase research to a full comprehensive package. 10. Be conscious of sustainable design issues

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ADG301

- **CLO1**: Broaden knowledge of the theoretical, cultural, and historical contexts of packaging design
- **CLO2**: Define package design and understand marketing and bussiness factors such as Planning, execution, pricing, placement, promotion, advertising, merchandising, distribution, and sales are all part of the mix of activities involved in the packaging design in order to draws people to buy the products
- **CLO3**: Define the basic elements of Package Design Understand materials and production styles
- **CLO4**: Understanding the packaging design process from Pre-design, develope the design to finalize and Pre-production
- **CLO5**: Choose, define the product and design the package by using the tools and techniques for printing a single color that enable you to bring creative visual ideas to your design.
- **CLO6**: Understand the structure and material for the package
- **CLO7**: Understand the fundamentals of packaging design business management. For a products and its package to be successful, it must satisfy a consumer’s need on multiple levels. Communication with the client is perhaps the most critical management skill at a packaging design firm
- **CLO8**: know how to design the package for a product from concept to completion
- **CLO9**: LO9: Broaden knowledge of the Consumer Product Categories Materials and Tools
