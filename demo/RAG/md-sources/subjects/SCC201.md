### Description - SCC201
After completing the course of color science, students can master and apply The Experience of Color / Color Awareness / The Uses of Color / Color-Order Systems / Color Study in their design work. The course focuses on color theory, operation and influence of color in diverse areas of design and user experience. Besides, the subject supports students to form creative ability and arrange layout to form a balanced and coherent whole for the composition. Enhance the perception of backgrounds, colors, layouts and applications in specific decorative products. In addition, the subject helps students practice independent working habits and self-arrange personal work plans. Develop creative thinking ability through the diverse expression of ideas as the foundation for the project system in the following semesters. In addition, form an attitude of being willing to learn and develop yourself when given the opportunity.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SCC201

- **CLO1**: Recognize the relationship between colors and factors related to reading comprehension and analysis, creating meaning for colors
- **CLO2**: Define and explain color through the connection between 3 properties of color: Hue, Value, Saturation.
- **CLO3**: Detected Color instability by looking at color composition, Ground and Carried Colors, Placement and Color Change, Equilibrium, Simultaneous Contrast, Afterimage and Contrast Reversal, Complementary Contrast. Then, know how to apply this instability to the design to actively decide the outcome of the product.
- **CLO4**: Manage the Color Illusions, The Illusion of Depth, Spatial Effects of Colors, Transparence Illusion, Fluting, Vibration, Vanishing Boundaries, Luminosity, Bezold Effect, Optical Mixes
- **CLO5**: Construct and Organize the color in line with modern trends through understanding the history and theory of color.
- **CLO6**: Create color harmony in design products
- **CLO7**: Applying color and light in media and commercial design, interaction design and graphics-related fields
- **CLO8**: Having a professional style in design activities. Love to research and perfect themselvs in visual arts.
