### Description - DGT201
This course covering basic to advanced digital concepts with an emphasis on problem solving, troubleshooting, and applications.  Assessment scheme:  1) On-going asessment: - 03 Quizzes: 30% - 01 Assignment: 20% - Excercises: 10% 2) Final exam: 40% 3) Final Result: 100%  Completion Criteria: 1) Every on-going assessment component >0 2) Final Exam Score >=4 & Final Result >=5

### Student Tasks - DGT201
- Students must attend more than 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Usually access http://bigdatauniversity.com/ for downloading materials and reading

### Tools - DGT201
Technical facilities: Electronics basic components, Logic gates, Combinational Digital Ics
