### Description - TTM203
-Objective: The subject is aimed at providing a vital understanding of contemporary tourist behaviour and its application in a variety of sectors of tourism. -Description: Students will acquire broad knowledge of consumer behavior in tourism and hospitality industry. They will be able to describe and explain tourist behaviors through the perspectives of human psychology.They can also determine the role of tourist behaviors in the process of tourism product development, quality management and other marketing decisions. -Teaching Method: Basic input will be given by the lecturer in order to initiate discussions on the relevant topics. Therefore, students are required to prepare the reading material in advance. In class, short group works will take place. This should further stimulate discussion between students and allow them to immediately apply theoretical knowledge to mini-case studies. Additionally, discussion should allow students to reflect on observations of own or (other) tourists’ behaviour and identify linkages with management and marketing of tourism (products).

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - TTM203

- **CLO1**: To explain the concept and models of consumer behavior in tourism and hospitality industry
- **CLO2**: To get insight into the how today’s consumers select, purchase, use and experience the tourism and hospitality products.
- **CLO3**: To identify different market segments and analyse the consumer behavior in different market and across the sectors in tourism and hospitality industry
- **CLO4**: To update with the development trend and controversial issues of tourism behaviour internationally and in Vietnam
- **CLO5**: To practice the professional attitude and skills of achieving understanding of consumer behavior before any decision in business decisions
- **CLO6**: To apply the understanding of the consumer behavior into product development, quality management and other marketing decisions in tourism
