# IOC321 - IoT for Smart City_IoT cho thành phố thông minh


## Course Information - IOC321

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) =
45h (60 sessions) contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: IOT102, IOC311
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - IOC321
The course provides a deep understanding of the digital technologies, infrastructure, and social political forces shaping the future of our urban environments. This course begins by defining Smart Cities through lectures and case studies and drill down into the technologies shaping new and existing cities.

### Student Tasks - IOC321
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Student is reponsible to do all Worklabs - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose - Promptly access to the FU FLM at https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up-to-date course information

### Tools - IOC321
- Raspberry Pi - Arduino IDE - VS code - NodeMCU - List of devices attached

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IOC321

- **CLO1**: Basic understanding of Smart City concepts
- **CLO2**: Detailed knowledge of each components of Smart City
- **CLO3**: Learn about the basics of Hardware Infrastructure of Smart City
- **CLO4**: Learn about the basics of Software Infrastructure of Smart City
- **CLO5**: Planning the Smart City service design
- **CLO6**: Basic design ability of Internet of Thing and Cloud Data Center
- **CLO7**: Skill to develop smart city program
- **CLO8**: Practical understanding of Smart City infrastructure
- **CLO9**: Learn the management basics of Smart City projects
