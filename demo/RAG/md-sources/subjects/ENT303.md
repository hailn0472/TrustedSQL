# ENT303 - Tiếng Anh 4


## Course Information - ENT303

- **Credits**: 0
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor

Interactive Classroom Teaching

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5

### Description - ENT303
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises.

### Student Tasks - ENT303
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible for doing all exercises, assignments, and labs given by the instructor in class or at home and submitting them on time - Use a laptop in class only for learning purposes - Access the course website for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online support from teachers and other students, and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - ENT303
Course book ActiveTeach

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENT303

- **CLO1**: Apply a wide range of vocabulary and structures in the text book to initiate and maintain short conversations in familiar situations and to discuss topics in the text book.
- **CLO2**: Perform understanding of the main points, speaker's key messages and important details of clear standard conversations and short speeches at an average speed on familiar topics
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate ability to identify main ideas, supporting details and organization of B1 reading articles
- **CLO4**: Make basic inferences and predictions from context in B1 reading articles
- **CLO5**: Write simple, compound and some complex sentences
- **CLO6**: Write 150-word opinion paragraphs with a logical organization
- **CLO7**: Delegate tasks and demonstrate responsibilities in group work
- **CLO8**: Perform basic critical thinking over others' opinion with supporting arguments
- **CLO9**: Manage time effectively and perform certain independent learning

### Final exam
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: 4
- CLO: LO1-LO9
- Question Type: Listening: multiple choice, short answersReading: multiple choice, short answers, true/false/not given, matchingWriting: a paragraphSpeaking: cue-card question and follow-up questions
- Note: 1. Grammar - Vocabulary: 20%
Vocab 20 questions + Grammar 20 questions; MCQ 4 options, gap filling, matching; 20 minutes
2. Reading: 20%
18-20 questions; 2-3 texts -- 300-450 words/text; MCQ 4 options, gap filling, matching; Level: A2+, B1-; 25 minutes; Topic: 10 units in the book;
3. Listening: 20%
18-20 questions; 3 audios; MCQ 4 options, gap filling, matching; Level: A2+, B1-; around 3 mins/audio; 2 times listening; 25 minutes
4. Writing: 20%
30 minutes; 180-200 words ~ 2 paragraphs - one topic
5. Speaking: 20%
10 mins - campus' questions
