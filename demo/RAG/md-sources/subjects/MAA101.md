## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - MAA101

- **CLO1**: Solve a system of linear equations using Gaussian elimination
- **CLO2**: Perform basic operations on matrices and manipulate matrix transformations
- **CLO3**: Find the determinant, inverse and diagonalization of a matrix
- **CLO4**: Use vector operations to analyze and describe geometric onjects in the spaces R^2 and R^3
- **CLO5**: Find the dimension and bases of a subspace in R^n
- **CLO6**: Find the dimension and bases of an abstract vector subpace.
- **CLO7**: Analyze linear transformations between two vector spaces
- **CLO8**: Carry out orthogonalization process and use orthogonality to solve applications.
