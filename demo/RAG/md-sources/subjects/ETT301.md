### Description - ETT301
Objectives: The course is designed to help students have the essential foundation of stones of language assessment with ample practical examples to illustrate application in language. Students will be able to develop assessment literacy by understanding, addressing issues and applying concepts. Description: The course introduces students to fundamental principles and tools for language assessment. Concepts are presented with practical, understandable explanations and concise references to supportive research. Students can recognize different forms of testing and assessment and are equipped to design and evaluate practical, effective assessment techniques and apply them to classroom contexts. Teaching Methods: The teaching process includes various methods: - Lecture the lessons and exercises varied from individuals, pair or group work, whole-class discussion. - Guide students' learning with research, self-study with feedback. - Have interactive discussions of questions and issues that may arise during lessons. - Manage group work and enhance their interests in testing and assessments throughout the course. Assessment scheme: 1) On-going assessment: 100% - Progress test: 20% - Assignment 1: 50% - Assignment 2: 15% - Assignment 3: 15% 2) Final Result: 100% 3) Completion Criteria: a) On-going assessment >0, Each component >0 b) Final Result >=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ETT301

- **CLO1**: Understand assessment concepts and issues LO1.1 Understand differences between assessment and testing LO1.2 Distinguish among five different types of language tests and apply them in different contexts LO1.3 State primary current assessment issues LO1.4 Appreciate historical antecedent and language assessment research.
- **CLO2**: Understand principles of language assessment LO2.1 Understand the five major principles of language assessment and subcategories LO2.2 Analyze the relative, variable importance of each principle LO2.3 Apply each principle to classroom-based assessment instruments.
- **CLO3**: Design classroom language tests LO3.1 Analyze the purpose of a proposed test LO3.2 State the specific abilities to be assessed LO3.3 Understand test specifications for a proposed test LO3.4 Design a variety of items (test methods) for a proposed test LO3.5 Administer a classroom test LO3.6 Construct a rationale for scoring, grading, and giving feedback on a proposed test.
- **CLO4**: Understand standards-based assessment LO4.1 Understand the crucial role of standards in educational instruction and assessment LO4.2 Examine a set of standards for a specified age, level, and context and apply them to contexts LO4.3 Analyze the purpose, advantages, and disadvantages of standards-based assessment LO4.4 Apply principles of standardization to the construction of teacher-based standards.
- **CLO5**: Develop a standardized test LO5.1 Appreciate the link between standards-based assessment and standardized testing LO5.2 Evaluate and adapt standardized tests LO5.3 Analyze the steps taken to perform a construct validation of a standardized test LO5.4 Examine constructs underlying various standardized language proficiency tests.
- **CLO6**: Design four skills assessments, including listening, speaking, reading, and writing LO6.1 State a rationale for assessing each skill separately and with an integrated approach LO6.2 Develop assessments that focus on one or several micro-and macro skills of each skill performance LO6.3 Design assessments that target one or several modes of each skill performance.
- **CLO7**: Design grammar and vocabulary assessments LO7.1 State a rationale for treating form-focused assessment LO7.2 Discern, through the backdrop of the nature of grammar, the purposes and contexts for assessing grammatical knowledge LO7.3 Analyze the components of lexical ability and apply them to the assessment of vocabulary knowledge LO7.4 Design assessments that target one or several of the modes of performance.
- **CLO8**: Apply guidelines for assigning letter grades LO8.1 Develop scoring rubrics to assess student performance and provide feedback LO8.2 Evaluate that goes "beyond" letter grading.
- **CLO9**: Understand consideration of the ethnics of testing in an educational and commercial world driven by tests and respect in group work.
