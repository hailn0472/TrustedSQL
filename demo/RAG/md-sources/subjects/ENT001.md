### Description - ENT001
This course is designed to further develop the learner's confidence and fluency in using the English language and to enrich their learning experience through interactive lessons and communicative exercises. Upon completion of the course, students should be able to: 1. Demonstrate knowledge of a vocabulary range covered in lessons (including meaning, opposites, spelling and pronunciation) 2. Show understanding of grammar rules taught through exercises, know of the role of conjunctions, articles and prepositions in writing, and understand the simple present and past tense 3. Show understanding of the reading texts by responding to text questions 4. Make simple 'wh' and ‘yes/no’ questions, use vocabulary, acceptable expressions, and grammatical structures to respond to social communicative tasks 5. Use simple sentences correctly to respond to writing exercises in the textbook appropriately and write paragraphs on simple topics (my favorite subject, a person I like, the most beautiful place, etc…) 6. Listen and respond (orally and in writing) to each other, recorded texts and video clips 7. Develop effective communication and teamwork in different academic and social tasks 8. Develop ethics, self-study, professionalism, respect in different disciplines, group work, and creativity.
