### Description - CMC201c
This specialization explores three key genres of creative writing: short story, narrative essay, and memoir. You will learn the techniques used by skilled writers to craft compelling stories with memorable characters, engaging settings, and fresh descriptive styles. You will also analyze and provide constructive feedback on peer writing. In the Capstone project, you will draft, revise, and complete an original story in the genre of your choice. Through four courses, each focusing on a specific aspect of writing and taken in any order, you will enhance your writing skills, refine your ability to critique others' work, and draw inspiration from the literature you already enjoy reading.
