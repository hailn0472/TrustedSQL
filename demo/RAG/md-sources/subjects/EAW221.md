# EAW221 - English Academic Writing 2_Viết học thuật tiếng Anh 2


## Course Information - EAW221

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Advanced
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 0.7 hour final exam + 104.3 hours self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed EAW211
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - EAW221
This course is designed to provide a systematic introduction to and a thorough analysis of common writing assignments.  Teaching Methods: flexible. Teachers can select approaches and methods that are suitable for their class contexts.  Assessment structure: 1) On-going assessment - Assignments 03*15% 45% - Peer Reviews 03*5% 15% 2) Final Examination (FE) 40% 3) Final Result 100% 4) Completion Criteria: Each assignment >0, average of 3 peer reviews >0, Final Exam Score >=4/10 & Final Result >=5/10 - If FE < 4/10, student must resit exam;

### Student Tasks - EAW221
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to take the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by teacher after class - Use laptop in class only for learning purposes

### Tools - EAW221
- Internet - foxit reader for PDF files

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - EAW221

- **CLO1**: *Identify 6 stages of writing a comparative essay; * identify two types of outlines for comparative essays; *review the language of comparison; *review punctuation (commas, semicolons, colons, and apostrophes); *write a comparative essay including evidence/ statistics.
- **CLO2**: *Identify 6 stages of writing a problem-solution essay; *identify two types of outlines for problem-solution essays; *review the language of problems, solutions and evaluation; *write a problem-solution essay including evidence/ statistics; *review sentence fragments, comma splices and run-on sentences.
- **CLO3**: *Identify 6 stages of writing a cause-effect essay; * identify two types of outlines for problem-solution essays; *review the language of cause and effect; *write a cause-effect essay including evidence/ statistics.
- **CLO4**: *Identify 6 stages of writing an argumentative essay; * identify two types of outlines for argumentative essays; *review the language of opinions, arguments and concession; *review the technique of writing the counter-arguments and of how to avoid overgeneralization; *write an argumentative essay including evidence/ statistics.
- **CLO5**: *Identify 5 stages of writing a summary; *practice paraphrasing; *practice vocabulary of attribution; *write a brief summary of a book excerpt.
- **CLO6**: *Identify 5 stages of writing a response paper; *practice paraphrasing; *practice vocabulary of attribution; *write a brief response to a news article.
- **CLO7**: *Characterize ethics and professionalism in writing and group work/peer review.
