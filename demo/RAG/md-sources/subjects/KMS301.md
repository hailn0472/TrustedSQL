# KMS301 - Knowledge management system_Hệ thống quản trị tri thức


## Course Information - KMS301

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h) = 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: ITA301, ISM302
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - KMS301
By the end of this course Students will be able to: a) Knowledge: (what will students know?) • Understand knowledge and knowledge management • Understand knowledge management models • Understand concepts, structure, processes, techniques, and issues related to knowledge management. • Understand knowledge management tools and applicable situations • Know how to how to build and manage knowledge management system in the enterprise. b) Skills: (what will students be able to do?) • Build and manage knowledge management system in the real-time enterprise

### Student Tasks - KMS301
- Attend at least 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfil tasks given by intructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

### Tools - KMS301
- Microsoft office for documents and presentation. - Mindmapping tool, visual mapping tools. - Weka software

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - KMS301

- **CLO1**: Understand the importance of knowledge and knowledge management
- **CLO2**: Understand concepts, structure, processes, techniques, and issues related to knowledge management.
- **CLO3**: Develop a good understanding of knowledge management models
- **CLO4**: Understand knowledge management tools
- **CLO5**: Identify the appropriate KM tools to elicitiate techniques to build and manage knowledge management system
- **CLO6**: Understand Knowledge Management Strategy and Planning
- **CLO7**: Understand the arrangements, organization, and evaluation of a knowledge management system
- **CLO8**: Be able to build and manage knowledge management system in the enterprise.
- **CLO9**: Prepare and deliver coherent and structured verbal and written technical reports
