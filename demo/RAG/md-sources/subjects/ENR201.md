# ENR201 - SKILLFUL 2 - READING 2


## Course Information - ENR201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor level
- **Time Allocation**: Hours Per Course: 30 sessions
1 session = 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: Passed ENR101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENR201
This is Skillful Reading 2 course, extracted from Skillful reading and writing 2 and intended to use for Upper-intermediate students. After the course, students can be able to use reading techniques (previewing, scanning, skimming, etc.) to comprehend reading passages related to academic topics such as psychology, Sociology, Biology, Chemistry, Business Studies.

### Student Tasks - ENR201
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENR201

- **CLO1**: Identify the purpose of parts of the text
- **CLO2**: Identify types of supporting details
- **CLO3**: Skim, take-note and map the information
- **CLO4**: Identify definitions
- **CLO5**: Summarize the information
- **CLO6**: Identify main ideas, causes and effects
- **CLO7**: Take note with their own words
- **CLO8**: Identify supporting details
- **CLO9**: Identify reasons, facts, opinions
- **CLO10**: Identify supporting evidence for main ideas

- Completion Criteria: >0

- Note: Teacher is allowed to select which units content (topic or learning outcome) to focus on each examination session.

### Project/Assignment
- Weight: 50.0%
- Completion Criteria: >0
- CLO: LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, LO6, LO7, LO8, LO9, LO10
- Question Type: Each student chooses one book/novel in English (supposed to be a masterpiece in English) to read and write a report following the format provided by teacher.

### Final exam
