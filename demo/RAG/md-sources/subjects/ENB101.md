# ENB101 - Business Writing


## Course Information - ENB101

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor in English Language
- **Time Allocation**: 1 course = 30 sessions
1 session= 90 minutes
- **Pre-Requisite**: ENS411 & ENS421 & ENR401 & ENW401
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ENB101
This course focuses on the skills of writing the texts used in the sutras such as specific types of correspondence, meeting minutes, records, proposals, analysis reports, other forms of spoken and written communication in the workplace.

### Student Tasks - ENB101
- Attend more than 80% of contact hours in order to be accepted to the final examination - Actively participate in class activities - Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class - Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose - Read the textbook in advance - Access the course website (www.cms.fpt.edu.vn) for up-to-date information and material of the course, for online supports from teachers and other students and for practicing and assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENB101

- **CLO1**: Distinguish Common Forms of Business Communication
- **CLO2**: Review Personality Types in Business Communication
- **CLO3**: Identify and Understand Your Audience
- **CLO4**: Write Within Workplace Constraints
- **CLO5**: Choose Most Effective Wording
- **CLO6**: Understand and apply Basic Writing Principles in Common Forms of Business Communication
