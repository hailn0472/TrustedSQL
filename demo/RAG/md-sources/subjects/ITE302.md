# ITE302 - ETHICS IN INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY


## Course Information - ITE302

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Contact time: 30 slots
Lectures: 15
Tutorials/discusstion: 15
Home study: 26 slots
1 slot = 90'
- **Pre-Requisite**: Student have to be admitted to on-job training period
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - ITE302
This course covers the following topics: • An Overview of Ethics • Ethics for IT Professionals and IT Users • Computer and Internet Crime • Privacy • Freedom of Expression • Intellectual Property • Software Development • The Impact of Information Technology on the Quality of Life • Social Networking • Ethics of IT Organization

### Student Tasks - ITE302
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. '- Student is responsible to do all exercises given by instructor before, in and after class and submit on time - Constantly follow announcements on intranet/CMS at http://cms.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information - respect to company regulations during on-job training period
