## Course Information - ENS411c

- **Credits**: 3

- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5

### Description - ENS411c
This module aims at improving students' listening comprehension and speaking skills. Listening to class lectures and practice of note taking benefit studens in enhancing listening skills. In addition, discussion and presentation tasks offer students opportunities to practice speaking and improve their presentation skills.  1) Ongoing assessment: Student gets 0.5 bonus points for each course completed on time. The total bonus point is not greater than 1. 2) Practical Exam (PE) 3) Final Result (FR) =min (10, PE+ Bonus) 4) Completion Criteria: PE>=4 and FR>=5

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ENS411c

- **CLO1**: Know how and use note-taking techniques in listening to lectures
- **CLO2**: Explain complex ideas vividly and accessibly, design clear and compelling presentation slides, convey passion for a topic while maintaining professional credibility, and speak dynamically from notes and/or a manuscript.
