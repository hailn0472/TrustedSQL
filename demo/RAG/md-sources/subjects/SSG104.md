### Description - SSG104
This course covers both working in groups and communication skills. The course covers theories of communication, working in group, and activities for students to practice applying the theories in academic and working contexts.

### Student Tasks - SSG104
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises and assignments given by the instructor in class or at home and submit on time - Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - SSG104

- **CLO1**: Understand concepts of team work & communication skills
- **CLO2**: Organizing a business meeting
- **CLO3**: Develop career skills
- **CLO4**: Apply team work & communication skills
- **CLO5**: Understand the Psychology of Groups
- **CLO6**: Explain and apply thinking and Analysis in Critical & Creative Thinking
- **CLO7**: Apply business writing like email, letter, proposal, report…
- **CLO8**: Analyse ways to become effective and powerful leaders.
- **CLO9**: Understand leadership theories and model of leadership effectiveness.
- **CLO10**: Understand how conflicts appear in groups and how to manage them in cohesion.
- **CLO11**: Apply successful verbal and nonverbal in teamwork
- **CLO12**: Be able to present persuasive message professionally and effectively in learning and working environment
