## Course Information - DGP201

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)= 45h contact hours + 105h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: DRP101
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - DGP201
This digital painting course equips students with the foundational knowledge and skills to create compelling artwork using digital tools. Throughout the semester students will gain proficiency in industry-standard software (Adobe Photoshop) and explore various techniques to translate traditional art principles into a digital workflow and master creative problem-solving using digital drawing techniques.

### Student Tasks - DGP201
1. Class attendance is strongly encouraged. Attend at least 80% of class hours in order to be accepted to the final examination 2. Actively participate in class activities 3. Fulfill tasks given by instructor after class 4. Use their own laptop in class only for learning purpose 5. Read the textbook in advance 6. Access the course website (https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/) for up-to-date information

### Tools - DGP201
Student: - Personal Laptop - Graphic Drawing Tablet

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - DGP201

- **CLO1**: Recognise the use of basic tools and techniques of Adobe Photoshop software for digital painting.
- **CLO2**: Explain the principles of art forms, light and composition as they apply to digital painting.
- **CLO3**: Execute textures and materials in digital paintings to enhance realism.
- **CLO4**: Apply basic and advanced digital painting techniques to create original artworks, including, still life, portraits and landscapes
- **CLO5**: Design and create a comprehensive digital painting project that integrates all elements learned, including composition, lighting, color, and texture.
- **CLO6**: Demonstrate a professional and positive attitude toward the creative process in digital painting.
