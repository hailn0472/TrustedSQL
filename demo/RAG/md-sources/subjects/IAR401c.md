### Description - IAR401c
This course will introduce the student to the essential aspects of information assurance and incident response (IR). The student will be provided with the tools, techniques and industry accepted methodologies so that upon completion of the course the student will be able to describe key concepts of network security and IR and how those concepts apply to themselves and their organization. The course is based on Courses 3 and 5 of the SSCP Professional Training Certificate, the whole will show employers you have the IT security foundation to defend against cyber attacks – and puts you on a clear path to earning SSCP certification.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - IAR401c

- **CLO1**: Understand the risk management process
- **CLO2**: Define procedures to implement and operate wireless technologies
- **CLO3**: Perform security assessment activities
- **CLO4**: Describe processes for operating and maintaining monitoring systems
- **CLO5**: Analyze and report monitoring results
- **CLO6**: Understand the IR and recovery process
- **CLO7**: Describe the supporting role in forensic investigations, BC and DRP processes
- **CLO8**: Understand security issues related to networks
- **CLO9**: Identify protective measures for telecommunication technologies and define processes for controlling network access
- **CLO10**: Identify processes for managing LAN-based security and describe procedures for operating and configuring networked-based security devices
