### Description - LAE102
"This course explores some of the fundamental theories, models and concepts in the field of media ethics and law. The course will also introduce influential ethical theories and perspectives, explore changing societal demands and expectations of media creation and media use, and we will elaborate on existing law and ethical norms for media professionals. The course includes major parts: - Press and media law; - Professional ethics of journalists; - Intellectual property rights in media - Social responsibility in communication activities Furthermore, this course will guide students to further explore many legal issues related to the emergence of AI technology products, especially issues related to intellectual property, when AI can answer questions that users ask, besides being able to write poetry, write reports, write essays, media master plan, etc. So how do countries' intellectual property laws regulate about AI? In addition, students will also be exposed to case studies about ethics and responsibility on Social media platforms. To meet the objectives of the course, in addition to common teaching methods such as lecture, brainstorming, group discussion, instructor will use some teaching methods unique to law, such as case studies, problem solving, moot court, etc. After following this course, you will be able to understand law and regulations in media and communication. They also understand ethical dilemmas and develop a well-substantiated argumentation for ethical decision making in a variety of media-related contexts."

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - LAE102

- **CLO1**: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of laws and ethics in communications in local and global context
- **CLO2**: Develop expertise in identifying intellectual property infringements in communications, including legal challenges with emerging AI technology integration.
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate proficiency in establishing efficient protection strategies and employing relevant licencing laws to secure intellectual property in media and communication.
- **CLO4**: Apply laws and ethical principles in communication, ensuring alignment with social responsibilities and ethical standards.
- **CLO5**: Develop the capacity for lifelong self-learning to adapt and excel in constantly evolving communication environments.
