# VNC104 - Vietnamese Culture _ Cơ sở văn hóa Việt Nam


## Course Information - VNC104

- **Credits**: 3
- **Degree Level**: Bachelor
- **Time Allocation**: Study hour (150h)
= 45h contact hours + 1h final exam + 104h self-study
- **Pre-Requisite**: None
- **Min Avg Mark To Pass**: 5
- **Scoring Scale**: 10

### Description - VNC104
This course is designed to provide students with a understanding of the foundations and the dimensions of Vietnamese culture, origin, characteristic; pay particular attention to the relationship between Vietnamese culture and the present-day life of the Vietnamese people and to the different elements of the culture which are under the social structures, belief systems, literature, arts, customs, etc... Topics mainly include: (1) the foundations of Vietnamese civilization: its geography, language, and history; (2) the core concepts in Vietnamese thoughts and religions: Aninism, Taoism, Buddhism, Confucianism, Catholicism, the new sects religion;...(3) Literature (4) Society and Life, including education, the role of women, Vietnamese food, festivals and leisure activities ; (5) Arts and Architecture (6) Vietnamese performing arts like Ca trù, Tuồng, Hát Bội... -Teaching method: Presentation, Discussion, teamwork, critical thinking, field-trip.

### Student Tasks - VNC104
- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination. - Student is responsible to do all exercises, assignments and labs given by instructor in class or at home and submit on time. - Use laptop in class only for learning purpose. - Promptly access to the FLM at https://flm.fpt.edu.vn for up-to-date course information.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - VNC104

- **CLO1**: Understand the foundations and dimensions of Vietnamese culture. Interpret and absorb the essence of Vietnamese culture.
- **CLO2**: Explain Vietnam's history and the change of Vietnamese culture over the different historical periods.
- **CLO3**: Analyze cultural phenomena.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate the problem from the cultural perspective, aesthetic creation and thriving for success in the globalization context.
- **CLO5**: Developing essential skills in building or constructing a typical product reflecting cultural aspects.
- **CLO6**: Having a professional attitude, critical thinking and teamwork.
