### Description - BKG302
The course provides students with continuous information and knowledge about financial appraisal of investment projects, how to manage the investment projects more effectively in order to survive competition and takeover in practical environment. Topics include: Capital budgeting; Project Cash flow and its forecasting; Essential formulae in Project appraisal; Project analysis under certainty; Project analysis under risk; Sensitivity and break-even analysis; Simulation concepts and methods; Financial modeling case study in forestry project evaluation

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - BKG302

- **CLO1**: Understand definition of capital structure, cost of capital and how optimize capital struture is formed up. Identify ways to calculate capital budeting through methods of accounting returns, payback period, NPV (net present value)
- **CLO2**: Understanding and applying quantitative forecasting techniques for a given project
- **CLO3**: Identify risks in finance and how these risks are managed in the context of financial stress and mismanagement
- **CLO4**: Able to explain and apply concept of "sensitivity analysis" and "break even analysis"
- **CLO5**: Able to deploy and apply simulation to the evaluation of project financial risks
- **CLO6**: Understand the purpose and Identify structure of linear programming models
