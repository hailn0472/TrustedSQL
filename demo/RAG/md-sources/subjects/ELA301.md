# ELA301 - Phân tích văn học Tiếng Anh_English literary analysis

- **Credits**: 3

### Description - ELA301
-Objective: Introduce students to a variety of literary genres and their key characteristics. Develop students’ ability to perform close readings of literary texts. Enhance students’ critical thinking and analytical skills. Foster students’ understanding of the relationship between literature and its historical, cultural, and social contexts. Improve students’ written and oral communication skills through literary analysis. Apply the students' understanding of literary analysis into projects. -Description:This course provides a foundational understanding of literary analysis. Students will explore a diverse range of literary genres, including fiction, poetry, drama, and film. Through close reading and critical thinking, students will develop essential skills in interpreting and analyzing literary texts. The course emphasizes the development of analytical frameworks and the ability to identify and discuss key literary elements such as character, plot, setting, theme, style, and language. Students will also learn to situate literary works within their historical, cultural, and social contexts. By the end of the course, students will be equipped with the tools to engage in sophisticated literary analysis and articulate their interpretations effectively in written and oral forms through text-oriented, author-oriented, reader oriented, context-orriented approahces -Teaching method: Lecture, seminar, interview, Flipped-classroom format

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) - ELA301

- **CLO1**: Identify and analyze key literary genres and elements (e.g., character, plot, theme, language, style) to understand how literary texts reflect and challenge societal norms.
- **CLO2**: Apply multiple analytical frameworks (text-oriented, reader-oriented, context-oriented, author-oriented) and close reading techniques to interpret literary works critically and independently.
- **CLO3**: Synthesize insights into structured academic and creative outputs, such as persuasive arguments, scripted performances (e.g., mock trials), and analytical essays, supported by appropriate use of MLA citation and secondary sources.
- **CLO4**: Develop empathy and critical awareness of diverse human experiences—particularly related to gender, power, and morality—through engagement with literary characters and ethical dilemmas.
- **CLO5**: Cultivate a reflective mindset by examining one’s own values in response to the social, cultural, and historical contexts embedded in literature.
