# BEN_CHN_K19B - CTĐT ngành Ngôn ngữ Anh, chuyên ngành Anh-Trung


**English Name**: Bachelor of English Studies, English-Chinese major


## Curriculum Information - BEN_CHN_K19B

- **Decision**: 1374/QĐ-ĐHFPT dated 12/29/2023

### Description - BEN_CHN_K19B
Objectives of the program
General objective:
The bachelor's program in the English language, majoring in English - Chinese aims to train bachelors of good quality: having the qualities and capacity to meet the actual needs of society; have the ability to use English fluently with a minimum level equivalent to level 5 according to The Vietnam Six-level of Foreign Language Proficiency Framework or level C1 according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and Chinese with the minimum level is equivalent to HSK4 according to the six-level Chinese Proficiency Test or B2 level according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR); have specialized knowledge of the language, culture, as well as the knowledge and skills necessary to work in the fields related to the specialized training. Students have a strong self-study ability, are capable of self-training and accumulating professional knowledge, qualities, and skills to participate in the study, research at higher levels, or become experts in the field of expertise, flexible working in a domestic and international environment.
Detailed objectives:
PO1: Enable students to develop physically, mentally, and intellectually, as well as develop students' outlook on life, and sense of national pride by equipping students with general knowledge of political theory, law, economics, social, physical education, music, and national defense education.
PO2: Provide students with basic knowledge of English and Chinese languages, and knowledge of countries, cultures, and people of English or Chinese-speaking countries.
PO3: Develop students' language skills, combined with communication skills, study, active research, and flexibility in studying and working in a multicultural environment; at the same time, equip knowledge and sharpen the entrepreneurship mindset and entrepreneurial experience.
PO4: Orientate students towards having the right attitude and work ethic, being able to think creatively, working in groups, working independently, and having the ability to solve problems related to jobs and industries that use English and Chinese languages, and effectively combining professional knowledge, being capable of lifelong learning for personal and professional development.
PO5: Help students to master the English language at an advanced level and the Chinese language at an intermediate level.
Career options after graduation
Students majoring in English, focusing on English - Chinese can work in domestic enterprises, foreign-invested enterprises, representative offices, and domestic and international organizations in the fields of tourism, commerce, business, import-export, communication, aviation, etc.,

## Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs) - BEN_CHN_K19B

- **PLO1**: Demonstrate basic knowledge of social sciences, politics, law, and national defense and security, contributing to the formation of worldview and scientific methodology.
- **PLO2**: Demonstrate the mindset and ability to implement start-up projects, creative spirits, critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
- **PLO3**: Communicate and work in groups effectively in academic and practical environments.
- **PLO4**: Demonstrate professional behavior, ethicalness, social responsibility, and willingness to serve the community.
- **PLO5**: Cultivate strong mental and physical health, express national identity, and be confident in global integration.
- **PLO6**: Develop self-study and lifelong learning skills and capabilities to adapt to the constant change of technology and society.
- **PLO7**: Demonstrate English language capacity in 4 skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing fluently and achieve language proficiency in level 5 according to the 6-level Foreign Language Proficiency Framework for Vietnam or equivalent to level C1 according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), to serve effectively for academic, social, and handle professional situations.
- **PLO8**: Acquire and apply basic knowledge of levels and aspects of the English language; integrate language competence and skills in research, synthesis, analysis, and evaluation of data and information into academic and professional work, and problem-solving in a variety of written/oral forms.
- **PLO9**: Recognize and understand basic human, cultural, and ethical knowledge and characteristics; and apply them to professional learning and working environments, as well as multicultural environments.
- **PLO10**: Demonstrate Chinese language capacity in 4 skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing at a level equivalent to HSK4 according to the six-level Chinese Proficiency Test or equivalent to level B2 according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) or to level 5 according to the 6-level Foreign Language Proficiency Framework for Vietnam, to serve effectively in academics, social, and work professional situations.
- **PLO11**: Acquire the vocabulary, sentence patterns, terminology, and corresponding expressions in the profession; as well as comprehend the definitions, professional characteristics, and basic professional knowledge, competencies, attitudes, and necessary qualities in the chosen profession.
- **PLO12**: Be able to apply in practice the knowledge and skills of language, professional knowledge and skills, and interdisciplinary knowledge of languages, culture, technology, economy, politics, society and science, hence be able to study and work in the chosen field with confidence and a professional attitude and demeanor.
